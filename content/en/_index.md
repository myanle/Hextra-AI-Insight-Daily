### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  🏋️‍♀️ **Claude Code** just launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to combat that pesky **"AI brain drain"** problem from over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding you to pause at crucial steps, roll up your sleeves, and complete tasks yourself. The goal? To truly master those coding skills.

2.  ✨ **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, super lightweight large model, is making waves! With a mere 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  🤯 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just dropped some mind-blowing plans for OpenAI's future: talk about investing trillions (!) into data centers and next-gen AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** with goals to totally disrupt search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like an internet bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release? That's just the beginning, signaling "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  ✨ **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its tenth anniversary, officially kicking off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI talent, recruiting for roles like AI Industry Lead Reporter, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editor Interns. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts driving their concerns about potential AI risks.
