1.  **Claude Code** just dropped its brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** that comes from leaning too hard on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at key steps and actually *do* the work themselves, so they truly master those programming skills. 💪
2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a total powerhouse despite having only 270M parameters. It slays **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building super light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀
