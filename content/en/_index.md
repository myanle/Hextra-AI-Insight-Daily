1.  Claude Code just dropped a fresh "Learning Mode" to combat that scary "AI brain drain" from over-reliance on AI. 2.  Google just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a super lightweight large language model packing a punch with just 270M parameters! This little tiger isn't messing around; it slays at instruction following and text structuring, even outperforming some much bigger models. Plus, its low power consumption means it can run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, perfect for building snappy, low-cost AI apps on the go. 🚀📱

Altman reckons we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will mark "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯💰 Buckle up!

12. Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton have some contrasting views on AI safety. Li Fei-Fei is all about the "design, governance, and values" side of things, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligent AI running amok, suggesting we design "AI that cares about humans." Both "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts for potential AI risks. 🤯🤖
