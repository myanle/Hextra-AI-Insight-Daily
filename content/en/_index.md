### **Today's AI News**

1.  🤖💡 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**. This mode aims to tackle the issue of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by over-reliance on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks hands-on, helping them truly master coding skills.

2.  🚀✨ **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google, boasts only 270M parameters. Yet, it performs exceptionally well in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it ideal for building lightweight, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  🤯💰 **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has outlined an ambitious roadmap for OpenAI. This grand vision includes investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface** (BCI) domain with plans to disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we are currently in a critical AI era, akin to the internet bubble, with the release of GPT-5 marking "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  🎉🥳 **Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its tenth anniversary**, marking the dawn of the ASI era! They're actively recruiting for various AI-related positions, including lead AI industry reporter, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join them! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Job Openings](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  ✨📊 **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. 🤔🤖🛡️ **Li Fei-Fei and Geoffrey Hinton** hold differing views on AI safety concerns. Li Fei-Fei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton worries about uncontrolled superintelligence, advocating for the creation of **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are presented as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
