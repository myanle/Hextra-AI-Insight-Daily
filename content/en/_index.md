### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-spanking-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🎉 This cool feature is all about tackling that dreaded **"AI Brain Atrophy"** issue from relying too much on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and really master those programming skills. No more mental laziness! 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, is a real powerhouse! 🚀 Even though it only has 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. How cool is that? 😎

3.  **OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman**, has painted a seriously ambitious picture for the company's future! 🌌 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and rolling out new AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** space, with plans to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI era, kind of like the dot-com bubble, and the launch of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, and the worst of times." Wild, right? 💰

4.  **Xinzhiyuan's** 10th-anniversary bash is here, kicking off the AGI era! 🎉🥳 And guess what? They're on the hunt for AI talent, including AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they're rolling out the red carpet for you! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  Say hello to **Hi3DEval**! 😎 This is a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system cooked up by Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with a bunch of universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to give 3D models a multi-dimensional check-up on their overall shape, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** have some differing views on AI safety—and it's a big deal! 🤖 Fei-Fei Li is zeroing in on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we design **AI that actually cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts highlighting AI's potential risks. Food for thought! 🤔
