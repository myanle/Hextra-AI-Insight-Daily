### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that whole **"AI brain drain"** issue stemming from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, prompting users to pause at key steps and actually *do* the tasks themselves, helping them truly nail down those programming skills.
2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, rocks a tiny 270M parameters but still crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building quick, cost-effective, and featherlight AI apps.
3.  💰🤯 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has sketched out a seriously ambitious vision for the company: we're talking trillions of dollars invested in data centers and next-gen AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interface (BCI)** tech with plans to shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in a critical AI phase, kinda like the dot-com bubble, with the GPT-5 launch signaling "the best of times and the worst of times."
4.  🥳🎯 **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the AGI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns, so if you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join the crew! "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts at the heart of AI's potential risks.
