**Claude Code** just dropped a "Learning Mode" to help users dodge the "AI brain drain" and keep their skills sharp! 🧠 Meanwhile, **Google** open-sourced its super-lightweight Gemma 3 270M model, which is crushing it on performance and is easy to deploy. And **OpenAI**? Their CEO Altman is dreaming big, talking trillions in investments, BCI tech, and rocking the AI world during this critical phase. 🚀

### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🤖🧠 This awesome feature tackles the dreaded "AI brain drain" that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of just giving answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause at key steps and get hands-on, so you truly master those programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, is a real powerhouse! 🐯🚀 Packing only 270M parameters, it totally crushes tasks like **instruction following** and **text structuring**, even outperforming some much larger models. Its super low power consumption means you can even run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building fast, cheap, and lightweight AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Altman just laid out a massive vision for the company's future! 💰🌌 We're talking trillions in investments for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**. The goal? To totally revolutionize search and social media. He believes we're in a crucial AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release will mark "the best of times, and the worst of times."

It's **XinZhiYuan's (New Intelligence)** 10th anniversary celebration, kicking off the ASI era! 🎉🥳 They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. 5.  Get ready for **Hi3DEval**! 😎 This new, more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system was cooked up by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to give a multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. **Li Feifei** and **Hinton** are on different pages when it comes to AI safety! 🤖 Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is worried about super-intelligent AI spiraling out of control, advocating for designing **AI that cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "tool convergence" are the two core concepts highlighting potential AI risks.
