### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out its brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠 This bad boy aims to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** problem caused by relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," pausing users at key steps and encouraging them to get hands-on. The goal? True mastery of programming skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's latest open-source, lightweight large model, and guess what? It packs a punch with only 270M parameters! 🚀 It's absolutely killing it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 💡

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out an epic vision for the future! 💰 We're talking trillions of dollars poured into data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** realm. The plan? To totally disrupt search and social media as we know it. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI moment, much like the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, and the worst of times." 🌌

4.  It's a party! 🎉🥳 **Xinzhiyuan's 10th-anniversary celebration** is here, kicking off the ASI era! Xinzhiyuan is actively looking for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! 5.  **Hi3DEval** is a new, super scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system developed by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. 😎 It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol covering object-level, component-level, and material themes, offering a multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. Check it out: [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and the [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard).

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** have some different takes on AI safety issues. 🤖 Fei-Fei Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we design **AIs that care about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
