### **Today's AI News**

1.  🔥 **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that dreaded **"AI brain drain"** issue stemming from over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," nudging you to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on, so you genuinely master those programming skills.
2.  🌟 Say hello to **Gemma 3 270M**! Google just open-sourced this lightweight large model, clocking in at a mere 270M parameters. But don't let its size fool you—it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Plus, its low-power footprint means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps.
3.  🌐 **OpenAI**'s CEO, Sam Altman, just laid out a seriously ambitious roadmap for the company. We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** realm with plans to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI moment, kinda like the internet bubble days, and the GPT-5 launch will mark "the best of times, the worst of times."

The big scary concepts here are "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" – two core ideas for potential AI risks.
