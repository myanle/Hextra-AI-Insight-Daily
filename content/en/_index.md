### **Today's AI News**

1.  💡 **Claude Code** has launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode,"** aiming to tackle the issue of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at critical steps and complete tasks themselves, thereby truly mastering programming skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google with only 270M parameters, performs exceptionally well in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption allows it to run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  🌟 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out OpenAI's ambitious blueprint, which includes investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware. OpenAI even plans to venture into **brain-computer interfaces**, aiming to disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we are at a critical AI juncture, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  🥳 **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! Xinzhiyuan is currently recruiting for various AI-related positions, including AI industry reporter, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. They warmly invite AI enthusiasts to join! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  🔬 **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It employs a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. 🧐 **Li Feifei and Hinton** hold differing views on AI safety issues. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about the loss of control over superintelligent AI, suggesting the creation of **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts of potential AI risks.
