### Today's AI News

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 💡, designed to tackle the infamous **"AI brain drain"** problem caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about giving you direct answers; instead, it acts like a programming "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at key steps and complete tasks hands-on to truly master their coding skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** 🚀, a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google, boasts a mere 270M parameters yet shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, outperforming some significantly larger models. Its low-power footprint means it can even run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a grand vision for OpenAI 💰🌌: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface** arena. He plans to disrupt search and social media, believing we're in a pivotal AI era reminiscent of the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release signaling "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its tenth anniversary 🎉, marking the dawn of the ASI era! They're currently on the lookout for AI industry reporting leads, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns, among other roles. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** 😎 is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to perform multi-dimensional analysis on the overall shape, local structure, and material realism of 3D models. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project 💪 offers learning paths to construct various technologies from scratch, empowering users to master programming skills through hands-on practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** 🚀 provides a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan, covering every aspect from basic certifications to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  **awesome-mac** 🍎 is a treasure trove for Mac users, compiling a collection of advanced software for macOS. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **parlant** 🤖 is an LLM agent that simplifies large language model (LLM) applications, making them super easy to deploy and use. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** 🤔 is an open-source AI productivity platform that integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, instant messaging, and an online collaborative office system. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou broke down the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** 🤯 in his Stanford CS25 course, explaining that LLM inference is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and exploring techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton hold different views on AI safety concerns 🤖. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton worries about superintelligent AI losing control and suggests designing **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts highlighting potential AI risks.
