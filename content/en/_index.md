This declaration is seen as a response to a recent DONGCHEDI assisted driving test, but it underscores Tesla's commitment to enhancing driving safety through **AI technology**.

5.  **Intel** is making massive manufacturing plan adjustments – factory delays, shelved projects, is its future looking grim? 📉 Intel's Q2 earnings report spilled the beans: the company is undergoing a huge structural overhaul. CEO Pat Gelsinger mentioned that investments in capacity over the past few years were "**ahead of demand and not wise enough**," leading to fragmented factory layouts. Intel is canceling several manufacturing projects, including shelving chip factories and assembly-test facilities in Germany and Poland, and consolidating its Costa Rica test operations into Vietnam and Malaysia. The $28 billion Ohio chip factory construction? Delayed again! ![Intel](https://pic.chinaz.com/picmap/201811151633430117_47.jpg) Intel has already laid off 15% of its workforce, planning to reduce total employees to 75,000 by year-end, and has slashed management ranks by 50%. This whole shebang aims to boost efficiency, cut redundancy, and enhance market competitiveness. But does this signal a decline in Intel's competitiveness in the **global semiconductor market**? We'll have to wait and see what the future holds!
