### **Today's AI News**

1.  💪 **Claude Code** just dropped its new **"Learning Mode,"** tackling the **"AI brain atrophy"** problem that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like a coding "personal trainer," nudging users to pause at key steps and get hands-on, truly mastering their programming skills.

2.  💡🚀 **Google's open-sourced Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight large language model, boasting only 270M parameters. Despite its size, it shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models! Its low power consumption means you can run it on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  💰💫 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has sketched out a grand vision for OpenAI: pouring trillions into building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interface** tech, with plans to shake up search and social media. He believes we're in a pivotal AI era, much like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release will mark a "best of times, worst of times" moment.

4.  🥳✨ **New Intelligence** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the Age of ASI! They're currently scouting for AI industry lead reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! The core concepts behind AI's potential risks are 'goal misgeneralization' and 'instrumental convergence'.
