To truly nail those coding skills! 💪
2.  🚀 Say hello to **Gemma 3 270M**! This super lightweight large model, open-sourced by Google, is making waves. Despite boasting a mere 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. What's even cooler? Its low-power consumption means it can happily run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. ✨
