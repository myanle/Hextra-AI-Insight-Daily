### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  `Claude Code` has rolled out a brand-new "Learning Mode" 💡, aiming to tackle the problem of "AI brain atrophy" caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks, helping them truly master programming skills. 💪

2.  `Gemma 3 270M`, a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google, is making waves! 🚀 It boasts only 270M parameters yet excels in instruction following and text structuring tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption allows it to run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it ideal for building light, fast, and cost-effective AI applications. ✨
