### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🤖🧠 This mode is designed to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** problem that comes from relying too much on AI for too long. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at critical steps and get their hands dirty, so they genuinely master programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large model 🐯🚀, packs a punch with only 270M parameters. It absolutely shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models! Its low-power consumption means it can run smoothly on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and low-cost AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Altman just dropped an epic blueprint 💰🌌 for the company: we're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** realm, planning to totally disrupt search and social media! Altman believes we're smack dab in a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the release of GPT-5 truly marks "the best of times, and the worst of times."

4.  **New Intelligence** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! 🎉🥳 They're on the hunt for AI industry lead reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they're calling your name! [🔗 New Intelligence Recruitment Info](link_not_provided)

5.  **Hi3DEval** 😎, a more scientific, comprehensive, and professional 3D generation quality evaluation system, was jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to conduct multi-dimensional analysis on 3D models, covering their overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. Check it out! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou 🤯 gave a mind-blowing lecture on **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanisms during Stanford's CS25 course. 12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** 🤖 have differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about superintelligence spiraling out of control, suggesting we design **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
