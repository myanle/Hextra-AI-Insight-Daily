1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 🤯! This cool feature tackles the real problem of **"AI brain drain"** from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," pushing you to pause at key steps and get your hands dirty, so you genuinely master those programming skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large model, is a total game-changer! 🚀 Clocking in at just 270M parameters, it absolutely crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run smoothly on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building super light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 📱✨
