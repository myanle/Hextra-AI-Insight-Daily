### Today's AI News

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"**! This cool feature tackles the real problem of "AI brain drain" that happens when we get too comfy relying on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding "gym coach," nudging you to pause at key points and actually get your hands dirty with tasks. The goal? To truly master those programming skills, not just fake it 'til you make it! 😉

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is seriously impressive! Even though it only packs 270M parameters, this bad boy crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its super low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. Talk about a little engine that could! 🚀

3.  **OpenAI's** CEO, Sam Altman, just spilled the tea on their mega-ambitious blueprint! We're talking trillions of dollars poured into building data centers and developing new AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** space, aiming to completely shake up search and social media as we know it. Altman reckons we're smack-dab in a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble days, and the GPT-5 launch? He says it marks "the best of times, the worst of times." Buckle up, folks! 🤯
