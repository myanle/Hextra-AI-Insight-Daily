### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code just dropped a brand-new **'Learning Mode'**! 🤯 It's designed to tackle that pesky **'AI brain drain'** problem from leaning on AI too much. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding fitness coach, guiding you to pause at key steps and get hands-on with tasks to really nail those programming skills. 💪

2.  Google just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**! 🎉 This lightweight large language model boasts just 270M parameters but absolutely shines at **instruction following** and **text structuring**, even outperforming some heftier models. Its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up lightweight, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a wild grand vision 🌠 for OpenAI: we're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces (BCIs)**, all aimed at shaking up search and social media. He reckons we're in a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release? That's gonna mark 'the best of times, and the worst of times.' Whoa! 💰

5.  **Hi3DEval** is here to shake things up! 😎 Developed by Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities, it's a super scientific and comprehensive system for evaluating 3D generation quality. It uses a three-layered evaluation protocol – object-level, part-level, and material themes – to dive deep into a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

Heads up, folks: 'Goal misgeneralization' and 'instrumental convergence' are two core concepts for potential AI risks.
