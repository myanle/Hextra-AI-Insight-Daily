### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a fresh "Learning Mode" designed to tackle the "AI brain atrophy" issue stemming from over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at key steps and get hands-on with tasks to truly master their coding skills. 💪
2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, boasts excellent performance in instruction following and text structuring tasks, despite its mere 270M parameters, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 🚀
3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has laid out an ambitious blueprint for OpenAI: investing trillions into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even venturing into the brain-computer interface (BCI) arena, with plans to disrupt search and social media. He reckons we're in a pivotal AI moment, much like the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times and the worst of times." 💰🧠
4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is throwing a party for its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI era! They're currently on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns, so if you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! 🎉 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](URL_NOT_PROVIDED_IN_ORIGINAL)

It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to run multi-dimensional analysis on the overall form, local structure, and material realism of 3D models. 🔬 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
