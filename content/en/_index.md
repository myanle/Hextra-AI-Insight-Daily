### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** 🚀, designed to tackle the infamous **"AI brain drain"** problem caused by over-reliance on AI. Instead of just giving you the answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "gym trainer," guiding you to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks to truly master your programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch with just 270M parameters! 💪 It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, outperforming even some much bigger models. Its low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has sketched out an epic blueprint for the company 🤯: we're talking trillions of dollars for data centers, new AI hardware development, and even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch marks "the best of times, the worst of times" for tech.

4.  It's **Xinzhiyuan's** tenth-anniversary celebration, kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're also on the hunt for AI talent, including lead AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a new, super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system 💯, cooked up by Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with multiple universities. This system uses a three-tier assessment protocol (object-level, component-level, and material-theme) to conduct multi-dimensional analysis on the overall form, local structure, and material realism of 3D models. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist **Denny Zhou** broke down the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** in his Stanford CS25 course 🧠. 12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** have different takes on AI safety concerns 🤔. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts underlying potential AI risks.
