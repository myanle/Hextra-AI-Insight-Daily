### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"learning mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** problem stemming from long-term AI dependence. 🧠 Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like a coding "fitness coach," pausing users at key steps and nudging them to complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they truly master programming skills. 💪

2.  **Google's** open-source **Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight large model with a mere 270M parameters, but it's crushing it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. 🚀 Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for crafting nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 📱

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman painted a colossal picture for the company: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop cutting-edge AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interface** tech, with plans to totally disrupt search and social media. 🤯 He reckons we're at a pivotal AI moment, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the launch of GPT-5 signals "the best of times, and the worst of times." 💰

When it comes to AI safety, Li Feifei and Geoffrey Hinton have some seriously different takes. 🧐 Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we design **human-caring AI**. The core concepts of potential AI risks are "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence."
