### **Today's AI News**

1. Claude Code just dropped a brand new "Learning Mode" to tackle that pesky "AI brain drain" problem that comes from relying on AI too much. 💪🧠 Instead of just handing you the answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks so you can truly master those coding skills.

2. Say hello to Gemma 3 270M! 🚀 This lightweight large model from Google is open-source, rocks only 270M parameters, and totally slays at instruction following and text structuring tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Plus, its low-power mojo means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 📱💻

3. Sam Altman, OpenAI's CEO, just laid out OpenAI's super ambitious roadmap! 🌌 We're talking trillions of dollars poured into data centers and new AI hardware, even diving headfirst into brain-computer interfaces to totally disrupt search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble days, and the GPT-5 release screams "it was the best of times, it was the worst of times." 💰🤯

Hi3DEval, a new 3D generation quality assessment system, was jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. 😎 This system is way more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, checking out their overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. Denny Zhou, DeepMind's Chief Scientist, recently dropped some serious knowledge on **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanisms during a Stanford CS25 course. 🤯 He pointed out that LLM reasoning happens by generating a sequence of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Mind blown! [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. Li Feifei and Hinton have some differing takes on AI safety, but it's all good! 🤖 Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligent AI running wild and suggests we build **AI that actually cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts highlighting potential AI risks. Fascinating stuff.
