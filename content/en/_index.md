### **Today's AI News**

1.  🧠 **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode."** This innovative mode aims to tackle the **"AI brain drain"** issue stemming from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they truly master programming skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's lightweight open-source large model, packs a punch with just 270M parameters. It excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  💰 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a super ambitious blueprint for OpenAI. We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** realm, with plans to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI era, kind of like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release? That's gonna be a "best of times, worst of times" moment.

4.  🎉 Xinzhiyuan just kicked off its 10th-anniversary celebration, marking the dawn of the ASI era! They're also on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their team! 5.  ✨ **Hi3DEval**, a new, super scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. This system uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across various dimensions, including overall form, local structure, and material realism. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts for potential AI risks.
