### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a totally new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠💻 This cool feature is here to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** (aka skill atrophy) that comes from relying too heavily on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, prompting you to pause at crucial steps and get your hands dirty, helping you truly master those programming skills. No more AI dependency blues! 💪

2.  Get ready for **Gemma 3 270M**! 🚀 Google just open-sourced this seriously lightweight large model, clocking in at a mere 270M parameters. But don't let its size fool you—it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building super-fast, low-cost, and light AI apps. Talk about efficiency! 📱⚡

We're talking trillions of dollars poured into building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, with plans to even dive into **brain-computer interfaces** and totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble. He's saying the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, and the worst of times." Wild! 💰🌌

Say hello to **Hi3DEval**! 👋 Developed by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, this is a seriously comprehensive and scientific evaluation system for 3D generation quality.
