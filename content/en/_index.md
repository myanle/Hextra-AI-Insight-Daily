### Today's AI News

1.  🚀 **Claude Code** just dropped a slick new **"Learning Mode"**! This cool feature aims to tackle "AI brain drain," that pesky skill atrophy from leaning too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding users to pause and actually *do* the work at critical steps, so they truly master coding skills.

2.  ✨ Google just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a super lightweight large model. Clocking in at just 270M parameters, it totally crushes tasks like **instruction following** and **text structuring**, even outperforming some much larger models! Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building quick, cost-effective, and super light AI apps.

3.  🤯 OpenAI CEO Altman just laid out a mind-blowing vision for OpenAI! We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in a critical AI phase, kinda like the internet bubble days, and the GPT-5 launch screams, "It's the best of times, it's the worst of times."

4.  🥳 Xinzhiyuan just celebrated its 10th anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI talent, with openings for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  ✨ **Hi3DEval**, a snazzy new 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been jointly rolled out by the Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. This system is super scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tiered assessment protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to dive deep into a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and material realism with multi-dimensional analysis. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. 🧠 DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou recently dropped some serious knowledge at a Stanford CS25 course, breaking down the inference mechanism of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. 12. 🧐 Fei-Fei Li and Hinton have some differing takes on AI safety. Li is all about focusing on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests we design **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
