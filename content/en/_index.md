### Today's AI News 🚀

1.  `Claude Code` has launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 🧠 to tackle the issue of **"AI brain drain"** caused by prolonged AI dependency. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at critical steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they genuinely master programming skills. 💪

2.  `Google's Gemma 3 270M` is a lightweight open-source large model, with a mere 270M parameters. Yet, **Gemma 3 270M** excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low power consumption allows it to run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and cost-effective AI applications. ✨

3.  `OpenAI CEO Sam Altman` has laid out a colossal vision for `OpenAI`: investing trillions of dollars into data centers and new AI hardware development, even venturing into **brain-computer interface (BCI)** tech with plans to disrupt search and social media. `Altman` believes we're in a pivotal AI era, akin to the internet bubble, with the GPT-5 launch signaling "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯

4.  `Xinzhiyuan` is celebrating its 10th anniversary 🎉, marking the dawn of the ASI era! `Xinzhiyuan` is currently recruiting for various AI-related roles, including AI industry lead writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join! 🌟 `[🔗 Xinzhiyuan Job Openings](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)`

5.  `Hi3DEval` is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. Launched by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in collaboration with several universities, `Hi3DEval` uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. 📊 `[🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/)` `[🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)`

6.  The `build-your-own-x` GitHub project offers learning paths to build various technologies from scratch, helping users master programming skills through hands-on practice. 🛠️ `[🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)`

7.  `90DaysOfCyberSecurity` provides a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan, covering all aspects from basic certifications to advanced techniques. 🛡️ `[🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)`

8.  `awesome-mac` is a treasure trove for Mac users, compiling a collection of advanced software for macOS. 🍎 `[🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)`

11. `DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou`, in his Stanford CS25 course, explained the inference mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. `Zhou` pointed out that LLM reasoning is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also discussed techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. 🎓 `[▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)`

12. `Fei-Fei Li and Hinton` hold differing views on AI safety. `Li` focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while `Hinton` worries about superintelligent AI running amok, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks. 🤔
