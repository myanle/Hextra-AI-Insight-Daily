### **Today's AI News**

1. Claude Code has launched a brand-new "Learning Mode" 🤔. This mode aims to tackle the issue of "AI brain atrophy" caused by long-term reliance on AI. It doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks themselves, thereby truly mastering coding skills.

2. Gemma 3 270M, an open-source lightweight large model from Google, boasts a mere 270M parameters yet excels in instruction following and text structuring tasks, even outperforming some larger models ✨. Its low-power consumption allows it to run on devices like mobile phones or Raspberry Pis, making it ideal for building lightweight, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has outlined an ambitious blueprint for OpenAI 🤯. This includes investing trillions of dollars to build data centers, develop new AI hardware, and even venture into brain-computer interface (BCI) technology, aiming to revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we are currently in a critical AI era, reminiscent of the dot-com bubble, with the release of GPT-5 marking "the best of times and the worst of times."

5. Hi3DEval, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in collaboration with multiple universities 📊. 12. Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton hold differing views on AI safety concerns 🤔. Fei-Fei Li focuses on design, governance, and values, while Hinton is concerned about superintelligence spiraling out of control, suggesting we design AI that cares about humanity. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are identified as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
