### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠💻 This awesome feature aims to combat "AI brain atrophy" – you know, when you rely *too* much on AI and your skills start to fade. Instead of just handing you answers, it acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding you to pause at crucial steps and get your hands dirty, helping you truly master those programming skills. No more mental laziness! 😉

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large language model, is a total game-changer! 🚀 Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it totally crushes tasks like **instruction following** and **text structuring**, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its super low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. How cool is that? ✨

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a seriously ambitious roadmap! 💰🌌 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware. They're even eyeing **brain-computer interface** tech, with plans to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI era, kind of like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will mark "the best of times, the worst of times." Buckle up! 🎢
