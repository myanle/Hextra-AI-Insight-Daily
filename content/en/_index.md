### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** problem caused by over-reliance on AI. 🤖🧠 This isn't about giving you direct answers; it's more like a coding "fitness coach" that guides users to pause at critical steps, get hands-on, and actually master programming skills. 💡

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a total game-changer! 🐯🚀 With just 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for whipping up lightweight, super-fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just dropped a bombshell, outlining OpenAI's ambitious roadmap! 💰🌌 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** realm. The plan? To totally revolutionize search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 launch marks "the best of times, the worst of times."

Check this out: **Hi3DEval** is a brand-new, super scientific, comprehensive, and rigorous 3D generation quality evaluation system! 😎 It's a joint effort from the Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. Using a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—it provides a multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

When it comes to AI safety, **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some differing views. 🤖 Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild, advocating for the creation of **AI that cares about humans**. The two core concepts underpinning potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence."
