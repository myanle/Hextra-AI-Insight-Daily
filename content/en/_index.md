### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code just dropped a brand-new "Learning Mode" 🤯! It's designed to fight off "AI skill atrophy" – that pesky problem where you become too reliant on AI and your own skills start to fade. Instead of just spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy 🏋️, guiding you to pause at key steps and actually *do* the work yourself. The goal? To truly master those programming skills, not just outsource them.

2.  Say hello to **Gemma 3 270M**! 🚀 This is Google's open-source, lightweight large language model, boasting a mere 270M parameters. But don't let its size fool ya! This model absolutely crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger competitors. Its super low power consumption means it can run smoothly on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. Talk about a pocket-sized powerhouse! 💪

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing grand vision for the company 💰🌌. We're talking trillions of dollars poured into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface** realm. The goal? To completely shake up search and social media as we know them! Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI moment, akin to the internet bubble, with the upcoming GPT-5 launch signaling "the best of times and the worst of times." Buckle up! 🎢

4.  Huge congrats to Xinzhiyuan on its tenth anniversary! 🎉🥳 They're kicking off the ASI era and looking for some awesome AI talent to join their squad. They're on the hunt for roles like AI Industry Reporting Lead Writer, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love to hear from you! 12. When it comes to AI safety, **Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton** have some differing opinions 🤖. Li is all about focusing on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more concerned about superintelligence running wild, advocating for the creation of **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. They highlight two major potential AI risks: "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence." Deep stuff! 🤔
