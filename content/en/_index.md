### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the notorious problem of **"AI brain atrophy"** that comes from relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding gym trainer, nudging users to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks to truly master their programming skills. 😉

2. Say hello to **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large language model! 🚀 Despite its modest 270M parameters, this bad boy shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can happily run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 📱

3. **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a super ambitious roadmap, talking about investing trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** game. 🤯 The plan? To totally revolutionize search and social media as we know it! Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times, the worst of times." 🌌

When it comes to AI safety, **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some differing opinions. 🤖 Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests we ought to design **AI that actually cares about humanity**. The two big scary concepts for potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "tool convergence." Food for thought! 🤔
