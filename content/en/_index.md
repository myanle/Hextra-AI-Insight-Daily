### Today's AI News

1.  🧠 **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that notorious **"AI brain drain"** issue stemming from over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; it's more like your personal coding "fitness coach," prompting users to pause at crucial steps and get their hands dirty, truly mastering coding skills. 💪

2.  🚀 Google just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large language model packing only 270M parameters. But don't let the size fool ya – this little tiger absolutely crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models! Its low-power vibe means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for cranking out super-light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 📱💡

3.  💸🌌 **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** just laid out a wild, ambitious blueprint for OpenAI: we're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing fresh AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** game, planning to totally disrupt search and social media as we know it! Altman reckons we're smack-dab in an AI pivotal moment, kinda like the internet bubble era, and the GPT-5 launch? He says it marks "the best of times and the worst of times." 🤯

5.  😎 Say hello to **Hi3DEval**, a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system rolled out by the Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities. This bad boy uses a three-tier assessment protocol – object-level, component-level, and material-theme – to dive deep into a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. Pretty cool, right? Check out the [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and the [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)!

Two core concepts highlighting potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence." Deep stuff! 🧐
