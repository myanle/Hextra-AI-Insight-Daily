### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** just dropped its brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠 This bad boy is here to tackle "AI brain atrophy" – that pesky problem where relying too much on AI makes your skills atrophy. Instead of spoon-feeding you answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," pausing you at crucial steps to get your hands dirty and really master those programming skills. 💪

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is totally crushing it! 🐯🚀 With only 270M parameters, it still shines in instruction following and text structuring tasks, even outperforming some bigger models. Its low-power design means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building speedy, affordable, and lightweight AI apps. How cool is that?

3. **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing grand vision! 💰🌌 He's talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into brain-computer interfaces to completely disrupt search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the dot-com bubble. For him, the GPT-5 launch signals "the best of times, the worst of times." Wild stuff!

4. **Xinzhiyuan** is throwing a massive tenth-anniversary bash, kicking off the ASI era! 🎉🥳 They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and even editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! Check out their [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) for more deets.

5. **Hi3DEval**, a collaboration between Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, just dropped a way more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. 😎 It uses a three-tier protocol – object-level, component-level, and material theme – to give a multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty slick, huh? Check out the [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and the [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard).

The two big concepts for potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence." Food for thought, for real.
