### Today's AI News

1.  **Claude Code** just launched its brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🚀 This feature is designed to combat **"AI Brain Atrophy"** caused by over-reliance on AI. Instead of handing out direct answers, this mode acts like a coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on, and truly master their programming skills. 💪

2.  Get ready for **Gemma 3 270M**! 🚀 This lightweight open-source large model from Google packs a punch with only 270M parameters. It excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. ✨

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just painted a seriously grand vision for the company! 💰 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in data centers and new AI hardware development, even venturing into **brain-computer interfaces** to shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times and the worst of times." 🤯

4.  It's **Xinzhiyuan's** tenth-anniversary bash, kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! 🥳 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  Check out **Hi3DEval**! 🔥 This new 3D generation quality assessment system, launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, is more scientific and comprehensive. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to multi-dimensionally analyze 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. 📈 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
