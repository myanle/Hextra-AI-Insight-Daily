### **Today's AI News 🔥**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the **"AI Brain Atrophy"** issue 🧠 caused by long-term reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach" 🏋️‍♀️, guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on with tasks, and truly master coding skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, an open-source lightweight large model from Google with a mere 270M parameters, shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks ✨, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption allows it to run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and cost-effective AI applications 🚀.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has painted a grand vision ✨ for OpenAI: pouring trillions into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interface** tech, with plans to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're in a crucial AI era ⚠️, reminiscent of the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its 10th anniversary 🎉 and the dawn of the ASI era! They're currently on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns 🔍. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join! [Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system 🔬, has been launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The **`build-your-own-x`** GitHub project offers learning paths to build various technologies from scratch 🧱, helping users master coding skills through hands-on practice. [Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  **`90DaysOfCyberSecurity`** provides a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan that covers every aspect of cybersecurity 🛡️, from fundamental certifications to advanced techniques. [Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  **`awesome-mac`** is a treasure trove 💰 for Mac users, compiling various advanced software for macOS. [Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **`parlant`** is an LLM agent designed to simplify large language model (LLM) applications ✨, making it easy to deploy and use. [Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **`magic`** is an open-source AI productivity platform ⚙️ that integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. [Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. 12. Fei-Fei Li and Hinton have differing views 👥 on AI safety. While Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, Hinton is concerned about superintelligence running out of control and suggests designing **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts behind AI's potential risks.
