### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** designed to combat "AI brain atrophy" caused by over-reliance on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "gym trainer," guiding users to pause at critical steps, get hands-on, and truly master programming skills. 💪🧠

2.  Google's **Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight, open-source large model with a mere 270M parameters, yet it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models! 🚀 Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for building speedy, affordable, and lightweight AI applications. 📱💡

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just unfurled OpenAI's ambitious blueprint: investing trillions into data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** with plans to totally shake up search and social media. 🤯 He believes we're smack dab in a critical AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release marks "the best of times, the worst of times." 🔮✨
