### Today's AI Buzz

1.  Claude Code just dropped a brand-new "Learning Mode"! 🚀 This cool feature aims to combat "AI brain atrophy" – that's right, skill decay from over-relying on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and actually DO the work themselves. The goal? To truly master those coding skills. 💪

2.  Google's Gemma 3 270M just got open-sourced, and it's a game-changer! ✨ This lightweight LLM, with only 270M parameters, absolutely crushes it in instruction following and text structuring tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power nature means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for building speedy, low-cost AI apps. Talk about efficiency! 📱💡

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just painted a seriously grand picture for the future! 🤯 His vision for OpenAI includes investing trillions of dollars into building data centers, developing new AI hardware, and even diving into brain-computer interface tech to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times and the worst of times." Buckle up! 🎢

Hi3DEval is here to make 3D generation assessments way better! ✨ The Shanghai AI Lab, in collaboration with several universities, rolled out this more scientific and comprehensive quality evaluation system. It uses a three-tier protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, checking out their overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. Pretty neat, huh? Check out the [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and the [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard).
