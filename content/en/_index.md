### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"learning mode"** to tackle that pesky **"AI brain atrophy"** issue caused by relying too much on AI. Think of this mode not as a direct answer-giver, but more like your personal coding "gym trainer" – it guides you to pause at crucial points, get hands-on, and genuinely master your programming skills. 😎🧠

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is quite the performer! Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some much larger models. Its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for cranking out nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀✨

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman laid out an epic vision for the company: think trillions in investments for data centers and new AI hardware, even jumping into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI moment, akin to the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯 futurism!

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a snazzy new 3D generation quality assessment system, way more scientific and comprehensive, launched by Shanghai AI Lab alongside several universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to dive deep into a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and material realism with multi-dimensional analysis. Wanna learn more? Check out the [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and the [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)! ✨🔬

Li Feifei is all about focusing on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligent AI spiraling out of control, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of AI's potential risks. 🤔🤖
