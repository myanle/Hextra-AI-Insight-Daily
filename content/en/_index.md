1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** (aka skill atrophy from over-reliance on AI). 🧠 This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers. Nope, it's more like your personal coding coach, nudging you to pause at key steps and get hands-on, so you truly nail those programming skills. ✨
2.  Guess what? **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, is a total powerhouse! 💪 Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱
