### Today's AI News

1. Claude Code has dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that pesky **"AI brain drain"** problem caused by relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding gym trainer, nudging users to pause at key steps, get their hands dirty, and truly master those programming skills. 🧠✨

2. Gemma 3 270M, Google's open-source lightweight large model, rocks with just 270M parameters! It absolutely crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Thanks to its low-power mojo, it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up lightweight, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱
