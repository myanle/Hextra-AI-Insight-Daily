### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** issue caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on, and truly master their coding skills. 🧠💪

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google with a mere 270M parameters, is seriously crushing it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models! Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building speedy, affordable, and lightweight AI apps. 🚀📱

3. **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has painted a grand vision for the company: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** realm, aiming to revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, much like the dot-com bubble, with the release of GPT-5 signaling "the best of times, the worst of times." 💰🤯

12. **Li Feifei and Geoffrey Hinton** have some differing takes on AI safety. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild, advocating for the creation of **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks. Deep thoughts, indeed! 🤖🧐
