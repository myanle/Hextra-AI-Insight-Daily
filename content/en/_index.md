### **Today's AI News**

1.  🤖 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** problem caused by long-term reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on with tasks, and truly master their coding skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is crushing it! Despite its tiny 270M parameters, it excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Its low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  💰 **OpenAI's** CEO, Sam Altman, has unveiled a mind-blowing master plan for the company: investing trillions in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interface** tech to disrupt search and social media. He reckons we're in a crucial AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's launch marking "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯

4.  🥳 **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary, marking the dawn of the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporting leads, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! 💼 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](Xinzhiyuan Official Recruitment Link, Not Provided in Original Text)

5.  🔬 **Hi3DEval** is a new, more scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material-theme) to multidimensionally analyze the overall form, local structure, and material authenticity of 3D models. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

10. ✨ **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that packs a punch, integrating AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. 🧠 DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou, in his Stanford CS25 course, unpacked the reasoning mechanisms of **large language models (LLMs)**. He highlighted that LLM inference happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. 🤔 Li Feifei and Hinton have some differing takes on AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more concerned about superintelligence running wild, advocating for designing **AI that actually cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are pegged as the two core concepts of potential AI risks.
