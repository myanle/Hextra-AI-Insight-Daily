### Today's AI News

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-spanking-new "Learning Mode"! 🧠 This bad boy aims to tackle the dreaded "AI brain drain" problem caused by over-reliance on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and truly master those programming skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, a super lightweight large model open-sourced by Google, rocks with just 270 million parameters! 🚀 But don't let its size fool you—it crushes tasks like instruction following and text structuring, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power magic means it can run on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building quick, nimble, and cost-effective AI apps. ✨

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unfurled OpenAI's ambitious roadmap, talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware, even eyeing the brain-computer interface (BCI) arena! 🤯 The plan? To totally revolutionize search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in a critical AI phase, kinda like the internet bubble. He says GPT-5's release signals that "It was the best of times, it was the worst of times." Talk about high stakes! 💰✨

The big baddies they're talking about? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence"—two core concepts for potential AI risks. Food for thought! 🤔
