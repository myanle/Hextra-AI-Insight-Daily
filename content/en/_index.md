### **Today's AI News**

1.  Hold up! 🤖🧠 **Claude Code** just rolled out its spanking new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that looming **"AI Brain Atrophy"** problem when folks lean too heavily on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal programming "gym coach," nudging users to pause, dive in, and actually *do* the work to truly master those coding skills. Pretty neat, huh?

2.  Get ready for some serious small-package power! 🐯🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, rocks a tiny 270M parameters but still crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring**, even outperforming some bigger models. Its super low-power draw means it can run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. Sweet!

3.  Woah, hold onto your hats! 💰🌌 **OpenAI's** CEO Sam Altman just laid out an absolutely wild vision for the company: talking trillions of dollars into data centers and new AI hardware, even jumping into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. He's convinced we're in a make-or-break AI era, kinda like the dot-com boom, with GPT-5's launch screaming, "It's the best of times, it's the worst of times!" Big moves!

Pretty sweet for evaluating 3D content! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

FYI, "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts driving these potential AI risks. Deep stuff!
