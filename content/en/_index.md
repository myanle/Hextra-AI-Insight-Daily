### Today's AI News

1.  🤖 **Claude Code** has dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** designed to tackle the **"AI brain drain"** issue that pops up from relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about spitting out direct answers; instead, it guides users like a coding "personal trainer," nudging them to pause at key steps and get hands-on, so they truly master coding skills. 🧠💡

2.  🚀 **Google's open-source Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight large language model, rocking a mere 270M parameters. But don't let its size fool you! It slays **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for whipping up nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 📲✨

3.  💰 **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has laid out a super ambitious roadmap for the company: think trillions of dollars pumped into data centers and new AI hardware, and even diving headfirst into the **brain-computer interface** game, aiming to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're smack-dab in a pivotal AI moment, akin to the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times, the worst of times." 🌌🤯

4.  🎉 **Synced (Xinzhiyuan) is celebrating its 10th anniversary** and kicking off the ASI Era! Pretty neat! ✨

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts highlighting AI's potential risks. Food for thought! 🤔
