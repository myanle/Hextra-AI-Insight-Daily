1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out its brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! It's designed to combat "AI brain atrophy" – that skill fade when you lean too much on AI. This mode won't just hand you answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding gym trainer, nudging you to pause at key steps and actually *do* the work. The goal? To truly master those programming skills. 🧠💡

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, is a real marvel! Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it totally crushes **"instruction following"** and **"text structuring"** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building those nimble, quick, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱
