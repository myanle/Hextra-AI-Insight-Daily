### Today's AI News

1.  🧠 **Claude Code** just rolled out a totally new "Learning Mode," aiming to combat that dreaded "AI brain atrophy" (aka skill degradation) from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," nudging you to pause at key junctures, get your hands dirty, and truly nail those coding skills yourself.

2.  ⚡ **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a total game-changer! With only 270M parameters, it's crushing it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power tech means it can run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up super light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  🚀 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing blueprint for the company: we're talking trillions of dollars poured into building data centers and pioneering new AI hardware, even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interface** tech. The goal? To totally shake up search and social media as we know it! Altman reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a pivotal AI era, kind of like the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's launch marking what he calls "the best of times, and the worst of times."

4.  🥳 **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI era! And guess what? They're on the hunt for AI talent, including a lead AI industry reporter, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd absolutely love for you to hop aboard! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Job Openings](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  🔬 **Hi3DEval**, a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, just dropped! It's a joint effort from Shanghai AI Lab and a bunch of universities. This bad boy uses a three-tier protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to seriously dig into 3D models, analyzing their overall shape, local bits, and how real their materials look. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. 🤝 When it comes to AI safety, **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** have some pretty different takes. Fei-Fei Li is all about focusing on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's main worry is superintelligence spiraling out of control. He suggests we need to design **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. Heads up: "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two big buzzwords for potential AI risks.
