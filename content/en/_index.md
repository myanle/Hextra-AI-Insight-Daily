### Today's AI News

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that pesky "AI brain atrophy" problem from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym trainer, nudging you to pause at key steps and actually *do* the tasks yourself, so you truly master those programming skills. Genius! 💡
2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's super cool open-source lightweight large model, is kicking butt! With just 270M parameters, it totally nails **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, perfect for building speedy, low-cost AI apps. 🚀
3.  **OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman**, has laid out a wild, ambitious blueprint for the company: think trillions of dollars pumped into building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface (BCI)** arena, aiming to completely shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in a critical AI moment, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's launch signaling "the best of times, and the worst of times." Talk about high stakes! 🤯

This bad boy uses a three-layer protocol (object-level, component-level, and material themes) to dive deep into a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and material realism. Super detailed! ✨ [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big scary concepts defining potential AI risks. Food for thought! 🤔
