**Claude Code** has rolled out a "Learning Mode" to combat "AI brain atrophy," helping users avoid skill degradation from over-reliance on AI. **Google** also open-sourced its lightweight yet mighty large model, Gemma 3 270M, which boasts stellar performance and easy deployment. Meanwhile, **OpenAI**'s CEO, Altman, envisions a grand future involving trillions in data center investments, new AI hardware, and even diving into brain-computer interfaces, believing AI is at a crucial, internet-bubble-like stage.

### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! Its mission? To tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** that comes from leaning too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, nudging users to pause at critical junctures, roll up their sleeves, and actually *do* the tasks. The goal? To truly nail those programming skills.
2.  **Google** has open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model that's surprisingly mighty. Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps.
3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a super ambitious blueprint for the company. We're talking trillions of dollars poured into building data centers, developing cutting-edge AI hardware, and even venturing into **brain-computer interfaces**! The plan? To totally shake up search and social media as we know it. Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a pivotal AI era, kind of like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will herald "the best of times, and the worst of times."
4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is throwing a massive celebration for its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they're practically begging you to join the team! 5.  **Hi3DEval** is a new, super-scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system cooked up by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in partnership with several universities. It uses a three-tiered assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to dissect 3D models from multiple angles, analyzing their overall shape, local structure, and material realism. 12. **Li Feifei** and **Hinton** are on different pages when it comes to AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's big worry is superintelligence running amok. He suggests we need to design **AI that actually cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts highlighting AI's potential risks.
