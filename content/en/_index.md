### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just launched its brand-new **"Learning Mode"**, tackling the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** issue that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at key steps and get hands-on, ensuring they truly master their coding skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's lightweight open-source large language model, packs a punch despite its mere 270M parameters! It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, outperforming even some larger models. Its low-power nature means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lean, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a super ambitious blueprint: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop next-gen AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** realm. He's got plans to shake up search and social media, believing we're in a pivotal AI moment, much like the dot-com bubble. The release of GPT-5, he says, truly marks "the best of times, the worst of times." 🌌

4.  Xinzhiyuan's 10th-anniversary celebration is officially kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 Xinzhiyuan is on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love to have you on board! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It uses a three-tiered evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—for multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project offers step-by-step learning paths to build various technologies from scratch, helping users master coding skills through hands-on practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** provides a comprehensive 90-day cybersecurity learning plan, covering everything from basic certifications to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  **awesome-mac** is a treasure trove for Mac users, compiling a collection of various advanced software for macOS. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **parlant** is an LLM agent that simplifies large language model (LLM) applications, making them super easy to deploy and use. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. The reasoning mechanism of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** was brilliantly explained by DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou in his Stanford CS25 course. He pointed out that LLM reasoning is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. Li Feifei and Hinton have differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about superintelligent AI running amok, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts for potential AI risks. 🧐
