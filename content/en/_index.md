### **Today's AI News**

1. 💪🧠 **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that pesky "AI brain atrophy" from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym coach, pausing you at crucial points to make you dive in and actually finish tasks, helping you truly master those programming skills.

2. 🤯📱 **Google** just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model with only 270M parameters. This little powerhouse crushes "instruction following" and "text structuring" tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models! Its low-power vibe means it can run on your phone or Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building fast, cheap, and lean AI apps.

3. 🤯🚀 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a seriously ambitious vision for OpenAI. He's talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware, even jumping into brain-computer interfaces to totally shake up search and social media! Altman believes we're smack dab in a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 is basically screaming, "It's the best of times, it's the worst of times."

4. 🥳🎉 **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary, kicking off the AGI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns – basically, if you love AI, they want you! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5. ✨🔬 **Hi3DEval** is the name of a brand-new, super-scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab alongside several universities. This bad boy uses a three-tier assessment protocol – object-level, component-level, and material-theme – to dive deep into 3D models, analyzing their overall form, local structure, and material realism from multiple angles. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6. 🛠️🚀 The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project is a game-changer, offering learning paths to build all sorts of tech from scratch! It's all about hands-on practice to help users really nail down their programming skills. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7. 🛡️💻 **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** hooks you up with a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan. This plan covers literally everything cyber-related, from basic certifications to advanced-level hacks and tricks! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8. 💎💻 For all you Mac users out there, **awesome-mac** is your ultimate treasure trove! It's packed with a collection of seriously cool and advanced software for macOS. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9. ✨🤖 Meet **parlant**, an LLM agent designed to totally simplify large language model (LLM) applications. It's super easy to deploy and use! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. 🪄🚀 **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that's basically got it all! It integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. 🧠💡 DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou, recently dropped some serious knowledge at a Stanford CS25 course, breaking down the inference mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. He explained that LLM reasoning happens by generating a sequence of **intermediate tokens** and even delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. 🤔⚠️ When it comes to AI safety, **Li Feifei and Hinton** actually have some different takes. Li Feifei is all about focusing on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we build **AI that truly cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts for potential AI risks.
