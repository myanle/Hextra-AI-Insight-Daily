### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  Claude Code has dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to combat **"AI brain drain"** — that skill degradation from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding fitness coach, nudging you to pause at crucial steps and get your hands dirty, so you actually master those programming skills.

2.  Gemma 3 270M, Google's lightweight open-source large model, only rocks 270M parameters but absolutely crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building fast, cheap, and lean AI apps.

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has painted a massive vision for the company: think trillions of dollars pumped into data centers and next-gen AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a critical AI moment, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's launch signaling "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the AGI era! 🥳 They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love to have you join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  Hi3DEval, launched by Shanghai AI Lab alongside several universities, is a game-changer: a more scientific and comprehensive evaluation system for 3D generation quality. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions for overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
