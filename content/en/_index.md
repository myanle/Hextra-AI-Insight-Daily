### **Today's AI News**

1.  🤖 **Claude Code** has rolled out a fresh new **"Learning Mode"** to fight off the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by too much AI reliance. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like a programming personal trainer, prompting users to pause at critical junctures, encouraging them to get hands-on and truly grasp coding skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch despite its tiny 270M parameters. It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  💸 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a super ambitious roadmap for OpenAI: we're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  🥳 **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary and kicking off the AGI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns, among other roles. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智yuan官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  🤩 **Hi3DEval**, a collaboration between Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, introduces a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—for multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  💡 The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project offers learning paths to construct various technologies from scratch, helping users master coding skills through hands-on practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  🛡️ The **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** project provides a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan, covering all aspects of cybersecurity, from basic certifications to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

11. 🧠 DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou, in his Stanford CS25 course, unpacked the **Large Language Model (LLM)** reasoning mechanism, explaining that LLM inference occurs by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and exploring techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. 💬 **Li Feifei and Hinton** hold differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about uncontrolled superintelligence, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
