### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the notorious "AI brain drain" problem that comes from over-relying on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like a programming "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get their hands dirty with tasks, truly helping them master coding skills.

2.  Google's open-source **Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight large model with a mere 270M parameters that's seriously punching above its weight. This little powerhouse shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power consumption means you can run it on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  Buckle up, because **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing roadmap! OpenAI is planning to invest trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing cutting-edge AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** (BCI) space, aiming to completely shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're smack-dab in a critical AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release? That's just the beginning of what he calls "the best of times, and the worst of times."

12. There's a fascinating split in opinions between **Li Feifei and Hinton** when it comes to AI safety. Li Feifei is laser-focused on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more concerned about superintelligence spiraling out of control, advocating for the creation of **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. These two titans of AI identify "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" as the core concepts behind AI's potential risks.
