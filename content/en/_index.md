### **Today's AI News**

1.  🧠 **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** from relying too much on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on, and truly master coding skills.

2.  🚀 **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is a new open-source, lightweight large model with only 270M parameters. Yet, it absolutely crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Thanks to its low power consumption, it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building speedy, low-cost, and lightweight AI applications.

3.  💰🌌 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman laid out a colossal vision for OpenAI: pouring trillions into data centers, crafting new AI hardware, and even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're at a pivotal, internet-bubble-esque moment for AI, with GPT-5's launch signaling "the best of times, the worst of times."

5.  📊 The **Hi3DEval** system, a joint effort by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, is a spiffy new evaluation framework for 3D generation quality. It's more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tier assessment protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to dive deep into a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and material realism. 12. 🤔 **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild, suggesting we design **AI that actually cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts highlighting AI's potential risks.
