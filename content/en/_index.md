### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  💡 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"learning mode"** to tackle **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by relying on AI too much. This mode isn't about just spitting out answers; it acts like your coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get their hands dirty to truly master those programming skills.

2.  ✨ **Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google, with only 270M parameters. This little powerhouse performs exceptionally well in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even surpassing some larger models. Its low-power consumption allows it to run on devices like mobile phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  🤯 **OpenAI CEO Altman** just dropped the bomb on OpenAI's super ambitious blueprint: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interfaces**, with plans to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're deep in a critical AI phase, kinda like the dot-com bubble days, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  🎉 **Xinzhiyuan's 10th-anniversary celebration** kicked off the ASI era! Xinzhiyuan is currently hiring for various positions, including AI industry lead writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern, inviting all AI enthusiasts to join! 5.  🔬 **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. This system is a game-changer for evaluating 3D generation quality, using a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to analyze the overall form, local structure, and material authenticity of 3D models from multiple dimensions. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts of potential AI risks.
