### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out an all-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** (aka skill atrophy) from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "gym coach," nudging users to pause at crucial steps and get their hands dirty, ensuring they truly master programming skills. 🧠

2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is making waves as a lightweight, open-source large model. Despite having just 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for cooking up quick, cheap, and snappy AI apps. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman,** has laid out an epic blueprint for the future: talking trillions of dollars for data centers, next-gen AI hardware, and even diving into the **brain-computer interface** field to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're smack dab in a pivotal AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and with GPT-5 on the horizon, it feels like "the best of times, the worst of times." 🌌💸

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI talent, including an AI industry reporting chief writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they're calling your name! 🎉 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  Say hello to **Hi3DEval**, a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system! It's a joint effort from Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, using a three-tiered protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to give 3D models a multi-dimensional check-up on their overall shape, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, huh? 😎 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

7.  Get ready to level up your security game with **`90DaysOfCyberSecurity`**! This project offers a jam-packed 90-day learning plan, diving into every nook and cranny of cybersecurity, from basic certifications to pro-level tricks. 🚀 [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

Mind blown! 🤯 [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. There's a bit of a split between **Li Feifei and Hinton** when it comes to AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue, pushing for AI that actually **cares about humans**. The big bads here? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" – two core concepts of potential AI risks. 🤖
