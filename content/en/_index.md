Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym coach, pushing you to pause at key steps and get hands-on. The goal? To truly nail those programming skills. 💪

2.  Google just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M** – and it's a game-changer! 🤯 This lightweight large model, with a mere 270M parameters, absolutely crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power design means it can run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, perfect for building super-fast, low-cost AI apps. 🚀📱
