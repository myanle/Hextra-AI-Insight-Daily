### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the "AI brain drain" problem caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at critical steps, get hands-on, and truly master their programming skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large language model, packs a punch with just 270M parameters. It's crushing **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Thanks to its low power consumption, you can run this bad boy on phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lean, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman painted a colossal picture of OpenAI's future: multi-trillion-dollar investments in data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯

4.  It's **Xinzhiyuan**'s 10th-anniversary bash, kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a new, super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, launched by the Shanghai AI Lab with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol – object-level, component-level, and material-theme – to break down 3D models' overall shape, local structure, and material realism from multiple angles. Gotta get those 3D models looking just right! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

It's like a choose-your-own-adventure for developers! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

8.  **awesome-mac** is a treasure trove for Mac users, crammed with all sorts of advanced software. Seriously, if you're on a Mac, you NEED this repo! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **parlant** is an LLM agent that makes large language model (LLM) applications super simple to deploy and use. Basically, it's making LLMs less of a headache! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that bundles AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system all into one slick package. Talk about a one-stop shop for getting stuff done! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** recently broke down the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** in a Stanford CS25 course. He highlighted that LLM inference works by cranking out a series of **intermediate tokens** and delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Mind. Blown. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** are on different pages when it comes to AI safety. Feifei's all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is seriously worried about super-intelligent AI going rogue, suggesting we craft **AI that actually cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two big concepts at the heart of AI's potential risks. It's a real debate! 🧐
