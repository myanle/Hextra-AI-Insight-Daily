### **Today's AI Buzz! 🚀**

1.  Claude Code just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that pesky **"AI brain drain"** issue that pops up from long-term AI reliance. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it's like having a coding "fitness coach" that guides users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on, and truly master their programming skills. 🧠💡

2.  Gemma 3 270M is Google's latest open-source lightweight large model. This little tiger 🐯, with only 270M parameters, is seriously crushing it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models! Its low-power design means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building speedy, budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing vision for the company's future. 🤯 He's talking trillions of dollars for data centers, next-gen AI hardware, and even diving into **brain-computer interfaces (BCIs)**, aiming to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch signals "it was the best of times, it was the worst of times." Talk about ambition! ✨

4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI era with a bang! 🎉 They're currently on the hunt for AI Industry Reporting Lead Writers, Senior Video Editors, Senior Editors/Editors, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join the team! Check out the [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) to apply. 🥳

Hi3DEval is a new, super comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system brought to you by the Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. ✨ It uses a fancy three-tier protocol – object-level, component-level, and material-theme – to really dig deep into 3D models, analyzing their overall form, local structure, and material authenticity from all angles. Check out the [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and the [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard). 📊

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou recently blew minds 🤯 at Stanford's CS25 course, breaking down the **large language model (LLM)** inference mechanism. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

Key concepts like "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are at the heart of AI's potential risks. It's a hot debate! 🔥
