### **Today's AI News**

1. 🤖🧠**Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that pesky **"AI brain drain"** problem from over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding gym trainer, nudging users to pause at critical steps, get hands-on, and truly master those programming skills.

2. 🐯🚀Google's **Gemma 3 270M**, an open-source lightweight large model, is crushing it! Despite its tiny 270M parameters, it totally aces tasks like **instruction following** and **text structuring**, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low power consumption means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building fast, light, and cheap AI apps.

3. 💰🌌**OpenAI** CEO Altman just spilled the beans on OpenAI's super ambitious blueprint: think trillions of dollars pumped into data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**! 🧠 The goal? To totally revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI moment, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times, and the worst of times."

4. 🎉🥳**Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're currently on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5. 😎**Hi3DEval**, developed by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities, is a spiffy new 3D generation quality assessment system. It's way more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tiered evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material themes) to analyze 3D models' overall shape, local structure, and material realism from multiple angles. Check it out! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6. 💪The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project offers awesome learning paths to build various technologies from scratch. It's all about helping users get hands-on and truly master their programming skills through practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7. 🚀**90DaysOfCyberSecurity** is offering a sweet 90-day cybersecurity learning plan that covers everything from basic certifications to advanced techniques. Time to level up your cyber game! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8. 🍎**awesome-mac** is a real treasure trove for Mac users, jam-packed with all sorts of advanced software for your Apple machine. Go grab those goodies! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9. 🤖**parlant** is a super slick LLM agent designed to simplify large language model (LLM) applications. It's a breeze to deploy and use, making your LLM life much easier! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. 🤔**magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that's packed with features. It integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system all in one neat package. Pretty cool, huh? [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. 🤯DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou blew minds in his Stanford CS25 lecture, explaining how **Large Language Models (LLMs)** actually do their reasoning. He pointed out that LLM inference happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and dove into cool techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Wanna see? [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. 🤖Fei-Fei Li and Hinton have some differing views on AI safety, which is totally fair! Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's worried about superintelligence going rogue and suggests building **AI that genuinely cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts at the heart of AI's potential risks.
