### Today's AI News

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** 🚀🧠 to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** from leaning too heavily on AI. This isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it's like having a coding "fitness coach" that nudges users to pause at crucial points, get their hands dirty, and truly nail those programming skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large model, is seriously impressive! 🚀 Packing just 270M parameters, it absolutely crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building snappy, affordable AI applications. 📱💡

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just painted an epic picture of OpenAI's future! 🤯 We're talking trillions invested in data centers and new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI moment, reminiscent of the internet bubble, and the GPT-5 release marks "the best of times, the worst of times." 🚀💸

It's **Xinzhiyuan's** tenth anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! 🎉 They're on the lookout for awesome talent, including AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join the team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](URL_PLACEHOLDER_NOT_PROVIDED_IN_ORIGINAL)

5.  Check out **Hi3DEval**! 🤩 It's a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab alongside several universities. This bad boy uses a three-tier protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to thoroughly analyze 3D models' overall shape, local structure, and material realism across multiple dimensions. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

[🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

9.  Meet **parlant**! 🤖 This LLM agent is all about simplifying large language model (LLM) applications, making them incredibly easy to deploy and use. Talk about a lifesaver! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. Ever heard of **magic**? ✨ It's an open-source AI productivity platform that packs a punch, integrating AI agents, workflow engines, IM, and even an online collaborative office system. Pretty neat, right? [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou gave an eye-opening talk at Stanford's CS25 course! 🤯 He unpacked the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, revealing that LLM inference works by generating a series of **intermediate tokens**. Plus, he dove into cool techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** are on different pages when it comes to AI safety. 🤔 Li Fei-Fei emphasizes **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's main concern is superintelligence running wild, advocating for AI designed to **care about humanity**. The big scary concepts here are "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence"—two core ideas behind AI's potential risks.

13. Say hello to **FormulaOne**! 🏎️ This new AI evaluation benchmark is no joke, featuring 220 graph-structured dynamic programming problems. It completely made top-tier large models like GPT-5 "crash," scoring a big fat zero across the board! 🤯 [FormulaOne](https://image.jiqizhixin.com/uploads/editor/656fe086-cbec-46b3-b135-a600f7a68fda/640.png) [🔗 FormulaOne Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/double-ai/FormulaOne-Leaderboard)
