### **Today's AI News**

1.  🚀 **Claude Code** just launched a brand-new **'Learning Mode'**! This awesome feature aims to combat that nagging **'AI brain rot'** problem from over-relying on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, nudging you to pause at key steps, get your hands dirty, and truly master those programming skills.
2.  🔥 **Google** just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a super lightweight large model! Even though it's only 270M parameters, this bad boy crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power footprint means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building fast, cheap, and snappy AI apps.
3.  💸🚀 **OpenAI's** CEO Sam Altman has unfurled a massive blueprint for the company's future! We're talking trillions in investments to build out data centers and develop cutting-edge AI hardware, even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interfaces**. The goal? To totally revolutionize search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI moment, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will mark 'the best of times, the worst of times.' Big vibes!
4.  🥳✨ **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its 10th anniversary, officially kicking off the **ASI Era**! They're on the hunt for some fresh talent, including AI industry journalists, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they want YOU! It uses a three-tier protocol—object-level, component-level, and material themes—to analyze 3D models from all angles, checking out their overall form, local structures, and material realism. They pinpoint 'misaligned goal generalization' and 'instrumental convergence' as two core concepts for potential AI risks. Deep thoughts!
