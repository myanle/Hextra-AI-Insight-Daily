### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand new **"Learning Mode"**! This cool feature is here to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** (aka skill atrophy from relying too much on AI for too long). Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding you to pause at crucial steps and actually get your hands dirty with tasks. The goal? To truly nail those programming skills! 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a total game-changer! Packing only 270M parameters, it's crushing **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its super low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a mind-blowing grand vision for the company! We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** game to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's launch signaling "the best of times, and the worst of times." Talk about high stakes! 💰

4.  It's a party! 🎉🥳 **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its 10th anniversary, kicking off the ASI era in style! And guess what? They're on the hunt for AI talent, with openings for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  Meet **Hi3DEval**, a game-changing 3D generation quality assessment system! Developed by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, it's way more scientific and comprehensive. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to dive deep into 3D models, analyzing their overall form, local structure, and material realism from multiple angles. Super cool stuff! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

The two big concepts fueling AI's potential risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence." Deep stuff!
