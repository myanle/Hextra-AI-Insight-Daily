### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  🧠 **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"learning mode"** to tackle that pesky **"AI brain drain"** problem caused by relying too much on AI. This isn't about giving you all the answers; instead, it's like having a coding "personal trainer" that guides you to pause at crucial steps, roll up your sleeves, and actually get hands-on with tasks. The goal? To truly nail those programming skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, is a total game-changer! Clocking in at just 270M parameters, it totally crushes tasks like **instruction following** and **text structuring**, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power mojo means it can run smoothly on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  🤯 **OpenAI** CEO Altman has painted a seriously ambitious picture for OpenAI's future: we're talking trillions in investments to build out data centers and develop cutting-edge AI hardware, even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interface** tech! The grand plan? To totally disrupt search and social media as we know it. Altman reckons we're smack-dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release? He says it's gonna mark "the best of times, the worst of times." Talk about high stakes!

4.  🥳 Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns – basically, if you're passionate about AI, they want you to join the team! Check out their [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供).

5.  ✨ **Hi3DEval** is a game-changing 3D generation quality assessment system, jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and multiple universities. It's way more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material-theme) to analyze 3D models from all angles – overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty cool, right? You can dive deeper on the [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) or check out the [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard).

Get started with the [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)!

11. 🧠 DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou dropped some serious knowledge in his Stanford CS25 course, explaining the inference mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. He pointed out that LLM reasoning goes down by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Wanna see him in action? Hit up the [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)!

12. 🚨 Li Feifei and Hinton are totally on different pages when it comes to AI safety. Li Feifei is all about focusing on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligent AI going rogue and suggests we design **AI that actually cares about humans**. He highlights "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" as two core concepts for potential AI risks. Food for thought, right?
