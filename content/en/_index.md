### Today's AI News

1.  **Claude Code** just launched its brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at key steps and get hands-on, ensuring they truly master programming skills. 🤯

2.  Google's open-source, lightweight **Gemma 3 270M** model is a real powerhouse! 💪 With only 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power design means it can run on tiny devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building quick, cheap, and lean AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing blueprint for the future! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars for data centers, next-gen AI hardware, and even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** with plans to totally flip the script on search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI era, kind of like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 launch marks "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  It's a party! 🎉🥳 **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're looking for passionate AI folks to join their team, with openings for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you love AI, they want YOU! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Job Openings](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  Get ready for better 3D! 😎 **Hi3DEval**, a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Lab alongside several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to dig deep into 3D models' overall shape, local structure, and material realism. Check it out! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

When it comes to AI safety, Li Feifei and Hinton are on different pages! 🤖 Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humans**. The two big concepts at the heart of AI's potential risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence."
