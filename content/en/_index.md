### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"learning mode"**! 🤯 This isn't about giving you direct answers; it's designed to fight **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by over-reliance on AI. Think of it as your personal programming "fitness coach," guiding you to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and truly master coding skills.

2.  Holy moly, **Gemma 3 270M** is here! 🚀 This lightweight large model, open-sourced by Google, packs a punch with just 270M parameters. It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for crafting lean, fast, and wallet-friendly AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing blueprint for the future! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in data centers and new AI hardware, even dabbling in **brain-computer interfaces**. The goal? To totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a crucial AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release will mark "the best of times and the worst of times." Wild, right?

5.  Meet **Hi3DEval**! 😎 This new, super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system was cooked up by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to give a multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall shape, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

When it comes to AI safety, **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** have some differing views. 🤔 Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more concerned about superintelligence spiraling out of control, suggesting we design **AI that actually cares about humans**. The big ideas here are "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
