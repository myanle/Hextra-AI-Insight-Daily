### **Today's AI Buzz**

1. **Claude Code** has just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🤯 It's designed to tackle that pesky **"AI brain drain"** problem from relying too much on AI. This mode won't just hand you answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding gym trainer, pausing you at crucial steps to make sure you get your hands dirty and truly master those programming skills. 💪

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a total powerhouse! 🚀 Clocking in at just 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for cranking out light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 📱💡

3. **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing master plan! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the dot-com boom, and the GPT-5 launch marks 'the best of times and the worst of times.' Get ready! 💰🚀

4. **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! 🎉🥳 They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns – basically, if you love AI, they want you! Check out their **[🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)** and join the squad! 🤝

5. **Hi3DEval** is a super cool, more scientific, and way more comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system! 😎 It was launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. This bad boy uses a three-layer protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, checking out their overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. Dive deeper on the **[🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/)** or peek at the **[🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)**. ✨

'Goal misgeneralization' and 'instrumental convergence' are the two big core concepts behind these potential AI risks. 👀
