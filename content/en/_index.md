### **Today's AI News**

1.  🤖🧠 **Claude Code** has launched a brand-spanking-new "Learning Mode" to tackle the "AI brain drain" issue that comes with relying on AI for too long. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like a programming "fitness coach," nudging users to pause at critical steps, get hands-on, and really master those coding skills. 💪

2.  🐯🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch with just 270M parameters! It totally crushes it in "instruction following" and "text structuring" tasks, even outperforming some bigger models. Plus, its low-power mojo means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 📱💨

3.  💰🌌 **OpenAI's** CEO, Sam Altman, has laid out a mind-blowing grand plan for the company: think trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** game! 🤯 The goal? To totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 launch signals "the best of times and the worst of times." Talk about high stakes! 🚀

5.  😎 **Hi3DEval** is a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system cooked up by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol – object-level, component-level, and material-theme – to do a multi-dimensional deep dive into a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, right? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. 🤖 **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** have some pretty different takes on AI safety. Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is worried sick about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we ought to design **AI that actually cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts highlighting AI's potential risks. Deep thoughts, indeed! 🤔
