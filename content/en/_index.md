### **Today's AI Buzz 📰**

1.  ✨ **Claude Code** has dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** designed to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** problem caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers. Instead, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, roll up their sleeves, and complete tasks themselves. The goal? To truly master programming skills. 💪

2.  🤯 **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is making waves! This lightweight, open-source large model, with a tiny 270M parameter count, absolutely crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like smartphones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications. 📱💡

3.  🤯 **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has unveiled a super ambitious blueprint for the company! We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, with plans to even dive into the **brain-computer interface** (BCI) arena and totally revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 signals that "it's the best of times, it's the worst of times." 🌌💰
