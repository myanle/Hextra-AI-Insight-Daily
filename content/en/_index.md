### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out its brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠 It's here to combat that nasty "AI brain drain" that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause at key steps and tackle tasks yourself to truly master programming skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's latest open-source gem! 💎 This lightweight large model, with a mere 270M parameters, absolutely crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, perfect for whipping up speedy, budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman painted a wild picture of OpenAI's grand master plan: talking trillions in investments for data centers and killer new AI hardware, even getting into **brain-computer interfaces**! 🤯 They're looking to totally flip the script on search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in an AI crunch time, kinda like the early internet bubble, and the GPT-5 launch signals it's "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  Xinzhiyuan just dropped a bomb with its 10th-anniversary bash and the kick-off of the **ASI era**! 🎉🥳 They're on a hiring spree for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they want YOU! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a new, super-scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities. 😎 It uses a three-layer evaluation protocol focusing on object-level, component-level, and material themes, analyzing 3D models across multiple dimensions for overall form, local structure, and material realism. ✨ [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
