### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that pesky **"AI brain atrophy"** issue caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand you the answers; instead, it acts like a coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at critical steps, get their hands dirty, and truly master programming skills. 💪🧠

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, an open-source lightweight large model from Google, is a total game-changer! Clocking in at just 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱

3.  **OpenAI's** CEO, Sam Altman, has laid out an epic blueprint for the future: talking trillions in investments for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** game to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a pivotal AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release signaling "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯💰

4.  **Xinzhiyuan's** 10th-anniversary bash just kicked off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns – if you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! 🎉 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a collaboration between Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, is a spanking new, more scientific, and super comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, checking out their overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. ✨ [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project offers learning paths to build various technologies from scratch, helping users nail programming skills through hands-on practice. 🛠️ [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** presents a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan that covers every nook and cranny of the field, from basic certifications to advanced tricks. 🛡️ [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  **awesome-mac** is a treasure trove for Mac users, compiling a stellar collection of advanced software for macOS. 🍎 [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **parlant** is an LLM agent designed to simplify large language model (LLM) applications, making it super easy to deploy and use. 🤖 [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform, packing AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system all in one neat package. ✨ [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind's Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** unpacked the inference mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** during a Stanford CS25 course, explaining that LLM inference works by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and discussing techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. 🧠 [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts behind potential AI risks. 🤖⚖️
