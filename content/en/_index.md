### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 🤖🧠! This cool feature tackles the dreaded **"AI Brain Atrophy"** problem that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding you to pause at key steps and get hands-on with tasks. The goal? To truly master those programming skills!

2.  Google just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M** 🐯🚀, a super lightweight large model with a mere 270M parameters! But don't let its size fool you—it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Thanks to its low power consumption, you can run it on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building fast, affordable, and nimble AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman painted a wild picture 💰🌌 of the company's grand future: we're talking trillions of dollars invested in data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**! The goal? To totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI moment, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release marks "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  Hold up! Xinzhiyuan just celebrated its 10th anniversary 🎉🥳 and kicked off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for some awesome AI talent, including AI industry journalists, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** 😎 is a new, super comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system brought to you by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, checking their overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. Pretty neat, right? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou, recently dropped some serious knowledge 🤯 at Stanford's CS25 course, explaining the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. 12. Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton have some contrasting views on AI safety 🤖. Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests we design **AI that cares about humanity**. Key concepts for potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "tool convergence" are at the heart of their discussions.
