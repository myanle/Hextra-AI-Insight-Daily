### **Today's AI Buzz** 🚀

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode,"** aiming to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** issue that comes from long-term AI reliance. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on with tasks, and truly master programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google, boasting a mere 270M parameters. Yet, it performs exceptionally well in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has painted a grand vision for OpenAI, detailing plans to invest trillions of dollars in building data centers and developing new AI hardware. They even aim to venture into the **brain-computer interface** field, intending to revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, with the GPT-5 launch signaling "it was the best of times, it was the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan's 10th-anniversary celebration** marked the dawn of the ASI era! Xinzhiyuan is actively recruiting for various AI-related positions, including AI Industry Reporter Lead, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. 5.  **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific, comprehensive, and rigorous 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in collaboration with multiple universities. It utilizes a three-tier evaluation protocol covering object-level, part-level, and material themes to conduct multi-dimensional analyses of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism.
