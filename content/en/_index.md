### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out its brand-new **"Learning Mode"!** 🚀 This awesome feature aims to combat **"AI brain atrophy"** – that skill decay from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal programming "fitness coach," guiding you to pause at key steps and get your hands dirty with tasks. The goal? To truly nail those coding skills! 🧠💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source star, is a super lightweight large model, rocking just 270M parameters! 💥 But don't let its size fool you—it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 📱💡

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** just laid out a mind-blowing, trillion-dollar blueprint for the future! 🤯 We're talking massive investments in data centers, cutting-edge AI hardware, and even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**. The goal? To totally shake up search and social media as we know it. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI moment, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the arrival of GPT-5 truly signals "the best of times, and the worst of times." 💰✨
