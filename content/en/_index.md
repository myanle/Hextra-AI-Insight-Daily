### **Today's AI News**

1.  🚀 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** designed to tackle the problem of **"AI brain drain"** (or "AI skill atrophy") resulting from over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at critical steps and complete tasks hands-on, helping them truly master coding skills.

2.  ✨ **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, boasts a mere 270M parameters yet performs outstandingly in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  🌐 **OpenAI** CEO Altman has laid out an ambitious blueprint for the company, planning trillions of dollars in investments to build data centers and develop new AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** realm, aiming to revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI phase, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 signals "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  🎉 **New Intelligence** is kicking off its tenth-anniversary celebration, marking the dawn of the ASI era! The company is currently on the lookout for AI industry reporting leads, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns, among other roles. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join their team! [🔗 New Intelligence Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  🔬 **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
