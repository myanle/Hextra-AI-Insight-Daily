### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** has dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! It's designed to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** problem that comes from relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; nope, it's more like your personal coding fitness coach. It guides users to pause at crucial junctures, get their hands dirty, and truly nail down those programming skills. 💪

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large model, is quite the marvel! With a mere 270M parameters, it totally shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 🚀

3. **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has painted a seriously grand vision for OpenAI. We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and cooking up new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** arena to completely shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in an AI watershed moment, much like the dot-com bubble. He says the GPT-5 launch signals "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯

4. **Xinzhiyuan** is throwing a massive tenth-anniversary bash, kicking off the AGI era! 🎉 They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5. **Hi3DEval**, a new 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been rolled out by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in partnership with several universities. This bad boy is more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tiered assessment protocol (object-level, component-level, and material themes) to conduct multi-dimensional analysis on 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. Check it out: [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** dropped some serious knowledge in his Stanford CS25 course, explaining the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. He pointed out that LLM inference is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Fascinating stuff! [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk) 🧠

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton** are kinda on different pages when it comes to AI safety. Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's more worried about superintelligent AI running wild and suggests we ought to design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts at the heart of AI's potential risks. 😬
