### **Today's AI News**

1.  🤖 **Claude Code** launched a brand-new **"learning mode."** This innovative mode aims to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** issue stemming from long-term AI dependence. Instead of directly providing answers, it functions like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks themselves, ensuring they truly master programming skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's open-source lightweight large model. Boasting only 270M parameters, it excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption allows it to run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it ideal for building lightweight, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  💰 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman sketched out OpenAI's ambitious blueprint. This vision includes investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface** field, with plans to disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI period, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times and the worst of times."

5.  😎 **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. Launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities, it employs a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—for multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. 🤖 **Li Feifei and Hinton** hold differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton worries about uncontrolled superintelligence, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts of potential AI risks.
