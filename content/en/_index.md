### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code has launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** problem that comes with long-term AI reliance. 🏋️‍♀️ Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks manually, helping them truly master their programming skills.

2.  Google has open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large language model. 📱 Despite its mere 270M parameters, this model truly shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has laid out an ambitious blueprint for the company. 💸🧠 His vision includes investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interface** tech, with plans to disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, much like the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times and the worst of times."

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a cutting-edge 3D generation quality evaluation system. Developed by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, it's designed to be more scientific and comprehensive. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
