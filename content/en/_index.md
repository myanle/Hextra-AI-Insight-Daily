### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** (or skill atrophy) issue that comes from relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about spitting out direct answers; instead, it acts like a programming "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get their hands dirty, and truly master coding skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's open-source, lightweight large model. Sporting just 270M parameters, this bad boy shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for cranking out light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has painted a mind-blowing roadmap for the company: we're talking trillions of dollars poured into building data centers and new AI hardware, even diving headfirst into the **brain-computer interface (BCI)** game, with plans to totally disrupt search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI era, kind of like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 launch will mark "the best of times, and the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** (AI Era) recently celebrated its 10th anniversary, kicking off the ASI (Artificial Superintelligence) era! They're currently on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! 5.  **Hi3DEval** is a snazzy, more scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system launched by the Shanghai AI Laboratory alongside a bunch of universities. This system rocks a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to dig deep into 3D models' overall form, local structures, and material realism with multi-dimensional analysis. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  **build-your-own-x**, a cool GitHub project, offers learning paths to build all sorts of tech from scratch. It's designed to help users really get their hands dirty and master programming skills through practical application. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** serves up a sweet 90-day cybersecurity learning plan that covers absolutely everything, from foundational certifications to advanced tricks of the trade. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  **awesome-mac** is a killer collection of advanced software for Mac, basically a treasure trove for anyone rocking an Apple machine. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **parlant** is a neat LLM agent that simplifies large language model (LLM) applications, making them super easy to deploy and use. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that bundles AI agents, a workflow engine, instant messaging (IM), and an online collaborative office system all into one neat package. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** broke down the inference mechanism of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** during a Stanford CS25 course. He highlighted that LLM inference works by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** actually have different takes on AI safety. Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's more concerned about superintelligence running wild, advocating for designing **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts when we talk about AI's potential risks.
