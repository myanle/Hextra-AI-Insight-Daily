### **Today's AI Buzz** 🐝

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped its brand-new **"Learning Mode,"** which is seriously cool for tackling the **"AI brain drain"** issue that pops up from relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," nudging you to pause at key steps and get your hands dirty, so you actually master those programming skills. 💪🧠

2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M** has been open-sourced, and it's a game-changer! This lightweight large model, with only 270M parameters, absolutely crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱

3.  **OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman,** just laid out a wild, ambitious blueprint for the company: think trillions of dollars pumped into data centers and fresh AI hardware, plus a leap into the **brain-computer interface** arena, all aimed at shaking up search and social media as we know it. He's convinced we're smack-dab in a critical AI moment, kinda like the dot-com bubble days, and the GPT-5 launch? That's just marking "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯💰

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is throwing a massive tenth-anniversary bash, kicking off the ASI era in style! And guess what? They're on the hunt for AI talent, including AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! ✨🤝 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a new 3D generation quality assessment system, jointly unveiled by the Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. It's way more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to give us a multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall shape, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, right? 🤩🔍 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as the two core concepts behind potential AI risks. 🤖⚖️
