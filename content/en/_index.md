### **Today's AI News**

1.  🧠💪 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**. This innovative mode aims to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** issue caused by long-term reliance on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like a programming "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, actively complete tasks, and truly master coding skills.

2.  🚀✨ **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight, open-source large language model (LLM) with a mere 270M parameters. Yet, it truly shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  🚀🤯 **OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman**, has laid out a super ambitious blueprint for the company. This includes investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface (BCI)** space, all with plans to completely shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  🎉🚀 **XinZhiYuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary, marking the dawn of the ASI era! They're actively recruiting for various AI-related roles, including AI industry lead writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 XinZhiYuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  ✨🔬 **Hi3DEval** is a new, more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in collaboration with several universities. It employs a three-tiered assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

10. ✨🚀 **`magic`** is an open-source AI productivity platform that brilliantly integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. 🧠💡 **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** recently broke down the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** in a Stanford CS25 course. He highlighted that LLM reasoning works by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. 🤔💬 When it comes to AI safety, **Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton** have some differing views. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more concerned about superintelligent AI running amok, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts highlighting potential AI risks.
