1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the "AI brain atrophy" issue stemming from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like a programming "fitness coach," prompting users to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks to truly master coding skills. 💡
2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model with only 270M parameters, totally crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building fast, light, and budget-friendly AI apps. ⚡️
