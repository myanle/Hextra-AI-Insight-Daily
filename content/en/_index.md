### Today's AI Buzz

1.  Claude Code just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠 It's designed to fight that dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** problem from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding fitness coach, guiding you to pause at key steps and actually *do* the work. The goal? To truly master those programming skills. 💪

2.  Google unleashed **Gemma 3 270M**, a super lightweight large language model! 🚀 Weighing in at just 270M parameters, this bad boy absolutely shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building nimble, blazing-fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🤯

3.  OpenAI CEO Altman just laid out a mind-blowing grand vision! 🌌 He's talking about pouring trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** game, aiming to totally disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a pivotal AI moment, akin to the dot-com bubble. He says GPT-5's release signals that "it's the best of times, and it's the worst of times." 💰

Hi3DEval is a new, super comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system! 😎 Developed by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities, it uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall shape, local structure, and material realism. Basically, it's making 3D evaluation way more scientific! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

The two core concepts behind these potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence." Deep stuff! 🧐
