### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand new **"Learning Mode"** 💡 to tackle the issue of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by long-term reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "personal fitness coach," guiding users to pause at critical steps and complete tasks hands-on, helping them truly master programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** ✨, Google's open-source lightweight large model, weighs in at just 270M parameters. Yet, it shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power characteristics mean it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out the company's grand vision 🚀: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface** field with plans to revolutionize search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI period, akin to the internet bubble, and the release of GPT-5 signals "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! 🙌 They're currently on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns, among other roles. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** 🔬 is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It utilizes a three-tiered assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The **build-your-own-x GitHub project** 🛠️ offers learning paths to build various technologies from scratch, helping users master programming skills through hands-on practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** 🔒 provides a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan, covering all aspects of cybersecurity, from basic certifications to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  **awesome-mac** 💎 collects a variety of advanced software for Mac, making it a treasure trove for Mac users. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

10. **magic** 🪄 is an open-source AI productivity platform that integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou 🧠 broke down the **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanism during his Stanford CS25 course. He explained that LLM reasoning is achieved through generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** ⚠️ hold differing views on AI safety. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about superintelligent AI losing control and suggests designing **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts explaining potential AI risks.
