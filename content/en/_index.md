### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to combat that pesky "AI brain drain" where your skills start fading from relying too much on AI. Think of it less as a cheat sheet and more like your personal coding gym buddy. It won't just spoon-feed you answers; instead, it'll nudge you to pause at key moments, get your hands dirty, and really own those programming skills. You got this! 💪

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, super-lightweight large model, is turning heads. With only 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up speedy, budget-friendly AI apps. Pretty neat, huh? ✨

3. **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has painted a mind-blowing vision for the company: think trillions of dollars pumped into building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interfaces**. The goal? To completely shake up search and social media as we know it! Altman reckons we're smack-dab in the middle of a pivotal AI era, kind of like the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release signaling that "it was the best of times, it was the worst of times." Buckle up! 🤯

4. **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the Age of ASI! They're on the hunt for some fresh talent, with open positions ranging from lead AI industry reporter and senior video editor to senior editor/editor and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) Sounds like a cool place to be! ✨

5. **Hi3DEval**, a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been rolled out by the Shanghai AI Lab alongside a bunch of universities. This system uses a cool three-tier protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme-level—to give a multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and how real its materials look. Super detailed! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard) Check it out! 👍

6. The **`build-your-own-x`** GitHub project is a goldmine! It lays out clear learning paths to help you build all sorts of tech from the ground up, letting you really get your hands dirty and master those programming skills through practice. No better way to learn, right? [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7. **`90DaysOfCyberSecurity`** offers an awesome 90-day cybersecurity learning plan. It literally covers everything, from foundational certifications to all the advanced tricks you'll need. Time to get hacking (ethically, of course)! 🛡️ [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8. For all you Mac users out there, **`awesome-mac`** is your ultimate treasure trove! This project gathers a fantastic collection of advanced software specifically for macOS. You'll find some real gems here. 💎 [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9. Meet **`parlant`**, an LLM agent designed to make large language model (LLM) applications a breeze! It's super easy to deploy and use, simplifying your LLM workflow. Sweet! ✨ [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **`magic`** is an open-source AI productivity platform that's got it all! It pulls together AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. Talk about an all-in-one solution for getting things done! 🪄 [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. DeepMind's Chief Scientist **Denny Zhou** recently spilled the tea on **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanisms during a Stanford CS25 course. He highlighted that LLMs reason by cranking out a series of **intermediate tokens** and also dove into cool techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Super insightful stuff! [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** are on slightly different pages when it comes to AI safety. Li Feifei is all about focusing on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more concerned about superintelligence running wild, advocating for the creation of **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. Key concepts for potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" – pretty deep stuff to ponder! 🤔
