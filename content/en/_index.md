### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  🤖🧠 **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **'Learning Mode,'** designed to combat the dreaded **'AI skill atrophy'** that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding 'fitness coach,' prompting users to pause at key steps, get their hands dirty, and truly master those coding skills.

2.  🐯🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large model, boasts a mere 270M parameters but absolutely slays in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building quick, nimble, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  💰🌌 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out an audacious vision for the company: investing trillions of dollars in data centers and new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interfaces (BCIs)**, with plans to totally shake up search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI era, much like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release will mark a period where, as he puts it, "It was the best of times, it was the worst of times."
