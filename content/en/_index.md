### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out its innovative **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** issue caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at critical junctures, empowering them to complete tasks hands-on and truly master coding skills. 🧠💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, boasts a mere 270M parameters yet excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low power consumption allows it to run on devices like smartphones or Raspberry Pis, making it ideal for building light, fast, and cost-effective AI applications. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a grand vision for OpenAI: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers, develop new AI hardware, and even venture into **brain-computer interface (BCI)** technology, aiming to revolutionize search and social media. He believes we're in a pivotal AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, with the release of GPT-5 signaling "the best of times, the worst of times." ✨💰

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the AGI Era! The company is currently hiring for various AI-related roles, including Lead AI Industry Reporter, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join their team! 🥳💼 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and multiple universities. It employs a three-tiered assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—for multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. 📊🧪 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou, in a Stanford CS25 course, elucidated the **large language model (LLM)** reasoning mechanism. He explained that LLM inference is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and explored techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. 🧠🤔 [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** hold divergent views on AI safety concerns. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton worries about superintelligent AI losing control, suggesting the design of **AI that cares for humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks. ⚠️⚖️
