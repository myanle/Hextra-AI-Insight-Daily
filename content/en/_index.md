### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just launched its brand-new **"Learning Mode,"** designed to tackle the infamous **"AI brain atrophy"** problem from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like a programming "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, roll up their sleeves, and complete tasks themselves to truly master coding skills. 🤯

2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is making waves! This lightweight, open-source large model boasts a mere 270M parameters, yet it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman,** is painting a huge picture for the future! He's talking about investing trillions in data centers and next-gen AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interface** tech, with plans to shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI moment, akin to the internet bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch signals "the best of times and the worst of times." Talk about ambition! ✨

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they want you! 🎉 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system, was jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. **Hi3DEval** utilizes a three-tiered evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material-theme) to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. Check it out! 👇 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. **DeepMind's Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** broke down the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** at Stanford's CS25 course. **Denny Zhou** pointed out that LLM reasoning occurs through generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and explored techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Fascinating stuff! 🧠 [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)
