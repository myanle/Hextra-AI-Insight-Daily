### **Today's AI Buzz** 🚀

1.  **Claude Code** rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** issue caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about spitting out direct answers; instead, it acts like your personal programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at critical steps, get hands-on, and truly master their coding skills. 💪

2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight open-source large model with just 270M parameters, is truly crushing it! This little powerhouse performs exceptionally well in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some larger models. Its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 📱💨

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** laid out OpenAI's massive future plans, including investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware. He's even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** space, aiming to completely shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI era, similar to the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times and the worst of times." 🤯✨

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th Anniversary, and the ASI Era is officially kicking off! They're also actively recruiting for various AI-related roles, including AI Industry Reporter, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) 🎉

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system, was jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and multiple universities. This system utilizes a three-tiered evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions for their overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard) 🔍

6.  **The "build-your-own-x" GitHub project** offers learning paths to build various technologies from scratch, helping users master programming skills through hands-on practice. It's a fantastic resource for learning by doing! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x) 🛠️

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** provides a comprehensive 90-day learning plan that covers every aspect of cybersecurity, from foundational certifications to advanced techniques. Get ready to level up your security game! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity) 🛡️

8.  **awesome-mac** is a true treasure trove for Mac users, curating a fantastic collection of advanced software for macOS. It's like finding a hidden gem for your Apple setup! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac) 💎

9.  **parlant** is an LLM agent designed to simplify large language model (LLM) applications, making them super easy to deploy and use. Smooth sailing for your LLM projects! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant) 🤖

10. **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that packs a punch, integrating AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. Talk about an all-in-one solution! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic) ✨

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou**, during his Stanford CS25 course, dove deep into the inference mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. He explained that LLM reasoning occurs through the generation of a series of **intermediate tokens** and also explored techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Fascinating stuff! [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk) 🧠

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton** are on different pages regarding AI safety issues. Li zeroes in on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more concerned about superintelligent AI spiraling out of control, advocating for designing **AI that "cares about humanity."** Key potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence." 🤔
