### Today's AI News

1.  🚀 **Claude Code** just launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the "AI brain drain" issue that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at critical steps and get their hands dirty to truly master programming skills.

2.  ✨ **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large model, is a true superstar. With just 270M parameters, it absolutely crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models! Its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building fast, cheap, and lightweight AI apps.

3.  💥 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a massive vision for the company. We're talking trillions in investments for data centers and cutting-edge AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interface** tech to completely shake up search and social media. He believes we're in a pivotal, "internet bubble"-esque era for AI, and the GPT-5 launch will mark "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  🎈 **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're currently hiring for a bunch of roles, including AI industry lead writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Job Openings](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  📈 **Hi3DEval** is a new, more scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

7.  🔐 **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** offers a sweet 90-day cybersecurity learning plan that covers absolutely everything, from basic certifications to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

10. ✨ **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that brings together AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. 🧠 DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou, in his Stanford CS25 course, broke down the **Large Language Model (LLM)** reasoning mechanism. He pointed out that LLM reasoning happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. 🤝 Li Feifei and Hinton have different takes on AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests designing **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts for potential AI risks.
