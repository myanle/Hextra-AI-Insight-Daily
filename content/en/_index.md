### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a cool new **'Learning Mode'**! 🤯 It's designed to fight off that **'AI brain drain'** many of us face from relying too much on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, pausing at critical steps and nudging you to get hands-on. The goal? To truly master your coding skills!

2.  Check this out: **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is a game-changer! 🚀 This open-source, lightweight large model boasts a mere 270M parameters but absolutely crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, outperforming even some much bigger models. Its super low power consumption means it can run on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building fast, cheap, and agile AI apps. Sweet!

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just unveiled a mind-blowing blueprint for the future! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in data centers and cutting-edge AI hardware, with plans to even dive into **brain-computer interfaces** and totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a crucial AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will mark "the best of times and the worst of times."
