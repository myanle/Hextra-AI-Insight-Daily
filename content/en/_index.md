### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the notorious "AI-induced brain drain" issue that comes from relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about just handing out answers; instead, it acts like a personal coding fitness coach 🏋️‍♂️, guiding users to pause at critical junctures, get hands-on, and truly master their programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's open-source, lightweight large language model. Packing just 270M parameters, it totally crushes it on **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models! 🚀 Its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for cranking out lightweight, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has painted a mind-blowing vision for the company's future 🌠: think trillions of dollars invested in data centers and new AI hardware, even a deep dive into **brain-computer interfaces**! The goal? To totally revolutionize search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack-dab in a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will mark "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its tenth anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! 🎉 They're on the hunt for talent, including an AI Industry Reporting Lead Writer, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a snazzy, more scientific, and super comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system unveiled by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. 🎓 It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to dive deep into 3D models, analyzing their overall form, local structure, and material authenticity from multiple angles. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project is a treasure trove, offering learning paths to build all sorts of tech from scratch 🛠️. It's designed to help users really get a grip on programming skills through hands-on practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** offers an awesome 90-day cybersecurity learning plan that covers absolutely everything cybersecurity-related, from foundational certifications all the way to advanced techniques. 🛡️ [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  **awesome-mac** is an absolute goldmine 💎 for Mac users, compiling a stellar collection of advanced software for macOS. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **parlant** is a slick LLM agent that streamlines large language model (LLM) applications, making them super easy to deploy and use. 🤖 [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that's packed with features: it integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, instant messaging (IM), and online collaborative office systems. ✨ [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou blew minds 🧠 in his Stanford CS25 lecture, where he broke down the **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanism. He pointed out that LLM reasoning works by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. Li Feifei and Hinton have some differing opinions when it comes to AI safety. Li Feifei is all about focusing on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligent AI running wild and suggests we design AI that actually **cares about humans**. 💖 The two biggies in potential AI risks are "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence."
