### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  🧠 **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on, really mastering those programming skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, rocks! With just 270M parameters, it totally shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on gadgets like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building speedy, low-cost AI apps.

3.  🌌 OpenAI CEO Sam Altman laid out a wild blueprint for the company: investing trillions in data centers, developing new AI hardware, and even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a pivotal AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 launch marks "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  🥳 Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its 10th anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they're rolling out the red carpet for you to join! 5.  **Hi3DEval**, launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, is a more scientific and comprehensive evaluation system for 3D generation quality. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions for overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

Li Feifei and Hinton have some differing views on AI safety; Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's worried about superintelligence running wild, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts behind AI's potential risks.
