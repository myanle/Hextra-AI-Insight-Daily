### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out its brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 🧠. This awesome feature aims to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** problem that comes from leaning too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause at crucial steps and actually get your hands dirty building things. The goal? To truly master those programming skills! 💪
2.  Meet **Gemma 3 270M** 🚀, Google's freshly open-sourced lightweight large language model. This little powerhouse packs a punch with just 270M parameters, yet it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models! Its low-power design means it can run smoothly on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building super light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🐯
3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a truly grand vision for the company 🌌! We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building out data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware. Altman reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch? He sees it as marking "the best of times, and the worst of times." 💰🤯
