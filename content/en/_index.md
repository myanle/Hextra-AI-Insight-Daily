### Today's AI News

1. **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠 It's all about tackling that dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** from over-relying on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "gym coach," nudging users to pause at crucial steps and get their hands dirty, truly mastering those coding skills. 💪

2. Say hello to **Gemma 3 270M**! 🚀 Google just open-sourced this lightweight large language model. Packing only 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi – perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 📱💡

3. **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman laid out some seriously ambitious plans! 🌌 He's talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a make-or-break AI moment, kinda like the dot-com bubble. The release of GPT-5? He calls it "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯💰

4. 🎉 **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its tenth anniversary and the dawn of the AGI era! Guess what? 5. **Hi3DEval** is here to level up 3D! 🤩 Shanghai AI Lab, teaming up with several universities, just rolled out this super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. It uses a three-tier protocol – object-level, component-level, and material theme – to break down 3D models and analyze their overall shape, local structure, and material realism from multiple angles. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. There's a bit of a friendly debate going on! 🤖 **Li Feifei and Hinton** have different takes on AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's more worried about superintelligence running wild, suggesting we design **AI that genuinely cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts for potential AI risks. Food for thought! 🤔
