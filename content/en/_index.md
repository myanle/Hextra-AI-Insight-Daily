### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-spanking-new **'Learning Mode'**! 🧠 This bad boy aims to tackle the infamous "AI brain atrophy" problem that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at key steps and get their hands dirty, truly mastering those programming skills. No more AI spoon-feeding!

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, super lightweight model, is making waves! 🚀 With only 270M parameters, it totally shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building quick, cheap, and agile AI apps. How cool is that?

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman laid out a mind-blowing master plan for the company! 🤯 He's talking trillions in investments for data centers and killer new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interfaces**. The goal? To totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a crucial AI era, kinda like the internet bubble. He sees GPT-5's launch as a sign that "it's the best of times, it's the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is kicking off its 10th-anniversary celebration and the dawn of the AGI era! 🎉 They're currently on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. 5.  **Hi3DEval** is here to revolutionize 3D generation quality! 🤩 This new, super scientific and comprehensive evaluation system was cooked up by the Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities. It uses a three-tier protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to deliver multi-dimensional analysis on the overall form, local structure, and material authenticity of 3D models. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
