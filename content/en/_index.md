### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the issue of **"AI skill atrophy"** (or "AI brain drain") caused by long-term reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on with tasks, and truly master their programming skills. 💪

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large language model (LLM), only has 270M parameters but still shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models! ✨ Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and cost-effective AI applications. 📱💡

3. OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman, has laid out an ambitious blueprint for the company, outlining plans to invest trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing novel AI hardware. 🤯 They're even looking to venture into **brain-computer interface** tech, aiming to disrupt search and social media as we know it. He believes we're in a pivotal, "internet bubble"-like era for AI, with the GPT-5 launch signaling "the best of times, and the worst of times." 🚀
