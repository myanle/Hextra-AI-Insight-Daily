### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to combat that pesky **"AI brain drain"** from relying too much on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, pausing you at key steps and encouraging you to get hands-on. That way, you actually *master* those programming skills! 🧠💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google, is making waves! With only 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its super low-power design means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi – perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱

3.  **OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman**, is dreaming BIG! His grand vision for OpenAI includes pumping trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**. He's aiming to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're at a pivotal AI moment, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release? He calls it "the best of times, the worst of times." 💰✨

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI era! And guess what? They're on the hunt for AI talent, including AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! 🎉🥳 Check out the [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a collaboration between Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, introduces a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. This bad boy uses a three-tier protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to dive deep into 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty cool, right? 😎 Take a peek at the [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and the [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard).

11. **Denny Zhou**, Chief Scientist at DeepMind, recently broke down **Large Language Model (LLM)** reasoning mechanisms in his Stanford CS25 course. He pointed out that LLM reasoning happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Fascinating stuff! 🤯📺 [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligent AI running wild, suggesting we should design AI that *cares* about humans. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts at the heart of AI's potential risks. 🤖💡
