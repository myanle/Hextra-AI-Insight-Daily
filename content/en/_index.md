### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code just unveiled a brand-new "Learning Mode" 🧠. This cool feature tackles the gnarly problem of "AI brain atrophy" that pops up from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding users to pause at key steps and get hands-on, so you actually master those programming skills.

2.  Gemma 3 270M, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is turning heads! 🚀 This tiny titan, with only 270M parameters, absolutely crushes it in "instruction following" and "text structuring" tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power draw means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just painted a seriously grand vision for the company 🌌. OpenAI plans to pour trillions into building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving into brain-computer interfaces, aiming to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, with the GPT-5 launch signaling "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  Xinzhiyuan just celebrated its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're actively on the hunt for AI talent, including AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editing interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  Hi3DEval is a new, more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system 🤩. Launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, it uses a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

[🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou blew minds at Stanford's CS25 course 🤯. He explained the reasoning mechanisms of Large Language Models (LLMs), pointing out that LLM inference works by generating a series of "intermediate tokens," and delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. 12. Fei-Fei Li and Hinton have some differing views on AI safety concerns 🤖. Li Fei-Fei focuses on "design, governance, and values," while Hinton worries about superintelligent AI getting out of control, suggesting we design "AI that cares about humanity." "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts for AI's potential risks.
