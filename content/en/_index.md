### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to fight off "AI brain atrophy" from leaning too heavily on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym trainer, nudging you to pause at key steps and get your hands dirty, so you really master those programming skills.
2.  Google just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model with only 270M parameters. But don't let that fool you! This little powerhouse crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power design means it can run on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.
3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman recently laid out OpenAI's massive vision: think trillions of dollars poured into data centers and new AI hardware, even jumping into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a pivotal AI moment, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 launch will be a "best of times, worst of times" vibe.

It's way more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tier assessment protocol (object-level, component-level, and material-theme) to give 3D models a multi-dimensional check-up on overall form, local structure, and material realism. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more concerned about superintelligent AI running wild and suggests we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts of AI's potential risks.
