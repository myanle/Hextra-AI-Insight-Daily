### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out its brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🎉 This mode is designed to tackle the dreaded **"AI skill atrophy"** problem that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding "gym trainer," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and actually get their hands dirty with tasks, so they genuinely master programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a total game-changer! 🤯 Despite having only 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just laid out OpenAI's epic **grand vision**! 🚀 We're talking trillions of dollars poured into data centers and new AI hardware, even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interfaces (BCIs)** with plans to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a pivotal AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release screams, "It was the best of times, it was the worst of times."

4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its 10th anniversary, kicking off the **ASI era**! 🤯 They're on the hunt for AI talent, including an AI Industry Reporting Lead Writer, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join the crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** 🚀, a new evaluation system from the Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities, just dropped! It’s a more scientific and super comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol covering object-level, component-level, and material themes to conduct a multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models’ overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we design **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. "Goal Misgeneralization" and "Instrumental Convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts for potential AI risks.
