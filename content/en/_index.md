### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code has just dropped a game-changing **"learning mode"** designed to tackle "AI brain atrophy" – yeah, that skill drain from leaning on AI too much. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, prompting you to pause and get hands-on with tasks at key steps, so you genuinely master those programming skills. 💪🧠

2.  Gemma 3 270M, Google's open-source, super-lightweight large model, is crushing it! Despite having only 270M parameters, it totally shines in **instruction-following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱

3.  OpenAI's CEO Sam Altman just laid out a seriously ambitious roadmap for the company: think trillions of dollars pumped into building data centers and next-gen AI hardware, even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interfaces**! The goal? To totally shake up search and social media as we know it. Altman believes we're in a pivotal, "internet bubble"-esque era for AI, and the GPT-5 launch? That's marking "the best of times, and the worst of times." 💰🌌

Xinzhiyuan just celebrated its 10th anniversary, kicking off the ASI era with a bang! 🎉 They're currently on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. Wanna watch? ▶️ [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. Li Feifei and Hinton are on different pages when it comes to AI safety. Li Feifei is zeroed in on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is seriously worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we craft **AI that actually cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts at the heart of AI's potential risks. 🤖🤔
