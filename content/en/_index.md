### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code just launched its brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** problem from relying too much on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get their hands dirty, ensuring they truly master coding skills. 💪

2.  Google's **Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight, open-source large model with just 270M parameters. But don't let its size fool you! It totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and cheap AI apps. 🚀

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just laid out a seriously ambitious blueprint for **OpenAI**: talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** space, with plans to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 launch will mark "the best of times, and the worst of times." Wild, right? 🤯

And hey, they're on the hunt for AI-loving folks, with open positions like AI industry reporter, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. Pretty neat! ✨ [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

Fascinating stuff! 🧠 [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

Fei-Fei Li and Hinton have some differing takes on AI safety. **Fei-Fei Li** is all about **design, governance, and values**, while **Hinton** is more worried about superintelligent AI running wild, suggesting we design **AIs that genuinely care about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big core concepts when it comes to AI's potential risks. Food for thought! 🤔
