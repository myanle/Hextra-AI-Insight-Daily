### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-spanking-new **"Learning Mode,"** aiming to tackle the infamous **"AI brain drain"** that comes from leaning too heavily on AI. This isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding "gym coach," nudging you to pause at key steps, get your hands dirty, and truly nail those programming skills. 💪🧠

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, super lightweight large model (just 270M parameters!), is absolutely crushing it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. 🤯 Its low-power mojo means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, blazing-fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱

3.  **OpenAI's** CEO, Sam Altman, just dropped their massive blueprint for the future: talking trillions in investments for data centers and killer new AI hardware, even eyeing a leap into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. 💰 He reckons we're smack dab in a critical AI moment, kinda like the internet bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release? That's just the start of "the best of times, the worst of times." Wild, right? 🌌

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and the launch of the AGI era – what a party! 🎉 They're actively hiring for a bunch of AI-related roles, including AI industry lead writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they're super keen to have you on board! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

Check it out! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. **DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou,** recently broke down the **Large Language Model (LLM)** reasoning mechanism in a Stanford CS25 course. 🤯 He explained that LLM inference happens by generating a sequence of **intermediate tokens** and dove into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Wanna see? [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton** are definitely on different pages when it comes to AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's super worried about superintelligence running rampant, suggesting we need to craft **AI that actually cares about humanity**. The two big concepts for potential AI risks? 'Goal misgeneralization' and 'instrumental convergence.' Food for thought, eh? 🤔
