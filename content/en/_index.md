### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠 This bad boy aims to tackle the dreaded **"AI skill atrophy"** that comes from leaning on AI too much. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," prompting you to pause at key steps and get your hands dirty, so you truly master those coding skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large language model, is a real powerhouse! 🚀 Clocking in at just 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman laid out a wild vision for the company's future! 🤯 He's talking about investing trillions in data centers and next-gen AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI moment, much like the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is kicking off its tenth-anniversary celebration and ushering in the ASI era! 🎉 They're currently on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join the team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Job Openings](link-to-xinzhiyuan-careers-page-not-provided-in-original)

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a collaboration between Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, introduces a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. ✨ It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct a multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. Check out the project! 12. **Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton** are on different pages when it comes to AI safety! 🤔 Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligent AI running wild, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts for potential AI risks.
