### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠💡 This cool feature aims to tackle the dreaded **"AI-induced brain drain"** that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding users to pause at key steps, get their hands dirty, and truly master programming skills.

2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is making waves! 🚀✨ This open-source, lightweight large language model boasts a tiny 270M parameters but absolutely crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its super low-power footprint means it can totally rock on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, perfect for building speedy, low-cost AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has unfurled a wildly ambitious blueprint for the company! 🤯 He's talking about investing trillions upon trillions of dollars to build data centers, develop new AI hardware, and even dive into the **brain-computer interface** game, all to disrupt search and social media as we know it. Altman reckons we're at a super critical moment for AI, much like the dot-com boom, and the launch of GPT-5 truly signals "it was the best of times, it was the worst of times."
