### **Today's AI News**

1. Claude Code has rolled out a brand-new "Learning Mode" 💡, aiming to tackle the "AI brain atrophy" issue that comes from long-term AI dependence. This mode isn't about just giving answers; instead, it acts like a coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at critical steps and complete tasks themselves to truly master programming skills. 💪

2. Gemma 3 270M ✨, Google's lightweight large model, has been open-sourced! Boasting only 270M parameters, it totally crushes it on instruction following and text structuring tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption feature means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀

3. OpenAI CEO, Altman, has laid out a mind-blowing grand blueprint for OpenAI 🤯: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers, develop new AI hardware, and even dive into the brain-computer interface field, aiming to totally disrupt search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI era, much like the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, and the worst of times."
