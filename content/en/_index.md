2.  Gemma 3 270M, Google's latest open-source gem, is a lightweight large model packing only 270M parameters! 🤯 But don't let the size fool you – it crushes "instruction following" and "text structuring" tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power nature means it can run on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱
