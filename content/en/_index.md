### **Daily AI Buzz**

1.  💡💪 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand you answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding gym instructor, guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with the tasks, helping them truly master their programming skills.

2.  ✨📱 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, boasts only 270M parameters but absolutely shines in instruction following and text structuring tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like mobile phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  🚀🤯 OpenAI CEO Altman has painted an ambitious vision for **OpenAI**: investing trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even venturing into brain-computer interfaces (BCI), with plans to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI era, much like the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks that "it's the best of times, it's the worst of times."

5.  🔬 **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It utilizes a three-tiered evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to multidimensionally analyze the overall form, local structure, and material authenticity of 3D models. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. ⚖️ Fei-Fei Li and Hinton differ in their views on AI safety issues. Fei-Fei Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about uncontrolled superintelligence, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. **"Goal misgeneralization"** and **"instrumental convergence"** are two core concepts of AI's potential risks.
