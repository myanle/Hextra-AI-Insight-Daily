### Today's AI News

1.  🧠 **Claude Code** just rolled out its brand-new **"Learning Mode,"** aiming to tackle the dreaded "AI brain atrophy"—AKA skill degradation from over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it's like having a personal programming "fitness coach" that prompts users to pause, dive in, and actually complete tasks themselves to truly master coding skills. Pretty cool, right?

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's latest open-source gem, a lightweight large model with only 270M parameters. But don't let the size fool you! It punches above its weight, delivering stellar performance in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for building speedy, low-cost AI applications.

3.  💰 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman laid out his ambitious vision: pouring trillions into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interfaces (BCIs)**, with plans to disrupt search and social media. He reckons we're at a pivotal AI moment, akin to the dot-com bubble, stating that the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, and the worst of times." Talk about high stakes!

They're on the hunt for top talent, including an AI Industry Reporter Lead, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. 5.  ✨ **Hi3DEval** is a game-changer! This more scientific and comprehensive evaluation system for 3D generation quality was launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It uses a three-layer evaluation protocol covering object-level, component-level, and material themes, offering multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. Check it out: [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "tool convergence" are flagged as two core concepts of potential AI risks. Food for thought!
