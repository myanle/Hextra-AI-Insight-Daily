### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  💡 **Claude Code** rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode,"** specifically designed to tackle the **"AI brain drain"** issue caused by long-term AI dependence. Instead of directly spitting out answers, this mode acts like a programming 'fitness coach,' guiding users to pause at critical steps and complete tasks themselves, ensuring they truly master their coding skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google, boasting only 270M parameters. Yet, it absolutely rocks at **instruction following** and **text structuring tasks**, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building speedy, low-cost, and super lightweight AI applications.

3.  💰 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman painted a grand vision for the company: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interface** tech, with plans to totally disrupt search and social media. He believes we're at a pivotal, internet-bubble-like moment for AI, and the upcoming release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, the worst of times."

5.  📈 **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct a multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. 12. 🤔 Li Feifei and Hinton hold differing views on AI safety issues. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton worries about superintelligent AI losing control and suggests designing **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts defining AI's potential risks.
