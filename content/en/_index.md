### **Today's AI News**

1.  🧠💪 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the issue of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at critical steps and complete tasks hands-on to genuinely master coding skills.

2.  ✨🚀 **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's open-source, lightweight large language model. With only 270M parameters, it shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like smartphones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  🤯💸 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a wild blueprint for the company: investing trillions into data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**, with plans to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  ✨🎂 **Xinzhiyuan's** 10th-anniversary celebration kicked off the ASI era! Xinzhiyuan is currently hiring for several AI-related roles, including AI industry lead writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. 5.  📏📊 **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system. Launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, it uses a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, including overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. 🧠💡 **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** dropped some serious knowledge during a Stanford CS25 course, explaining the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. He highlighted that LLM inference is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and dove into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. 🤝⚠️ **Li Feifei and Geoffrey Hinton** have different takes on AI safety. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton frets about superintelligence spiraling out of control, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts behind AI's potential risks.
