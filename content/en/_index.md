### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has launched a brand-new "Learning Mode." This mode tackles the "AI brain atrophy" problem caused by long-term AI dependence. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at critical steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they truly master programming skills. 🧠✨

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large language model open-sourced by Google, boasts only 270M parameters. Yet, it performs exceptionally well on instruction following and text structuring tasks, even outshining some larger models! 🚀 Its low power consumption means it can run on devices like mobile phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and cost-effective AI applications. 📱💡

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a super ambitious blueprint for the company. The plan? Invest trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into the brain-computer interface (BCI) world, with the goal of totally disrupting search and social media. 💰🌌 Altman feels we're smack dab in a crucial AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's release signaling "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary, kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're on the hunt for AI-related talent, with openings for an AI Industry Reporting Lead, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they want you to join the team! 🥳 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a super cool, more scientific, and comprehensive system for evaluating 3D generation quality. Launched by the Shanghai AI Laboratory alongside a bunch of universities, it uses a three-tier protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct a multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall shape, local structure, and how real their materials look. Check it out! 😎 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
