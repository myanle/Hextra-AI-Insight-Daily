### Today's AI News

1. Claude Code has just launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 🚀, aiming to tackle the **"AI brain drain"** problem caused by over-reliance on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like a "programming fitness coach" 🏋️, guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks to truly master coding skills.

2. **Gemma 3 270M** 🌟 is Google's new open-source, lightweight large model. Weighing in at just 270M parameters, this bad boy delivers stellar performance on **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some much larger models! Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a truly ambitious blueprint for **OpenAI** 🤯: we're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** space, with plans to totally disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI era, reminiscent of the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release marks, as he put it, "It was the best of times, it was the worst of times."
