### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 💪 It's tackling the pesky issue of **"AI brain atrophy"** that comes from over-relying on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "gym coach," nudging users to pause at crucial steps and get their hands dirty with tasks, ultimately helping them truly nail down programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, is quite the performer! 🚀 Even though it only has 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some much larger models. Plus, its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building quick, lightweight, and super affordable AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** recently painted a seriously ambitious picture for the company's future! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars poured into data centers, new AI hardware development, and even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interface** tech. The goal? To totally revolutionize search and social media as we know it. Altman believes we're in a pivotal, "internet bubble"-like era for AI, with the GPT-5 release marking "the best of times and the worst of times."

5.  Get ready for **Hi3DEval**! 😎 This new, super scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system was co-developed by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, checking out their overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts for potential AI risks.
