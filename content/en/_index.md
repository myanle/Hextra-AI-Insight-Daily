### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! This mode is designed to tackle the infamous **"AI brain drain"** (aka skill atrophy) that comes from relying too much on AI. Think of it less as an answer machine and more like a personal programming coach, guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on, and truly master coding skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large language model, is a total game-changer! Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it punches way above its weight, excelling in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks—even outperforming some much larger models. Its low power consumption means it can run smoothly on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing vision for the company's future! 🤯 He's talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interface** tech with plans to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's launch signaling "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  It's a big moment for **Xinzhiyuan**! They're celebrating their 10th anniversary and kicking off the AGI era! 🥳 Plus, they're on the hunt for AI-loving talent, including AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they want you! Check out the [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](missing_Xinzhiyuan_recruitment_link_in_original).

5.  Say hello to **Hi3DEval**! This is a cutting-edge, more scientific, and super comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to deliver multi-dimensional analysis on 3D models' overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. Get the full scoop on the [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and check out the [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard).

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts highlighting these potential AI risks.
