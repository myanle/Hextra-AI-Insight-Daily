2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, super-lightweight large model, is turning heads! Weighing in at just 270M parameters, this bad boy absolutely crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low power consumption means it can run smoothly on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱
