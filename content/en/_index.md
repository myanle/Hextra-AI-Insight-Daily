### **Today's AI News**

1.  💪 **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that gnarly "AI brain drain" problem from relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; it's more like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding users to pause, get hands-on with crucial steps, and truly master their programming chops.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch despite its tiny 270M parameters. It absolutely crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building fast, cheap, and snappy AI apps.

3.  💰 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a wild, ambitious blueprint for the company: think multi-trillion-dollar investments in data centers and next-gen AI hardware, maybe even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're smack-dab in an "AI boom" moment, much like the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's launch signaling "the best of times, and the worst of times."

4.  🥳 **Xinzhiyuan** is throwing a massive 10th-anniversary bash, kicking off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! 5.  🔬 **Hi3DEval** is a brand-new, more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system cooked up by the Shanghai AI Lab alongside a bunch of universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol covering object-level, component-level, and material themes, diving deep into 3D models' overall shape, local structure, and material realism from multiple angles. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

7.  🔒 **`90DaysOfCyberSecurity`** offers a comprehensive 90-day cybersecurity learning plan that covers absolutely everything cybersecurity-related, from foundational certifications to advanced tricks. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

10. ✨ **`magic`** is an open-source AI productivity platform that brings together AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. 🤯 DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou, totally blew minds at his Stanford CS25 lecture, explaining the **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanism. He pointed out that LLM reasoning happens by cranking out a series of **intermediate tokens** and dove into cool stuff like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. 🧐 **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** are on different pages when it comes to AI safety. Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we design **AI that actually cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts at the heart of AI's potential risks.
