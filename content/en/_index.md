### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  Claude Code just dropped its brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** from over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; it's more like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause and actually do the work at crucial steps, so they genuinely master programming skills. 💪

2.  Google's open-source **Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight large model with a mere 270M parameters, yet it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building fast, cheap, and nimble AI apps. 🚀

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just laid out a massive roadmap for **OpenAI**: talking trillions in investments for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**. The plan? To totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI moment, akin to the internet bubble, with the GPT-5 launch marking "the best of times and the worst of times." 🤯

4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its tenth anniversary, kicking off the AGI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) ✨

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. This system uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. Fei-Fei Li and Hinton have different takes on AI safety concerns. Fei-Fei Li zeroes in on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of AI's potential risks.
