### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! It's designed to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** problem that comes from relying too much on AI. This mode won't just hand you the answers; instead, it's like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause at key steps and actually *do* the work yourself. The goal? To truly master those programming skills. ✨

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's open-source, lightweight large model! Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has painted a seriously ambitious picture for the company! He's talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** with plans to shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI era, kind of like the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯

4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its 10th anniversary and kicking off the AGI era! 🎉 They're currently on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a new, super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system! It was launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. This system uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, right? 👇 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton have some contrasting views on AI safety. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests designing **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks. 🤖⚖️
