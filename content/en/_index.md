### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  🧠 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the problem of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by long-term reliance on AI. Instead of just giving answers, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they truly master programming skills. 💪

2.  🚀 **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight, open-source large model. Despite having only 270M parameters, it totally shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and cost-effective AI applications. 📱

3.  💰 **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has laid out an ambitious roadmap for OpenAI. We're talking trillions of dollars invested to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface** sector with plans to disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI period, akin to the internet bubble, and the release of GPT-5 signifies "the best of times and the worst of times." 🌌

4.  🎉 **Xinzhiyuan (新智元)** is celebrating its 10th anniversary, marking the dawn of the ASI era! They are currently hiring for various AI-related positions, including lead AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join! 🌟 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  ✨ **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. Launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities, it employs a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. Check it out! 👇 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. 🧠 **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** delved into the **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanism during a Stanford CS25 course. Fascinating stuff! 🤯 [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. 🤔 **Li Feifei and Hinton** hold differing views on AI safety issues. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about uncontrolled superintelligence and suggests designing **AI that truly cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are identified as two core concepts of potential AI risks. Deep thoughts here! 💬
