### Today's AI News

1.  🧠 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode."** This innovative mode aims to tackle the "AI brain atrophy" issue stemming from long-term AI reliance. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like a coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at critical junctures, complete tasks hands-on, and truly master their programming skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, boasts excellent performance in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, despite its mere 270M parameters, even outshining some much larger models. Its low-power consumption makes it suitable for running on devices like mobile phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for building light, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  🌌 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a super ambitious blueprint for the company. This grand vision includes investing trillions of dollars in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even pushing into the **brain-computer interface** realm, aiming to revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI phase, similar to the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release marks "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  🥳 **Xinzhiyuan** just kicked off its tenth-anniversary celebration, marking the dawn of the ASI era! They're actively recruiting for several AI-related positions, including Lead AI Industry Reporter, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  🔬 **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. This system utilizes a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. 🤯 DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou recently lectured on **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanisms in Stanford's CS25 course. He explained that LLM reasoning is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and explored techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. 🤝 **Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton** hold differing views on AI safety issues. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about uncontrolled superintelligence and suggests designing **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
