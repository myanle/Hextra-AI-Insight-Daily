### **Today's AI Scoop**

1. **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new "Learning Mode" aimed at tackling the notorious "AI brain drain" problem caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't spoon-feed answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding trainer, guiding users to pause at critical steps, get their hands dirty, and truly master programming skills.

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's latest open-source lightweight large model, boasts a tiny 270M parameters yet shines in instruction following and text structuring tasks, outperforming even some much larger models. Its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like mobile phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3. **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has painted a grand vision for the company: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even diving into brain-computer interfaces (BCI). OpenAI plans to disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're in a crucial AI period, akin to the dot-com bubble, with the GPT-5 launch signaling "It was the best of times, it was the worst of times."

4. **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're actively recruiting for multiple roles, including AI industry reporter-in-chief, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. They're inviting all AI enthusiasts to join the crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Job Openings](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5. **Hi3DEval**, a spiffy new 3D generation quality evaluation system, was launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It adopts a more scientific and comprehensive approach, using a three-tiered evaluation protocol covering object-level, component-level, and material themes, for multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6. **build-your-own-x** is a cool GitHub project that offers learning paths to build various technologies from scratch, helping users master programming skills through hands-on practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7. **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** offers a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan, covering all aspects of cybersecurity, from basic certifications to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

9. **parlant** is an LLM agent that simplifies large language model (LLM) applications, making it super easy to deploy and use. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that integrates AI agents, workflow engines, instant messaging (IM), and online collaborative office systems. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou**, recently delved into the reasoning mechanisms of Large Language Models (LLMs) at Stanford's CS25 course. He pointed out that LLM reasoning is achieved by generating a series of intermediate tokens and discussed techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton** hold differing views on AI safety concerns. Li Fei-Fei focuses on design, governance, and values, while Hinton worries about superintelligent AI running amok, suggesting we design AIs that care about humanity. "Target misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts behind AI's potential risks.
