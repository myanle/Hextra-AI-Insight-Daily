### **Today's AI News**

1.  🧠💻 Claude Code has launched a brand new **"Learning Mode"** aiming to tackle the problem of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by long-term AI reliance. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks hands-on to truly master coding skills.

2.  🚀🤖 Google has open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model with only 270M parameters. It really shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and low-cost AI applications.

3.  💸🔮 OpenAI's CEO Altman has outlined an ambitious blueprint for OpenAI: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interface** technology, with plans to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're currently at a critical AI juncture, much like the internet bubble, and the release of GPT-5 signifies "the best of times, and the worst of times."

Li Fei-Fei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about superintelligence spiraling out of control, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts highlighting AI's potential risks.
