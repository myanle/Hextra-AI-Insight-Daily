### **Today's AI News** 🚀

1.  **Claude Code's "Learning Mode"**: Claude Code just launched a fresh "learning mode" to tackle that pesky "AI brain atrophy" from relying too much on AI. Instead of just giving you the answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," nudging you to pause, get your hands dirty, and truly master those programming skills. Smart, right?
2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M**: Google went ahead and open-sourced Gemma 3 270M, a super lightweight large model (only 270M params!). But don't let the size fool ya – it crushes "instruction following" and "text structuring" tasks, even outperforming some bigger models. Plus, its low power use means it can chill on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, perfect for building speedy, low-cost AI apps. 🤯
3.  **OpenAI's Grand Vision**: OpenAI CEO Sam Altman laid out a wild future: we're talking trillions invested in data centers and next-gen AI hardware, even diving into brain-computer interfaces to revolutionize search and social media. Altman reckons we're at a critical AI moment, reminiscent of the internet bubble, and GPT-5's arrival signals "the best of times, the worst of times." Buckle up! 🚀
4.  **Xinzhiyuan's 10th Anniversary & Recruitment**: Xinzhiyuan just celebrated its big ten-year milestone, kicking off the ASI era! Pretty cool, huh? ✨ [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
