### Today's AI Buzz

1. **Claude Code** has unleashed a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! This mode is all about tackling the infamous "AI brain atrophy" (aka skill degradation) that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," pausing you at crucial steps and nudging you to get hands-on and actually complete tasks. This way, you truly master those programming skills! 💪

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large language model, is a total powerhouse! Clocking in at just 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Thanks to its low-power footprint, you can run this bad boy on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up lightweight, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀

3. **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing grand blueprint for the company! His vision? Trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and forging new AI hardware, even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interface** tech, with plans to totally flip search and social media on their heads. Altman believes we're smack-dab in a critical AI era, feeling a bit like the internet bubble days, and the release of GPT-5 is set to mark "the best of times, the worst of times." 🌌

4. Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they're extending a warm invitation to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Job Openings](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) 🎉

5. **Hi3DEval** is a snazzy new 3D generation quality evaluation system! It was cooked up by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities to be way more scientific and comprehensive. This system uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to run multi-dimensional analyses on a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. Check it out! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard) 😎

12. Li Feifei and Hinton have some pretty distinct takes on AI safety. Li Feifei zeroes in on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild, advocating for the creation of **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big core concepts when we talk about potential AI risks. 🤖
