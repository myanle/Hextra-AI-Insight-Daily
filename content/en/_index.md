### Today's AI News

1.  💪🧠**Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! It's designed to fight off that **"AI brain drain"** we get from leaning too heavily on AI. Instead of handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym coach, guiding you to pause at crucial steps and get your hands dirty, so you really nail those programming skills.
2.  🚀📱**Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a total powerhouse despite having just 270M parameters! This little champ excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some way larger models. Its low-power design means it can run smoothly on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it clutch for building quick, affordable AI applications.
