### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** has dropped a fresh "Learning Mode" 🤯. This bad boy aims to tackle the "AI brain drain" that comes from leaning too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, pushing you to pause at key moments, get hands-on, and really nail down those programming skills.

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is quite the performer! 🚀 Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it crushes "instruction following" and "text structuring" tasks, even outshining some beefier models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3. **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing blueprint for the company. 🤯 We're talking trillions invested in data centers and next-gen AI hardware, even diving into brain-computer interfaces to totally disrupt search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a critical AI moment, kinda like the dot-com bubble days, and GPT-5's launch? That's the signal that "it's the best of times, it's the worst of times."

4. **Xinzhiyuan** is throwing a massive bash for its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5. **Hi3DEval**, a joint effort by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system. This cool system uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to dive deep into 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

[▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** are on different pages when it comes to AI safety. 🤖 Li Feifei is all about the "design, governance, and values" angle, while Hinton's more worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests we design "AI that cares about humans." "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big core concepts for potential AI risks.
