### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** just dropped its brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 🧠! This awesome feature aims to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** (aka skill degradation) caused by relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding trainer, guiding you to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks to truly master your programming skills.

2. **Gemma 3 270M** 🚀, Google's open-source, lightweight large model, rocks with a mere 270M parameters! It absolutely shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Thanks to its low power consumption, it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just painted a massive vision for **OpenAI** 💰🧠: we're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even dabbling in **brain-computer interfaces**, with plans to turn search and social media on their heads! He believes we're at a critical AI juncture, much like the dot-com bubble era, and the release of GPT-5 signals that "It's the best of times, it's the worst of times."

4. It's a 🎉 party time! XinZhiYuan is celebrating its tenth anniversary and ushering in the ASI era! **XinZhiYuan** is currently on the hunt for AI-related talent, including AI industry reporting lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. 5. **Hi3DEval** ✨ is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to perform multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

7. **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** 🛡️ offers a 90-day learning plan that covers all angles of cybersecurity, from basic certifications to advanced tricks. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

10. **magic** ✨ is an open-source AI productivity platform that integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. In a Stanford CS25 course, **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** 🧠 broke down the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. He highlighted that LLM reasoning is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also explored techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** 🧐 hold differing views on AI safety issues. Fei-Fei Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton worries about superintelligence running amok and suggests designing **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
