1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand new **"Learning Mode"**! 🚀 This mode is all about tackling the "AI brain atrophy" problem that pops up from relying on AI too much. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal programming "fitness coach," nudging you to pause at key steps and actually *do* the work yourself. That's how you truly master those coding skills!
2.  Say hello to **Gemma 3 270M**! 🐯🚀 This lightweight large language model from Google is a real stunner. With just 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some beefier models. Plus, its super low power consumption means it can run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps.

To totally revolutionize search and social media. It analyzes their overall shape, tiny structural details, and how real their materials look, all from multiple angles.
