### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has just rolled out a brand-new **"learning mode"**! 🎉 This mode is designed to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** problem that comes from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts more like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks. The goal? To truly master their programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large language model, is a real game-changer! 🚀 With a mere 270M parameters, it totally crushes it on **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power design means you can run it on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI's** CEO, Sam Altman, has laid out an absolutely wild blueprint for the company's future! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interface** tech with plans to totally disrupt search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release? That's gonna mark "the best of times and the worst of times."

This bad boy uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analyses of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
