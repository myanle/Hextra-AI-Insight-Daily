### **Today's AI News**

1. **Microsoft Edge's new Copilot mode** is here, and it's a total game-changer! This bad boy can actually *understand* the web pages you're browsing, helping you *organize information*, and even *generate content*. Think of it as your super-savvy personal assistant for all things web, from booking tickets to writing summaries – it handles it all with ease. What's even cooler? It can "see" all your open tabs simultaneously, pulling info together. For example, if you're checking flights and hotels, it'll automatically compare prices and info, recommending the best options! Copilot also rocks powerful visual AI, so it can "read" your screen, analyze charts, and pull out key points from papers! ![Image](https://assets-v2.circle.so/jucv4odkdb4nyeapuk15iws8crcn)

2. **Anthropic's research** is getting wild! They've managed to give AI a "personality monitor." Large language models are becoming more human-like, complete with "personalities" and even "emotions," but these "personalities" can be unpredictable. Anthropic's team developed a method to pinpoint the neural code in an AI's "brain" that controls its "personality"—what they call **personality vectors**. It's like they've hooked up a "personality monitor" to the AI, allowing them to track its "moods" in real-time, catching and correcting undesirable behaviors pronto. Their experiments showed this method doesn't just monitor personality shifts; it can also prevent AI from "going rogue" during training and even identify training data that might lead to "bad" AI behavior. Pretty neat, right? ![Image](https://assets-v2.circle.so/3yb0jz5eq75uyhroecvxy26yapst)

3. **Xiaomi has fully open-sourced its MiDashengLM-7B multimodal large model**, and it's a *massive* breakthrough in audio understanding! This model doesn't just recognize speech; it can also understand environmental sounds and music, which is a super rare "full-domain audio understanding" capability in the industry. What's even wilder? Its inference speed is over 20 times faster than other similar models! Xiaomi plans to deploy it on terminal devices, meaning you'll soon be able to use this powerful audio AI directly on your phone or smart speaker, all while protecting your privacy! ![Simultaneous interpretation, audio, wireless headphones](https://pic.chinaz.com/picmap/202507241717414354_2.jpg)

4. **"Ask Xiaobai" team** has dropped a bombshell: their fourth-generation open-source large model, **XBai o4**, is here and it's making *major* waves in **complex reasoning**! This bad boy uses an innovative **"reflective generation paradigm,"** blending **Long-CoT reinforcement learning** with **process scoring learning**. It can think through multiple steps just like a human and automatically pick the best reasoning path. **XBai o4's advantages?** In Medium mode, it blew past OpenAI's o3-mini, and in some tests, it even outshone Anthropic's Claude Opus! This means XBai o4 delivers top-tier performance in areas like **mathematical reasoning (AIME24, AIME25), programming (LiveCodeBench v5), and Chinese understanding (C-EVAL)**. Plus, it slashes **inference time by a whopping 99%**! **What's the big deal about open-sourcing?** "Ask Xiaobai" has open-sourced XBai o4's training and evaluation code on GitHub: [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/MetaStone-AI/XBai-o4). **Looking ahead,** XBai o4's arrival marks a crucial stride for open-source large models in the **complex reasoning** domain. And hey, while we're at it, another exciting project, **dyad**, a free, local, open-source AI application builder, is also grabbing a lot of attention: [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dyad-sh/dyad).
