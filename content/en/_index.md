### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧑‍💻🧠 This cool mode tackles the issue of **"AI brain atrophy"** that can creep in from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding 'fitness coach,' guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on, and truly nail down their programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's latest open-source lightweight large model! 🚀💡 Clocking in at just 270M parameters, this model truly shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a super ambitious blueprint for the company! 💰🌟 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in data centers, new AI hardware development, and even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**. The ultimate goal? To totally disrupt search and social media as we know it. Altman believes we're in a crucial AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 will mark "the best of times, and the worst of times."
