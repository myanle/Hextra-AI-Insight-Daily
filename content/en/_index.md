### Today's AI Buzz

1.  Claude Code just dropped its brand-new **"learning mode,"** aimed squarely at tackling the notorious **"AI brain drain"** problem that comes from relying too much on AI. 🤖🧠 Instead of spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "gym coach," nudging users to pause at critical junctures, get their hands dirty, and truly master programming skills.

2.  Google's **Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight, open-source large model that's causing a buzz! 🐯🚀 With just 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building speedy, cost-effective, and featherlight AI applications.

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing vision for **OpenAI**! 💰🌌 He's talking about investing trillions of dollars to build data centers, develop new AI hardware, and even dive headfirst into **brain-computer interfaces**, with plans to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a crucial AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the launch of GPT-5 signals that "it's the best of times, it's the worst of times."
