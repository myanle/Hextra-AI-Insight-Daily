### Today's AI News

1.  💡🧠 **Claude Code** has dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** from relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it's like a coding "personal trainer" that nudges users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on, and genuinely master their programming skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, rocks with just 270M parameters! It totally shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, outperforming even some chunkier models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building super light, fast, and cheap AI apps.

3.  💰🌌 **OpenAI's** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing master plan: think trillions invested in data centers and next-gen AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**! The goal? To totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a make-or-break AI moment, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  🥳💼 **Xinzhiyuan** is throwing a massive party for its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI era! And guess what? They're on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they're super keen to have you on board! "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts at the heart of AI's potential risks.
