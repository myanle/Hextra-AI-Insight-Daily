### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code has launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode”** designed to tackle the infamous **"AI brain drain”** problem, which often comes from relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about giving you direct answers. Nope! Instead, it acts like your personal coding gym instructor, guiding you to pause at crucial steps and get your hands dirty, so you can genuinely master those programming skills. 🤯

2.  Gemma 3 270M, Google's open-source, lightweight large language model, is quite the performer! Despite packing only 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some much larger models. Plus, its low-power consumption means it can happily run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up lightweight, lightning-fast, and super affordable AI applications. 🚀

3.  OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman, has laid out an absolutely epic blueprint for the company. We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving headfirst into the **brain-computer interface** arena, aiming to totally shake up search and social media. He believes we're smack dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 truly marks a 'best of times, worst of times' moment. 🤯

4.  Xinzhiyuan's 10th-anniversary celebration and the dawn of the ASI era are here! And guess what? Xinzhiyuan is currently hiring for a bunch of roles, including AI Industry Lead Reporter, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. So, if you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! 🎉 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  Hi3DEval, developed by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, is a spiffier, more comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis on the overall form, local structure, and material realism of 3D models. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

It's awesome for anyone who loves to learn by doing! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou, during his Stanford CS25 course, totally unpacked the **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanism. Mind blown! 🤯 [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. Fei-Fei Li and Hinton actually have differing views when it comes to AI safety. Li Fei-Fei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more concerned about superintelligence spiraling out of control, suggesting we design **AI that truly cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of AI's potential risks. Talk about a heavyweight debate! 🤔
