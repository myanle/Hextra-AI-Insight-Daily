### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** has launched its new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the "AI brain drain" issue from over-reliance on AI. This mode won't just spoon-feed answers; instead, it acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at key steps and get hands-on, ensuring they truly master their programming skills.

2.  Google just dropped **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight open-source large language model that's packing a punch! 🚀 Despite its mere 270M parameters, it totally shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power design means you can run this bad boy on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for cooking up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing vision for the company: talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**! 🤯 Their plan? To totally revolutionize search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a make-or-break AI moment, kinda like the internet bubble era, and the GPT-5 launch will be a real "best of times, worst of times" situation.

4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era – what a party! 🎉 They're also on the hunt for some awesome AI talent, including AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  Get ready for **Hi3DEval**, a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system cooked up by the Shanghai AI Lab and several universities! ✨ This bad boy uses a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to give you a multi-dimensional deep dive into 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The **`build-your-own-x`** GitHub project is an absolute gem! 💎 It lays out learning paths for building all sorts of tech from scratch, helping folks really get a grip on programming skills through hands-on practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  Wanna get seriously good at cybersecurity? Check out **`90DaysOfCyberSecurity`**! 🛡️ This project dishes out a 90-day learning plan that covers absolutely everything cybersecurity, from basic certifications to pro-level hacks. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  For all you Mac users out there, **`awesome-mac`** is your new best friend! 💻 This project is a total treasure trove of advanced software for Macs. Seriously, it's a must-see! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  Meet **`parlant`**, an LLM agent designed to make deploying and using large language model (LLM) applications a breeze! ✨ It's super easy to get up and running. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. Say hello to **`magic`**, an open-source AI productivity platform that's got it all! 🎩 It integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, instant messaging, and an online collaborative office system. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. Denny Zhou, DeepMind's Chief Scientist, spilled the beans on **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanisms during a Stanford CS25 course. 🧠 He broke down how LLMs reason by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and delved into cool techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. When it comes to AI safety, **Li Feifei and Hinton are on different pages**. 🧑‍🔬 Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is seriously worried about superintelligence going rogue and suggests we build **AI that actually cares about humanity**. Two big concepts behind potential AI risks are "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence."
