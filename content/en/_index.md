### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** 💡 has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the issue of "AI brain atrophy" caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they truly master programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** ✨, Google's open-source lightweight large model, boasts only 270M parameters but shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** 🤯 has outlined an ambitious vision for OpenAI: investing trillions in data centers and new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interfaces** with plans to revolutionize search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI era akin to the dot-com bubble, with the GPT-5 launch signaling "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** 🥳 is celebrating its tenth anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! Xinzhiyuan is actively recruiting for various AI-related roles, including AI industry lead reporter, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** 📊, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It utilizes a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions for overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** 🛡️ provides a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan, covering all aspects of cybersecurity, from basic certifications to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

9.  **parlant** 🧑‍💻 is an LLM agent that simplifies large language model (LLM) applications, making it easy to deploy and use. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** 🪄 is an open-source AI productivity platform that integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** 🧠 delved into the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** during a Stanford CS25 course. He explained that LLM reasoning is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and discussed techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** 🧐 hold differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about superintelligence spiraling out of control, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts defining AI's potential risks.
