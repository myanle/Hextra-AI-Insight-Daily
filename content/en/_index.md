### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠 It's designed to combat that pesky **"AI brain drain"** from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, pausing you at key steps to get your hands dirty and really nail those programming skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large language model, is quite the performer! 🚀 Despite its tiny 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some bigger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building fast, cheap, and cheerful AI apps. 📱

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman laid out a wild vision for the company! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and GPT-5's launch is a sign that it's "the best of times, and the worst of times." 💰✨

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** celebrated its 10th anniversary, marking the dawn of the ASI era! 🎉 They're on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love to have you join the squad! ✨ [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a game-changing 3D generation quality assessment system, jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. 😎 It's way more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to analyze 3D models across overall shape, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, right? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two big concepts when we talk about potential AI risks. 🤖
