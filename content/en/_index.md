### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-spanking-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🤯 This genius feature aims to fight off "AI brain atrophy" – yeah, that's when leaning too heavily on AI makes your skills shrivel. Instead of spoon-feeding you answers, this mode acts like your personal coding fitness coach 💪. It guides you to pause at critical junctures, get your hands dirty, and actually *do* the work, helping you truly nail down those programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** ✨ is Google's latest open-source lightweight large model, packing a punch with only 270M parameters! This little powerhouse totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some much larger models. And get this: its super low power consumption means it can happily run on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it an ideal choice for whipping up lightweight, rapid, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing roadmap for the company! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers, developing new AI hardware, and even venturing into **brain-computer interfaces**. The ultimate goal? To completely revolutionize search and social media as we know it. Altman believes we're smack dab in a critical AI period, echoing the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming launch of GPT-5 truly heralds "the best of times, the worst of times."

They're actively recruiting for various roles, including AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. 5.  **Hi3DEval** 🔬 is making waves as a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system! Developed by Shanghai AI Laboratory in partnership with several universities, it employs a robust three-layer evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models, scrutinizing their overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou, recently dropped some serious wisdom in his Stanford CS25 lecture, breaking down the intricate reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** 🧠. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some pretty different takes on AI safety 🤔. Li Feifei is focused on **design, governance, and values**, emphasizing how we build and manage AI. Hinton, on the other hand, is more concerned about superintelligent AI spiraling out of control, suggesting we design **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. The two major buzzwords for potential AI risks are "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence."
