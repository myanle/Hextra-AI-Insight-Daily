### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh "learning mode" to tackle that "AI brain atrophy" issue caused by relying too much on AI. 🧠 This mode isn't about giving you answers on a silver platter; instead, it's like a coding personal trainer, prompting users to pause at key steps and actually get their hands dirty with the tasks. The ultimate goal? To truly nail those programming skills! 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's new open-source lightweight large model. 🚀 Boasting only 270M parameters, it totally shines in instruction following and text structuring tasks, even outperforming some heftier models! Its low power consumption means it can run smoothly on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it super ideal for whipping up speedy, budget-friendly AI applications. 📱✨

3.  **OpenAI CEO Altman** just spilled the beans on OpenAI's grand vision, y'all! 💰 We're talking trillions of dollars poured into data centers and developing bleeding-edge AI hardware, even diving into brain-computer interfaces. The ultimate goal? To totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in a pivotal AI era, echoing the internet bubble days. And GPT-5's release? He says it's signaling "the best of times, and the worst of times." 🌌🤯

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its tenth anniversary and officially kicked off the ASI era – talk about a party! 🎉 They're currently on the hunt for AI industry reporting lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're totally passionate about AI, they'd absolutely love for you to join their crew! 🥳 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a super cool, more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, newly launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with a bunch of universities. It uses a snazzy three-tier assessment protocol—we're talking object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to dissect 3D models across multiple dimensions, checking out everything from overall form and local structure to how real those materials look. Pretty slick, right? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** dropped some serious knowledge at a Stanford CS25 course, explaining the inference mechanisms of Large Language Models (LLMs). 🤯 He pointed out that LLM inference goes down by generating a whole series of intermediate tokens, and he even delved into cool techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some pretty different takes when it comes to AI safety. 🤖 Li Feifei is totally focused on design, governance, and values, whereas Hinton is more concerned about superintelligence potentially going rogue and suggests we design AI that actually *cares* about humanity. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts for potential AI risks.
