### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** issue caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like a programming 'fitness coach' 🧠, guiding users to pause at critical junctures and complete tasks themselves, ensuring they truly master programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large language model, packs a punch despite its modest 270M parameters! It excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 📱

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just unveiled a super ambitious blueprint for the company! Think trillions of dollars poured into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** realm with plans to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release? That's gonna mark "the best of times, and the worst of times." 🤯

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! 🥳 And guess what? They're on the lookout for AI talent, actively recruiting for various positions like AI Industry Reporting Lead, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a super cool, more scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system! 🔬 It was launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities and uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, checking their overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project is a treasure trove! 🛠️ It offers learning paths to build all sorts of technologies from scratch, helping users really get a grip on programming skills through hands-on practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** is an awesome GitHub project that serves up a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan! 🔒 It covers every single angle of cybersecurity, from basic certifications all the way to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  **Awesome-mac** is seriously a Mac user's dream! 💻 This project collects tons of advanced software for Mac, making it a true treasure trove for anyone rocking an Apple laptop. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **Parlant** is a handy LLM agent designed to simplify Large Language Model (LLM) applications. ✨ It's super easy to deploy and use, making your LLM projects a breeze! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **Magic** is a seriously cool open-source AI productivity platform! 🌟 It bundles together AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system, basically everything you need to supercharge your work. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** dropped some serious knowledge in his Stanford CS25 course, explaining the **Large Language Model (LLM)** reasoning mechanism! 🤓 He pointed out that LLM reasoning actually works by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also dove into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some differing views when it comes to AI safety, which is super interesting! 🤔 Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's main worry is superintelligence spiraling out of control, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. Two core concepts for potential AI risks are "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence."
