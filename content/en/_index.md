### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **'Learning Mode,'** and it's here to fight that pesky **'AI brain drain'** from relying too much on artificial intelligence. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; it's more like your personal coding gym buddy, nudging you to stop at key points and actually *do* the work. That way, you genuinely pick up those coding skills. 🧠✨

2. **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is making waves! This open-source, lightweight large model only packs 270M parameters, but it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some bigger models. Plus, its super low power consumption means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for cranking out quick, cheap, and tiny AI apps. 🚀🤖

3. **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just dropped some mind-blowing plans for the company's future. We're talking trillions in investments for new data centers and AI hardware, plus a dive into **brain-computer interfaces** – all to completely shake up search and social media! Altman reckons we're smack-dab in an AI gold rush, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 is gonna kick off 'the best of times, and the worst of times.' Wild, right? 🤯💰

4. **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its 10th anniversary, officially kicking off the AGI/ASI era! 5. **Hi3DEval** is here to level up 3D model evaluation! Developed by Shanghai AI Lab and a bunch of universities, this new system offers a super scientific and comprehensive way to assess 3D generation quality. It uses a three-tier protocol – object-level, component-level, and material theme – to really dig into a 3D model's overall form, tiny details, and how realistic its materials look. Pretty cool, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard) ✨📊

Two big scary concepts to chew on: 'goal misgeneralization' and 'instrumental convergence' are the core potential risks here. 🤔🤖
