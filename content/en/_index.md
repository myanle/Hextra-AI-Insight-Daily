### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** from leaning too hard on AI. 🧠💪 Instead of just spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, prompting you to pause at key steps and actually get your hands dirty, helping you truly master those programming skills.

2.  Guess what? **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, is here! 🚀 This tiny titan, with only 270M parameters, totally crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. ✨

3.  Hold on to your hats! **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing grand vision for the company. 🌌 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and next-gen AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**! The goal? To totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a crucial AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release will mark a "best of times, worst of times" moment. 🤯💰

4.  Big news! **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary, kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they're inviting *you* to join the squad! `[🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)`

5.  Say hello to **Hi3DEval**! 😎 This slick, new 3D generation quality assessment system was cooked up by Shanghai AI Lab alongside several universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to run a multi-dimensional analysis on 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty comprehensive, right? `[🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/)` `[🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)`

11. Mind blown! 🤯 DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou recently spilled the beans on **Large Language Model (LLM)** reasoning mechanisms during a Stanford CS25 course. 12. There's a fascinating split on AI safety between Li Feifei and Hinton. 🤖 Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts highlighting AI's potential risks.
