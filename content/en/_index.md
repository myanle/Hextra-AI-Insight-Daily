1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new "learning mode" 🤔💡 to tackle the "AI brain drain" problem caused by long-term reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at key steps and complete tasks themselves to truly master programming skills.
2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch with just 270M parameters! ✨📱 It excels in instruction following and text structuring tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for building nimble, speedy, and affordable AI applications.
