### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has just dropped a brand new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** problem from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," prompting users to pause at key junctures, get hands-on, and truly master their programming skills. 💪🧠

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source, lightweight large model, packs a punch with just 270M parameters! It totally slays **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or Raspberry Pi, perfect for cooking up quick, cheap, and snappy AI apps. 🚀📱

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman laid out an insane grand vision for the company, talking trillions of dollars for data centers, new AI hardware, and even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**. The goal? To totally revolutionize search and social media! Altman thinks we're in a super critical, "internet bubble-esque" AI era, with GPT-5's launch signaling "it was the best of times, it was the worst of times." 🤯💰
