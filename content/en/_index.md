### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** has just launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 🚀, aiming to tackle the "AI brain atrophy" issue caused by long-term reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks to truly master their coding skills.

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, an open-source lightweight large model from Google, boasts a mere 270M parameters yet excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and cost-effective AI applications. ✨

3. **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has laid out an ambitious vision for OpenAI: pouring trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface (BCI)** domain, with plans to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, the worst of times." 🚀

4. It's **Xinzhiyuan's** 10th-anniversary celebration 🎉, marking the dawn of the ASI era! They're actively recruiting for various AI-related roles, including AI Industry Reporting Leads, Senior Video Editors, Senior Editors/Editors, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their team! ✨ [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info]([Recruitment link here, not provided in original])

5. **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. This system utilizes a three-layer evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme-level—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. 🤩 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** hold differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton worries about uncontrolled superintelligence, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts for potential AI risks. 🧐
