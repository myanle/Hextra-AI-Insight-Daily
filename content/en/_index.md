### Your Daily AI Buzz!

1.  Claude Code has just dropped a brand-spanking-new "Learning Mode"! 🤖🧠 This cool feature aims to tackle the dreaded "AI brain drain" problem that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding you to pause at key steps and get hands-on with tasks. The goal? To truly master those programming skills!

2.  Google's Gemma 3 270M is now open-source, and wow, it's a lightweight champ! 🐯🚀 This compact large language model boasts only 270M parameters but totally rocks at "instruction following" and "text structuring" tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low power consumption means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman, just laid out a seriously ambitious vision for the company! 💰🌌 We're talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware, even a dive into "brain-computer interface" tech, all with plans to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack-dab in a critical AI phase, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times, the worst of times."

Hi3DEval, a brand new 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. 😎 This system is way more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to analyze 3D models across various dimensions, checking out their overall shape, local structure, and material realism. Fei-Fei Li and Hinton actually have different takes on AI safety! 🤖 Fei-Fei Li is all about "design, governance, and values," while Hinton's more worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests designing AI that "cares about humanity." The two core concepts for potential AI risks are "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence."
