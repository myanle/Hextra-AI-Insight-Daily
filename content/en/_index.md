### Today's AI News

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the notorious problem of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by over-reliance on AI. 🤔 Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like a "coding fitness coach," nudging users to pause at critical junctures, get hands-on, and truly master their programming skills. 💡

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is crushing it despite its tiny 270M parameters. 🤯 It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Thanks to its low power consumption, you can run this bad boy on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has painted a wild, ambitious picture for the company's future. 💰 His grand blueprint includes investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** realm with plans to shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in an AI "bubble" era, similar to the internet boom, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, the worst of times." 🌌

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary, marking the dawn of the ASI era! 🎉 They're currently on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they're eager for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a collaboration between Shanghai AI Laboratory and several universities, has launched a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. 🧐 This bad boy uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to analyze the overall form, local structure, and material realism of 3D models from multiple angles. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

The **`build-your-own-x` GitHub project** is a total gem, offering learning paths to build various technologies from scratch. 💪 It's all about helping users get their hands dirty and truly master coding skills through practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  **`90DaysOfCyberSecurity`** offers an intensive 90-day cybersecurity learning plan. 🚀 It covers everything from foundational certifications to advanced tricks, ensuring you're squared away on all fronts. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  **`awesome-mac`** is a treasure trove for Mac users, jam-packed with a collection of advanced software for macOS. 🍎 It's essentially your go-to vault for all things Mac. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **`parlant`** is an LLM agent designed to simplify large language model (LLM) applications. 🤖 It's super easy to deploy and use, making your LLM life a breeze. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **`magic`** is an open-source AI productivity platform that brings together AI agents, a workflow engine, instant messaging (IM), and an online collaborative office system. ✨ It's basically an all-in-one suite for getting stuff done. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** dropped some serious knowledge at the Stanford CS25 course, breaking down the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. 🧠 He pointed out that LLM reasoning goes down through the generation of a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some differing takes on AI safety issues. 🤔 Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts highlighting AI's potential risks.
