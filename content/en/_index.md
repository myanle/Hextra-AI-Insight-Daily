### **Today's AI News**

1.  💡 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the gnarly problem of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like a coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get their hands dirty completing tasks, so they truly master their coding skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch despite its mere 270M parameters. It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, outperforming even some larger models. Thanks to its low power consumption, it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  🔮 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out an ambitious blueprint for the company: think trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** space, aiming to completely shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, with the GPT-5 launch signaling "the best of times, and the worst of times."

4.  🎉 It's a party! **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary, kicking off the ASI era with a bang! They're actively recruiting for several AI-related roles, including AI industry lead writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  ✅ **Hi3DEval**, a new evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, provides a more scientific and comprehensive approach to assessing 3D generation quality. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. 🤔 Fei-Fei Li and Hinton have some differing takes on AI safety. Li Fei-Fei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligent AI running rogue and suggests we design **AI that cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts highlighting potential AI risks.
