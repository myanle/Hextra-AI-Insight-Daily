### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** 🧠 has launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the issue of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by long-term reliance on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like a coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at critical steps, get their hands dirty, and truly master programming skills.

2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M** 🚀 is a lightweight, open-source large language model (LLM) with a mere 270M parameters. This powerhouse excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, outperforming even some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like smartphones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman**, 💰 has unveiled a grand vision for the company: investing trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface (BCI)** domain, with plans to revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, much like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release signifies "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  **XinZhiYuan** 🎉 is celebrating its **10th Anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era**! The company is currently recruiting for various AI-related positions, including Lead AI Industry Reporter, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join their team! [🔗 XinZhiYuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** 📊, a collaboration between Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, is a new, more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** 🔒 provides a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan, covering all aspects of cybersecurity, from fundamental certifications to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

10. **magic** ✨ is an open-source AI productivity platform that integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, instant messaging (IM), and an online collaborative office system. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou**, 💡 lectured on the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** in a Stanford CS25 course. He highlighted that LLM inference is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** 🤔 hold diverging views on AI safety issues. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about the uncontrolled rise of superintelligence, suggesting the design of **AI that cares for humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are identified as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
