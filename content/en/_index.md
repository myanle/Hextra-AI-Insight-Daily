### **Today's AI News**

1. 🤖 **Claude Code** has launched a brand new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** from relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it's like having a coding "fitness coach" that prompts users to pause at crucial junctures and get their hands dirty, ensuring they truly master programming skills.

2. 🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch despite its modest 270M parameters. It absolutely crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Plus, its low-power footprint means it can run smoothly on devices like phones or Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for cranking out nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3. 💰 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing blueprint for the future: trillions of dollars poured into building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware. He's even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** (BCI) realm, aiming to completely flip the script on search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in a crucial AI era, akin to the dot-com boom, with GPT-5's launch signaling "the best of times, the worst of times."

The big scary concepts here? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" – two core ideas highlighting AI's potential pitfalls.
