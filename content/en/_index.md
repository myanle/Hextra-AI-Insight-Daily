### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** has dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the gnarly problem of **"AI brain drain,"** which happens when you rely too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding coach, prompting you to pause at key junctures and actually get your hands dirty with the tasks. The goal? To truly nail those programming skills! 🧠💪

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is making waves! Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it totally crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its super low-power consumption means you can run this gem on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building quick, light, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱

3. **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has unfurled a massive blueprint for the future: talking trillions in investments for data centers and cutting-edge AI hardware, and even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interfaces (BCI)** with plans to shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of an AI "dot-com bubble" moment, and the upcoming GPT-5 release? That's just a sign that it's "the best of times, and the worst of times." Talk about ambitious! 🤯💰

4. **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary, kicking off the ASI era with a bang! They're actively on the hunt for top talent, including an AI industry reporter-in-chief, Senior Video Editors, Senior Editors/Editors, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they're calling your name to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) 🚀✨

5. **Hi3DEval**, a new, super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been rolled out by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. This bad boy uses a three-layer evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material-theme) to give 3D models a multi-dimensional analysis of their overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat for assessing 3D content! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard) ✨

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** recently broke down the inference mechanism of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** in a Stanford CS25 course. He highlighted that LLM inference works by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Super insightful stuff! [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk) 🧠💡

12. **Li Feifei and Geoffrey Hinton** are on different pages when it comes to AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild, advocating for AI that's specifically designed to **care about humanity**. They both highlight two core concepts behind AI's potential risks: 'goal misgeneralization' and 'instrumental convergence.' Food for thought! 🤔✨
