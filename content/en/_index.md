### AI News Today

1. 🧠 **Claude Code** just dropped a brand new **"Learning Mode"**! This awesome feature aims to tackle the **"AI Brain Atrophy"** problem, where folks get too reliant on AI and their skills start to fade. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding you to pause at crucial steps, get your hands dirty, and truly master those programming skills.

2. 🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a total game-changer! Clocking in at just 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its super low power consumption means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building lightweight, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3. 🌌 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing blueprint for the future! He's talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to completely shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI era, kind of like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 release screams "it was the best of times, it was the worst of times."

Key concepts for potential AI risks include "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence."
