### Today's AI News

1.  **Claude Code** has just rolled out its brand-spanking-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🎉 This awesome feature aims to tackle the dreaded **"AI-induced skill atrophy"** that happens when folks rely too much on AI for too long. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding fitness coach 🏋️‍♂️, nudging you to pause at crucial steps, get your hands dirty, and truly master those programming skills. How cool is that? ✨

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model from Google, is making waves! 🌊 With just 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its super low power consumption means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. Pretty neat, huh? 😉

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just dropped some serious bombshells about OpenAI's **grand blueprint**! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building massive data centers and developing cutting-edge AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface (BCI)** realm, aiming to completely shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a pivotal AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release signals "it was the best of times, it was the worst of times." Buckle up! 🚀

5.  Get ready for **Hi3DEval**! ✨ This super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system was cooked up by the Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities. It uses a clever three-tiered protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to dive deep into 3D models, analyzing their overall shape, local structure, and material realism from all angles. Pretty slick, right? Check out the [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and the [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)! 📈
