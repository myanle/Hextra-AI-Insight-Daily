### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that notorious **"AI brain drain"** issue from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, nudging users to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and really nail those programming skills.

2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight, open-source large model, clocking in at just 270M parameters! Yet, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building snappy, cheap, and lightweight AI apps. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** laid out an epic blueprint for OpenAI: we're talking trillions invested in data centers and next-gen AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal, "internet bubble"-esque era for AI, and the GPT-5 launch? He says it marks "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its tenth anniversary and kicked off the ASI era – big news! They're currently on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join the team! 5.  **Hi3DEval**, a new 3D generation quality assessment system, was jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. This bad boy offers a more scientific and comprehensive evaluation, using a three-tier protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to multi-dimensionally analyze 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

10. **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that bundles AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system all into one. It's like a productivity superpower! ✨ [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** dropped some serious knowledge in his Stanford CS25 course, explaining how **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference actually works. He pointed out that LLM reasoning happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and dove into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Pretty mind-blowing stuff! 🧠 [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue and suggests designing **AI that actually cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts driving these potential AI risks. Food for thought! 🤔
