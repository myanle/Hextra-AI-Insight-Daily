### **Today's AI News** 🚀

1.  💡 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**. This mode aims to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** problem caused by relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal programming "fitness coach," prompting you to hit pause at key steps and get hands-on with tasks, so you can truly master those coding skills.

2.  🚀 Google has open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a super lightweight large model with just 270M parameters. **Gemma 3 270M** truly shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models! Its low-power nature means it can run on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, perfect for cooking up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  💰 OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has sketched out a colossal vision for OpenAI. We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing fresh AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** game. The plan? To totally flip search and social media on their heads! Altman reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a pivotal AI era, kind of like the internet bubble. He says GPT-5's release signals that "it's the best of times, and it's the worst of times."

4.  🎉 Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its 10th anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! **Xinzhiyuan** is on the hunt for AI talent, actively recruiting for roles like AI industry lead writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Job Openings](Placeholder: Xinzhiyuan recruitment link not provided in original)

5.  🔬 **Hi3DEval** is a game-changing 3D generation quality evaluation system. Launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in cahoots with multiple universities, **Hi3DEval** employs a savvy three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to dive deep into 3D models. It analyzes their overall form, local structure, and material authenticity from all angles. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. 🤔 Li Feifei and Hinton have some differing opinions when it comes to AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we craft **AI that actually gives a darn about humanity**. 'Goal misgeneralization' and 'instrumental convergence' are the two big concepts to grasp when thinking about potential AI risks.
