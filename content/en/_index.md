2. MIRIX: Giving AI a Real Memory Boost! Say Goodbye to 'Goldfish Brains'! ✨
Researchers from UC San Diego and NYU have open-sourced MIRIX, the world's first genuine multimodal, multi-agent AI memory system. Forget AI's 'goldfish memory'! MIRIX isn't just about simple Q&A anymore; it writes 'memory' directly into the AI's core operating system, empowering agents with **long-term memory**. MIRIX packs six memory modules: Core, Episodic, Semantic, Procedural, Resource, and a Knowledge Vault, all coordinated by a meta-memory manager and its sub-modules. This system has absolutely crushed it in various benchmark tests, for instance, boasting 35% higher accuracy than traditional methods on ScreenshotVQA tasks and slashing storage overhead by a massive 99.9%. And get this: you can now snag the MIRIX desktop app to have your very own AI personal assistant! How cool is that? 😎
[🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/Mirix-AI/MIRIX) [🔗 Official Website](https://mirix.io/)

18. Self-Evolving Agents: The Path to Artificial Superintelligence? 🤔✨
Sure, **Large Language Models (LLMs)** are super powerful right now, but they're kinda like a 'stubborn mule' – unable to adapt to new tasks or constantly changing environments. To fix this, researchers are now zeroing in on 'self-evolving agents' that can **autonomously learn and adapt**. Pretty neat, huh? This review article dives deep into self-evolving agents, systematically reviewing them from three angles: 'what evolves,' 'when to evolve,' and 'how to evolve.' It covers all sorts of evolutionary mechanisms, including the evolution of models, memory, tools, and architecture, plus different adaptation methods for various stages. The paper also dissects evaluation metrics and benchmarks, explores self-evolving agents' applications in coding, education, and healthcare, and tackles challenges like safety, scalability, and co-evolutionary dynamics. Ultimately, the article points out that self-evolving agents are a crucial leap towards **Artificial Superintelligence (ASI)** – and that's gonna be a HUGE direction for future AI research. Buckle up! 🚀

19. 4D Spatial Intelligence Reconstruction: Peeking Behind the World's Dynamics! 🌐👁️‍🗨️
Reconstructing **4D spatial intelligence** – basically, rebuilding dynamic 3D scenes from visual observations – has always been a biggie in computer vision. It's not just crucial for entertainment like movies, but also super vital for applications like **embodied AI**. Wild, right? This review article breaks down existing methods into five awesome levels: starting with reconstructing low-level 3D attributes (think depth, pose, and point clouds), moving on to rebuilding 3D scene components (like objects, humans, and structures), then reconstructing full 4D dynamic scenes, simulating interactions between components, and finally, integrating physical laws and constraints. The article also chats about the key challenges at each level and points out where future research should head. Super helpful stuff for anyone in the field.
[🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/yukangcao/Awesome-4D-Spatial-Intelligence)
