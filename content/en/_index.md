### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 🎓. This cool feature tackles the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** (aka skill atrophy) that comes from relying too heavily on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "gym trainer" 🏋️, guiding users to pause at key steps, get their hands dirty, and truly master programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** 🤯, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, is a real game-changer! Clocking in at just 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis 📱, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just unveiled a super ambitious blueprint for the company 🚀. We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** game 🧠, aiming to totally revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble days, and the release of GPT-5 marks this as "the best of times, and the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is throwing a huge bash for its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI era! 🥳 They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love to have you join the squad! ✨ [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a new, more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system brought to us by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities 🎓. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

It's designed to help users really nail down their programming skills through hands-on practice. Get building! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** dropped some serious knowledge at a Stanford CS25 course 🧠, explaining the inference mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. He pointed out that LLM reasoning happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and explored techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Fascinating stuff! 12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some different takes on AI safety issues. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values** in AI, while Hinton frets about superintelligence running wild and suggests we design **AI that cares about humans** 🤖❤️. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts for potential AI risks.
