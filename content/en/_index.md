### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out its brand-new **"Learning Mode,"** specifically designed to tackle the **"AI brain drain"** issue that comes with long-term AI dependency. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on, and truly master their coding skills. 💪🧠
2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google with a mere 270M parameters, absolutely shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power consumption means you can run it on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 🚀📱
3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has painted a grand vision for the company: investing trillions into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface** realm with plans to disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, akin to the internet bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, the worst of times." 💰🌌💡
4.  **Xinzhiyuan's** (New Intelligence) tenth-anniversary celebration and the dawn of the ASI era have officially kicked off! Xinzhiyuan is currently hiring for several AI-related positions, including Lead AI Industry Reporter, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns.
