### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped its brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠 This bad boy aims to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get their hands dirty, and truly master those coding skills. 💪

2.  Say hello to **Gemma 3 270M**! 🚀 Google just open-sourced this lightweight large language model with only 270M parameters. But don't let the size fool you – it absolutely crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building fast, cost-effective, and nimble AI apps. 💡

3.  Get ready for **OpenAI's** mega-vision! 🌌 CEO Sam Altman just laid out an ambitious blueprint, talking about investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop cutting-edge AI hardware. He's even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** arena, aiming to completely shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI moment, much like the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 signals "the best of times and the worst of times." Talk about high stakes! 💰

It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
