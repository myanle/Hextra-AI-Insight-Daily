### **Today's AI News**

1.  ✨ **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! It's designed to tackle the pesky problem of "AI brain drain"—that skill atrophy from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding fitness coach, guiding you to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and truly master those programming skills. No more AI dependency, just pure coding power-ups! 💪

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is quite the performer! Boasting a mere 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some much larger models. Plus, its low-power footprint means you can run it on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for crafting nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. How cool is that? ✨

3.  🤯 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just dropped some mind-blowing news about the company's grand blueprint: we're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware! They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** realm, aiming to completely disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's release signaling "it was the best of times, it was the worst of times." Wild, right? 💰

4.  🎊 It's Xinzhiyuan's 10th-anniversary bash, kicking off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for top talent, including AI industry chief writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and even editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love to have you join the crew! 🚀 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  📊 **Hi3DEval**, a new 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in collaboration with several universities! This system offers a more scientific and comprehensive approach, using a three-tiered evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material-theme) to conduct multi-dimensional analysis on the overall form, local structure, and material authenticity of 3D models. Pretty slick! ✨ [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

10. ✨ **`magic`** is an open-source AI productivity platform that packs a punch! It integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system all into one. Talk about a productivity superpower! 🚀 [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. 🧠 DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou recently spilled the beans on **Large Language Model (LLM)** reasoning mechanisms during a Stanford CS25 course! He highlighted that LLM inference works by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Super insightful stuff! 💡 [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. 🗣️ Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton are on different pages when it comes to AI safety! Li Fei-Fei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about uncontrolled superintelligence, advocating for the creation of **human-caring AI**. They both touch on "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" as two core concepts for potential AI risks. Deep thoughts! 🤔
