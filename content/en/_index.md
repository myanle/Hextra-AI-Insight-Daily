### Today's AI News

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** from relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding "gym coach," prompting you to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on, and genuinely master your programming skills. ✨

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's open-source lightweight large model, rocking a mere 270M parameters but absolutely crushing it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has sketched out some seriously ambitious plans for the company: talking trillions in investments for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a critical AI moment, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release will mark "the best of times and the worst of times." 🤯

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its tenth anniversary and kicked off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join the crew! 5.  **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, was launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol – object-level, component-level, and material-theme – to perform multi-dimensional analysis on the overall form, local structure, and material authenticity of 3D models. Pretty neat, right? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** presents a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan that covers absolutely everything cybersecurity, from basic certifications to advanced tricks. Time to level up your security game! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

9.  **parlant** is an LLM agent that simplifies large language model (LLM) applications, making it super easy to deploy and use. Sweet! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that integrates AI agents, workflow engines, IM, and online collaborative office systems. Talk about a powerhouse! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist **Denny Zhou**, during his Stanford CS25 course, broke down the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. He pointed out that LLM inference happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Mind blown! [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some differing views on AI safety. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts for potential AI risks. Quite the debate! 🗣️
