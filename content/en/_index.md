### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  Claude Code just dropped a brand-new **"learning mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** from relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like a programming "personal trainer," nudging users to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and actually master coding skills. 💪

2.  Gemma 3 270M, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a real powerhouse! Despite its tiny 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building snappy, low-cost AI apps. 🤯

3.  OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman, is painting a seriously ambitious picture for **OpenAI**: talking trillions in investments for data centers and new AI hardware, even pushing into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the early internet bubble, and the GPT-5 launch marks "the best of times, the worst of times." 🚀

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts behind these potential AI risks. 😬
