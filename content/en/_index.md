### **Today's AI Buzz** ⚡

1.  🧠💡 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the issue of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by long-term AI reliance. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; it acts like a coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks themselves, ensuring they genuinely master programming skills.

2.  🚀✨ **Gemma 3 270M**, an open-source lightweight large model from Google, boasts only 270M parameters yet excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  💰🌌 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out OpenAI's ambitious blueprint, detailing plans to invest trillions of dollars in data centers and new AI hardware development. OpenAI even aims to enter the **brain-computer interface** field, with a vision to revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI phase, akin to the dot-com bubble, stating that the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times and the worst of times."

Xinzhiyuan is actively recruiting for various AI-related positions, including AI industry lead reporter, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts defining potential AI risks.
