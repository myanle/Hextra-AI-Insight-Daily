### **Today's AI News** 🚀

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand new **"Learning Mode"** 🧠💡. This mode aims to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** issue that comes from over-relying on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," prompting users to pause at key steps and get hands-on, ensuring they truly master coding skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** 🚀✨, a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google with only 270M parameters, is a total superstar. It excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has unveiled an ambitious blueprint for the company 💰🌌. This vision includes investing trillions in data centers and next-gen AI hardware, even branching into **brain-computer interfaces**, with plans to disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, reminiscent of the dot-com bubble, and that the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times and the worst of times."

If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** 🔬📊, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in collaboration with several universities. It employs a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. 12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** 🧐🤝 hold differing views on AI safety concerns. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is wary of superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts of potential AI risks.
