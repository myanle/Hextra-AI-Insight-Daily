### **Today's AI News**

1. 🧠💡 Claude Code has rolled out a fresh **"learning mode"** to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** issue that comes from relying too heavily on AI. This isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it's like having a coding "fitness coach" who pauses you at key moments, nudging you to actually do the work and truly master programming skills.

2. 🚀🌟 Gemma 3 270M, Google's open-source lightweight large language model (LLM) with just 270M parameters, is seriously impressive! It shines in **instruction-following** and **text-structuring** tasks, even outperforming some bigger models. Plus, its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building quick, cost-effective AI applications.

3. 💰🌌 OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has laid out a colossal vision for the company: investing trillions in building data centers and developing novel AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**! He plans to shake up search and social media, believing we're currently in a pivotal AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble. With GPT-5's release, Altman sees it as "the best of times, the worst of times."

12. 🤔⚖️ Li Feifei and Geoffrey Hinton have some diverging views on AI safety. Li Feifei is zeroing in on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligent AI spiraling out of control, suggesting we engineer AI that genuinely cares about humanity. The two big concepts highlighting AI's potential risks are "goal misgeneralization" and "tool convergence."
