### **Today's AI News**

1. 💡🧠 **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like a coding "personal trainer," nudging users to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and really master their programming skills.

2. ✨📱 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large language model, is a real powerhouse! Even with just 270M parameters, it totally shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run smoothly on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3. 🤯💸 **OpenAI CEO Altman** just unveiled a super ambitious blueprint for OpenAI: we're talking trillions of dollars poured into building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even jumping into the **brain-computer interface** game with plans to shake up search and social media. He reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch? That's gonna be the moment that shouts, "It was the best of times, it was the worst of times."

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts for potential AI risks.
