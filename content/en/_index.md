### **Today's AI News** 🚀

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a fresh "Learning Mode" designed to tackle "AI brain atrophy" – a common pitfall of over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about handing out answers; instead, it acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at critical junctures, get hands-on, and truly master coding skills.
2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch despite its tiny 270M parameters. It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Thanks to its low power consumption, it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications.

He reckons we're in a pivotal AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 signals "the best of times, the worst of times."
