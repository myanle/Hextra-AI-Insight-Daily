### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code has just dropped a brand-new "Learning Mode" 🧠! It's here to fight off that "AI brain atrophy" many of us get from leaning too heavily on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding fitness coach, nudging you to pause at key points and get your hands dirty with the tasks yourself. The goal? To truly master those coding skills! 💪

2.  Gemma 3 270M, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is seriously impressive! 🚀 Packing just 270M parameters, it totally crushes instruction-following and text-structuring tasks, even outshining some beefier models. Plus, its low-power mojo means it can run smoothly on gadgets like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 📱✨

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a mind-blowing grand vision for OpenAI 🌌! He's talking about investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and cook up new AI hardware, even diving into the brain-computer interface (BCI) game 🧠, all with the goal of shaking up search and social media as we know it. Altman believes we're smack-dab in a pivotal AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the arrival of GPT-5 signals "the best of times, and the worst of times." 🤯
