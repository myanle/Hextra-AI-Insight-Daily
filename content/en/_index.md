### **Today's AI Buzz**

1. 🧠 **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode,"** aiming to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** issue caused by long-term AI reliance. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness private coach," guiding users to pause at key steps and complete tasks themselves to truly master programming skills.

2. 🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, with only 270M parameters, performs exceptionally well on **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low power consumption allows it to run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and low-cost AI applications.

3. 💰 OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman, has outlined the company's ambitious blueprint: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface** field, with plans to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're at a critical AI juncture, much like the dot-com bubble era, and the release of GPT-5 signals, "it's the best of times, it's the worst of times."

5. 🔬 **Hi3DEval**, developed by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system. It uses object-level, component-level, and material theme three-layer evaluation protocols to analyze the overall shape, local structure, and material realism of 3D models from multiple dimensions. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about uncontrolled superintelligence, suggesting the design of **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts for potential AI risks.
