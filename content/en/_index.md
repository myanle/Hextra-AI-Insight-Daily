### **Today's AI News**

1. ✨🚀 **Claude Code** just launched a fresh **"learning mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," prompting users to pause at key steps and get hands-on with tasks. The goal? To truly master those coding skills.

2. 📱⚡ **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's open-source, lightweight large model. Packing only 270M parameters, it totally crushes it on **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some way larger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3. 🤯💰 **OpenAI's** CEO, Sam Altman, just spilled the beans on OpenAI's mind-blowing grand blueprint. He's talking about dropping trillions of dollars into building data centers and rolling out new AI hardware, even diving headfirst into the **brain-computer interface** game to shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the GPT-5 launch? That's just the start of "the best of times and the worst of times."

5. 🎯✨ **Hi3DEval** is a new, more scientific, comprehensive, and rigorous 3D generation quality evaluation system rolled out by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, part-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis on the overall form, local structure, and material authenticity of 3D models. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is worried about uncontrolled superintelligence, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts underlying potential AI risks.
