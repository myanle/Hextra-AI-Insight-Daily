### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out its brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** problem caused by relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about handing out answers; it acts more like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and truly master their programming skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google, boasts only 270M parameters yet shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low power consumption allows it to run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and cost-effective AI applications. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI's CEO Altman** painted a grand vision for the company: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interfaces**, aiming to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're at a critical AI juncture, akin to the dot-com bubble, with the GPT-5 release signaling "the best of times, the worst of times." 💰🌌

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI era! They're currently on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their team! 🎉 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a new, more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system, launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, including overall form, local structure, and material realism. Check it out! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The **`build-your-own-x` GitHub project** offers learning paths to construct various technologies from scratch, helping users master programming skills through hands-on practice. It's a fantastic way to learn by doing! 💪 [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  **`90DaysOfCyberSecurity`** is an awesome GitHub project that provides a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan, covering everything from basic certifications to advanced techniques. Get ready to level up your skills! 🚀 [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  **`awesome-mac`** is a treasure trove for Mac users, gathering a collection of advanced software for macOS. If you're on a Mac, you gotta check this out! 🍎 [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **`parlant`** is an LLM agent designed to simplify large language model (LLM) applications, making it super easy to deploy and use. Pretty neat, huh? 🤖 [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **`magic`** is an open-source AI productivity platform that integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. Talk about a one-stop shop for productivity! ✨ [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** recently explained the **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanism during a Stanford CS25 course. He highlighted that LLM inference works by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Mind-blowing stuff! 🤯 [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** hold different views on AI safety concerns. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton worries about superintelligent AI running amok, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts defining AI's potential risks. 🤖

It completely stumped top-tier large models like GPT-5, resulting in zero scores across the board!
