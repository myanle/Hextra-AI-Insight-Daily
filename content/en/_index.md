1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🤖 This cool feature is all about tackling "AI brain atrophy" – that skill rust that can set in when you rely too much on AI. Instead of just spoon-feeding answers, it's like having a coding personal trainer. It nudges you to pause at key steps, get your hands dirty, and truly nail those programming skills. No more mental laziness, folks! 💪
2.  Google just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M** – and it's a game-changer! 🚀 This lightweight large model, with only 270M parameters, absolutely crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even beating out some beefier models. Plus, its super low power consumption means it can run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi. Talk about building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps! 🐯
