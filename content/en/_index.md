### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** issue caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at critical steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they truly master programming skills. 🧠💡

2.  Google has open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model with only 270M parameters. **Gemma 3 270M** shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, outperforming even some larger models. Its low-power consumption makes it perfect for running on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, ideal for building nimble, fast, and cost-effective AI applications. 🚀📱

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a grand vision for OpenAI: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface** field, with plans to disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we are at a critical AI juncture, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, the worst of times." 💰🌌

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. **Hi3DEval** uses a three-layer evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. ✨ [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

8.  **`awesome-mac`** is a treasure trove for Mac users, compiling various advanced software for macOS. 🍎 [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** demystified the **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanism during his Stanford CS25 course. Denny Zhou explained that LLM inference is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and explored techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. 🤯 [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** hold differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about the uncontrolled emergence of superintelligence, suggesting the design of **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts defining AI's potential risks. 🤔
