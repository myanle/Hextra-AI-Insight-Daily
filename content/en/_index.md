### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped its brand-new **'Learning Mode,'** designed to tackle the infamous 'AI brain atrophy' problem that comes from relying too much on AI. This isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, pausing you at crucial steps and nudging you to actually get your hands dirty and complete tasks. The goal? To truly master those coding skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is seriously impressive! 🚀 Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means you can even run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. Pretty sweet, right? ✨

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a massive vision for the company, talking trillions in investments for data centers and killer new AI hardware. He's even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** game, aiming to totally shake up search and social media. 🤯 Altman reckons we're smack-dab in a critical AI era, kinda like the early internet bubble, with GPT-5's launch signaling 'the best of times, and the worst of times.' Hold onto your hats! 🎢

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** just threw a massive party for its tenth anniversary, kicking off the Age of ASI! 🎉 And hey, they're on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they want YOU to join their crew! ✨ [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a super cool 3D generation quality assessment system, just dropped courtesy of the Shanghai AI Lab teaming up with a bunch of universities. This system is way more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tiered evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to give a multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall shape, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat for pinpointing what's hot and what's not in 3D gen! 🔥 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
