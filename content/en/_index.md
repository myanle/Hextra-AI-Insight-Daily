### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out its fresh new "Learning Mode" 🧠 to combat "AI brain atrophy" — that pesky skill degradation from relying too much on AI. Instead of handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," prompting users to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and truly master programming skills.

2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M** 🚀, a lightweight open-source large model with only 270M parameters, is crushing it in "instruction following" and "text structuring" tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for building speedy, low-cost AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** 🚀 painted a massive picture of the future: trillions of dollars invested in data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into brain-computer interfaces, aiming to shake up search and social media. He believes we're in a pivotal AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns, inviting all AI enthusiasts to join their squad! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** ✨, a new, super-scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system, was launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) for multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
