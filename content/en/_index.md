### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** 🧠 issue caused by long-term AI dependency. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks hands-on 💪, ensuring they truly master coding skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's open-source lightweight large model. With a tiny 270M parameters, it totally shines ✨ in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones 📱 or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Altman has painted an epic roadmap 🚀 for the company: investing trillions of dollars 💰 into data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interface** tech with plans to totally disrupt search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI phase, much like the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 signals "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its tenth anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! 🎉 They're actively recruiting for multiple AI-related roles, including AI Industry Reporter, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** 🔬 is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

He also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. Fei-Fei Li and Hinton hold differing views ⚖️ on AI safety issues. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton frets about superintelligence running wild and suggests designing **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of AI's potential risks.
