### Today's AI News

1.  🌟 **2025 WAIC: AI Giants' Tech Breakthroughs & Ethical Challenges**
    **At the 2025 WAIC, AI powerhouses SenseTime, JD, and Tencent** showcased some truly impressive tech advancements. **SenseTime** rolled out its 'Wuneng' Embodied AI Platform, equipping robots with robust perception and multi-view video generation capabilities. **JD** unveiled its JoyAI large model, spanning retail, logistics, and healthcare, and even open-sourced its JoyAgent intelligent agent collaboration engine. And **Tencent** introduced its Hunyuan 3D World Model 1.0, which rapidly generates high-quality 3D scenes, plus they open-sourced both Hunyuan TurboS and Hunyuan Vision. While these achievements clearly highlight AI's immense potential, they've also ignited vital discussions around the speed of technological development, ethical security, and open-source risks.

2.  🤝 **Tencent's "1+3+N" AI System & Zhihuijun's "Zhuyuan Lingqu OS" Open-Source Plan**
    **Tencent** launched its "1+3+N" panoramic AI system, built on the Hunyuan large model, featuring the Tencent Embodied AI Open Platform Tairos. Separately, **Zhihuijun** open-sourced "Zhuyuan Lingqu OS," an embodied intelligent operating system reference framework, alongside his humanoid robot Lingxi X2. These two distinct approaches—closed-source versus open-source—each have their advantages and disadvantages, and only time will tell which will prevail. ![Tencent AI Panorama](https://pic.chinaz.com/picmap/201811151633427149_5.jpg) [🔗 Zhuyuan Lingqu OS](https://github.com/Genesis-Embodied-AI/Genesis)

6.  🚨 **AI Butler Security Grand Challenge: IS-Bench Test Benchmark**
    **IS-Bench**, a test benchmark developed by researchers at Shanghai AI Laboratory and Beihang University, reveals a significant safety concern: current AI butlers have a safety completion rate of less than 40% for household tasks, indicating a huge potential for hazards. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/AI45Lab/IS-Bench)

This tech enables high-definition and complete scanning of extra-large cultural relics, such as "Kunyu Wanguo Quantu" (A Map of the Myriad Countries of the World), significantly aiding cultural heritage preservation.

8.  📉 **LLM's "Trust Crisis": PRMBench Benchmark Test**
    **LLMs** are facing a "trust crisis," as new research indicates. The **PRMBench** benchmark test specifically highlights that existing Process-level Reward Models (PRMs) are severely inadequate at identifying subtle errors in the reasoning process, with their safety completion rate falling far below human levels.
