### **Today's AI News**

1. ✨ **Claude Code** just dropped its brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! This isn't just another feature; it's designed to combat the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** from over-reliance on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal coding 'fitness coach,' nudging users to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and truly master their programming skills.

2. 🚀 **Gemma 3 270M** just hit the open-source scene! This super lightweight large model from Google, with a tiny 270M parameters, totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3. 💰 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing blueprint for the future! We're talking trillions of dollars for data centers and killer new AI hardware, maybe even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will mark 'the best of times, and the worst of times.'

The two big concepts for potential AI risks are 'goal misgeneralization' and 'instrumental convergence.' Food for thought!
