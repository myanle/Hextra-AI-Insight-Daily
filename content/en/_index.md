### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  🤖 **Claude Code** has dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** designed to combat the notorious **"AI Brain Atrophy"** issue that creeps up from over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it's like having a coding "personal trainer" that nudges users to pause at critical steps, get their hands dirty, and truly master programming skills. 💪

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, rocks a tiny 270M parameters but absolutely crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Its low-power vibe means it can run smoothly on gadgets like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 📱

3.  💰 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out OpenAI's epic blueprint: think trillions invested in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**. The goal? To totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a make-or-break AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the arrival of GPT-5 screams "it's the best of times, it's the worst of times." 🤯

4.  🥳 It's **Xinzhiyuan's** 10th-anniversary bash, kicking off the ASI era! Xinzhiyuan is currently on the hunt for talent across multiple AI-related roles: AI Industry Reporter, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. 5.  😎 **Hi3DEval**, a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been rolled out by the Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with a bunch of universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to dissect 3D models for their overall form, local structure, and material realism from multiple angles. Fei-Fei Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is seriously worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests we cook up **AI that actually gives a hoot about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts flagged as potential AI risks.
