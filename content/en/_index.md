### **Today's AI Buzz**

1. Claude Code just dropped its brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 💡 This cool feature aims to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** (aka skill degradation from relying too much on AI). Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding users to pause at key steps and actually *do* the tasks themselves, so you really nail those programming skills.

2. Google's gone and open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**! ✨ This lightweight large language model packs a punch with only 270M parameters, yet it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Thanks to its low-power nature, it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 📱

3. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing vision for **OpenAI**! 🚀 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers, developing new AI hardware, and even diving into the **brain-computer interface** space, all to shake up search and social media as we know it. Altman believes we're in a crucial AI era, akin to the internet bubble, with the GPT-5 launch signaling "the best of times, and the worst of times."

5. **Hi3DEval** just dropped! 📊 This new, super comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system was cooked up by the Shanghai AI Lab in cahoots with a bunch of universities. It uses a three-tier protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to give a multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. Check it out: [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
