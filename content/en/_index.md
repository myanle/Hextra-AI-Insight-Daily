### **Today's AI News**

1.  🚀🧠 **Claude Code** just rolled out its brand-spanking-new **"Learning Mode"**! This isn't just a fancy feature; it's designed to tackle the infamous **"AI brain drain"** problem that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, it acts like your personal coding "gym coach," nudging users to pause at crucial steps and actually get their hands dirty to truly master programming skills.

2.  ✨📱 **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is making waves! This lightweight, open-source large model packs a punch with just 270M parameters. It really shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Plus, its low-power consumption means you can run it on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  🤯🚀 **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has just spilled the beans on a super ambitious blueprint for the company! We're talking trillions of dollars for data centers and developing brand-new AI hardware, with plans to even dive into **brain-computer interfaces**! The goal? To completely revolutionize search and social media. Altman reckons we're right in the middle of a pivotal AI era, kind of like the internet bubble days, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will truly mark "the best of times, and the worst of times."

They've pinpointed "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" as two core concepts for potential AI risks.
