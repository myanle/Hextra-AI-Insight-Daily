### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just launched a spanking new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that notorious **"AI brain drain"** issue stemming from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "gym trainer," nudging users to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks. The goal? To truly master those coding skills! 🧠💻
2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch despite its mere 270M parameters! It's an absolute rockstar in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some beefier models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱
3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman laid out the company's ambitious blueprint: talking trillions in investments for data centers and new AI hardware, even eyeing a leap into **brain-computer interfaces**. The grand plan? To totally flip search and social media on their heads. Altman believes we're smack dab in a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's launch signaling "the best of times, and the worst of times." 🤯💰
4.  **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its 10th anniversary, kicking off the AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and intern editors. If you're an AI enthusiast, they're extending a warm invite for you to join their crew! 🎉 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)
5.  **Hi3DEval**, a new 3D generation quality evaluation system, was jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. This bad boy offers a more scientific and comprehensive approach, using a three-tier assessment protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to dive deep into a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. Pretty cool, right? 😎 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence spiraling out of control, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts at the heart of AI's potential risks. 🤔🤖
