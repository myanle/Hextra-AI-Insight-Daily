### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! This mode aims to tackle **"AI skill atrophy"** (that's right, brain drain from relying too much on AI 😉). Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like a coding fitness coach, guiding users to pause at key steps and get hands-on with tasks to truly master their programming skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, is a total game-changer! 🤯 Despite having only 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building lean, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a seriously ambitious roadmap! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces (BCIs)**, with plans to totally disrupt search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a critical AI period, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 launch signals that "it was the best of times, it was the worst of times." 💰✨

4.  Synced is celebrating its 10th anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! 🎉 They're currently hiring for multiple AI-related positions, including Lead AI Industry Reporter, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. So, if you're an AI enthusiast, they're calling you to join the team! [🔗 Synced recruitment info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific, comprehensive, and rigorous 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities! 👏 It uses a three-tiered evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to conduct multi-dimensional analysis on the overall form, local structure, and material authenticity of 3D models. [🔗 Hi3DEval project homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project is a goldmine for learning! 🛠️ It offers learning paths to build various technologies from scratch, helping users truly master programming skills through hands-on practice. [🔗 Project repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** offers an awesome 90-day cybersecurity learning plan! 🛡️ It covers all aspects of cybersecurity, from basic certifications to advanced techniques. Time to level up your defense! [🔗 Project repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  **awesome-mac** is an absolute treasure trove for Mac users! 🤩 It collects a variety of advanced software for macOS, making it your go-to repository for all things Mac. [🔗 Project repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **parlant** is a slick LLM agent designed to simplify large language model (LLM) applications! ✨ It's super easy to deploy and use, making your LLM projects a breeze. [🔗 Project repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** is a cool open-source AI productivity platform that does it all! 🚀 It integrates AI agents, workflow engines, instant messaging (IM), and online collaborative office systems. Talk about an all-in-one solution! [🔗 Project repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou dropped some serious knowledge in his Stanford CS25 lecture, breaking down the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**! 🧠 He pointed out that LLM reasoning happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Fascinating stuff! [▶️ Video demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. Fei-Fei Li and Hinton have some differing takes on AI safety issues. 🤔 Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about uncontrolled superintelligence, suggesting we design **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of AI's potential risks. Pretty deep stuff! 🤯

13. **FormulaOne**, a new AI evaluation benchmark, features 220 graph-structured dynamic programming problems that made even top large models like GPT-5 totally "crash and burn," scoring a big fat zero! 🤯 Talk about a reality check!
