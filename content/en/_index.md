### **Today's AI Buzz** 🚀

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🤯 This isn't just another feature; it's designed to combat "AI brain atrophy" – that dreaded skill decay from over-relying on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," prompting you to pause, get hands-on, and truly master programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a total game-changer! 🐯🚀 With just 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for building speedy, low-cost AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing blueprint for the future! 💰🌌 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers, developing new AI hardware, and even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the launch of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, the worst of times."

Developed by Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities, it uses a three-tier assessment protocol (object-level, component-level, and material-theme) to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, covering overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. It turns out, 🤖 **Li Feifei** and **Hinton** have some diverging opinions on AI safety! Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more concerned about superintelligent AI running wild, suggesting we should design **AIs that care about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts for potential AI risks. Food for thought!
