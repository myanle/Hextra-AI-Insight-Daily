### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just launched a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** (aka skill atrophy from over-reliance on AI). This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like a personal coding trainer, prompting users to pause at crucial junctures and get hands-on, ensuring they truly master their coding chops. 🧠
2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, rocks! With only 270M parameters, it totally shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, outperforming even some beefier models. Its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building speedy, cost-effective, and featherlight AI apps. 🚀
3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman laid out a massive vision for the company: think trillions of dollars pumped into data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** with plans to totally disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI moment, much like the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times, the worst of times." 🌌💰
4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're actively recruiting for various AI-related roles, including AI Industry Reporter, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they're inviting you to join their crew! 🎉 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)
5.  **Hi3DEval** is a brand-new, super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol covering object-level, component-level, and material themes to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, checking out their overall shape, local structure, and material realism. ✨ [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

10. **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that's packed with features like AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. Talk about a magic combo! ✨ [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)
11. DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou, totally broke down the **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanism during his Stanford CS25 lecture. He highlighted that LLM reasoning happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also touched on techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. 🤯 [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)
12. **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** actually have different takes on AI safety. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts underlying AI's potential risks. 🤖🤔
