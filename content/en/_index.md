### **Today's AI News**

1.  **PhysRig Framework:** Researchers from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and Stability AI have developed the **PhysRig** framework. This cool framework uses **differentiable physics simulation** to create super realistic character animations! It models characters as a combo of "**rigid skeletons + elastic soft bodies**" and applies the **Material Point Method (MPM)** to mimic real-world physics. This helps characters ditch that old "plastic look" from traditional **Linear Blend Skinning (LBS)** techniques. Pretty neat, right? [🔗 Project Homepage](https://physrig.github.io/) [🔗 Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20936) [🔗 Code Repository](https://github.com/haoz19/PhysRig)

This happened after Google poached most of their employees and signed a licensing agreement within just 96 hours. Talk about a crazy **AI talent war** and big companies' appetite for snapping up **AI startups**! 💸

3.  **REST Framework for AI Model Performance:** Researchers from Shanghai AI Lab, Tsinghua University, and Renmin University of China developed the **REST (Reasoning Evaluation through Simultaneous Testing)** framework to stress-test **AI models**. Their findings? Top models like **DeepSeek-R1** saw a significant drop in accuracy under high pressure! This really shows that current **AI models** still have limits when handling multiple tasks and complex scenarios. Good news is, **long2short** technology can help boost their performance. [🔗 Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10541) [🔗 Project Page](https://opendatalab.github.io/REST) [🔗 Code Repository](https://github.com/opendatalab/REST)

4.  **V2M4 Algorithm:** The research team at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST) has released the **V2M4** algorithm! This awesome tech can convert videos into **4D mesh animations** that are ready for game engines, seriously boosting efficiency and solving the tough problem of generating **continuous animated mesh assets** from videos. Its core is a systematic five-stage process: camera trajectory recovery, mesh appearance optimization, frame-by-frame registration and topological unification, global texture creation, and GLTF animation file generation. Pretty slick!
