### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! This cool feature tackles the dreaded "AI brain atrophy" — that skill degradation from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding fitness coach, guiding you to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on. The goal? To truly master your coding skills. 🚀

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a real powerhouse! 💪 Despite its tiny 270M parameters, it crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building speedy, low-cost AI apps. 📱✨

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing vision for the future! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**, with plans to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI era, kind of like the dot-com bubble. The release of GPT-5? He says it marks that classic Dickensian vibe: "It was the best of times, it was the worst of times." 💫

5.  Shanghai AI Lab, in collaboration with multiple universities, has launched **Hi3DEval**! This is a super scientific and comprehensive system for evaluating 3D generation quality. It uses a three-tiered protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to analyze 3D models from multiple angles, assessing their overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat! 🤩 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
