### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code dropped a brand new "Learning Mode" 🧠, specifically designed to tackle that pesky "AI brain drain" problem caused by relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding you answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get their hands dirty, so they can genuinely master those programming skills.

2.  Gemma 3 270M, Google's open-source lightweight large model 🐯🚀, is making waves! Packing just 270M parameters, it totally crushes it on "instruction following" and "text structuring" tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for building super lightweight, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing blueprint for OpenAI 💰🌌: we're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into brain-computer interface tech! The plan? To totally revolutionize search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release will mark "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its 10th anniversary, marking the dawn of the ASI era! 🎉🥳 They're also actively recruiting for a bunch of AI-related roles, including AI industry lead writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  Hi3DEval 😎 is a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system, jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

It's all about helping users get hands-on and truly master those programming skills through practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

8.  `awesome-mac` 🍎 is a treasure trove for Mac users, compiling a fantastic collection of advanced software for macOS. It's basically a must-have for anyone looking to supercharge their Mac! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  `parlant` 🤖 is an LLM agent designed to simplify large language model (LLM) applications. It's super easy to deploy and use, making LLMs more accessible. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. `magic` 🤔 is an open-source AI productivity platform that brings together AI agents, a workflow engine, instant messaging (IM), and an online collaborative office system. It's a complete package for boosting your productivity! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou just dropped some knowledge in his Stanford CS25 course 🤯, explaining the reasoning mechanisms of large language models (LLMs). He highlighted that LLM inference is achieved by generating a series of "intermediate tokens" and delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Definitely worth a watch! [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. On the hot topic of AI safety, Li Feifei and Hinton have some differing views 🤖. Li Feifei is all about "design, governance, and values," while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild, suggesting we design "AI that cares about humanity." The two core concepts for potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence."
