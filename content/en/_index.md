### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode,"** tackling the tricky issue of **"AI brain atrophy"** that crops up from relying too much on AI. 🧠 Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding users to pause at crucial junctures, get their hands dirty, and truly master programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is quite the performer! 🚀 Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it totally shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outdoing some bigger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing blueprint for the company. 🤯 OpenAI plans to pump trillions into building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** realm, aiming to totally disrupt search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 release marks 'the best of times and the worst of times.'

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editing interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in collaboration with several universities. 🧪 It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to multi-dimensionally analyze 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

Li Feifei and Hinton have some differing views on AI safety issues. 🤔 Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's more worried about superintelligent AI running wild, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humans**. 'Goal misgeneralization' and 'instrumental convergence' are two core concepts for AI's potential risks.
