### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code has dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the gnarly **"AI brain drain"** issue that pops up from relying on AI too much. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers. Instead, it acts like your personal coding "gym coach," prompting users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on, and really nail those programming skills. 🧠💡

2.  Gemma 3 270M, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a total game-changer! Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building fast, light, and cheap AI apps. 🚀📱

3.  OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman, just laid out a seriously ambitious roadmap for OpenAI. We're talking trillions of dollars for data centers, next-gen AI hardware, and even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media! Altman reckons we're in a make-or-break AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and GPT-5's release signals that "it's the best of times, and the worst of times." 🤯💰

4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns – basically, anyone who loves AI is invited to join the squad! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) 🥳 hiring!

5.  Hi3DEval, a spiffy new 3D generation quality assessment system, has been rolled out by the Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with multiple universities. This system is way more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to dive deep into a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and material realism. Check it out! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard) ✨

9.  parlant is an LLM agent that makes large language model (LLM) applications super easy to deploy and use. No fuss, no muss! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant) Get your LLMs rolling! 🤖

10. magic is an open-source AI productivity platform that's got it all: AI agents, a workflow engine, instant messaging (IM), and an online collaborative office system. Talk about a one-stop shop! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic) Poof! Productivity boost! ✨

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou recently broke down the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** in a Stanford CS25 course. He pointed out that LLM reasoning goes down by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Fascinating stuff! [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk) 🧠

12. Fei-Fei Li and Hinton have some differing takes on AI safety. Li Fei-Fei's all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. 'Goal misgeneralization' and 'instrumental convergence' are the two big concepts when it comes to potential AI risks. 🧐

Every single top-tier large model, including GPT-5, totally "crashed and burned," scoring a big fat zero! Yikes!
