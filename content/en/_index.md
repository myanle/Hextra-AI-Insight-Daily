### **Today's AI News 📰**

1.  **Claude Code** 🧑‍💻💡 Claude Code has launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the issue of **"AI dependence syndrome"** resulting from long-term AI reliance. This mode isn't about giving direct answers; instead, it acts like a personal programming coach, guiding users to pause at key steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they truly master programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** 🚀 miniaturized Gemma 3 270M is Google's open-source lightweight large model, with a tiny 270M parameters, yet it excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low power consumption allows it to run on devices like mobile phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** 💰🌌 OpenAI CEO Altman has laid out a grand vision for the company: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even expanding into the **brain-computer interface (BCI)** field, with plans to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI era, reminiscent of the dot-com bubble, with the release of GPT-5 marking "the best of times, the worst of times."

5.  **Hi3DEval** 🔍📊 Hi3DEval, developed by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities, is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system. It employs a three-tier evaluation protocol covering object-level, component-level, and material themes, conducting multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** 🧐🤝 Li Feifei and Hinton hold differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton worries about superintelligence losing control, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
