### **🚀 Today's AI News**

1.  💡 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** problem caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at key steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they truly master their coding skills.

2.  💻 **Google** has open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model with only 270M parameters. It excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for building light, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  🌐 **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has painted an epic vision for OpenAI: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** realm, with plans to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI period, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, and the worst of times."

They are actively recruiting for various AI-related positions, including AI industry journalists, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  🧪 **Hi3DEval** is a new, more scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It utilizes a three-layer assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
