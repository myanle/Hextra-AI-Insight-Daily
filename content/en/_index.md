### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 🧠 to tackle that dreaded "AI brain drain" problem that comes from relying too heavily on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding you to pause at key steps and actually *do* the tasks yourself, helping you truly nail down those programming skills. 😎

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is totally crushing it! 🚀 With just 270M parameters, it outperforms some much bigger models on **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks. Plus, its low-power mojo means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building super light, fast, and wallet-friendly AI apps. 💸

3.  **OpenAI's** CEO Altman just laid out a wild, trillion-dollar roadmap 🌌 for the company: investing in data centers, developing new AI hardware, and even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**. The plan? To completely shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release? He says it marks "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** just threw a huge 10th-anniversary bash, kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 And guess what? They're on the hunt for AI industry lead reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they're seriously inviting you to join their crew! Check out the [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供).

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a super cool 3D generation quality evaluation system, just dropped from Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with a bunch of universities. 😎 This system is way more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to dive deep into the overall shape, local structure, and material realism of 3D models from multiple angles. Wanna learn more? Peep the [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and the [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard).

The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project is a total game-changer! 💪 It dishes out learning paths to build all sorts of tech from scratch, helping users seriously level up their programming skills through hands-on practice. Get building here: [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x).

10. **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that's packed with features! 🤔 It integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system all in one spot. Pretty neat, huh? Grab it here: [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic).

11. DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou, just blew our minds 🤯 at the Stanford CS25 course, breaking down the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. He explained that LLM reasoning happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and then dove into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Want to watch? Hit up the [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk).

12. Fei-Fei Li and Hinton have some differing views on AI safety, which is pretty interesting. 🤖 Fei-Fei Li is focused on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we need to design **AI that cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts at the heart of AI's potential risks. 🤔
