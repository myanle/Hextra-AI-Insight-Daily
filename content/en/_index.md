### **Today's AI Scoop**

1. **Claude Code** 💡 has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! It's designed to combat the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** (aka skill atrophy) that comes from relying too much on AI. This mode won't just spit out answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks, so they can truly master those programming skills. 😎

2. **Gemma 3 270M** ✨, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is quite the performer! With a mere 270M parameters, it absolutely shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi 📱, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications. Whoa!

3. **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman 🚀 has painted a truly grand vision for the company! We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing cutting-edge AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** realm 🤯. The plan? To totally disrupt search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release just screams "the best of times, the worst of times."
