1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"learning mode"** 💡 to tackle that dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** problem from leaning too hard on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach" 💪, prompting users to pause and actually *do* tasks at key steps, so they truly master programming skills.

2.  Google just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M** 🚀, a lightweight large model with only 270M parameters. But don't let its size fool you! This little powerhouse crushes "instruction following" and "text structuring" tasks, even outperforming some bigger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on your phone 📱 or a Raspberry Pi, perfect for building speedy, budget-friendly AI apps.

Li Feifei is all about the **"design, governance, and values"** side of things, while Hinton's worried about super-intelligent AI running amok, suggesting we design AI that **"cares about humanity."** **"Goal misgeneralization"** and **"instrumental convergence"** are flagged as two core concepts for AI's potential risks.
