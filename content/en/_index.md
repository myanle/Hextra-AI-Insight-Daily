2.  **Google** dropped a bomb with **Gemma 3 270M**, a super lightweight open-source large model. Clocking in at just 270M parameters, it's crushing it in **instruction following** and **text structuring**, even outshining some bigger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run this bad boy on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building quick, light, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀
