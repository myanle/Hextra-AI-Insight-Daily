### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI Brain Atrophy"** issue caused by over-relying on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at critical steps, get hands-on, and truly master their programming skills. 🧠💡

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large model, is a total game-changer! 🚀 With only 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power consumption means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building super-fast, low-cost AI apps. 📱✨

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** just laid out a seriously ambitious vision for OpenAI. 🌌 We're talking trillions of dollars poured into building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interface** tech with plans to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 launch marks "the best of times, the worst of times." 💰🤯

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're currently on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join the team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) ✨

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a new, super comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system rolled out by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. 😎 It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to run multi-dimensional analysis on 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. Check it out! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

8.  **awesome-mac** is an absolute treasure trove for Mac users, compiling a fantastic collection of advanced software for macOS. 🍎 Seriously, if you're a Mac user, you need to check this out! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **parlant** is a super cool LLM agent designed to simplify large language model (LLM) applications. 🤖 It's a breeze to deploy and use, making LLM integration smoother than ever. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that's got it all: AI agents, a workflow engine, instant messaging (IM), and an online collaborative office system. 🤔 Talk about an all-in-one solution! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** recently broke down the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** in his Stanford CS25 course. 🤯 He pointed out that LLM reasoning happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Fascinating stuff! [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton** have some differing views when it comes to AI safety. 🤖 Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild, suggesting we need to design **AI that cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts for potential AI risks. Deep thoughts! 🤔
