### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** designed to combat that dreaded **"AI brain drain"** from over-reliance on AI. This isn't about spoon-feeding answers; think of it like your personal coding fitness coach! It guides users to pause at crucial points, encouraging them to get hands-on and truly nail down those programming skills. ✨

2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is a super lightweight open-source large model, clocking in at just 270M parameters! Yet, it crushes it on **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for whipping up nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🤯

3.  **OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman**, laid out the company's ambitious blueprint: talking multi-trillion-dollar investments for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release signaling "the best of times, the worst of times." 🚀

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is throwing its 10th-anniversary bash, kicking off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. So, if you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join the crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) 🎉

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a new, super-scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system rolled out by Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to dissect 3D models across multiple dimensions, checking out overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. Pretty cool, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts for AI's potential risks. Food for thought! 🤔
