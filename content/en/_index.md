### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped its brand-spanking-new **"Learning Mode"** 🤯! This cool feature is all about tackling "AI brain drain"—you know, that skill atrophy from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause at crucial steps and get your hands dirty with tasks. The goal? To truly master those coding skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is here to impress! 🚀 Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run this bad boy on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building super light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing blueprint for the future 🤯. We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers, developing new AI hardware, and even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interfaces**! The plan? To totally shake up search and social media as we know it. Altman reckons we're in a critical AI moment, kinda like the internet bubble era, and the release of GPT-5 is signaling that it's "the best of times, and the worst of times."

4.  It's a party! 🎉 **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and the kick-off of the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! Check out **Hi3DEval**! 😎 This new, super scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system was cooked up by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in collaboration with several universities. 12. When it comes to AI safety, **Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton** have some differing takes 🤖. Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests designing **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts highlighting AI's potential risks.
