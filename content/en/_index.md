### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that pesky **"AI brain drain"** problem from relying too much on AI. Think of it like a personal coding gym coach – it won't just hand you the answers, but guides you to pause, get hands-on, and really nail those programming skills. 💪
2.  Gemma 3 270M, Google's open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch despite its tiny 270M parameters! It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Plus, its low-power footprint means you can run it on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis – perfect for building nimble, quick, and budget-friendly AI apps. ⚡
3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just laid out a massive vision for OpenAI, talking trillions in investments for data centers and new AI hardware, even dipping into **brain-computer interfaces** to shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's launch marking "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯
4.  NewIntelligence is celebrating its 10th anniversary and kicking off the AGI era! Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts of potential AI risks. Quite the debate! ⚖️
