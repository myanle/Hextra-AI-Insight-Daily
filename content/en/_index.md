### **Today's AI News**

1. Claude Code just dropped its brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 💡. This isn't about giving you answers directly; it's designed to combat that pesky "AI brain drain" from over-reliance. Think of it like a coding "fitness coach" that guides you to pause at key steps, get your hands dirty, and truly master those programming skills.

2. **Gemma 3 270M** ✨, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is quite the performer! With only 270M parameters, it excels at **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for crafting speedy, low-cost AI apps.

3. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a truly mind-blowing blueprint 🚀 for the company. We're talking trillions in investments to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interface** tech, with plans to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in an AI "internet bubble" moment, with the GPT-5 launch signaling "the best of times, and the worst of times."

4. Big congrats to Xinzhiyuan 🎉, celebrating its 10th Anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! They're currently on the lookout for AI industry reporting leads, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. 5. **Hi3DEval** 🚀, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system, has been rolled out by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts for potential AI risks.
