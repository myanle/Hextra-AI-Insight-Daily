### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code has dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that pesky "AI brain atrophy" from relying too heavily on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, prompting you to pause at key steps and get your hands dirty, helping you truly master those programming skills. 💪

2.  Google's **Gemma 3 270M** is a game-changer: a lightweight open-source large language model with just 270M parameters! Yet, it crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power design means it can run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, perfect for whipping up nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀

3.  OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman, has laid out an epic blueprint for the company, talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware. They're even eyeing **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 launch will mark "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯

4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're looking for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they're eager for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](No recruitment link provided in original text) 🥳

5.  Meet **Hi3DEval**, a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system cooked up by Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities! It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to dive deep into 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project is a treasure trove, offering learning paths to build various tech from scratch. It's all about hands-on practice to really nail those programming skills! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x) 🛠️

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** offers a killer 90-day cybersecurity learning plan that covers everything from basic certifications to advanced techniques. Get ready to level up your security game! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity) 🛡️

8.  **awesome-mac** is seriously a gem, collecting all sorts of advanced software for Mac. It's a real treasure chest for any Mac user! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac) ✨

9.  **parlant** is a slick LLM agent that simplifies building Large Language Model (LLM) applications. It's super easy to deploy and use, making your life a whole lot easier! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant) 🤖

10. **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that's got it all: AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. Talk about a one-stop shop for getting things done! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic) ✨

11. DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou, pulled back the curtain on **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanisms during a Stanford CS25 course. He highlighted that LLM reasoning happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Mind-blowing stuff! [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. Li Feifei and Hinton are on different pages when it comes to AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. 'Goal misgeneralization' and 'instrumental convergence' are the two big concepts driving these potential AI risks. 🤔

13. **FormulaOne**, a brand-new AI benchmark, is shaking things up with 220 graph-structured dynamic programming problems. It made top large models, including GPT-5, totally "crash and burn," scoring a big fat zero across the board! Ouch!
