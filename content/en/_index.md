### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just launched a brand-new **'Learning Mode'**! 🤯 This genius feature aims to tackle the pesky **'AI brain drain'** that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spoon-feeding answers, it's more like your personal coding 'fitness coach,' prompting you to hit pause at key steps, get hands-on with tasks, and truly master those programming skills. Talk about smart learning! 🚀
2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's lightweight open-source large model, is seriously impressive! 💥 Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it totally aces **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some much larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can happily run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it a go-to for building nimble, fast, and super affordable AI applications. How cool is that? ✨
3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a seriously ambitious blueprint for the company. 🤯 He's talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers, developing new AI hardware, and even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interfaces**! The grand plan? To totally revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're smack-dab in a critical AI era, kinda reminiscent of the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will mark 'the best of times, the worst of times.' Wild! 🚀
