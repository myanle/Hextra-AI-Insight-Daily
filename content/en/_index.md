### Today's AI Buzz

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a new **"Learning Mode"** designed to combat "AI skill atrophy" from over-reliance on AI. This isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like a personal coding coach, guiding users to pause at crucial steps, roll up their sleeves, and complete tasks themselves to truly master coding skills.
2.  **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's new open-source lightweight large model. With only 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some bigger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building super light, fast, and cheap AI apps.
3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** laid out an ambitious blueprint for OpenAI: we're talking trillions of dollars invested in data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces (BCI)**, with plans to totally flip search and social media on their heads. Altman believes we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 launch signals "the best of times, the worst of times."
