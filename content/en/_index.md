### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** 🤖 has dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** – that skill degradation from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," prompting you to pause at key steps and get hands-on, so you actually master those programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** 🚀, Google's open-source lightweight large model with only 270M parameters, is crushing it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models! Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** 💸 has painted a super ambitious picture for OpenAI: pouring trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces (BCIs)**, aiming to totally shake up search and social media. He believes we're in a pivotal AI moment, akin to the dot-com bubble, with the GPT-5 launch signaling "it was the best of times, it was the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** 🥳 is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they want you to join the team! 5.  **Hi3DEval** ✨ is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system rolled out by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  **The build-your-own-x GitHub project** 💪 offers learning paths to build various technologies from scratch, helping users truly master programming skills through hands-on practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** 🚀 provides a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan that covers all aspects of cybersecurity, from basic certifications to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  **awesome-mac** 🍎 is a treasure trove for Mac users, gathering a variety of advanced software for macOS. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **parlant** 🤖 is an LLM agent that simplifies large language model (LLM) applications, making them easy to deploy and use. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** 🧐 is an open-source AI productivity platform that integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** 🤯 gave a talk at Stanford's CS25 course, breaking down the reasoning mechanisms of **large language models (LLMs)**. He explained that LLM inference is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and explored techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton** 🤖 hold differing views on AI safety. Fei-Fei Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton frets about superintelligent AI spiraling out of control, advocating for designing **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
