### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🚀 It's designed to fight against "AI brain drain" from relying too much on AI. Instead of giving you direct answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "gym coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks to truly master those programming skills. 🧠
2.  Google just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**! 🎉 This lightweight large model, with only 270M parameters, totally crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low power consumption means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, perfect for building speedy, low-cost AI apps. 💡📱
3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just painted a wild picture of OpenAI's future! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in data centers and new AI hardware, even jumping into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's launch marking "the best of times and the worst of times." 🔮💰
4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its 10th anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! 🥳 They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join the team! Check out the [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智yuan官网招聘链接，原文未提供). ✨
5.  **Hi3DEval** is a super cool new 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. 🚀 It's way more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tier assessment protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions like overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. Check out the [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and the [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)! 🌟
