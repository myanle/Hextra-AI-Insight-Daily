### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠 This mode is designed to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** problem that comes from over-relying on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts more like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding you to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks. The goal? To truly help you master those programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a real powerhouse! 🚀 With just 270M parameters, it excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption means you can run it on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 🐯

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a super ambitious blueprint for the company! 🌌 We're talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware, even a leap into **brain-computer interfaces** with plans to shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release? That's just the beginning, marking "the best of times and the worst of times." 💰

4.  Huge news! **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! 🎉🥳 They're also on the hunt for AI talent, with openings for AI industry chief writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  Check out **Hi3DEval**! 😎 This new, more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system was launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project is a game-changer! 💪 It provides learning paths to build various technologies from scratch, helping users master programming skills through hands-on practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  Wanna dive into cybersecurity? The **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** project offers an awesome 90-day learning plan! 🚀 It covers all things cybersecurity, from basic certifications to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  For all you Mac users out there, **awesome-mac** is your new go-to! 🍎 This treasure trove collects a ton of advanced software for macOS, making it an absolute gem. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  Meet **parlant**! 🤖 It's an LLM agent designed to simplify large language model (LLM) applications, making them super easy to deploy and use. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. Curious about **magic**? 🤔 It's an open-source AI productivity platform that integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou, recently broke down the **Large Language Model (LLM)** reasoning mechanism in a Stanford CS25 course! 🤯 He pointed out that LLM inference works by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. 12. When it comes to AI safety, **Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton** have some different takes! 🤖 Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence spiraling out of control, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts for potential AI risks.
