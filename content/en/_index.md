### **Today's AI Buzz**

1. Claude Code just dropped a brand new **"Learning Mode"** 💡, designed to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** problem caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about giving direct answers; instead, it acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks to truly master their programming skills. 💪

2. Google just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M** 🚀, a super lightweight large model with only 270M parameters. But don't let its size fool you! It punches above its weight, performing exceptionally well on **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it awesome for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 📱

3. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just spilled the tea on OpenAI's ambitious roadmap 🤯. We're talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** game! The goal? To totally disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release will mark "the best of times and the worst of times." What a wild ride! 🎢
