### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  Claude Code just dropped a brand-new 'Learning Mode' 🧠! This cool feature tackles the dreaded 'AI brain atrophy' by guiding users through critical steps, like a coding gym buddy, instead of just handing out answers. The goal? To truly help folks master their programming skills by getting hands-on.

2.  Google just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, and this lightweight large model is seriously impressive 🚀. Packing just 270M parameters, it crushes tasks like instruction following and text structuring, even outperforming some much bigger models. Plus, its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building speedy, affordable AI apps.

3.  OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman, just laid out some wild plans for the company's future 🌌💰. We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers, developing new AI hardware, and even diving into brain-computer interfaces to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a make-or-break AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release signaling "the best of times and the worst of times."

Catch the demo: [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. When it comes to AI safety, Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton have some differing views 🤔. Li champions **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more concerned about superintelligent AI running rogue and suggests we design AI that 'cares about humans'. Two big concepts in AI's potential risks are 'goal misgeneralization' and 'instrumental convergence'.
