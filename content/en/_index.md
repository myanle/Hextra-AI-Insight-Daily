### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped its brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🤖🧠 This cool feature is all about tackling "AI brain drain"—you know, that skill atrophy from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause at crucial steps and actually get your hands dirty with tasks, so you truly master those programming skills. 💪

2.  Say hello to **Gemma 3 270M**! 🐯🚀 This bad boy is Google's open-source, super lightweight large model, packing a punch with only 270M parameters. But don't let the size fool you—it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI's** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a seriously ambitious roadmap! 💰🌌 We're talking trillions of dollars poured into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interfaces**. The grand plan? To totally shake up search and social media as we know it. Altman reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble days, and the launch of GPT-5 will mark "the best of times and the worst of times." Talk about high stakes!

Get ready for **Hi3DEval**! 😎 This isn't just any evaluation system; it's a super scientific and comprehensive framework for 3D generation quality, cooked up by the Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to dive deep into 3D models, analyzing their overall form, local structure, and material authenticity from multiple angles. Check out the [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and the [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)!
