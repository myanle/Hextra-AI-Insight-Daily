2.  Talk about a tiny titan! **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is an open-source, lightweight large model with a mere 270M parameters. But don't let its size fool you—it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Its low power consumption means it can rock out on your phone or Raspberry Pi, perfect for cooking up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱

Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a pivotal AI era, echoing the internet bubble, and the arrival of GPT-5? He says it heralds "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯💰

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts highlighting AI's potential risks. 🤔🚨
