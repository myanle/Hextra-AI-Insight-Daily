### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** 🧠 has launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the issue of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode won't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," prompting users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks hands-on to genuinely master coding skills. 💪

2. **Gemma 3 270M** ✨, an open-source lightweight large model from Google, boasts only 270M parameters. Yet, it shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models! Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 📱

3. **OpenAI CEO Altman** 🚀 has unveiled a grand blueprint for **OpenAI**: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface** field, with plans to revolutionize search and social media. He believes we're in a pivotal AI era, akin to the internet bubble, and the release of GPT-5 signifies "It was the best of times, it was the worst of times." 🤯

4. **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! 🎉 They're currently on the lookout for AI industry chief writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their team! 🤝 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5. **Hi3DEval** ✨ is a cutting-edge, more scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. Jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, it employs a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis on the overall form, local structure, and material authenticity of 3D models. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

10. **magic** 🪄 is an open-source AI productivity platform that packs a punch by integrating AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and online collaborative office systems. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** 🎓, in his Stanford CS25 course, dove deep into the inference mechanism of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. He explained that LLM inference works by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also explored techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** 🧐 hold differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei emphasizes **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about uncontrollable superintelligence and proposes designing AI that genuinely **cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts for understanding AI's potential risks.
