### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  Claude Code has launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** designed to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** issue caused by long-term reliance on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like a coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks themselves, ensuring they truly master programming skills. 💪🧠

2.  Gemma 3 270M, Google's open-sourced lightweight large model, weighs in at only 270M parameters but crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 🚀📱

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has painted an epic blueprint for the company: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** realm, with plans to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI era, much like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming release of GPT-5 signals "the best of times, and the worst of times." 🤯💰

4.  XinZhiYuan (New Intelligence) is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the AGI era! The company is currently hiring for various positions, including AI industry lead writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join! [🔗 XinZhiYuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) 🎉🥳

5.  Hi3DEval is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It employs a three-tiered evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard) 😎

12. Fei-Fei Li and Hinton hold differing views on AI safety. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton worries about uncontrolled superintelligence and suggests designing **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks. 🤖🤔
