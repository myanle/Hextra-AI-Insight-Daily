### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"**! ✨ This isn't your typical "give me the answer" AI; it's designed to fight against "AI brain atrophy" caused by over-reliance on AI. Think of it more like your personal coding gym coach, guiding you to pause at crucial steps and tackle tasks yourself, so you truly master those programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, is seriously impressive! 🚀 Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its super low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing grand blueprint for the company! 🤯 He's talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release will mark "the best of times, and the worst of times."

Xinzhiyuan is kicking off its 10th-anniversary celebration, marking the dawn of the ASI era! 🥳 They're also on the hunt for AI talent, actively recruiting for roles like AI Industry Lead Reporter, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is here to revolutionize 3D generation! 🌟 The Shanghai AI Lab, in collaboration with several universities, has launched this super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to provide multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. Check it out: [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts for potential AI risks.
