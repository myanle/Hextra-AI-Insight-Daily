### **Today's AI News**

1. 🧠 **Claude Code** has just rolled out a brand-new **"learning mode”** to tackle that pesky **"AI brain atrophy”** problem from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts more like your personal coding "fitness coach," pushing you to pause at critical steps and get your hands dirty with tasks, so you actually *master* those programming skills. Pretty cool, right?

2. 🚀📱 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large model, is a total game-changer! With only 270M parameters, it seriously punches above its weight, crushing **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks – it even outshines some much larger models. Plus, its low power consumption means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up super light, fast, and wallet-friendly AI apps.

3. 💸🧠 Sam Altman, **OpenAI**'s CEO, just dropped an epic blueprint for the company! We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building out data centers and developing brand-new AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** space, with plans to totally shake up search and social media as we know it. Altman thinks we're in a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will mark "the best of times, the worst of times." Wild!

4. 🥳🤝 It's **Xinzhiyuan**'s tenth-anniversary bash, and get this: it's also marking the dawn of the ASI era! They're currently on the hunt for fresh talent, with openings for an AI Industry Reporter, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're super passionate about AI, they'd totally love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Job Postings](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5. 📊 The Shanghai AI Lab, teaming up with several universities, just dropped **Hi3DEval** – a super scientific and comprehensive system for evaluating 3D generation quality. This bad boy uses a three-tier protocol (object-level, component-level, and material-theme) to deep-dive into a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and how real its materials look. Pretty slick! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. 🎓💡 DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou, recently broke down the inference mechanism of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** in his Stanford CS25 course. He explained that LLM reasoning actually happens by whipping up a bunch of **intermediate tokens** and also dove into cool techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Super insightful! [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. ⚖️ So, **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** actually have different takes on AI safety. Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more concerned about superintelligence running wild, suggesting we should totally design **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. Keep an eye out for "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" – these are two big, core concepts for potential AI risks.
