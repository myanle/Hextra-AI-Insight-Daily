### Today's AI News

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh new "Learning Mode" 🤖🧠, specifically designed to tackle the dreaded "AI brain drain" that comes from leaning too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks. The goal? To truly master those coding skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model 🐯🚀, packs a punch despite its tiny 270M parameters. It shines in instruction following and text structuring tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for cranking out nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman laid out some seriously ambitious plans 💰🌌 for OpenAI: talking trillions in investments for data centers and new AI hardware, even dabbling in brain-computer interfaces, all aiming to shake up search and social media. He reckons we're smack-dab in a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble days, and the GPT-5 launch will herald "the best of times, the worst of times."

12. When it comes to AI safety, **Li Feifei and Hinton** are on different pages 🤖. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests we design **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts highlighting AI's potential risks.
