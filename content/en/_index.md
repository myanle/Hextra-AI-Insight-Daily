### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just launched its brand-new **"learning mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** problem caused by over-reliance on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like a programming "fitness private coach," guiding users to pause at critical steps, get hands-on with tasks, and genuinely master coding skills. 🧠💡

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's latest open-source, lightweight large model. Packing only 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power footprint means it can run on your phone or Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building lightweight, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman laid out OpenAI's grand vision: dropping trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**. He's planning to disrupt search and social media, believing we're smack dab in a pivotal AI moment, much like the dot-com bubble. He sees GPT-5's launch as "the best of times, and the worst of times." 💰🌌🤯

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're looking for AI industry reporting leads, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. Calling all AI enthusiasts to join the crew! 🎉🥳 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—for multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. 😎📊 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about uncontrollable superintelligence, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are pinpointed as two core concepts of potential AI risks. 🤖💭
