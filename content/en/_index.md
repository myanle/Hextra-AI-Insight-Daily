### Today's AI News 📰

1.  🧠💡 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the infamous **"AI brain atrophy"** issue that comes from relying too much on AI. This mode won't just hand you answers; instead, it acts like a programming "personal trainer," nudging users to pause at key steps and actually get their hands dirty with tasks, helping them truly nail down their coding skills.

2.  ✨📱 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is seriously impressive! With a tiny 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Thanks to its low-power mojo, it can run on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up lightweight, super-fast, and cheap-as-chips AI apps.

3.  💰🚀 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has painted a mind-blowing grand vision for the company! We're talking trillions of dollars poured into data centers and developing cutting-edge AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interface** tech, all with the goal of totally shaking up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack-dab in a crucial AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and that the GPT-5 launch will signal "the best of times, and the worst of times." Wild, right?
