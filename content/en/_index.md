2.  ✨ **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's latest open-source gem: a seriously lightweight large model rocking just 270M parameters! But don't let its size fool you; this little beast crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.
