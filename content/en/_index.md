### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has launched a brand-new **'Learning Mode'** designed to tackle the **'AI brain atrophy'** problem caused by long-term AI dependence. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like a programming 'fitness coach,' guiding users to pause at key steps and get hands-on with tasks, ensuring they truly master their coding skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google, packs a punch with just 270M parameters. It excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, quick, and budget-friendly AI applications. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled OpenAI's super ambitious blueprint: investing trillions into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface** realm, with grand plans to disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in a critical AI phase, reminiscent of the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, and the worst of times." 💰

4.  It's **Xinzhiyuan's** tenth-anniversary bash, kicking off the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! 🎉 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval**, developed by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities, introduces a more scientific and comprehensive evaluation system for 3D generation quality. It uses a three-tiered assessment protocol focusing on object-level, component-level, and material themes, offering multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. Pretty cool! ✨ [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. DeepMind's Chief Scientist Denny Zhou, in a Stanford CS25 course, explained the inference mechanisms of Large Language Models (LLMs). He highlighted that LLM reasoning is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and explored techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Super insightful! 🤯 [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton hold differing views on AI safety. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton worries about uncontrolled superintelligence and suggests designing 'human-caring AI.' **'Goal misgeneralization'** and **'instrumental convergence'** are two core concepts for potential AI risks. Food for thought! 🤔
