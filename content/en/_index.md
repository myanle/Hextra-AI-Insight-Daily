### **Today's AI News**

1.  💡 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the issue of **"AI-induced skill decay"** stemming from over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "gym coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they genuinely master coding skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's newly open-sourced lightweight large language model, packs a punch with just 270M parameters. It excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like smartphones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building fast, cost-effective, and lightweight AI applications.

3.  💰🌌 **Sam Altman**, OpenAI's CEO, has laid out an ambitious roadmap for the company. His vision includes investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interface** technology with plans to revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI phase reminiscent of the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming release of GPT-5 will mark "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  🎉 **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! They're also actively hiring AI-related talent, including AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  🔍 **Hi3DEval**, developed by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts related to AI's potential risks.
