### **Today's AI News**

1.  💡 Claude Code has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** issue stemming from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at key steps and get hands-on with tasks, truly mastering their programming skills.

2.  🚀 Google has open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model with a mere 270M parameters. Yet, it shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for building nimble, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  💰 OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has laid out OpenAI's ambitious vision: pouring trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interface** tech, with plans to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're at a pivotal AI moment, akin to the internet bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times and the worst of times."

5.  💪 Hi3DEval, developed by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities, is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. It employs a three-layer assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—for multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts behind potential AI risks.
