### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the gnarly problem of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by long-term AI reliance. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at critical steps and actually get their hands dirty with tasks, truly mastering coding skills. 💪🧠
2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch despite its tiny 270M parameters. It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱
3.  **OpenAI** CEO Altman laid out an epic blueprint for OpenAI: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop next-gen AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** game, aiming to flip search and social media on their heads. He reckons we're smack dab in an AI critical period, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's release signaling "the best of times, and the worst of times." 🤯💰
4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its 10th anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're currently on the hunt for talent, including AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! 🎉 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)
5.  **Hi3DEval** is a snazzy, more scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system rolled out by the Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to dive deep into 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. Pretty cool, right? ✨ [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

Li Feifei's focus is on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton frets about superintelligence running wild and suggests designing **AI that cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts highlighting AI's potential risks. 🤔🤖
