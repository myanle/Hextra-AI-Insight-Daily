### **Today's AI News**

1.  💡 **Claude Code** just launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** issue caused by relying too much on AI. This mode won't just spoon-feed you answers; instead, it acts like your personal programming "fitness coach," pushing you to pause at key steps, get hands-on with tasks, and truly master coding skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a real powerhouse! Despite having only 270M parameters, it crushes instruction following and text structuring tasks, even outshining some bigger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building fast, cheap, and tiny AI apps.

3.  💸 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman laid out OpenAI's epic blueprint: we're talking trillions in investments for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** realm, all with plans to totally flip search and social media on their heads. He reckons we're in a make-or-break AI moment, much like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 release? That's the signal for "the best of times, and the worst of times."

4.  🥳 **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and ushering in the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI talent, including lead AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors, and editorial interns. If you're a true AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  ✨ **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and thorough 3D generation quality evaluation system, just dropped, developed by the Shanghai AI Lab in cahoots with multiple universities. It uses a slick three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to seriously dig into 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity from all angles. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

10. 🚀 **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that's got it all: AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system, all rolled into one neat package. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. 🧠 DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou, during his Stanford CS25 course, totally unpacked the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. He highlighted that LLM reasoning happens by churning out a series of **intermediate tokens** and delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. 🤝 Fei-Fei Li and Hinton have some differing takes on AI safety. Fei-Fei Li is focused on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's pretty worried about superintelligence going rogue and suggests we build AI that actually **cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts when we talk about potential AI risks.
