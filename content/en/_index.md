### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  🤔💪 **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"**! It's designed to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** that comes from over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on, and truly master those coding skills.

2.  🌟📱 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch with just 270M parameters. It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Thanks to its low power consumption, you can run it on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  🔮🤯 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has painted a mind-blowing roadmap for OpenAI: we're talking trillions of dollars invested in data centers and new AI hardware development. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** space, aiming to revolutionize search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 launch signals "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  🥳👋 **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're currently on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their team! 5.  ✨📊 **Hi3DEval**, a collaboration between Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, introduces a more scientific and comprehensive evaluation system for 3D generation quality. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. 12. 🤔🚨 Fei-Fei Li and Hinton have differing views on AI safety. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about superintelligence spiraling out of control and suggests designing **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
