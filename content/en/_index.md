### **Today's AI News**

1. 🤖 **Claude Code** just dropped a **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that infamous **"AI brain drain"** problem from over-reliance on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, it acts like your personal coding gym coach, guiding you to pause and get hands-on at crucial steps. This way, you actually master those programming skills! 💪

2. 🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, is a total beast! With only 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, it's super low-power, so you can run this bad boy on your phone or a Raspberry Pi for quick, cheap AI apps. Talk about efficiency! 📱💨

3. 💰 **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** is out here painting a wild future! His grand vision for OpenAI involves dropping trillions of dollars on data centers and next-gen AI hardware. He's even eyeing **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a make-or-break AI moment, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and with GPT-5 on the horizon, he's quoting Dickens: "It was the best of times, it was the worst of times." Buckle up! 🤯

It uses a three-tier assessment protocol – object-level, component-level, and material-theme – to deep-dive into the overall form, local structure, and material realism of 3D models. Pretty neat stuff! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. 🤖 **Li Feifei and Hinton** are on different pages when it comes to AI safety. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is seriously worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests we design **AI that genuinely cares about humans**. They both zero in on "misaligned goals" and "instrumental convergence" as the core potential AI risks. Deep thoughts!
