### **What's Hot in AI Today**

1. **Claude Code** 🚀🧠 Just dropped its brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! This cool feature is here to fight against **"AI brain drain"**—you know, that skill atrophy from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing you answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding you to pause at key steps and actually *do* the work. The goal? To truly master those programming skills!

2. **Gemma 3 270M** 🐯🚀 Google just open-sourced this lightweight large model! Weighing in at only 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building super light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. Sweet!

3. **OpenAI** 💰🌌 CEO Sam Altman just laid out a seriously ambitious roadmap for OpenAI! We're talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**. The goal? To totally shake up search and social media as we know it. Altman reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com boom, and the GPT-5 release is basically saying, "It was the best of times, it was the worst of times." Wild, right?

4. **Xinzhiyuan** 🥳🎊 Is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. So, if you're an AI enthusiast, they're seriously inviting you to join the crew! It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to dive deep into 3D models' overall shape, local structure, and material realism with multi-dimensional analysis. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** 🤯 gave a killer lecture at Stanford's CS25 course, breaking down the inference mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. He pointed out that LLM reasoning happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and then delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Fascinating stuff! [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** 🤖 These two AI giants have got different takes on AI safety! Fei-Fei Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. The big scary concepts here are "goal misgeneralization" and "tool convergence"—the two core ideas behind potential AI risks. Deep thoughts! 🤔
