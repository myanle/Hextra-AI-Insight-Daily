### Today's AI News 🚀

1.  **Claude Code** has unveiled a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the **"AI brain drain"** issue stemming from over-reliance on AI. This mode won't just hand you answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they truly master coding skills.

2.  **Google's** open-source **Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight large model. It packs a punch with only 270M parameters, excelling in instruction-following and text structuring tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a grand vision for OpenAI. His ambitious blueprint includes investing trillions of dollars to build data centers, develop new AI hardware, and even dive into **brain-computer interfaces**, aiming to disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, with the release of GPT-5 marking "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Job Openings Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific, comprehensive, and rigorous 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. This system uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The **`build-your-own-x` GitHub project** offers learning paths to build various technologies from scratch. It's a fantastic way for users to master programming skills through hands-on practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  **`90DaysOfCyberSecurity`** provides a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan that covers all aspects of cybersecurity, from basic certifications to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  **`awesome-mac`** is a treasure trove for Mac users, compiling a collection of various advanced software for macOS. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **`parlant`** is an LLM agent designed to simplify large language model (LLM) applications. It's super easy to deploy and use. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **`magic`** is an open-source AI productivity platform. It integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system all in one neat package. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** recently explained the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** in a Stanford CS25 course. He highlighted that LLM inference is achieved by generating a series of **"intermediate tokens"** and also discussed techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** hold differing views on AI safety issues. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton worries about uncontrolled superintelligence and suggests designing **"AI that cares about humans."** "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts for potential AI risks.
