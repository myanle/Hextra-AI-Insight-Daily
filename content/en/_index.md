### **Today's AI Scoop** 📰

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 🧠💪! It's designed to tackle the infamous **"AI brain drain"** problem that crops up from relying too much on AI. This mode won't just hand you the answers; instead, it's like having a coding personal trainer, guiding users to pause at key steps and get hands-on, truly mastering those programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model 🚀📱, is a total game-changer! With only 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing vision for the future 🧠✨! We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers, cooking up new AI hardware, and even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interface (BCI)** tech, all aimed at shaking up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 release totally screams, "It was the best of times, it was the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary 🎂🚀, kicking off the AGI era! And guess what? They're on the hunt for talent! Xinzhiyuan is actively recruiting for a bunch of roles, including AI Industry Reporter Lead, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Job Openings](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a game-changing 3D generation quality evaluation system ✨📏! Launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities, it’s way more scientific, comprehensive, and practical. This system rocks a three-tiered evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to run multi-dimensional analyses on 3D models’ overall form, local structures, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. **DeepMind's Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** just dropped some serious knowledge 🧠🤯 in his Stanford CS25 lecture! He broke down the inference mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, revealing that LLM reasoning happens by churning out a sequence of **intermediate tokens**. Plus, he dove into cool techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts for potential AI risks.
