### **Today's AI News**

1.  Okay, so **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🤯 It's designed to combat that pesky "AI brain atrophy" we all risk from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing you answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, pushing you to pause at crucial steps and tackle tasks hands-on. The goal? To truly nail those programming skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, is a total game-changer! 🚀 With a tiny 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or Raspberry Pi, perfect for whipping up light, fast, and cheap AI apps. How cool is that? ✨

3.  Hold up, **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing grand vision! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars poured into data centers and next-gen AI hardware, even a leap into **brain-computer interfaces** with plans to totally disrupt search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack-dab in a crucial AI era, kinda like the internet bubble. He sees GPT-5's release as shouting, "It's the best of times, it's the worst of times." 📈📉
