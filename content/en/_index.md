### Today's AI News

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh new **"Learning Mode"**! 🤔 It's designed to combat that pesky **"AI brain drain"** we get from relying too much on AI. This mode won't just hand you the answers; instead, it's like having a coding "personal trainer" that guides you to pause at key steps and actually *do* the tasks yourself, so you can truly master those coding skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a real gem! 💎 With only 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building fast, low-cost AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI's** CEO, Sam Altman, just spilled the beans on a super ambitious roadmap! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** space, all with plans to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release marks "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  It's a huge milestone! 🚀 **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI era! They're also on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join the crew! 5.  Check out **Hi3DEval**! 🤩 This is a new, more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and material realism. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of AI's potential risks.
