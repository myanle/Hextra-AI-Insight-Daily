### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"**! This cool feature tackles the gnarly problem of "AI brain drain" from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," pushing you to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and really *own* those programming skills. 🧠💡

2.  Get ready for **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source, lightweight large language model! Clocking in at just 270M parameters, this bad boy shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building speedy, low-cost AI apps. 🚀✨

3.  Hold onto your hats! **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a massive vision for the company: think trillions of dollars invested in data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**. The plan? To totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will mark a "best of times, worst of times" moment. 🤯🌌

4.  Big news! **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary, kicking off the ASI era with a bang! They're on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and intern editors. If you're an AI enthusiast, they're inviting you to join the team! 5.  Check out **Hi3DEval**! This is a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, covering overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard) 📊✨

12. When it comes to AI safety, Fei-Fei Li and Hinton have some differing views. Fei-Fei Li is focused on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more concerned about uncontrolled superintelligence, suggesting we design **AIs that care about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts highlighted as potential AI risks. 🤖💭
