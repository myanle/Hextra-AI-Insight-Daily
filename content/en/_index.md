### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** problem from over-reliance on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like a coding "gym personal trainer," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and get their hands dirty, ensuring they truly master programming skills. No more brain drain! 🧠💪
2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch despite its mere 270M parameters. It absolutely shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power footprint means you can run it on your phone or Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🐯🚀
3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing master plan: think trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** space, aiming to completely shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a crucial AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 signals "the best of times, the worst of times." 💰🌌 Talk about ambition! ✨
4.  Xinzhiyuan just kicked off its 10th-anniversary celebration, heralding the dawn of the ASI era! They're actively recruiting for various AI-related roles, including AI Industry Lead Writer, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! ✨ [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)
5.  **Hi3DEval**, a new, more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, was jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. This bad boy uses a three-tier assessment protocol – object-level, component-level, and material theme – to multidimensionally analyze the overall form, local structure, and material authenticity of 3D models. Pretty slick, right? ✨ [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
