### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"**! This cool feature tackles the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** from relying too much on AI. Instead of handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, prompting you to pause at crucial steps and get your hands dirty, helping you truly master those coding skills. 🧠💻

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, is a real game-changer! Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, outperforming even some much larger models. Its low power consumption means it can rock on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** just unveiled a mind-blowing roadmap for OpenAI! We're talking trillions of dollars poured into building data centers, developing cutting-edge AI hardware, and even diving into **brain-computer interface (BCI)** tech, all aimed at disrupting search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a pivotal AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's launch signaling "the best of times, and the worst of times." 🤯💰

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is kicking off its 10th-anniversary bash and the dawn of the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join the crew! 5.  **Hi3DEval** is a snazzy, more scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system cooked up by Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to dive deep into 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism from multiple angles. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard) ✨

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** are on different pages when it comes to AI safety. Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is seriously worried about uncontrolled superintelligence, pushing for the creation of **AI that cares about humanity**. Two core concepts for AI's potential risks are "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence." It's a real head-scratcher! 😬
