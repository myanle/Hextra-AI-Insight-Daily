### **Today's AI News 🚀**

1. 💡 **Claude Code** has launched a brand-new **'Learning Mode'** to tackle the issue of **'AI brain atrophy'** resulting from prolonged reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming 'personal trainer,' guiding users to pause at critical steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they truly master programming skills.

2. 🌟 **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google, boasts a mere 270M parameters yet excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption allows it to run on devices like mobile phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3. 🤯 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has painted a grand vision for OpenAI: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface** field, with plans to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI period akin to the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks 'the best of times, and the worst of times'.
