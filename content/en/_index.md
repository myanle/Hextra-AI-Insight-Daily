### **Today's AI Buzz**

1. **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! This isn't just about giving you answers; it's designed to fight that dreaded "AI brain drain" from relying too much on AI. Think of it as your personal programming "gym coach" 🏋️‍♀️, guiding you to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and truly master coding skills.

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch with just 270M parameters! It totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building fast, cost-effective, and super lightweight AI apps.

3. **OpenAI** CEO Altman laid out an ambitious blueprint: investing trillions into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**. The plan? To totally revolutionize search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 launch marks "the best of times, the worst of times."

4. **Xinzhiyuan's** 10th-anniversary bash kicked off the ASI era! 🎉 They're currently on the hunt for AI talent, including AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they want YOU to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5. **Hi3DEval**, a collaboration between Shanghai AI Lab and multiple universities, just dropped! It's a more scientific, comprehensive, and human-perceptive 3D generation quality evaluation system. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests designing "AI that cares about humans." "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts behind these potential AI risks.
