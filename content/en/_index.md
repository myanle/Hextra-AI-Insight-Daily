1.  **Mistral AI's Eco-Pledge: Sustainable AI Development!** 🌍 Mistral AI has dropped an environmental impact analysis report for its Large2 language model. This report reveals that the model's training and inference stages hit the environment hardest, accounting for a whopping **85.5% of carbon emissions and 91% of water consumption**. Over 18 months, Large2 has churned out 20.4 kilotons of CO2 emissions and guzzled 281,000 cubic meters of water. Even though a single query's impact might seem small, the cumulative environmental footprint is massive when you consider billions of long-term users. Mistral AI acknowledges the study's limitations and promises to update the report in the future, calling on the entire AI industry to step up transparency and work together towards global climate goals. 🌱 ![Mistral AI Report Screenshot](https://upload.chinaz.com/2025/0729/6388939624781008072290329.png)

2.  **MIRIX: The Open-Source Project Giving AI Long-Term Memory!** 🧠 MIRIX, an open-source project from researchers at UC San Diego and NYU, is changing the game as the world's first true multimodal, multi-agent AI memory system. Say goodbye to AI's "goldfish memory"! MIRIX actually writes "memory" into AI's core operating system, making it more than just a Q&A bot – it's an agent with **long-term memory**. It boasts six memory modules: Core Memory, Episodic Memory, Semantic Memory, Procedural Memory, Resource Memory, and a Knowledge Vault, all coordinated by a meta-memory manager and six sub-modules. MIRIX has totally crushed it in multiple benchmark tests, for example, achieving 35% higher accuracy than traditional methods on ScreenshotVQA tasks and slashing storage overhead by an incredible 99.9%.
