### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"**! This cool feature aims to tackle the pesky problem of "AI skill atrophy" that comes from leaning too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and truly master those coding skills.

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, is a real powerhouse! Clocking in at just 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Plus, its super low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3. **OpenAI** has some colossal plans, as its CEO, Sam Altman, recently spilled the beans on their ambitious blueprint. We're talking trillions of dollars poured into building data centers and rolling out new AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** space and aiming to totally disrupt search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release will herald "the best of times, and the worst of times."

4. **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI era in style! And guess what? They're on the hunt for AI talent, looking for an AI industry reporter-in-chief, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](Placeholder for Xinzhiyuan official website recruitment link, not provided in the original text)

5. The **Hi3DEval** system, cooked up by the Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with multiple universities, is a game-changer! It's a way more scientific and comprehensive quality evaluation system for 3D generation. This bad boy uses a three-tier protocol – object-level, component-level, and material-theme – to really dig deep into 3D models, analyzing their overall form, local structure, and material authenticity from every angle. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6. The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project is super cool, offering learning paths to build all sorts of tech from scratch. It's designed to help you really get a grip on programming skills by getting your hands dirty and doing the work! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

9. Meet **parlant**, an LLM agent designed to make Large Language Model (LLM) applications a breeze. It's super easy to deploy and use! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. Check out **magic**, an open-source AI productivity platform that's got it all! It seamlessly integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou, recently dropped some serious knowledge in his Stanford CS25 course, explaining the **inference mechanism of Large Language Models (LLMs)**. He pointed out that LLM inference works by generating a sequence of **intermediate tokens** and even delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. When it comes to AI safety, Li Feifei and Hinton have some differing views. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we ought to design **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are key concepts they're looking at for potential AI risks.
