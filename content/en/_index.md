### **Today's AI News** 📰

1.  🤖 **Claude Code** has dropped a fresh **"learning mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** from too much reliance on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding fitness coach, nudging users to pause at key steps, get their hands dirty, and truly nail down programming skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, rocks with just 270M parameters! It totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and cheap AI apps.
