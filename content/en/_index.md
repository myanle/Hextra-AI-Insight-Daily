### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just launched its brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to fight off that notorious "AI brain drain" from over-reliance on AI. This isn't about giving you answers directly; it's more like a coding "personal trainer," guiding you to hit pause at key steps, get your hands dirty, and truly nail down those programming skills. 😎

2.  Google just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a super lightweight large model rocking just 270M parameters! Yet, it crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring**, outperforming even some chunkier models. Its low power consumption makes it perfect for running on your phone or Raspberry Pi, ideal for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman painted a massive vision for the company, talking trillions of dollars for new data centers and AI hardware, and even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** space to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a pivotal AI moment, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯

4.  It's Xinzhiyuan's tenth-anniversary bash and the dawn of the ASI era! 🎉 They're on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and intern editors. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a new, more scientific, comprehensive, and rigorous 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been rolled out by Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities. It uses a three-tier protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, checking out their overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou dropped some serious knowledge in his Stanford CS25 course, breaking down **Large Language Model (LLM)** reasoning mechanisms. He highlighted that LLM inference works by generating a string of **intermediate tokens**, and he even delved into cool stuff like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. AI safety is a hot potato, and **Li Feifei** and **Hinton** have some different takes. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we cook up **AI that actually cares about humans**. Key concepts in the mix for potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence." 🤖
