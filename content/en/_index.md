### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a fresh new **'Learning Mode'**! 💪🧠 This awesome feature aims to combat the dreaded **'AI brain drain'** that comes from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding you to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on, so you truly master those programming skills. How cool is that?

2.  Get ready for **Gemma 3 270M**! 🐯🚀 Google just open-sourced this super lightweight large model, rocking a mere 270M parameters. But don't let its size fool you – it absolutely crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models! Plus, its low-power footprint means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building those snappy, cost-effective AI apps. Sweet! 📱💡

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing blueprint for the future! 💰🌌 We're talking trillions of dollars poured into data centers, new AI hardware development, and even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interfaces**. The goal? To completely shake up search and social media as we know it! Altman believes we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will mark both "the best of times and the worst of times." Wild, right? 🤯

It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis on the overall form, local structure, and material realism of 3D models. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
