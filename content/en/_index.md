### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🚀 It's designed to tackle the pesky problem of **"AI-induced skill atrophy"** that comes from relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; nope, it acts more like a coding "gym coach," prompting users to pause at crucial steps and get their hands dirty, truly mastering programming skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large language model, is a real game-changer! 🐅 Despite having only 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power consumption means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up lightweight, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications. 📱✨

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing grand vision for the company! 🤯💰 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interface** tech, aiming to totally revolutionize search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack-dab in a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 signals that "it's the best of times, it's the worst of times." 🌌
