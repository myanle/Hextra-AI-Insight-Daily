### **Today's AI News**

1.  💡 **Claude Code** has launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the **"AI brain drain"** issue caused by long-term reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming 'fitness coach,' guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks themselves to truly master coding skills.

2.  ✨📱 **Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google, boasting only 270M parameters. It excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for building light, fast, and low-cost AI applications.

3.  💸🔮 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a massive vision for the company: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interfaces** to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI phase, similar to the internet bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks 'the best of times, the worst of times.'

4.  🥳📢 **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! Xinzhiyuan is actively recruiting for various AI-related roles, including AI industry lead writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  🔬📊 **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It utilizes a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, including overall shape, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

10. 💡💻 **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. 🧠💬 DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou recently lectured on **Large Language Model (LLM)** reasoning mechanisms in a Stanford CS25 course. 12. 🤝🤖 **Li Feifei and Hinton** have differing views on AI safety. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about superintelligence losing control and suggests designing **AI that cares about humanity**. 'Goal misgeneralization' and 'instrumental convergence' are two core concepts defining potential AI risks.
