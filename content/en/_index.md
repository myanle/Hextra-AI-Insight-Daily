### **Today's AI News** 👇

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode."** This innovative mode aims to tackle the issue of **"AI brain atrophy"** that comes from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts more like a personal coding trainer, guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on, and genuinely master programming skills. 🧠💻

2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight, open-source large model. Despite having only 270 million parameters, it absolutely shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building quick, cost-effective, and nimble AI applications. 🚀📱

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has sketched out OpenAI's massive blueprint. It includes investing trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** field with plans to completely shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI phase, much like the dot-com bubble, and that GPT-5's launch signals "the best of times, the worst of times." 💰🌌

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! They're currently on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns, among other roles. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their team! 🎉 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a new 3D generation quality evaluation system. Jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, it's designed to be more scientific and comprehensive. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, covering overall form, local structure, and material realism. ✨ [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** have contrasting perspectives on AI safety issues. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about superintelligent AI running amok, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts defining potential AI risks. 🤯🤖
