### Today's AI News

1.  **Claude Code** has launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode,"** aiming to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** issue caused by long-term AI dependency. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, roll up their sleeves, and complete tasks themselves, truly mastering coding skills. 💪💡
2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, with only 270M parameters, shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for building light, fast, and low-cost AI applications. ✨⚡
3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a colossal vision for the company: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interface** tech, with plans to disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI phase, reminiscent of the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release signifies "the best of times, the worst of times." 🚀🤯
4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're actively recruiting AI industry lead reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns, among other positions. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join! 🥳🤝 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Job Openings Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)
