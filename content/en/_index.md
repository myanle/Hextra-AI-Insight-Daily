### Today's AI Buzz

1.  **Claude Code** has dropped a fresh new **"learning mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, prompting users to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and really nail down those programming skills. 😎

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, weighs in at just 270M parameters but crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a colossal vision for the company: investing trillions into data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release signaling "the best of times and the worst of times." 🤯

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is throwing a massive 10th-anniversary bash, kicking off the ASI era! They're also on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! 5.  **Hi3DEval**, a new evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. This super scientific and comprehensive framework for 3D generation quality assessment uses a three-tier protocol (object-level, component-level, and material-theme) to multi-dimensionally analyze the overall form, local structure, and material realism of 3D models. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton** have some differing takes on AI safety. Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue and suggests building **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "tool convergence" are two core concepts for potential AI risks. 😱
