### **Today's AI News**

1.  🧠 **Claude Code** has launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the "AI brain atrophy" problem caused by long-term reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at critical steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they genuinely master programming skills.

2.  🚀 **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is an open-source, lightweight large model. With only 270M parameters, it excels in tasks like **instruction following** and **text structuring**, outperforming even some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like smartphones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  🌌 **OpenAI's CEO Sam Altman** has laid out a grand vision for the company: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware. They're even planning to delve into **brain-computer interfaces**, aiming to shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a crucial AI era, akin to the internet bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  🎉 **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary and launching the ASI era! They're actively recruiting for various AI-related positions, including AI industry lead writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join them! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  🏗️ **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system. Launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities, it employs a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. 👨‍🏫 **Denny Zhou, Chief Scientist at DeepMind**, delivered a lecture at Stanford's CS25 course, explaining the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. 12. ⚖️ **Li Feifei and Hinton** hold different perspectives on AI safety issues. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about superintelligence spiraling out of control, advocating for designing **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are presented as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
