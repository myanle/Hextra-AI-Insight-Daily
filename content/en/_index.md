### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new 'learning mode' 💪 to tackle the whole 'AI brain drain' issue that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding 'fitness coach,' nudging users to pause at key steps, get hands-on with tasks, and truly master those programming skills.
2.  **Google** just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M** 🚀, a super lightweight large language model packing only 270M parameters. But don't let its size fool ya! It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for cranking out quick, cheap, and agile AI apps.
3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman 💰 laid out a massive vision for the company: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop next-gen AI hardware. They're even looking to jump into the **brain-computer interface** game, aiming to completely shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI moment, kinda like the internet bubble, and the launch of GPT-5 marks 'the best of times, the worst of times.'
4.  It's a big celebration! **Xinzhiyuan** is kicking off its tenth anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era 🎉! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! Li's all about focusing on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we design **AI that genuinely cares about humans**. 'Goal misgeneralization' and 'instrumental convergence' are the two core concepts highlighting AI's potential risks.
