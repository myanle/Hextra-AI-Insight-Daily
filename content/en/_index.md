### **Today's AI News**

1.  🧠 Claude Code has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**, aiming to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** issue caused by long-term AI dependency. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it guides users like a programming "personal trainer" to pause at critical steps, get hands-on, and truly master coding skills.

2.  🚀 Google has open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large language model with just 270M parameters. It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power nature allows it to run on devices like smartphones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  💰🌌 OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman, has outlined an ambitious blueprint for the company: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface** (BCI) domain with plans to revolutionize search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, with the release of GPT-5 marking "the best of times, and the worst of times."

5.  📊 **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, including overall form, local structure, and material realism.
