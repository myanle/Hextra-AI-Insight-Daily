### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code just dropped its brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the gnarly problem of **"AI brain atrophy"** from over-relying on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it's like having a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get their hands dirty, and truly master programming skills. 🚀
2.  Google's open-source **Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight large model with only 270M parameters. This little powerhouse crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models! Its low-power vibe means it can run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 💡
3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing blueprint for OpenAI: trillions of dollars poured into data centers and new AI hardware, even a dive into **brain-computer interfaces**! 🤯 He's planning to shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI moment, much like the dot-com bubble, and GPT-5's launch signals "the best of times, the worst of times."

The two big concepts highlighting AI's potential risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence." Heavy stuff! 🤯
