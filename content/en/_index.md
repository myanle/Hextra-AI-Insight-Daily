### **Today's AI Buzz** 📰

1.  **Claude Code** just launched a brand-spanking-new **"learning mode"**! 🎉 This awesome feature aims to tackle that pesky **"AI brain atrophy"** issue from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding "gym coach," nudging you to pause at key steps, get your hands dirty, and truly nail those programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is quite the superstar! 🌟 With a tiny 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Plus, its low-power footprint means it can run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman just dropped some seriously mind-blowing plans for OpenAI's future! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interface** tech with a goal to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI moment, much like the dot-com bubble, and GPT-5's launch signals "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its 10th anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're currently on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a super cool 3D generation quality assessment system, has been rolled out by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in partnership with several universities. ✨ It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to thoroughly analyze 3D models across various dimensions: overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou, recently spilled the beans on **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanisms during a Stanford CS25 course. 💡 He pointed out that LLM reasoning happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton are totally on different pages when it comes to AI safety! 🤔 Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's worried sick about superintelligence running wild and suggests we ought to design **AI that cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts at the heart of AI's potential risks.
