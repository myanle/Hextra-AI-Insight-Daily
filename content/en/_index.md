### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode”** 🧠, tackling that pesky **"AI brain drain”** problem where folks over-rely on AI and their skills fade. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like a coding "personal trainer," nudging users to pause at key steps, get their hands dirty, and really master those programming skills. No more AI dependence, folks! 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** 🚀, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is seriously impressive! With a tiny 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power design means it can run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building fast, cheap, and lean AI apps. So cool! 📱

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has painted a massive vision for the company 💰🌌. We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** game, planning to totally flip search and social media on their heads. Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble days, and the GPT-5 release? That's just the beginning of what he calls "the best of times, and the worst of times." Wild, right? ✨

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary with the grand opening of the AGI era! 🎉🥳 And guess what? They're on the hunt for AI talent, including AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** 😎, a new 3D generation quality assessment system, has been jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. This setup is way more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to give a multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and material realism. Super detailed! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  **build-your-own-x** 💪, a GitHub project, offers learning paths to build various technologies from scratch. It's an awesome way for users to master coding skills through hands-on practice. Get building! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** 🚀 provides a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan that covers everything from foundational certifications to advanced tricks. Time to level up your cyber game! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  **awesome-mac** 🍎 is a treasure trove for Mac users, compiling a collection of various advanced software for macOS. Your Mac will thank you! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **parlant** 🤖 is an LLM agent that simplifies large language model (LLM) applications. It's designed for easy deployment and use, making LLM magic accessible! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** 🤔 is an open-source AI productivity platform that brings together AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. It's basically an all-in-one AI powerhouse! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** 🤯 gave a talk at Stanford's CS25 course, explaining the **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanism. He pointed out that LLM reasoning happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and then explored techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Fascinating stuff! [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** 🤖 have different takes on AI safety issues. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we need to build **AI that cares about humans**. The core concepts for potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "tool convergence." A lot to ponder! 🤔
