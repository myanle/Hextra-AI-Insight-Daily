### Today's AI News

1.  💡 **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the issue of **"AI brain drain"** stemming from over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand you the answers; instead, it acts like a coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on, and complete tasks themselves to truly master programming skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch with just 270M parameters. It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power nature means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  💰 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unfurled a massive blueprint for the company: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** realm, aiming to revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, with the release of GPT-5 signaling "the best of times, the worst of times."

Xinzhiyuan is currently on the hunt for talent, recruiting for various roles like AI Industry Lead Writer, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. 5.  🔬 **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions for overall form, local structure, and material authenticity.
