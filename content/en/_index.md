### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code has dropped a brand-new "Learning Mode" 🧠, aiming to tackle the dreaded "AI brain drain" that comes from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause at key steps and actually *do* the work yourself. The goal? To truly master those programming skills!

2.  Google just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M** 🚀, a super lightweight large model that's packing a punch! With only 270M parameters, it totally crushes "instruction following" and "text structuring" tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Plus, its low-power footprint means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI's** CEO, Sam Altman, has laid out an absolutely wild grand vision 🌌 for the company. We're talking trillions of dollars poured into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into brain-computer interfaces! 🧠 His goal? To completely revolutionize search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will be a "best of times, worst of times" moment.

4.  It's a party! 🥳 **Xinzhiyuan's** 10th Anniversary Celebration has kicked off, marking the dawn of the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join the team! 5.  **Hi3DEval** is here, and it's a game-changer! 😎 Developed by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, this system offers a way more scientific and comprehensive framework for evaluating 3D generation quality. It uses a three-tier protocol covering object-level, component-level, and material themes to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions – from overall shape and local structure to material realism. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. There's a fascinating split! 🤯 **Li Feifei and Hinton** have different takes on AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's got bigger worries about superintelligent AI running wild, suggesting we design AIs that actually **care about humanity**. The two big concepts for potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence." Food for thought!
