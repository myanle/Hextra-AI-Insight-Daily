### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"**! ✨🧠 It's all about tackling that dreaded **"AI brain drain"** from relying too heavily on AI. This mode isn't just handing out answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding you to pause at key steps and actually *do* the tasks yourself, so you truly master those programming skills.
2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a real powerhouse! 💪 With only 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building fast, cheap, and cheerful AI apps. 📱💨
3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just dropped a bombshell vision for the company's future! 🤯 He's talking trillions of dollars for data centers, new AI hardware development, and even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** with plans to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the internet bubble, and the GPT-5 release? That's marking "the best of times and the worst of times." 🚀💸

It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to deliver multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. Check it out! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
