### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠 This bad boy is designed to combat that dreaded "AI brain drain" from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing you answers, it acts like your personal coding "gym trainer," guiding you to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and truly master those programming skills.

2.  **Google's open-source Gemma 3 270M** 🚀 is a seriously lightweight large language model, rocking a mere 270M parameters! Yet, this little tiger shines brightly in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some bigger models. Its low-power design means it can run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** 🤯 just unveiled a mind-blowing blueprint for the company: we're talking trillions of dollars in investments for data centers and cutting-edge AI hardware, even diving headfirst into the **brain-computer interface (BCI)** space! The goal? To completely revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're smack-dab in a critical "Internet bubble"-like phase for AI, with the upcoming GPT-5 launch signaling, as he put it, "it's the best of times, it's the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** 🥳 is throwing a massive party for its tenth anniversary, kicking off nothing less than the AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) era! They're also seriously beefing up their team, recruiting for various AI-related roles like lead AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors, and even editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they're calling your name! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** ✨ is a game-changer! Shanghai AI Lab, in collaboration with several universities, just unveiled this super scientific and comprehensive evaluation system for 3D generation quality. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme-level—to give you a multi-dimensional deep dive into 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

[▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

Li Feifei zeroes in on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's sweating over uncontrolled superintelligence, pushing for the design of **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. Apparently, "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts fueling these potential AI risks.
