### **Today's AI News**

1. Claude Code has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the gnarly problem of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by over-reliance on AI. This isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it's like having a coding "fitness coach" that prompts users to pause at crucial steps and get their hands dirty, genuinely mastering programming skills. 💪

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, packs a punch despite its modest 270M parameters. It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Its low-power footprint means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI applications. ✨

3. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has unfurled OpenAI's audacious grand vision: pouring trillions into data center construction and new AI hardware development, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** arena, with plans to disrupt search and social media. He reckons we're smack-dab in an AI pivotal moment, akin to the dot-com bubble, with the GPT-5 launch signaling "the best of times and the worst of times." 🤯

4. Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its tenth anniversary, marking the dawn of the ASI era! They're currently on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [Xinzhiyuan Job Openings](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) 🚀

5. **Hi3DEval**, a new evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in collaboration with several universities. This setup offers a more scientific and comprehensive approach to assessing 3D generation quality. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. Check out the [Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and the [3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)! 🌟

6. The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project is a total game-changer, offering learning paths to build all sorts of tech from scratch. It's designed to help users really nail down programming skills through hands-on practice. Find the [Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x) here! 💻

7. **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** serves up a comprehensive 90-day cybersecurity learning plan, covering everything from fundamental certifications to advanced techniques. Ready to level up your security game? Check out the [Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)! 🛡️

8. The **awesome-mac** project is a treasure trove for Mac users, compiling a fantastic collection of advanced software for macOS. Your Mac setup is about to get a serious upgrade! Dive into the [Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac) now! 🍏

9. **parlant** is a slick LLM agent designed to simplify large language model (LLM) applications, making them a breeze to deploy and use. Get started with the [Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)! 🤖

10. **magic** is a cool open-source AI productivity platform that brings together AI agents, a workflow engine, instant messaging, and an online collaborative office system. It's a real all-in-one deal! Check out the [Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic) for more. ✨

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou gave a killer lecture at Stanford's CS25 course, breaking down the **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanism. He highlighted how LLM reasoning works by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Catch the [Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk) to learn more! 🧠

12. Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton have some differing views on AI safety concerns. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton frets about uncontrolled superintelligence, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts highlighting potential AI risks. 💬
