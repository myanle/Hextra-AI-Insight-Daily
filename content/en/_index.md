### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"**! This cool feature tackles "AI brain atrophy," a problem where relying too much on AI makes your skills rusty. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, it's like having a coding "personal trainer" – it guides you to pause at crucial steps, roll up your sleeves, and get hands-on, so you genuinely master those programming skills. 🧠💡

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight large model, is a real game-changer! Clocking in at just 270M parameters, this bad boy totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building super light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀✨

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing roadmap! The plan? Trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** game. OpenAI aims to totally shake up search and social media as we know it. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI moment, akin to the dot-com boom, with GPT-5's launch signaling "the best of times and the worst of times." 🤯💰

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its tenth anniversary, kicking off the ASI Era with a bang! They're on the hunt for passionate AI talent, recruiting for roles like AI Industry Lead Writer, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editor Intern. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) 🎉

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a collaboration between Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, is a spiffy new 3D generation quality assessment system that's way more scientific and comprehensive. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to give a multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard) 🛠️

6.  The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project is a total gem for anyone looking to truly get their hands dirty! It offers learning paths to build all sorts of tech from scratch, helping users master coding skills through practical application. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x) 🏗️

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** is serving up a comprehensive 90-day cybersecurity learning plan that covers absolutely everything—from basic certifications to advanced techniques. Time to level up your security game! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity) 🔒

8.  The **awesome-mac** project is a treasure trove for Mac users, jam-packed with a collection of high-quality software for macOS. Your Mac setup just got an upgrade! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac) 💻

9.  **Parlant** is a slick LLM agent that simplifies large language model (LLM) applications, making them super easy to deploy and use. Smooth sailing for your LLM projects! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant) 🤖

10. **Magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that's got it all: integrated AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. Talk about an all-in-one solution! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic) ✨

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist **Denny Zhou** dropped some serious knowledge at Stanford's CS25 course, breaking down the **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanism. He highlighted that LLM reasoning goes down by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Wanna see? [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk) 🧠

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** are on different pages when it comes to AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild, pushing for the creation of "AI that cares about humanity." "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts of potential AI risks. 🗣️
