### **Today's AI News**

1. 💡 **Claude Code** has launched a brand new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the "AI brain atrophy" problem caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; it acts like a programming "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at key steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they truly master programming skills.

2. ✨ **Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google. It only has 270M parameters, but it performs exceptionally well in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some larger models. Its low-power nature means it can run on devices like mobile phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3. 🤯 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has painted a grand vision for OpenAI: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interface** tech with plans to disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI phase, similar to the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, the worst of times."

4. 🥳 Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its 10th anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! They're currently hiring for various positions, including AI industry lead writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join! [Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5. 🔬 **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, including overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

7. 🛡️ **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** provides a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan, covering all aspects of cybersecurity, from basic certifications to advanced techniques. [Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)
