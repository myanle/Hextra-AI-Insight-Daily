### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! This isn't your typical answer machine; it's designed to combat **"AI brain atrophy"** from over-reliance. Think of it as your personal programming "gym coach" – it guides you through crucial steps, then makes you pause and actually *do* the work to truly master those coding skills. Pretty neat, right? ✨

2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is making waves! This open-source, super lightweight large model packs a punch with just 270M parameters. It's crushing it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Plus, its low-power footprint means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building quick, affordable, and lightweight AI apps. How cool is that? 🤩

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman is laying out a seriously ambitious vision! We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware. OpenAI even plans to dive into **brain-computer interfaces** and totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and with GPT-5 on the horizon, he's quoting Dickens: "It was the best of times, it was the worst of times." Wild stuff! 🤯

5.  **Hi3DEval** just dropped, and it's a game-changer! Developed by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, it's a more scientific and comprehensive assessment system for 3D generation quality. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, checking their overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. Pretty cool for 3D creators! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard) ✨
