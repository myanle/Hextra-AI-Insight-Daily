### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-spanking-new "Learning Mode." This cool feature aims to tackle the notorious "AI brain drain" issue that pops up when folks rely too much on AI for too long. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, nudging users to pause at crucial steps and actually get their hands dirty with tasks, so they can genuinely master those programming skills! 💪

2. **Gemma 3 270M** is Google's open-source, lightweight large model. This little powerhouse, with a mere 270M parameters, absolutely crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models! 🤯 Thanks to its low-power consumption, you can run it on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3. **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has painted a pretty grand picture for the company's future. His ambitious blueprint includes pumping trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving headfirst into the **brain-computer interface** arena, aiming to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a crucial AI period, kinda like the internet bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 release? That's just a sign that it's "the best of times, and the worst of times." 🚀🤯

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some differing opinions when it comes to AI safety, and it's quite the debate! Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild, suggesting we should design **AI that actually cares about humanity**. The two core concepts for potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence." 🤔
