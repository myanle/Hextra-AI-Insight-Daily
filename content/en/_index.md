### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out its brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠 This fresh feature is designed to combat **"AI brain atrophy"**—that's right, skill degradation from leaning too hard on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym coach 🏋️‍♀️, prompting users to hit pause at key stages, get hands-on, and complete tasks themselves. The ultimate goal? To truly master those programming skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a total rockstar! 🌟 This little powerhouse, clocking in at just 270M parameters, absolutely crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some bigger models. Its super low-power footprint means you can run it on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for cooking up light, fast, and super affordable AI apps. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has painted a seriously ambitious picture for the company's future! 🌌 We're talking trillions in investments to build massive data centers and develop cutting-edge AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** (BCI) arena, aiming to completely flip the script on search and social media. 🤯 Altman believes we're right in the middle of a crucial AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 will mark a period of "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its tenth anniversary, officially ushering in the ASI era! 🎉🥳 They're actively recruiting for several roles, including AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they're super keen for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a super cool, more scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system! 📈 It's been jointly rolled out by the Shanghai AI Lab and a bunch of universities. This system rocks a three-tiered evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to dive deep into 3D models, analyzing their overall form, local structure, and material authenticity from multiple angles. Check out the project homepage [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and the 3D generation leaderboard [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard).

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** totally blew minds in a Stanford CS25 course, explaining the reasoning mechanisms behind **Large Language Models (LLMs)**! 🤯 He pointed out that LLM reasoning happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also explored techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. 12. **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** are on slightly different pages when it comes to AI safety. 🤖 Li is hyper-focused on **design, governance, and values**, whereas Hinton is more concerned about superintelligent AI spiraling out of control, advocating for designing **AI that actually cares about humanity**. The two core concepts underpinning AI's potential risks are "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence."
