### **Today's AI News**

1. Claude Code has dropped a brand new "Learning Mode" 🤯. It's designed to tackle "AI brain atrophy," a.k.a. that skill drain from over-relying on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym instructor 🏋️, guiding you to pause at key steps, get hands-on, and genuinely master your programming skills.

2. Gemma 3 270M, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is quite the performer! 🚀 Despite its mere 270M parameters, it excels at instruction following and text structuring tasks, even outshining some beefier models. Its low power consumption means you can run it on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3. OpenAI CEO Altman has unveiled a truly epic blueprint for OpenAI 🌌. It includes pouring trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving headfirst into brain-computer interfaces. The goal? To totally shake up search and social media! Altman believes we're smack dab in a crucial AI era, kinda like the internet bubble. He sees the GPT-5 launch as marking "the best of times, and the worst of times."

5. Hi3DEval is a game-changing 3D generation quality evaluation system! 📈 Launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, it's more scientific and comprehensive. It uses a three-tier protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. 12. Li Feifei and Hinton have some differing views on AI safety concerns 🤔. Li Feifei focuses on design, governance, and values, while Hinton is more worried about uncontrolled superintelligence, suggesting we design AI that actually cares about humanity. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of AI's potential risks.
