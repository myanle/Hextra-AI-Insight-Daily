8.  **Household robots** might be handy, but a new study just spilled the beans on their safety flaws! Researchers from Shanghai AI Lab and Beihang University developed **IS-Bench**, a benchmark to evaluate the safety of household robots. The test results are a bit alarming: current mainstream household robots have a safety completion rate of less than 40%, meaning they've got some serious safety risks, like getting food dirty or even setting blankets on fire! 😬 The research points out that the robots' biggest safety weakness is their **insufficient risk perception and cognitive abilities**. Yikes! ![Image: Image](https://image.jiqizhixin.com/uploads/editor/f99841d3-7ba2-44a8-bb50-40918ff0b695/640.png) [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/AI45Lab/IS-Bench)

9.  **The 'unsung heroes' of LLMs—Process-level Reward Models (PRMs)—are facing a trust crisis**, according to a paper accepted at ACL 2025! This paper spills the tea on how **PRMs** often fall short when it comes to catching subtle errors during reasoning processes. To put them to the test, researchers developed **PRMBench** for evaluation. The experimental results show that existing PRMs have clear limitations and a "positive bias," making them vulnerable to "reward hacking" attacks. Not ideal! 🧐🚨 ![Image: Image](https://image.jiqizhixin.com/uploads/editor/e0b12cb4-f1a0-4dd9-9719-b341a8c28bed/640.png) [🔗 Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03124) [▶️ Explanatory Video](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1kgu8z8E6D)
