### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has just launched a new "Learning Mode," a game-changer designed to tackle "AI brain atrophy" – that skill degradation from relying too much on AI. This isn't about giving you direct answers; instead, "Learning Mode" acts like your personal coding gym instructor, guiding you to pause at crucial steps and get hands-on with tasks, so you genuinely master programming skills. 💪

2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is making waves as a lightweight, open-source large model. Despite its tiny 270M parameters, this bad boy crushes "instruction following" and "text structuring" tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power footprint means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 💥

3.  **OpenAI**, specifically its CEO Sam Altman, has laid out an absolutely wild, multi-trillion-dollar roadmap. We're talking massive investments in data centers and new AI hardware, even diving headfirst into brain-computer interfaces, all aiming to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's launch screaming, "It's the best of times, it's the worst of times!" 🤯

They're also on the hunt for top AI talent, looking to fill roles like AI Industry Report Lead Writer, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. 5.  **Hi3DEval** is Shanghai AI Lab's brainchild, developed with several universities, and it's a super scientific, comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system. This bad boy uses a three-tier evaluation protocol – object-level, component-level, and material theme – to deep-dive into the overall form, local structure, and material realism of 3D models. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

The two big concepts driving potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence." It's a heavy debate! ⚖️
