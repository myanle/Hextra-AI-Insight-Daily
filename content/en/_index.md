### **Today's AI News**

1.  💡🧠**Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"learning mode"** designed to tackle that pesky **"AI brain atrophy"** problem caused by leaning on AI too much. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it's like having a coding personal trainer! It guides users to pause at crucial points, get their hands dirty, and truly master programming skills.

2.  🚀**Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large model, packs a punch with just 270M parameters! It totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. And get this: its low-power consumption means it can run on gadgets like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up lean, mean, and cheap AI apps.

3.  💰🌌**OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a massive vision for the company: we're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and rolling out next-gen AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** game, aiming to flip search and social media on their heads! Altman believes we're in a make-or-break AI era, much like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch? He says it's gonna be "the best of times, the worst of times."

FYI, "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two big concepts to watch out for as potential AI risks.
