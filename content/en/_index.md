### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 🧠💪 to tackle the **"AI brain drain"** issue stemming from long-term AI dependency. This mode isn't about spitting out direct answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they genuinely master coding skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, boasts excellent performance 🚀 in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some larger models, despite having only 270M parameters. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has sketched out a grand vision 💰🌌 for the company: investing trillions into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** space, aiming to shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, much like the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary 🎉🥳 and the dawn of the ASI era! They're currently on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns, inviting all AI enthusiasts to join their team! 5.  **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system ✨, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in collaboration with multiple universities. It utilizes a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—for multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism.
