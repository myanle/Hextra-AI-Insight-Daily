### **Today's AI News** ✨

1.  💡 **Claude Code** just launched a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! It's here to tackle that gnarly **"AI brain drain"** problem caused by over-reliance on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on, and actually master those programming skills.

2.  ⚡ **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is turning heads! Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it crushes it on **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building super light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  🤯 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just dropped a bombshell, outlining OpenAI's ambitious roadmap! Think trillions of dollars poured into data centers and cutting-edge AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** space, with plans to totally flip search and social media on their heads. Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble days, and the GPT-5 launch marks "the best of times, the worst of times."

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts underlying potential AI risks.
