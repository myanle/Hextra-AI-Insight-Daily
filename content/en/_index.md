### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** just dropped a brand new **"Learning Mode"**! This cool feature tackles the dreaded "AI brain drain" problem that comes from over-relying on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause at key steps and get hands-on. The goal? To truly master those programming skills. 💪

2. **Google** just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a super lightweight large model. Don't let its tiny 270M parameters fool you—this bad boy crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models! Plus, its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀📱

3. **OpenAI's** CEO, Sam Altman, painted a truly grand vision for the company, talking trillions of dollars for data centers and new AI hardware development. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** space, aiming to shake up search and social media as we know it! Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble. He even quoted, "It's the best of times, it's the worst of times," with the GPT-5 launch signaling this pivotal moment. 🤯💰

4. Guess what? **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its tenth anniversary, marking the dawn of the ASI era! And get this, they're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! 👋 [Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5. **Hi3DEval** is here to level up 3D generation quality! This super scientific and comprehensive evaluation system was jointly launched by the Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze the overall form, local structure, and material realism of 3D models from multiple angles. Pretty slick, huh? ✨ [Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

9. Meet **parlant**, an LLM agent designed to simplify large language model (LLM) applications. It's super easy to deploy and use, making your LLM journey a breeze! 🤖💬 [Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. The **magic** project is an open-source AI productivity platform that's packed with features. It integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. Talk about an all-in-one solution! 🚀 [Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **Denny Zhou**, DeepMind's Chief Scientist, recently broke down the **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanism during a Stanford CS25 course. He explained that LLM reasoning happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Super insightful stuff! 🧠💡 [Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. When it comes to AI safety, **Fei-Fei Li** and **Hinton** have some pretty different takes. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild, suggesting we create AI that actually **cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks. Food for thought! 🤔⚖️
