### **Today's AI News** ✨

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"**! 🚀 This cool new feature tackles "AI brain drain," where relying too much on AI makes your skills rusty. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding gym buddy, pausing at key steps and encouraging you to get hands-on. The goal? To truly master your coding skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's new open-source lightweight LLM, is making waves! 🤯 Despite having only 270M parameters, it crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for building speedy, affordable, and lightweight AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI's** CEO, Sam Altman, just dropped a mind-blowing blueprint for the future. 🚀 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**! OpenAI aims to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal, "internet bubble-like" era for AI, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will truly mark "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** just hit its tenth anniversary, kicking off the AGI era with a bang! 🎉 They're on the hunt for passionate AI enthusiasts to join their team, with openings for AI industry chief writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you love AI, they want you! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a new 3D generation quality assessment system, just dropped! 🌟 Developed by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities, it's way more scientific and comprehensive. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, checking their overall form, local structure, and material realism. Check it out! [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. **DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou**, broke down **Large Language Model (LLM)** reasoning mechanisms during his Stanford CS25 course. 🧠 He highlighted that LLM inference happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. 12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some different takes on AI safety, sparking quite the debate. 🤔 Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue, suggesting we design **AIs that care about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are key concepts for understanding these potential AI risks.
