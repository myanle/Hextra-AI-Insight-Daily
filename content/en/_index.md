### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-spanking-new **'Learning Mode'**! 🤯 It's designed to combat 'AI brain atrophy' – that nagging problem of skills fading when you rely too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause at key steps and actually *do* the work yourself. The goal? To truly master those programming skills. No more AI hand-holding, just genuine skill-building! 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a real powerhouse! 🚀 Clocking in at just 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and super affordable AI apps. Pretty neat, huh? ✨

3.  OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman, has laid out an absolutely wild blueprint for the company. 🌌 He's talking trillions in investments for data centers and next-gen AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** game to totally shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the internet bubble era, with GPT-5's launch marking "the best of times and the worst of times." Talk about high stakes! 💰

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a game-changer! 😎 This scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system was launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol – object-level, component-level, and material-theme – to perform multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. Pretty slick, right? Check out the [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and the [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard) for more!
