### **Today's AI News**

1.  🧠🤖 **Claude Code** rolls out a fresh **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** problem that creeps up from over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at key steps and get hands-on with tasks to truly master programming skills.

2.  🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large model from Google, packs just 270M parameters but crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Its low-power vibe means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building quick, cost-effective, and feather-light AI apps.

3.  💰🌌 **OpenAI** CEO Altman has sketched out a super ambitious roadmap for OpenAI: we're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and rolling out new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** scene. The goal? To totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're smack-dab in a crucial AI era, kinda like the internet bubble days, and the GPT-5 release screams, "It was the best of times, it was the worst of times."

12. 🤖 **Li Feifei** and **Hinton** hold differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei hones in on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more concerned about superintelligent AI spiraling out of control, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
