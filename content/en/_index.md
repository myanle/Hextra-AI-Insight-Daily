### **Today's AI News**

1.  `Claude Code` just dropped a fresh `**"Learning Mode"**` to tackle the dreaded `**"AI brain drain"**` (aka skill atrophy) that comes from over-relying on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause at key steps and actually *do* the work. The goal? To truly master those coding skills! ✨
2.  `**Gemma 3 270M**`, `Google`'s new open-source lightweight large language model, is a total powerhouse despite its tiny 270M parameters! It absolutely crushes `**instruction-following**` and `**text structuring**` tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its super low power consumption means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building speedy, low-cost AI apps. 🤯
3.  `OpenAI` CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing blueprint for the future! `OpenAI` plans to invest *trillions* of dollars into building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware, even diving into the wild world of `**brain-computer interfaces**`. Their goal? To completely revolutionize search and social media as we know it. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI era, kind of like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming `GPT-5` release marks "the best of times, the worst of times." 🚀
4.  It's a huge celebration over at `Xinzhiyuan`! They're hitting their `**10th Anniversary**` and kicking off the `ASI Era`! `Xinzhiyuan` is actively scouting for awesome AI talent, including AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they're inviting *you* to join their squad! 🥳 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)
5.  Say hello to `**Hi3DEval**`! This new, super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system was cooked up by the `Shanghai AI Lab` in collaboration with several universities. `Hi3DEval` uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material themes—to deep-dive into 3D models, analyzing their overall form, local structures, and material realism from multiple angles. 📊 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts for potential `AI` risks. 🤯
