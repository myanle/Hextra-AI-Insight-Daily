### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** just rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 💡, aiming to tackle the "AI skill atrophy" problem caused by long-term AI reliance. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at key steps and get hands-on with tasks to truly master coding skills. 💪

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, an open-source lightweight large model from Google, packs a punch with just 270M parameters! 🤯 It really excels at **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Thanks to its low-power consumption, it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and low-cost AI applications. 📱⚡

3. **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has painted a grand vision for the company: 🚀 investing trillions of dollars in data centers and new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interface** tech, with plans to revolutionize search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks that "It was the best of times, it was the worst of times." 🧠💰

4. **Xinzhiyuan's** 10th-anniversary celebration is happening, kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're currently hiring for various roles, including AI industry lead writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join! 👋 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5. **Hi3DEval** 🔍, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol covering object-level, component-level, and material themes, conducting multi-dimensional analysis on the overall shape, local structure, and material realism of 3D models. 🎨 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** 🤔 have differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton worries about superintelligence spiraling out of control, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks. ⚖️
