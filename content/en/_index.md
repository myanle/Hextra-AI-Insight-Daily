17. **Embodied AI: Three core questions and the SLAP³ architecture**: Dr. Zhang Zhengyou, Tencent's Chief Scientist, is cutting straight to the chase about the core challenges in **embodied AI**. He reckons the choice between **end-to-end** and **layered architectures** is all about balancing efficiency with reality. Right now, due to data limits, a layered architecture (like Tencent's SLAP³ architecture, which boasts three big models for perception, planning, and action) is more practical. It's kinda inspired by the human brain, breaking down complex tasks to boost efficiency. In the SLAP³ architecture, the "cerebellum" handles quick reactions, while the "cerebrum" takes on complex decisions. Both swap info via a "memory bank" to enable self-learning. But hold up, this is just the beginning! The ultimate goal is to nail a **natively multimodal end-to-end** model, though that'll need a ton of data and a better "language" for building feedback loops. ![SLAP³ Architecture Diagram](https://image.jiqizhixin.com/uploads/editor/1943f9f9-a036-408f-a8fd-8d7264b5c3ed/640.png) Dr. Zhang stresses that **body-brain fusion** is the first principle of embodied AI; true embodied AI needs a deep understanding of itself and its environment. He believes action planning isn't just simple video generation but something way more abstract and self-centered. Lastly, he's urging folks to keep their **innovative focus** amidst the commercialization craze, avoiding short-term gains that might derail long-term goals. 🧠🤖
