### **Today's AI News**

1.  🤔💡 **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"**! It's designed to fight against that dreaded **"AI brain drain"** (aka skill atrophy) that comes from leaning too hard on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause, get hands-on with tasks, and truly master those programming skills.

2.  🚀🤏 Get this: **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large language model, is a total powerhouse! Despite having just 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Plus, its low-power footprint means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi – perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  🤯💸 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just unveiled his wild, ambitious vision for the company! We're talking trillions of dollars poured into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interfaces**. The goal? To totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal, "dot-com bubble"-esque era for AI, with the upcoming GPT-5 launch signaling both "the best of times and the worst of times."

They're on the hunt for AI industry lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love to have you join the team! 5.  🔬✨ Check out **Hi3DEval**! This spiffy new system is a more scientific and comprehensive framework for evaluating 3D generation quality. It's a joint effort from the Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, using a three-tiered protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to dive deep into a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. Pretty cool, right? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. 🧠📺 Ever wondered how LLMs *really* think? DeepMind Chief Scientist **Denny Zhou** spilled the beans in a Stanford CS25 course! Zhou explained that large language models (LLMs) perform inference by cranking out a series of **intermediate tokens**, and he also delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Fascinating stuff! [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

Heads up: "goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two big concepts when we talk about potential AI risks.
