### **Today's AI Buzz**

1. **Claude Code** just rolled out its brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 💪 This mode aims to tackle the pesky problem of **"AI skill degradation"** that comes from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding fitness coach, guiding users to pause at critical steps, get their hands dirty, and truly master those programming skills.

2. **Gemma 3 270M** 🤯 is Google's open-source, lightweight large model. With a tiny 270M parameters, it crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models! Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones 📱 or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3. **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** 🚀 has sketched out OpenAI's ambitious blueprint: talking trillions in investments for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** 🧠 to revolutionize search and social media. He believes we're in a pivotal AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release signaling "It was the best of times, it was the worst of times."

4. **Xinzhiyuan** is kicking off its 10th-anniversary celebration 🥳 and the dawn of the ASI era! They're currently on the hunt for AI industry reporting chief writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their team! 🤝 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](URL for Xinzhiyuan official recruitment page, not provided in original)

5. **Hi3DEval** ✨ is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol focusing on object-level, component-level, and material-theme aspects, conducting multi-dimensional analyses of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. Check out the [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) and the [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard).

The two core concepts behind potential AI risks? "Misaligned goal generalization" and "instrumental convergence."
