### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** ✨ just dropped a fresh new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the dreaded **"AI Brain Atrophy"** issue that comes from relying on AI too much. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on, and really master their programming skills.

2. **Gemma 3 270M** 🤯, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is quite the performer! This little powerhouse, with just 270M parameters, absolutely shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power nature means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3. **OpenAI** 🚀 CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing, ambitious blueprint for the company. We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** realm, all with plans to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks this as "the best of times, and the worst of times."

4. **Xinzhiyuan** 🥳 just celebrated its tenth anniversary and kicked off the AGI Era! They're on the hunt for AI industry news writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns, among other roles. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5. **Hi3DEval** ✨ is a super cool, more scientific, and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6. The **build-your-own-x** 🛠️ GitHub project is an awesome resource, offering learning paths to construct all sorts of tech from scratch. It's designed to help users really get a grip on programming skills through hands-on practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7. **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** 🔒 offers a solid 90-day cybersecurity learning plan that covers absolutely everything cybersecurity-related, from basic certifications to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8. **awesome-mac** 💎 is a goldmine for Mac users, pulling together a sweet collection of advanced software for macOS. It's essentially a treasure trove for anyone looking to supercharge their Mac experience! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9. **parlant** ✨ is an LLM agent designed to make deploying and using large language model (LLM) applications a breeze. It's all about simplifying things! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** 🪄 is an open-source AI productivity platform that packs a punch, integrating AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. It's pretty much a one-stop shop for all your AI-powered teamwork needs! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist **Denny Zhou** 🧠, while teaching the Stanford CS25 course, totally broke down the **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanism. He highlighted that LLM inference happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and then explored techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** 🛡️ actually have different takes on AI safety issues. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild and suggests we need to design **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as the two core concepts behind AI's potential risks.
