### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode,"** designed to tackle **"AI brain atrophy"** from over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at critical steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they truly master programming skills. 🤖🧠
2.  **Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google, boasting only 270M parameters. Yet, it absolutely nails **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models! Its low-power nature means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. 🐯🚀
3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has sketched out a massive vision for the company: think trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware. They're even eyeing **brain-computer interface** tech, with plans to totally shake up search and social media! Altman believes we're smack-dab in a critical AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the GPT-5 launch signals "the best of times, the worst of times." 💰🌌
4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is kicking off its 10th-anniversary celebration, marking the dawn of the ASI era! They're also on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns – basically, a bunch of cool roles. So, if you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) 🎉
5.  **Hi3DEval** is a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, brought to you by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to analyze 3D models across multiple dimensions, focusing on overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard) 😎

10. **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform, bringing together AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. Talk about an all-in-one solution! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic) 🤔
11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** dropped some serious knowledge in his Stanford CS25 course, breaking down the inference mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. He pointed out that LLM reasoning happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and dove into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Mind-blowing stuff! [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk) 🤯
12. **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some diverging views on AI safety. Li Feifei is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild, suggesting we design **AI that actually cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks. 🤖
