### ⚡️ **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  Claude Code just dropped its brand-new "learning mode," a total game-changer for tackling that dreaded "AI brain drain" from over-reliance on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; it's more like your personal coding gym buddy, hitting pause at key steps to get you hands-on with tasks and truly master those programming skills. 💡

2.  Google's open-source lightweight giant, Gemma 3 270M, is making waves! With a mere 270M parameters, this model totally crushes it on instruction-following and text structuring tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its super low-power usage means you can run this bad boy on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up lean, mean, and cost-effective AI apps. 🚀

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has laid out a mind-blowing, trillion-dollar vision for OpenAI. He's talking massive investments in data centers, developing cutting-edge AI hardware, and even diving into brain-computer interfaces to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a crucial AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's launch signaling "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯

4.  NewIntelligence is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era – what a party! 🎉 They're also on the hunt for AI talent, recruiting for roles like AI industry lead writer, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 NewIntelligence Recruitment Info]

5.  Hi3DEval, a snazzy new 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in partnership with several universities. It's way more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tier assessment protocol – object-level, component-level, and material theme – to deep-dive into the overall form, local structure, and material realism of 3D models. 🔬 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The `build-your-own-x` GitHub project is a treasure trove! It hooks you up with learning paths to build all sorts of tech from scratch, helping you get seriously hands-on with programming skills. 🛠️ [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  Looking to get into cybersecurity? `90DaysOfCyberSecurity` has your back with a 90-day learning plan that covers absolutely everything, from basic certs to advanced hacks. 🔒 [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  `awesome-mac` is seriously a Mac user's dream! It's a goldmine of advanced software for all things Mac. 🍎 [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  `parlant` is an LLM agent designed to make large language model (LLM) applications a breeze. It's super easy to deploy and use. ✨ [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. `magic` is an open-source AI productivity platform that's got it all: AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. Talk about a one-stop shop! 🧙‍♂️ [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou, recently dropped some serious knowledge at Stanford's CS25 course, breaking down how Large Language Models (LLMs) actually do their reasoning. He pointed out that LLM reasoning happens by generating a sequence of "intermediate tokens" and even dove into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. 🧠 [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. When it comes to AI safety, Fei-Fei Li and Hinton have some differing viewpoints. Li is all about design, governance, and values, while Hinton is more worried about super-intelligent AI running wild and suggests designing AI that genuinely cares about humans. "Goal misgeneralization" and "tool convergence" are two massive concepts at the heart of AI's potential risks. ⚖️

With 220 graph-structured dynamic programming problems, it completely wiped the floor with them – every single model scored a big fat zero! 💥 [FormulaOne](https://image.jiqizhixin.com/uploads/editor/656fe086-cbec-46b3-b135-a600f7a68fda/640.png) [🔗 FormulaOne Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/double-ai/FormulaOne-Leaderboard)
