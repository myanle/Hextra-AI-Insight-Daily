### Today's AI News! 📰

1.  **Claude Code** 🧠✨ has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the issue of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks hands-on, helping them truly master coding skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** 🐯💨, a lightweight large model open-sourced by Google, packs a punch with just 270M parameters. It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, outperforming even some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman**, 💰🌍 has painted a grand vision for the company: investing trillions in data centers and new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interfaces** to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, with the release of GPT-5 signaling "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan (New Intelligence)** 🥳🥳 is celebrating its 10th anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! They're currently on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns, inviting all AI enthusiasts to join their squad! 5.  **Hi3DEval** 🔬📏, launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, introduces a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to multi-dimensionally analyze 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism.
