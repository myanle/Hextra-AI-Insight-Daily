### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  Claude Code just dropped a fresh "Learning Mode" 🤯 to tackle that pesky "AI brain drain" problem from relying too much on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, guiding you to pause at key steps, get your hands dirty, and truly master those programming skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is turning heads! 🤩 With just 270M parameters, this bad boy shines in instruction following and text structuring tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power footprint means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building super light, fast, and cheap AI applications. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a wild, grand vision for the company. 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in data centers and new AI hardware, even dipping into brain-computer interfaces, all aiming to totally disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 release marks "the best of times, the worst of times."

"Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts highlighting AI's potential risks.
