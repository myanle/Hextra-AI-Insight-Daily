### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 🚀! It's here to tackle that dreaded **"AI Brain Drain"** from relying too much on AI. Instead of just spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "gym coach," nudging you to pause at key steps and get your hands dirty, so you genuinely master your programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is quite the performer! 🌟 With just 270M parameters, it totally aces **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some beefier models. Its low power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has laid out an ambitious roadmap 🤯 for OpenAI, envisioning trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware. They're even planning to jump into **brain-computer interfaces**, aiming to shake up search and social media! Altman believes we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the release of GPT-5 marks "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're currently on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join the team! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** is a snazzy new 3D generation quality assessment system 🤩, cooked up by Shanghai AI Lab with several universities. It's more scientific and comprehensive, using a three-tiered evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to give 3D models a multi-dimensional check-up on their overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The **build-your-own-x** GitHub project is a total gem ✨! It offers learning paths to build all sorts of tech from scratch, helping users master programming skills by actually doing the work. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** offers an awesome 90-day cybersecurity learning plan 🛡️, covering every nook and cranny of cybersecurity, from basic certs to advanced hacks. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  **awesome-mac** is seriously a treasure trove for Mac users 💎, gathering all sorts of premium software for your Mac. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **parlant** is a super user-friendly LLM agent 🤖 that streamlines large language model (LLM) applications, making them a breeze to deploy and use. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** is a cool open-source AI productivity platform ✨, packing AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system all into one neat package. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** spilled the beans on the **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanism during his Stanford CS25 course 🧠. He pointed out that LLM reasoning happens by generating a sequence of **intermediate tokens** and dove into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** have some differing takes on AI safety 🤔. Li is all about **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligent AI running amok, suggesting we design **AI that genuinely cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
