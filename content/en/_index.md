### **Today's AI News** 🔥

1.  **Claude Code** 🧠 has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the **"AI brain atrophy"** issue caused by long-term AI dependence. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at critical steps, get hands-on with tasks, and truly master coding skills.

2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M** ✨ is an open-source, lightweight large language model with just 270M parameters. It shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like smartphones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up nimble, fast, and cost-effective AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** 🤯 has laid out an ambitious blueprint for OpenAI: investing trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even venturing into the **brain-computer interface** realm with plans to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI era, reminiscent of the internet bubble, with the GPT-5 launch signaling "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  **NewGen AI (新智元)** 🎉 is celebrating its 10th anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! They're actively recruiting for several AI-related roles, including lead AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join! [🔗 NewGen AI Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval** 📐, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It utilizes a three-tiered assessment protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) for multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6.  The **`build-your-own-x` GitHub Project** 🛠️ offers learning paths to construct various technologies from scratch, helping users master coding skills through hands-on practice. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7.  The **`90DaysOfCyberSecurity` Project** 🛡️ provides a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan, covering all aspects of cybersecurity, from basic certifications to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8.  The **`awesome-mac` Project** 🍎 curates a collection of advanced software for Mac, making it a treasure trove for Mac users. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9.  **`parlant`** 💬 is an LLM agent designed to simplify large language model (LLM) applications, making them easy to deploy and use. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **`magic`** 🪄 is an open-source AI productivity platform that integrates AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** 💡 delved into the **Large Language Model (LLM)** inference mechanism during his Stanford CS25 course, explaining that LLM reasoning is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens**, and discussing techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Geoffrey Hinton** ⚖️ hold differing views on AI safety. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about uncontrolled superintelligence, suggesting the design of **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
