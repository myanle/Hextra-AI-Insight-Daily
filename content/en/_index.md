### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** has just dropped a game-changing **"Learning Mode"**! It's here to tackle that pesky **"AI brain drain"** problem that crops up from relying too heavily on AI. Instead of just spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," prompting users to pause at crucial junctures and get hands-on with tasks. The goal? To truly nail those programming skills.

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is seriously impressive! Packing just 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some much larger models. Plus, its low-power footprint means you can run it on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3. **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out an absolutely *bonkers* grand vision for the company! We're talking trillions of dollars poured into building data centers, developing cutting-edge AI hardware, and even diving headfirst into **brain-computer interfaces**. The goal? To completely revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a pivotal AI era, kind of like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will be a moment akin to "the best of times, the worst of times."

5. Check out **Hi3DEval**! This is a super scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system, jointly launched by Shanghai AI Lab and several universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct a multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

The two core concepts driving these potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence."
