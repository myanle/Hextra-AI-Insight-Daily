### **Today's AI News**

1.  Claude Code has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the "AI brain atrophy" issue stemming from long-term AI dependence. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks themselves, thereby truly mastering coding skills. ✨

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's lightweight open-source large model, boasts only 270M parameters yet shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption allows it to run on devices like smartphones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building light, fast, and cost-effective AI applications. 🚀

3.  OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has unveiled OpenAI's grand vision: investing trillions into building data centers and developing new AI hardware, even venturing into **brain-computer interface (BCI)** technology, with plans to disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI period, akin to the internet bubble, and the launch of GPT-5 marks 'the best of times and the worst of times.' 🤯

4.  Xinzhiyuan is celebrating its 10th anniversary and the dawn of the ASI era! Xinzhiyuan is actively recruiting for various AI-related roles, including AI Industry Lead Writer, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) 🎉

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tiered evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material-theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Page](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard) 📊

7.  **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** provides a 90-day learning plan covering all aspects of cybersecurity, from basic certifications to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity) 🛡️

11. DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou elucidated the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** in his Stanford CS25 course, explaining that LLM inference is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and discussing techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk) 🧠

12. Fei-Fei Li and Geoffrey Hinton hold differing views on AI safety. Fei-Fei Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about superintelligence spiraling out of control, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humanity**. 'Goal misgeneralization' and 'instrumental convergence' are two core concepts defining potential AI risks. 🧐
