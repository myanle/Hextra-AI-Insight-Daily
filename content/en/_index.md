### **Today's AI News**

1. **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"**! This cool feature is all about tackling that dreaded "AI brain drain" from relying too much on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," nudging users to pause at key steps and actually get hands-on with tasks. The goal? To truly nail those programming skills. 🧠💡

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is quite the performer! 🚀 Even with only 270M parameters, it crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some way bigger models. Plus, its low-power mojo means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for whipping up lightweight, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. ✨

3. **OpenAI** CEO Altman just laid out a seriously ambitious roadmap for OpenAI! 🚀 We're talking trillions of dollars poured into building data centers and rolling out new AI hardware, with plans to even dive into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally shake up search and social media. He reckons we're in a crucial AI era, kinda like the internet bubble, and the GPT-5 launch is gonna mark "the best of times and the worst of times." 🤯

4. **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're also on the hunt for AI talent, with openings for AI industry report lead writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join the crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供) ✨

5. **Hi3DEval** is a new, super comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system cooked up by Shanghai AI Lab in partnership with several universities. 🧑‍🔬 It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to dive deep into 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. DeepMind's Chief Scientist, Denny Zhou, totally broke down the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** in his Stanford CS25 lecture. 🧑‍🏫 He pointed out that LLM inference happens by churning out a series of **intermediate tokens** and even got into cool techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. Fei-Fei Li and Hinton are on different pages when it comes to AI safety. 🧐 Fei-Fei Li is focused on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton's worried about superintelligent AI going rogue and suggests we build **AI that actually cares about humans**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts behind AI's potential risks.
