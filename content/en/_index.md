### **Today's AI News**

1. 🧠 **Claude Code** just rolled out its brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** from over-relying on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it's like having a coding personal trainer, guiding users to pause at critical steps and complete tasks themselves to truly master coding skills. 💪

2. 🚀 **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, boasts only 270M parameters but still crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models! Its low-power vibe means it can run on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building nimble, speedy, and budget-friendly AI apps. ✨

3. 💸 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just painted a seriously grand picture for the company's future: trillions of dollars invested in data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally disrupt search and social media. He reckons we're in a critical AI phase, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 launch is gonna mark "the best of times and the worst of times." 🤯

4. 🎉🥳 **Xinzhiyuan** is throwing a massive 10th-anniversary bash, kicking off the ASI era! They're also on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their crew! ✨ [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

It rocks a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to dive deep into the overall shape, local structure, and material realism of 3D models. Pretty neat, huh? 👀 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
