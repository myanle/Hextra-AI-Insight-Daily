### Today's AI News

1.  **Claude Code** has launched a new "Learning Mode" 🤔 to tackle the "AI brain atrophy" issue caused by long-term reliance on AI. Instead of directly providing answers, this mode acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at critical steps and complete tasks manually, thereby truly mastering coding skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, has a mere 270M parameters but excels in instruction following and text structuring tasks, outperforming even some larger models. Its low-power consumption allows it to run on devices like mobile phones or Raspberry Pis, making it ideal for building light, fast, and cost-effective AI applications. 🚀

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has outlined OpenAI's ambitious blueprint 🌌: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers and develop new AI hardware, even venturing into brain-computer interfaces with plans to revolutionize search and social media. He believes we are at a critical AI juncture, akin to the internet bubble, with the release of GPT-5 marking "the best of times, the worst of times."

5.  **Hi3DEval** ✨ is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. It employs a three-tiered assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** 🤝 hold differing views on AI safety. Li focuses on design, governance, and values, while Hinton is concerned about superintelligent AI losing control and suggests designing AI that cares for humanity. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts for potential AI risks.
