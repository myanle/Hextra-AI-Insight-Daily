### Today's AI Buzz

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** 💡 designed to combat the dreaded "AI-induced skill atrophy" that comes with relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about giving you direct answers; it's more like your personal coding "fitness coach," guiding you to pause at crucial steps and actually *do* the tasks yourself, so you genuinely master those programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M** 🚀, Google's open-source lightweight large model, boasts a tiny 270M parameters but absolutely crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models! Its low-power design means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up lightweight, super fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out OpenAI's epic blueprint 🤯: we're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers, developing new AI hardware, and even diving into **brain-computer interface** tech, with plans to totally disrupt search and social media. Altman believes we're smack dab in the middle of a critical, "internet bubble-like" era for AI, and the upcoming GPT-5 release? That's just the start of "the best of times and the worst of times."

The core concepts behind these potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence." Deep stuff!
