### **Today's AI Buzz**

1. **Claude Code** just launched its brand-new **"Learning Mode,"** which is all about tackling the infamous **"AI Brain Atrophy"** problem that crops up from relying on AI too much. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding "gym trainer," guiding you to pause at crucial steps, get your hands dirty, and truly nail those programming skills. 💪

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is a total powerhouse despite its tiny 270M parameters! It totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power mojo means it can run on devices like your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. 🚀

3. **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out a super ambitious blueprint for the company: think trillions of dollars pumped into building data centers and developing next-gen AI hardware. They're even eyeing the **brain-computer interface** arena, aiming to completely shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in a critical AI period, kinda like the internet bubble, with GPT-5's launch signaling "the best of times, and the worst of times." 🤯

4. **Xinzhiyuan** just celebrated its 10th anniversary and the kickoff of the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI Industry Chief Writers, Senior Video Editors, Senior Editors/Editors, and Editor Interns. So, if you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join the crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5. **Hi3DEval**, a collaboration between Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, is a seriously scientific, comprehensive, and rigorous evaluation system for 3D generation quality. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to run multi-dimensional analyses on a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and material realism. Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

6. **build-your-own-x**, a fantastic GitHub project, offers learning paths for building all sorts of tech from scratch. It's designed to help users really nail down their programming skills through hands-on practice. Get building! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x)

7. **90DaysOfCyberSecurity** is an awesome 90-day learning program that covers *all* things cybersecurity, from basic certifications to advanced techniques. Ready to level up? [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

8. **awesome-mac** is a treasure trove for Mac users, packed with a collection of various high-end software for macOS. Your Mac just got an upgrade! [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac)

9. **parlant** is an LLM agent that really simplifies large language model (LLM) applications. It's super easy to deploy and use, making your LLM life a breeze. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/emcie-co/parlant)

10. **magic** is an open-source AI productivity platform that brings together AI agents, a workflow engine, IM, and an online collaborative office system. Talk about an all-in-one solution! ✨ [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/dtyq/magic)

11. **DeepMind's Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** broke down the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** in a Stanford CS25 course. He pointed out that LLM inference happens by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and dove into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. Fascinating stuff! [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** have some diverging views on AI safety. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence going rogue and suggests designing **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are the two core concepts behind AI's potential risks. A lot to chew on there! 🤔
