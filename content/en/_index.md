1.  💡🚫🤖 **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"** designed to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain drain"** that comes from leaning too heavily on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; it's more like a programming "personal trainer" that nudges users to pause at crucial points, get hands-on, and really nail those coding skills.

2.  🚀📱 **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is a lightweight, open-source large language model, rocking a mere 270M parameters! But don't let its size fool you; it absolutely crushes it in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some bigger models. Thanks to its low power consumption, Gemma 3 270M can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building fast, light, and cheap AI apps.
