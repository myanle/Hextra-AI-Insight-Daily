### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle that pesky **"AI brain drain"** problem caused by relying too much on AI. This mode isn't about spoon-feeding answers; instead, it acts like your personal coding "gym coach," guiding users to pause at key steps and get hands-on with tasks to truly master their programming skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source lightweight large model, is turning heads! Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power footprint means you can run it on your phone or even a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building those super-fast, low-cost AI apps.

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman just laid out a mind-blowing roadmap for the company, talking trillions in investments for data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**! OpenAI is looking to totally shake up search and social media. Altman believes we're in a critical AI moment, akin to the dot-com bubble, with the GPT-5 launch signaling "the best of times and the worst of times."

4.  Big news! **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary, marking the dawn of the ASI era! They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join their team! 5.  Get ready for **Hi3DEval**! This spiffy new 3D generation quality assessment system, launched by the Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with several universities, is way more scientific and comprehensive. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol covering object, component, and material themes to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** dropped some serious knowledge in Stanford's CS25 course, explaining the inference mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. 12. When it comes to AI safety, **Li Feifei and Hinton** have some differing views. Li Feifei emphasizes **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more worried about superintelligence running wild, suggesting we design **AI that cares about humans**. The two core concepts behind potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence."
