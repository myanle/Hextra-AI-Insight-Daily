Instead of spoon-feeding answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, pausing at key steps and nudging you to get your hands dirty and really nail those programming skills. No more AI dependency, just pure skill gains! 💪🧠

2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is a seriously cool, open-source lightweight large model, rocking just 270M parameters! But don't let its size fool ya – it totally crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much bigger models. Its low-power vibe means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for whipping up light, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps. Pretty neat, right? ✨📱

Altman reckons we're smack-dab in the middle of a critical AI boom, kinda like the early internet bubble, with GPT-5's release signaling "the best of times, and the worst of times." Buckle up! 🤯💸
