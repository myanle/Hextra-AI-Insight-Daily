### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Grok AI**, owned by Elon Musk's xAI, just dropped its "Imagine" feature, letting users whip up 30-second AI short videos with a single click! 🚀 This puts Grok AI directly in the ring with Google's Veo 3 AI video generation tool. Almost instantly, Grok-generated videos of all sorts exploded across the X platform, showing off Grok's insane **multimodal generation capabilities**. Tech bigwigs and regular folks alike are buzzing about how **fast and controllable** it is, hinting at a total game-changer for short video creation. Of course, it also sparks some big questions about copyright and ethics in AI-generated content 🤔. Ultimately, this Grok vs. Veo 3 showdown is heating up the AI video generation space, pushing the tech to new heights and making it more mainstream.

2.  **Xinzhiyuan** is hitting its big ten-year mark on September 7, 2025! 🎉 For a whole decade, this platform has brought together millions of users, experts, and partners, all witnessing AI's biggest breakthroughs. To celebrate this milestone and dive headfirst into the **ASI (Artificial Superintelligence)** era, **Xinzhiyuan** is rolling out the red carpet and inviting all you AI enthusiasts to join the party! 🥳 ![Xinzhiyuan 10th Anniversary Poster](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/sz_mmbiz_jpg/UicQ7HgWiaUb0ibmX943NR2Z5sPhTTQQn1Vcf6iaAXnzPev5NIb47hicJOr1YxmwzEtFThRpbE9tjcFSJnIuia8iaj4Nw/0?wx_fmt=jpeg)

3.  **DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis** recently dropped a wild idea: AI can totally model *anything* that's evolved in nature – and do it efficiently! 🔬 He reckons **classical learning algorithms** are great at sniffing out and modeling natural patterns, mainly because natural selection is, well, a super efficient search engine itself. Hassabis even suggests that **AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)** will be built on good old classical computer neural networks, ready to tackle tons of natural, evolved systems. This isn't just theory, folks; it means AI could make huge leaps in stuff like **protein folding prediction, fluid dynamics, and video generation**, maybe even helping scientists crack gnarly puzzles like the **P=NP problem**! 🤯 Oh, and he also gave a nod to AI's future in game development. [Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-HzgcbRXUK8)

4.  **Alibaba Cloud's newly released Qwen-MT translation model** is proving to be a real powerhouse – fast and accurate! 🗣️🌎 This bad boy handles over 92 languages for mutual translation and comes packed with features like **terminology intervention, domain hints, and memory banks**. Quantum Bit put it through its paces, and the model delivered lightning-fast responses and super high accuracy on re-translations, even tweaking styles based on user commands. While it might stumble a bit with super abstract stuff like bilingual puns (can't blame it, really), its overall performance is mind-blowing and totally **cost-effective**. 🤯 [Project Repository](https://bailian.console.aliyun.com/?tab=model#/model-market/detail/qwen-mt-turbo) ![Qwen-MT Model Interface](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/YicUhk5aAGtDf0s8AFS4Bia2cRyIARibFVicZumvhSMHqwSe2rfl1af8RGp2e4y7rap8snjCmgUTRBnJIolgeVoGRQ/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

5.  **360 Nano AI** just got a mind-blowing upgrade, transforming into a "multi-agent swarm" system that hits a jaw-dropping L4 level! 🎉 This means AI is ditching the solo act and teaming up like a hive of super-smart bees 🐝. We're talking over 50,000 L3-level agents working together, crushing complex tasks in over 1000 steps – like churning out a 10-minute, cinema-quality epic in just 20 minutes! 🎬 This is a massive leap for multi-agent collaboration, proving that sometimes, 1+1 really does equal way more than 2. And guess what? It's available for download right now! 🚀 ![Image: WeChat Screenshot_20250802180058.png](https://upload.chinaz.com/2025/0802/6388975452753743607088536.png)

6.  **GPT-5** is having a seriously tough time in development – a true "difficult birth" plagued by **talent drain, technical bottlenecks, and a wicked "reasoning curse."** 🤔 The journey has been anything but smooth, with core team members getting poached, internal chaos reigning, and progress stalling, even grinding to a halt at times! 🚧 Even with a whopping $8.3 billion in funding, GPT-5 hasn't hit that groundbreaking leap in performance we saw from GPT-3 to GPT-4. The culprits? High-quality data drying up, pre-training hitting a wall, and models losing their oomph when morphed from powerful reasoning engines into user-friendly chat versions. All these roadblocks are seriously stunting its growth. 🤦‍♀️

Right now, it's limited to the first 5 seconds of video and has some resolution caps, so getting your prompt game strong is key. You can fine-tune your results step-by-step using its "chat mode." 💬

18. **GPT-5** has a pretty cool "surprise" up its sleeve: OpenAI's Superalignment team cooked up a "prover-verifier" model. 🤔 This clever tech uses a smaller "verifier" model to check the reasoning of a much larger "prover" model. What's the big deal? It means even when future AI models become way smarter than us, we'll still have a "weaker but trustworthy" model to keep them in check and under control. 🛡️ [Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.13692)
