### Today's AI News

1. **Claude Code** just dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! This mode tackles the sneaky **"AI brain atrophy"** issue that pops up from over-reliance on AI. Instead of spoon-feeding answers, it acts like your personal coding "fitness coach," nudging you to pause at key steps, get hands-on with tasks, and genuinely master your programming skills.

2. **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large model, is quite the performer! Packing only 270M parameters, it excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some beefier models. Its low-power design means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI apps.

3. **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a seriously ambitious roadmap! We're talking trillions of dollars poured into data centers and next-gen AI hardware, even diving into the **brain-computer interface** arena, all aiming to shake up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI moment, much like the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's launch signaling "the best of times and the worst of times."

4. **Xinzhiyuan's** 10th-anniversary bash is kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're on the hunt for AI-loving talent, including AI industry report writers, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. Come join the crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](link_not_provided)

5. **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by Shanghai AI Lab in collaboration with multiple universities. This system utilizes a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—for multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall shape, local structure, and material authenticity. Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton frets about superintelligence running wild, advocating for the creation of **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are flagged as two core concepts for AI's potential risks.
