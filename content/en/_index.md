### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** just dropped a fresh **"Learning Mode"**! 🧑‍💻 Its mission? To tackle that pesky **"AI-induced skill atrophy"** that comes from leaning too hard on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like your personal programming fitness coach, guiding you to pause at key steps, get your hands dirty, and truly nail those coding skills. 💪

2.  **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is out there! 🚀 This open-source, lightweight large model packs a punch with just 270M parameters. It absolutely crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some heftier models. Plus, its low-power footprint means you can run it on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building speedy, cost-effective AI apps. 📱

3.  **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** just laid out OpenAI's epic blueprint! 🌠 We're talking trillions of dollars poured into data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**, all aimed at shaking up search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a pivotal AI moment, reminiscent of the dot-com bubble, and GPT-5's launch signals a "best of times, worst of times" scenario. 🤯

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a collaboration between Shanghai AI Lab and several universities, just dropped! 🌟 It's a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system. This bad boy uses a three-tiered evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to dive deep into a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism from multiple angles. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

11. **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** recently broke down the reasoning mechanisms of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** in a Stanford CS25 course. 🧠 He highlighted that LLM inference is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and delved into techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. **Li Feifei and Geoffrey Hinton** are on different pages when it comes to AI safety. 🤔 Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is more concerned about uncontrolled superintelligence, suggesting we build **human-caring AI**. The two core concepts for potential AI risks? "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence."
