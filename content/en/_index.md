### **Today's AI Buzz**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a fresh **"Learning Mode"** aimed at tackling the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** that comes from over-reliance on AI. Instead of just handing out answers, this mode acts like your personal coding gym buddy, pausing at crucial steps and nudging you to actually get your hands dirty and master those programming skills.
2.  Google just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**, a super lightweight large language model. This tiny titan, with only 270M parameters, totally rocks at tasks like **instruction following** and **text structuring**, even outperforming some much bigger models! Its low power consumption means you can run it on your phone or a Raspberry Pi, making it perfect for building fast, cheap, and compact AI applications. How cool is that?
3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled a seriously ambitious roadmap for OpenAI. We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers and new AI hardware, even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**. The goal? To totally disrupt search and social media as we know it! Altman believes we're in a pivotal, "internet bubble"-like era for AI, and the launch of GPT-5 really signals that it's "the best of times, and the worst of times." Big, big moves coming!
