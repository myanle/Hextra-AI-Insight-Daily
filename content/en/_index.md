### **Today's AI Buzz** 🚀

1.  **Claude Code** just rolled out its brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠 This mode tackles the notorious "AI brain drain" problem caused by over-reliance on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they truly master their coding skills.

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, Google's open-source, lightweight large language model, is quite the performer! ⚡ Weighing in at just 270M parameters, it excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outshining some much larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  **OpenAI CEO Altman** just laid out OpenAI's super ambitious blueprint! 🤯 We're talking trillions of dollars for data centers, new AI hardware development, and even diving into **brain-computer interfaces** to totally disrupt search and social media. Altman reckons we're in a critical AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the GPT-5 launch signals "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  It's party time! 🥳 **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're also on the hunt for talent, with openings for an AI Industry Chief Reporter, Senior Video Editor, Senior Editor/Editor, and Editorial Interns. 5.  Say hello to **Hi3DEval**! ✨ This more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system was launched by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in collaboration with multiple universities. It uses a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material themes—to analyze 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity across multiple dimensions.
