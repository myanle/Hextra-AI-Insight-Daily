### **Today's AI News** ✨

1.  **Claude Code** has dropped a brand-new **"Learning Mode"**! 🧠 This cool feature aims to tackle the dreaded **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by over-reliance on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, it acts like your personal coding "gym coach," guiding users to pause at key steps, get their hands dirty, and truly master programming skills. No more AI crutches! 💪

2.  **Google** just open-sourced **Gemma 3 270M**! 🚀 This lightweight large language model, with only 270M parameters, is a real powerhouse. It absolutely crushes **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Plus, its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building fast, affordable, and nimble AI apps. 💡

3.  **OpenAI's** CEO, Sam Altman, is totally going for broke with a mind-blowing vision for OpenAI! 🌌 We're talking trillions of dollars invested in building data centers, developing new AI hardware, and even diving into **brain-computer interfaces**. His goal? To completely disrupt search and social media. Altman reckons we're smack dab in the middle of a crucial AI era, kinda like the dot-com bubble, and the upcoming GPT-5 launch will mark "the best of times, the worst of times." 🤯

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its 10th anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! 🎉 They're on the hunt for AI industry reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns. If you're an AI enthusiast, they'd love for you to join their crew! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

Pretty neat, huh? [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)
