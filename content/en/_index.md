### **Today's AI News** 🔥

1.  🧠 **Claude Code** has launched a brand new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the issue of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by long-term reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand you answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps, get hands-on with tasks, and genuinely master coding skills.

2.  🚀 **Google's Gemma 3 270M** is an open-source, lightweight large language model with a mere 270M parameters. Yet, it shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some much larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building lightweight, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  🌌 **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has unveiled an ambitious blueprint for the company, outlining plans to invest trillions of dollars into building data centers and developing new AI hardware. They're even looking to jump into the **brain-computer interface** space, aiming to revolutionize search and social media. Altman believes we're in a pivotal AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, with the launch of GPT-5 marking "the best of times, and the worst of times."

4.  🎉 **New Intelligence** is kicking off its tenth-anniversary celebration and the dawn of the AGI era! They're actively recruiting for various AI-related positions, including lead AI industry reporter, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they invite you to join their team! [🔗 New Intelligence Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  📊 **Hi3DEval** is a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system developed by Shanghai AI Laboratory in collaboration with multiple universities. This system employs a three-tier evaluation protocol (object-level, component-level, and material theme) to conduct multi-dimensional analysis on the overall form, local structure, and material authenticity of 3D models. [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

7.  🛡️ **`90DaysOfCyberSecurity`** provides a 90-day cybersecurity learning plan that covers all aspects of cybersecurity, from basic certifications to advanced techniques. [🔗 Project Repository](https://github.com/farhanashrafdev/90DaysOfCyberSecurity)

11. 🤯 **DeepMind Chief Scientist Denny Zhou** explained the inference mechanism of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** in his Stanford CS25 course, pointing out that LLM reasoning is achieved by generating a series of **intermediate tokens** and discussing techniques like reinforcement learning fine-tuning. [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebnX5Ur1hBk)

12. 🤖⚠️ **Fei-Fei Li and Hinton** hold differing views on AI safety issues. Li focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton is concerned about superintelligence spiraling out of control, advocating for the creation of **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are identified as two core concepts of potential AI risks.
