### **Today's AI News**

1.  🧠✨ **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** to tackle the issue of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by long-term reliance on AI. Instead of just spitting out answers, this mode acts like a coding "personal trainer," guiding users to pause at key steps and complete tasks hands-on, ensuring they truly master programming skills.

2.  🐅💨 **Gemma 3 270M**, an open-source lightweight large language model from Google, boasts a mere 270M parameters yet shines in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, outperforming even some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, perfect for building lean, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications.

3.  🌌💰 **OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** has unfurled an ambitious blueprint for OpenAI: investing trillions of dollars to build data centers, develop new AI hardware, and even dive into **brain-computer interfaces**, aiming to disrupt search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI era, reminiscent of the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release signaling "the best of times, the worst of times."

4.  🥳🗓️ **Xinzhiyuan's 10th-anniversary celebration** has kicked off, marking the dawn of the ASI era! Xinzhiyuan is actively recruiting for various roles, including AI industry reporter, senior video editor, senior editor/editor, and editorial intern. If you're passionate about AI, they'd love for you to join! [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Information](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  🔬📊 **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality assessment system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in collaboration with several universities. It employs a three-tier evaluation protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of 3D models' overall form, local structure, and material authenticity. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are two core concepts behind AI's potential risks.
