### **Today's AI News**

1.  **Claude Code** has rolled out a brand-new **"Learning Mode"** designed to tackle the problem of **"AI brain atrophy"** caused by over-reliance on AI. This mode doesn't just hand out answers; instead, it acts like a programming "fitness coach," guiding users to pause at crucial steps and complete tasks themselves, ensuring they truly master programming skills. 💪

2.  **Gemma 3 270M**, a lightweight large language model open-sourced by Google, is making waves! With only 270M parameters, it excels in **instruction following** and **text structuring** tasks, even outperforming some larger models. Its low-power consumption means it can run on devices like phones or Raspberry Pis, making it perfect for building nimble, fast, and budget-friendly AI applications. ✨

3.  **OpenAI** CEO Sam Altman has laid out an ambitious blueprint for the company, envisioning multi-trillion-dollar investments in data centers and new AI hardware, even branching into **brain-computer interfaces** to shake up search and social media. He believes we're in a critical AI era, akin to the dot-com bubble, with GPT-5's release marking "the best of times and the worst of times." 🤯

4.  **Xinzhiyuan** is celebrating its tenth anniversary and kicking off the ASI era! They're currently on the hunt for AI industry lead reporters, senior video editors, senior editors/editors, and editorial interns, inviting all AI enthusiasts to join their squad! 🙌 [🔗 Xinzhiyuan Recruitment Info](此处应插入新智元官网招聘链接，原文未提供)

5.  **Hi3DEval**, a more scientific and comprehensive 3D generation quality evaluation system, has been launched by the Shanghai AI Laboratory in collaboration with several universities. It uses a three-tier assessment protocol—object-level, component-level, and material theme—to conduct multi-dimensional analysis of a 3D model's overall form, local structure, and material realism. 🔬 [🔗 Hi3DEval Project Homepage](https://zyh482.github.io/Hi3DEval/) [🔗 3D Generation Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/3DTopia/3DGen-Leaderboard)

Li Feifei focuses on **design, governance, and values**, while Hinton expresses concerns about uncontrolled superintelligence, advocating for the design of **AI that cares about humanity**. "Goal misgeneralization" and "instrumental convergence" are highlighted as two core concepts of potential AI risks. ⚖️
