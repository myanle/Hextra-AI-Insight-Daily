3. **DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis thinks AI can model anything evolved super efficiently!** He's floated a bold idea: anything that's naturally evolved can be efficiently modeled by AI. Hassabis reckons **classical learning algorithms** are super effective at spotting and modeling patterns in nature, largely thanks to natural selection itself being a powerhouse search process. He further points out that building **AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)** will hinge on neural networks running on classical computers, capable of efficiently tackling tons of evolved systems in nature. This means AI could totally revolutionize fields like **protein folding prediction, fluid modeling, and video generation** in the future, and might even help scientists crack tough nuts like the **P=NP problem**. He also spoke about the cool prospects for AI in game development. 🧠💡 [▶️ Video Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-HzgcbRXUK8)
