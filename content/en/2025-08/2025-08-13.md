### **Today's AI News**

1.  🎉 **Chinese Team Achieves Breakthrough in Diffusion Model Research**: Diffusion models, according to a study led by a Chinese team, are three times more powerful than traditional autoregressive models when it comes to data learning. These models process information bidirectionally, leading to higher learning efficiency, and even with overfitting, their performance doesn't significantly drop. This research trained a 1-billion parameter diffusion model with 1 billion tokens, achieving good results across various benchmarks. ![image](https://upload.chinaz.com/2025/0813/6389070449433588676837577.png)
