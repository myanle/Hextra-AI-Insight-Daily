### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're big fans of Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and squirrel away some info. These tracking goodies include beacons, tags, and scripts, all to gather data, track stuff, and make our Service even better. The technologies we use might include:

*   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: These little files chill out on your device. You can totally tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's coming. But if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service might still use 'em.
*   **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These let the Company, for example, count folks who've checked out those pages or opened an email, and help with other cool website stats (e.g., seeing how popular a section is and making sure our systems are solid).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" types. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

*   **Necessary / Essential Cookies** 🤩
    *   **Type**: Session Cookies
    *   **Administered by**: Us
    *   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for making sure you get the services available through the Website and can use its cool features. They help us confirm who you are and stop any funny business with user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use them to get those services to you.

*   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies** 👍
    *   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    *   **Administered by**: Us
    *   **Purpose**: These Cookies just check if you've given us the thumbs-up for using Cookies on the Website.

*   **Functionality Cookies** 🧠
    *   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    *   **Administered by**: Us
    *   **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember your choices when you're using the Website, like your login deets or what language you prefer. They're all about giving you a smoother, more personalized experience so you don't have to keep typing in your preferences every single time you visit.

Want the full scoop on our Cookies and your options? Hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy.
