### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
Alright, let's talk tracking! We use Cookies and similar tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and stash away some info. We're talking beacons, tags, and scripts – all used to gather and track data, helping us spruce up and analyze our Service. Here's a peek at what we use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that lands on your device. You're totally in charge: tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's coming your way. Just a fair warning though, if you say 'no thanks' to Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've specifically told your browser to block 'em, chances are our Service will be using Cookies. Just sayin'!
- **Web Beacons**: Look out for these tiny electronic files, sometimes called clear gifs or pixel tags, hanging out in some parts of our Service and emails. They're pretty slick: they let us count who's visited pages or opened emails, and give us other cool website stats, like how popular a section is or if our systems are running smoothly. Think of them as mini-trackers doing their thing! 👀

So, Cookies come in two flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session.' Persistent Cookies are the ones that stick around on your computer or phone even after you've logged off. Session Cookies, though? Poof! They're gone as soon as you shut down your browser. Talk about a quick exit! 👋

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies** <br> - **Type**: Session Cookies <br> - **Managed by**: Us <br> - **Purpose**: These Cookies are an absolute must for us to give you the services available on our Website and to let you use all its cool features. They're like the bouncers for user authentication and help prevent any shady business with accounts. Seriously, without these, the services you're asking for just wouldn't work, and we *only* use them for that! ✅

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies** <br> - **Type**: Persistent Cookies <br> - **Managed by**: Us <br> - **Purpose**: These little guys simply remember if you've already given us the thumbs-up to use Cookies on the Website. No need to ask you twice! 👍

- **Functionality Cookies** <br> - **Type**: Persistent Cookies <br> - **Managed by**: Us <br> - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super handy because they help us recall your choices while you're on the Website – things like your login deets or what language you prefer. The whole point is to give you a smoother, more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep typing in your preferences every single time. Convenience FTW! 🚀

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what options you have? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or peep the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy. All the deets are there! 📖
