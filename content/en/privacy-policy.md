### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking tech we're talking about includes beacons, tags, and scripts. We use them to collect and track info, plus to supercharge and analyze our Service. This tech might include:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A **Cookie** is just a tiny file that gets popped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't play nice with Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
-   **Web Beacons**: Parts of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called **Web Beacons** (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These bad boys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other related website stats, like seeing how popular a certain section is and checking the integrity of systems and servers.

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its features. They help verify users and put a stop to dodgy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies simply check if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies help us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more juicy details about the Cookies we use and your options concerning them, feel free to swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
