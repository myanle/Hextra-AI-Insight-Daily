### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and to stash away some info. The tracking technologies we roll with include things like Beacons, Tags, and Scripts. These are all about gathering and tracking information, plus helping us spruce up and analyze our Service. So, what tech might we use? It could be:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: **A Cookie?** It's just a tiny file chilling on your Device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But heads up: if you don't play nice with Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say 'no thanks' to Cookies, our Service might just be using them.
-   **Web Beacons**: **Web Beacons?** Some bits of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome little files let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other website stats (like checking out how popular a certain section is and making sure our systems and servers are all good).

Just so you know, Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. **Persistent Cookies** stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while **Session Cookies** vanish into thin air as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: **These Cookies?** They're absolutely crucial for hooking you up with the services available through the Website and letting you rock some of its features. They're super helpful for verifying users and putting a stop to any dodgy account use. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't fly, and we only use them to bring those services to you.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: **These Cookies** are all about remembering if users have given their 'okay' to using Cookies on the Website.

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: **Functionality Cookies** let us remember the choices you make when you're cruising around the Website, like keeping your login deets or language preferences in mind. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, saving you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you visit the Website.

For more deets on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
