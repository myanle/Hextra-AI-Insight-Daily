### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're big fans of **Cookies** and similar tracking tech! We use 'em to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain bits of info. These tracking tools, like beacons, tags, and scripts, help us gather and track information, plus continuously improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include: 🛠️

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: So, a **Cookie** is just a tiny file that lands on your Device. You're the boss: you can tell your browser to say 'nope!' to all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. Heads up, though! If you don't play nice with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work as smoothly for you. Unless you've tweaked your browser to reject 'em, our Service will likely be using Cookies. Just a friendly warning! 🚨
- **Web Beacons**: Fancy name, simple idea! Certain parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called **web beacons** (sometimes known as clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for example, count how many users have visited those pages or opened an email. They're also super handy for other website stats, like checking how popular a section is or making sure our system and servers are all good to go. Stealthy but useful! 👻

**Cookies** come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Think of it like this: **Persistent Cookies** stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've logged off, while **Session Cookies** vanish into thin air as soon as you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We totally use both **Session** and **Persistent Cookies** for the reasons we're about to lay out below. We've got our reasons! 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These **Cookies** are absolutely crucial for getting you the services available on our Website and letting you use all its cool features. They're like the bouncers at the door, helping to authenticate users and kick out any fraudulent account shenanigans. Seriously, without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just can't happen. And that's why we use them – to make sure you get what you came for! ✅

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These **Cookies** are here to remember if you, our awesome user, have given a thumbs-up to using cookies on the Website. It's all about remembering your choices! 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These **Cookies** are super helpful because they let us remember your choices when you're cruising around the Website – stuff like your login deets or what language you prefer. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep typing in your preferences every single time. Convenience, redefined! ✨

Wanna dive deeper into the Cookies we use and your options regarding them? No prob! Just swing by our **Cookies Policy** or check out the **Cookies section** of our Privacy Policy. All the deets are there! 📖
