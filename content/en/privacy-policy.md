### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking technologies we deploy include beacons, tags, and scripts. They're all about collecting and tracking info, plus helping us spruce up and analyze our Service. Here's a peek at what we use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies:** Cookies, sometimes called Browser Cookies, are those tiny files plopped onto your device by websites. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons:** Web Beacons are those small electronic files (you might also hear them called clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs) that some parts of our Service and our emails might contain. These cool little files let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They also get used for other related website stats, like tracking how popular a section is or verifying system and server integrity. Pretty neat, huh?

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline. Session Cookies, on the other hand, vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof!

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type:** Session Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are super important for dishing out services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help us verify users and stop any funky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't work, and we only use 'em to give you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given the thumbs up to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are the ones that let us remember your choices when you're cruising the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website. Sweet!

For more deets on the Cookies we use and your choices about 'em, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
