### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
Cookies and similar tracking technologies are our go-to for tracking activity on our Service and holding onto certain information. These tracking tech goodies — including beacons, tags, and scripts — help us collect and track info, and hey, they also boost and analyze our Service! The specific tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: These are just tiny files that hang out on your Device. You're totally in control: tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's sent. But, heads-up, if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've adjusted your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service will likely use 'em. 🍪
- **Web Beacons**: Heads-up! Some parts of our Service and our emails might secretly contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little digital trackers let the Company, for instance, count users who've visited certain pages or opened an email. They're also super useful for other website statistics, like logging how popular a section is and making sure our system and server integrity is A-OK. ✨

Cookies come in two main flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang out on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline. Session Cookies, however, peace out the moment you close your web browser. 👋

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's the scoop on why:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely essential! They're crucial for us to deliver the services available through the Website and empower you to use its features. Plus, they're super helpful for authenticating users and shutting down any fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these specific Cookies, the services you're asking for simply couldn't be provided. We literally only use these to get you those services. Phew!

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies have one job: to figure out if you've given us the thumbs-up to use cookies on the Website. ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are the "memory keepers"! They let us remember the choices you make when you're chilling on the Website – think keeping your login details or language preference in mind. The main goal? To give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to punch in your preferences every single time you visit. How convenient! 🥳

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options? Just scoot on over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. It's all there!
