### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
**We** use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and to store certain information. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track information, and to improve and analyze our Service. The technologies we might use include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file popped onto your Device. **You** can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to let you know when one is being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service just might use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). **These files** allow the Company, for example, to count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website statistics (e.g., figuring out how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity).

**Cookies** can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

**We** use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes listed below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: **These Cookies** are crucial for giving you the services available through the Website and enabling you to use some of its features. They help authenticate users and stop fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: **These Cookies** are used to figure out if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: **These Cookies** let us remember choices you make when you use the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personal experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more information about the Cookies we use and your options regarding Cookies, hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
