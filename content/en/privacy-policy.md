### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activity on our Service and stash away certain information. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, which help us collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one is being sent. But hey, if you don't take Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service will probably use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome little files let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like tracking the popularity of a certain section and making sure the system and server integrity are solid).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies get zapped as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for hooking you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to make sure you get those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to check if users have said "yes" to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more deets on the Cookies we use and your options concerning them, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
