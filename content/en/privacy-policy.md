### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. 🕵️‍♂️ These tracking tools include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track information, helping us level up and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that gets placed on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, if you don't play nice with Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say 'no thanks' to Cookies, our Service might just use 'em. 🍪
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like, seeing how popular a certain section is and checking system and server integrity). Pretty clever, huh? 💡

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. When you're offline, Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device. Session Cookies, though, vanish into thin air as soon as you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for hookin' you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help us authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you asked for just can't happen, and we only use 'em to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to check if users have given us the green light to use Cookies on the Website. ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you visit the Website. Super handy! ✨

Want more deets about the Cookies we use and what choices you've got? Just pop over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. It's all there! 📖
