### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and cool tracking tech (think tiny digital breadcrumbs 🍪) to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away some info. These tracking goodies include beacons, tags, and scripts, which help us gather and track info, plus make our Service even better and analyze how it's being used. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies:** A Cookie is a small file dropped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've set your browser to refuse Cookies, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons:** Certain spots in our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These nifty files let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other website stats (like seeing how popular a section is and making sure systems and servers are all good).

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones stick around on your computer or phone even when you're offline. Session Cookies? They vanish the moment you close your browser. Poof! 💨

We rock both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type:** Session Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are super crucial for making sure you can actually use the services on our Website and get all those cool features working. They're like bouncers for user identity and help stop any dodgy account use. No these Cookies, no services you asked for – plain and simple! We only use 'em for that.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies identify if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These clever Cookies let us remember your choices when you're hanging out on the Website, like your login deets or what language you prefer. The whole point? To give you a super personalized experience so you don't have to keep typing in your preferences every single time. How handy is that? ✨

Want more deets on our Cookies and what you can do about 'em? Pop over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy. 🍪
