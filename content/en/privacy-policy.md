### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪

We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash some info. These tracking goodies include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, helping us level up and analyze our Service. What kind of tech are we talking about? Well:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file chilling on your device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, heads up, if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service might still use 'em.
-   **Web Beacons**: Think of these as tiny electronic files (also called clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) hidden in some parts of our Service and emails. They help the Company, for example, count folks who've visited certain pages or opened an email. Plus, they're super handy for other website stats, like tracking a section's popularity or making sure our systems are solid.

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones stick around on your computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish as soon as you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We juggle both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Managed by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These cookies are like the backbone of our Website. They're absolutely crucial for delivering the services you see and letting you use all its cool features. They're also key for authenticating users and keeping those pesky fraudulent accounts at bay. Seriously, without these, we couldn't even give you the services you asked for, so we ONLY use them for that! 💪

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Managed by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These smart cookies just check if you've already given a thumbs-up to our use of cookies on the Website. Simple!

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Managed by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These handy cookies help us remember your choices when you're using the Website – think login deets or your favorite language. 🌐 The whole point is to give you a super personalized experience, so you don't have to keep typing in your preferences every single time. Easy peasy!

Wanna know more about the cookies we use and your options? Just hop over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. It's all there!
