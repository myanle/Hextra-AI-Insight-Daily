### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and other neat tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away some info. These tracking goodies include beacons, tags, and scripts – they're all about collecting and tracking data to make our Service better and help us understand how it's being used. The tech we deploy might include: 🔍

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies)**: These are tiny files dropped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But a fair warning: if you don't say yes to Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These gems let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email, and they help with other website stats (like checking how popular a section is or making sure our systems and servers are all good).

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones stick around on your computer or mobile even when you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We're using both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for dishing out the services available to you via the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're like bouncers, helping to verify users and stop any shady business with user accounts. Without these, the services you're asking for just can't happen, and we only use 'em to make sure you get those services. 💪

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are like a little memory jogger, letting us know if users have given the thumbs up to using cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty smart! They let us remember your choices when you're cruising around the Website, like keeping your login deets or language preference in mind. The whole point is to give you a smoother, more personalized experience, so you don't have to punch in your preferences every single time you visit. ✨

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what your options are? Head over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. You'll get the full scoop! 📖
