### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activity on our service and store certain information. These tracking technologies include beacons, tags, and scripts, used to collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our service. The technologies we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to indicate when a Cookie is being sent. However, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our service. Unless you've adjusted your browser settings to refuse Cookies, our service might just use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These files allow the Company (for example) to count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website statistics (e.g., recording the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies get wiped clean as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely essential for providing you with services available through the website and enabling you to use some of its features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use them to give you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have accepted the use of Cookies on the website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the website, like remembering your login details or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the website.

For more info about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding Cookies, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
