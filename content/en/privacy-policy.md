### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash some info. These tracking technologies include beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to gather and track info, and to jazz up and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file plunked down on your device. You can tell your browser to totally reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service might just use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might pack tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for instance) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other related website stats (like tracking how popular a certain section is and making sure our system and servers are all in one piece).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. When you're offline, Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device. Session Cookies, however, vanish into thin air the second you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for dishing out the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're like bouncers, helping to verify users and keep fraudulent account usage at bay. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for can't happen, and we only use them to provide you with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given the thumbs-up to our use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are the brainy ones, letting us remember the choices you make when you're rocking the Website. Think remembering your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized vibe and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you swing by the Website.

For more info on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
