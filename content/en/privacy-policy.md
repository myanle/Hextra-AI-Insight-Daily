### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're using Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and stash away some info. These tracking tools include cool stuff like beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to gather and track data, plus help us fine-tune and analyze our Service. Here's a peek at what we might use: 🍪

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on Your Device. You can tell Your browser to refuse all Cookies or to indicate when a Cookie is being sent. However, if You do not accept Cookies, You might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless You have adjusted Your browser setting so that it will refuse Cookies, our Service may use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other website stats (like seeing how popular a certain section is or checking system and server health).

Cookies? They come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session." Persistent ones chill on your computer or mobile even after you go offline, but Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We rock both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for getting you the services available on our Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're like the bouncers, helping to verify users and keep fraudsters away from accounts. No these Cookies, no services for you! So yeah, we only use them to make sure you get what you asked for.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply remember if you've already given us the nod to use cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super handy because they let us remember your choices when you're cruising through the Website – things like your login deets or what language you prefer. The whole point is to give you a smoother, more personalized experience so you don't have to punch in your preferences every single time you visit.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options? Just pop over to our Cookies Policy or hit up the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy.
