We may also scoop up info your browser sends our way when you visit our service or access it on a mobile device.

### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our service and store some info. The tracking technologies we roll with include beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to collect and track information, plus improve and analyze our service. These technologies might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file dropped onto your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our service might not work for you. Our service probably uses Cookies unless you've set your browser to say "no thanks!" to them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other website stats (like seeing how popular a section is or checking if our system and servers are running smoothly).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Think of Persistent Cookies sticking around on your computer or phone even when you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish as soon as you close your web browser. 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for making sure you get the services available through the website and can use its cool features. They help us verify who you are and stop any funny business with user accounts. Without 'em, the services you're asking for just can't happen, and we only use these Cookies for that exact purpose.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply check if you've already said "okay" to using cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty neat because they let us remember your choices when you're using the Website – like your login deets or what language you prefer. The whole point is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you pop onto the Website.

Want to know more about the Cookies we use and your choices? Go ahead and check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. 🍪
