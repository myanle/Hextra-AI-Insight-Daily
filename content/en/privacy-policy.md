### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away some info. These tracking tech tools — think beacons, tags, and scripts — help us gather and track data, which in turn helps us level up and analyze our Service. The technologies we might use include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a small file chilling on your device. You've got the power to tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But heads up! 🚨 If you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and even our emails might contain these tiny electronic files known as web beacons (you might also hear them called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for example, count folks who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also super handy for other website stats, like tracking the popularity of a section or double-checking system and server integrity.

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies – kinda like permanent vs. temporary guests. Persistent Cookies hang out on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline, but Session Cookies? Poof! ✨ They're gone the second you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following awesome reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are the real MVPs (Most Valuable Players)! They're absolutely essential for delivering the services you can snag via the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help us verify who you are and stop any shady, fraudulent use of user accounts. Honestly, without these Cookies, the services you're after just wouldn't work, and we only use 'em to make sure you get those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply help us remember if you've given us the thumbs-up on using Cookies on the Website. 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty smart! They let us remember your choices when you're cruising around the Website, like keeping your login info or language preferences tucked away. The big goal here? To give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you swing by the Website. 🚀

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your choices about 'em? Just head over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
