### Tracking Tech & Cookies 🍪
We're using Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash some info. These tracking tools — think beacons, tags, and scripts — help us scoop up and track info, plus improve and analyze what we're doing. Some of the tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: These are tiny files dropped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. Heads up though: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say 'no thanks' to Cookies, our Service will likely use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These let the Company (for instance) count folks who've visited certain pages or opened an email and help with other website stats — like tracking a section's popularity or checking system and server health.

Cookies come in two flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session.' Persistent Cookies chill on your computer or mobile device even after you go offline, but Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for us to even offer you the services on the Website and let you use some of its cool features. They help us verify who you are and stop any shady account use. Seriously, without 'em, the services you're asking for just wouldn't happen. We only use these to get you those services!

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply remember if you've given us the thumbs-up to use Cookies on the Website. 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies help us remember your choices when you're on the Website — like your login info or language preference. The whole point is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to punch in your preferences every single time. 🙌

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy.
