### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our service and stash away some info. The tracking tech we roll with includes Beacons, Tags, and Scripts, all designed to collect and track info, plus improve and analyze how our service is doing. Here's what we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file that gets cozy on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, a word to the wise: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our service might not play nice. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say 'no thanks' to Cookies, our service will likely be using them. 🍪
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company count users who've visited certain pages or opened an email, and help with other website stats, like checking a section's popularity and making sure our systems and servers are all good.

Cookies come in two main flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Essential / Necessary Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital for powering the services available to you via the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help verify user identity and put a stop to any dodgy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't work, and we only use them to get those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Notification Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are designed to figure out if users have given us the green light to use Cookies on the Website. ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super handy because they let us remember choices you've made while using the Website, like your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you swing by the Website.

For the full scoop on the Cookies we use and your choices about them, just zip over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy.
