### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. Here's what some of that tech might include: 👇

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: So, what's a Cookie? It's a tiny file that gets popped onto your device. You can totally tell your browser to say 'nope!' to all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one is coming your way. Just know, if you don't accept 'em, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've specifically adjusted your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: You might find tiny electronic files, sometimes called Web Beacons (or clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs), chilling in certain parts of our Service and our emails. These nifty files let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other cool website stats, like tracking a section's popularity and making sure our systems and servers are running smoothly.

Cookies can be either 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. When you go offline, Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device. Session Cookies, though? Poof! They disappear the second you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few key reasons: 👇

- **Essential / Necessary Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These are our go-to Session Cookies, managed by us. Why do we use 'em? They're super important for providing you with the services available on the Website and letting you tap into its cool features. They help us verify who you are and prevent any sneaky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Honestly, without these Cookies, the services you're looking for just wouldn't work, and we only use them to make sure you *get* those services. 💪

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These are Persistent Cookies, managed by us. Their goal? To figure out if you've already given us the green light to use Cookies on the Website. ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These awesome Persistent Cookies, managed by us, have one main purpose: to remember your choices when you're using the Website! Think remembering your login details or your language preference. The big idea here is to give you a super personalized experience, so you don't have to keep re-entering your preferences every time you visit. Pretty sweet, right? ✨

Want more deets about the Cookies we use and your choices? Go ahead and check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section right here in our Privacy Policy. 📖
