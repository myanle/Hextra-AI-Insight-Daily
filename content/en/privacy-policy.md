### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our Service and store certain information. The tracking technologies used include beacons, tags, and scripts, which help us collect and track information, as well as improve and analyze our Service. The technologies we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: Cookies are small files placed on your device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to indicate when a Cookie is being sent. However, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've adjusted your browser setting to decline Cookies, our Service may use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) are small electronic files that might be included in certain sections of our Service and in our emails. These allow the Company, for example, to count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website statistics (e.g., noting a section's popularity and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. While Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, Session Cookies are gone as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are essential for powering the services available to you through the website and enabling you to use some of its cool features. They help verify user identity and stop fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use them to give you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have accepted the use of cookies on the website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies allow us to remember the choices you make when you use the website, like remembering your login details or language preference. Their whole point is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you visit!

For more information about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding Cookies, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
