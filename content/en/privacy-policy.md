### Tracking Tech & Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away some info. These tracking tools — think beacons, tags, and scripts — help us collect and track data, all to spruce up and analyze how our Service is doing. The tech we might be using includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file chucked onto your Device. You can tell your browser to totally reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't play ball and accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service will probably be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some bits of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out how popular a certain section is and making sure systems and servers are all good).

So, Cookies can either be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones hang around on your computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air the moment you close your web browser.

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're super helpful for authenticating users and stopping any shady, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you. Simple as that!

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are basically our way of knowing if users have given the thumbs-up to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty neat because they let us remember choices you make when you're cruising around the Website, like keeping your login deets or language preferences in mind. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep re-entering your preferences every single time you hit up the Website.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what options you've got regarding them? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in our Privacy Policy. 😉
