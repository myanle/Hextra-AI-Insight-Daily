### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We use Cookies and other cool tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain info. This tech includes things like beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to gather and track info, helping us level up and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file a website places on your device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject 'em, our Service might just use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Web Beacons are these tiny electronic files (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs) that might pop up in some parts of our Service and emails. They let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other website stats (like checking a section's popularity and making sure systems and servers are all good).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few reasons, like:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for giving you services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help verify users and stop any dodgy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to spot if users have given the green light to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies help us remember choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you from typing in your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For the full scoop on the Cookies we use and your options concerning them, just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
