### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and to stash away certain info. The tracking technologies we deploy include beacons, tags, and scripts – all for collecting and tracking info, plus improving and analyzing our Service. Here's what we might be using:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies, whatever you wanna call 'em)**: These are tiny files dropped onto your Device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to warn you when one's coming your way. But heads up: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say 'no' to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear 'em called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). They let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email, and they're super useful for other website stats (like checking how popular a section is or making sure our systems and servers are all good).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, but Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for getting you the services available on the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help us confirm who you are and stop any shady business with user accounts. Seriously, without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just can't happen, and we only use them to make those services available to you.

-   **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies simply check if you've given us the thumbs up to use cookies on the Website.

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are awesome because they help us remember your choices when you're using the Website – things like your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you visit.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what choices you've got? Head over to our Cookies Policy or check out the Cookies section in our Privacy Policy.
