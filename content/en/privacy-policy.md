### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We roll with Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity within our Service and store certain info. This tracking tech includes things like beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include: 🍪

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Our Service might use Cookies unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to reject them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like, logging a section's popularity and verifying system and server integrity). Pretty cool, right?

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered By**: We
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for hooking you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its features. They help authenticate users and stop any dodgy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to give you those services. No jokes!

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Notice Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered By**: We
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about checking if users have given the green light to use Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered By**: We
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website. Smart, right?

For more info on the Cookies we use and your options regarding Cookies, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. Got it? 😉
