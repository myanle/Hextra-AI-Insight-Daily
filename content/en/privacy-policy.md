### Tracking Tech and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking tech we're talking about includes beacons, tags, and scripts – all used to gather and track info, and of course, to make our Service even better and analyze how it's doing. So, the specific tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is basically a tiny file that gets dropped onto your Device. You totally have the power to tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's trying to sneak in. But here's the kicker: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice with you. So, unless you've gone into your browser settings to specifically block Cookies, our Service will probably be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Heads up! Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (you might also hear 'em called clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These little helpers let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email, and they're also awesome for other website stats (like tracking how popular a section is or making sure our systems and servers are all good).

Cookies can be either 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you're offline, but Session Cookies? Poof! They vanish the second you close your web browser. 👋

We totally use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's why:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: Okay, these Cookies are seriously essential for serving up the goods on the Website and letting you tap into some of its awesome features. They're like bouncers, helping to authenticate users and stop any funky business with user accounts. No these Cookies, no services you've asked for! And yeah, we *only* use them to give you those services. Period.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply remember if you've given the 'okay' to using Cookies on our Website. No fuss, no muss. 👌

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super handy because they let us remember choices you make while browsing the Website – think login deets or language prefs. The big goal here? To give you a more personalized experience and save you the headache of re-typing your preferences every single time you swing by. Sweet! ✨

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. It's all there!
