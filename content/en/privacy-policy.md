### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We're big fans of Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we deploy might include: 👇

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, fair warning, if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say 'no' to Cookies, our Service will likely use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (you might also know them as clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and they're also used for other related website stats (like tracking the popularity of a certain section and making sure the system and server are running smoothly).

So, Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super essential for making sure you can get all the services available on our Website and use its cool features. They're basically the bouncers, helping to authenticate users and keep fraudulent use of accounts at bay. Without these, the services you asked for just wouldn't work, and we *only* use them to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are like a little checkmark, letting us know if you've given the green light to use cookies on the Website. ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These nifty Cookies let us remember your choices when you're chilling on the Website, like your login details or what language you prefer. The whole point? To give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-type your preferences every single time you visit. Sweet! 🍬

Wanna dive deeper into the Cookies we use and your choices regarding them? Pop over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. You'll get the full scoop! 🍪
