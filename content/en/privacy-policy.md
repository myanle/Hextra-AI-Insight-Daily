### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to monitor activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts, which help us collect and track info, and improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to alert you when a Cookie is sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've adjusted your browser settings to refuse Cookies, our Service might just go ahead and use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome files allow the Company (for example) to count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like tracking the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies get zapped as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super essential for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to give you those services.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more info about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding cookies, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
