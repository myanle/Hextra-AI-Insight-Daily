### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and other cool tracking tech (like beacons, tags, and scripts) to keep an eye on what's happening on our Service and store some info. These technologies are used to collect and track information and to improve and analyze Our Service. The technologies We use may include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie? It's just a tiny file chilling on your device. You can tell Your browser to ditch all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But if You don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. If you haven't told your browser to ditch Cookies, then our Service might be using 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email and help with other website stats (like seeing how popular a section is and making sure our systems are solid).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on Your personal computer or mobile device when You go offline, while Session Cookies get deleted the moment You close Your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes set out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for making sure you get all the services available on our Website and can use all its cool features. They help us verify users and prevent any sketchy stuff with user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services You've asked for just can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies for that exact reason.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given the green light to use cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let Us remember the choices You make when You use the Website, like keeping Your login info or language preference handy. The whole point of these Cookies is to give You a more personal experience and save You the hassle of re-entering Your preferences every single time You use the Website.

For more information about the cookies we use and your choices regarding cookies, feel free to swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
