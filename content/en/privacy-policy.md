### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store some info. These tracking goodies include beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to collect and track data, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file dropped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to totally reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't take Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service just might use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome files let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like, say, figuring out how popular a certain section is and making sure the system and server are in tip-top shape).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies totally vanish the second you close your web browser.

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's the lowdown on why:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for hooking you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're all about authenticating users and giving fraudulent user accounts the boot. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to make sure you get those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are basically our way of checking if users have said "yes" to Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or your language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every single time you hit up the Website. Pretty neat, huh?

For more info about the Cookies we use and what choices you have about 'em, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. It's all there!
