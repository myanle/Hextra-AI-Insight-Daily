### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and other sneaky tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and squirrel away some info. Think beacons, tags, and scripts – these guys help us gather and track data, so we can make our Service better and analyze how it's being used. Some of the tech we employ might be:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies, if you wanna get specific!)**: These are just tiny files that land on your Device. You've got the power to tell your browser to kick all Cookies to the curb or to give you a heads-up when one's trying to sneak in. Just a warning though, if you're not down with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Our Service will likely use Cookies unless you've specifically told your browser to block 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: You might find these tiny electronic files (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs) chilling in some parts of our Service and our emails. They let us do cool stuff like count how many folks have checked out certain pages or opened an email, and other website stats (like seeing which sections are super popular or making sure our systems are running smoothly).

So, Cookies come in two flavors: 'Persistent' and 'Session.' Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've logged off, but Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof!

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few reasons, check 'em out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital for us to deliver the services you find on the Website and to let you use some of its awesome features. They're like the bouncers that check your ID, making sure users are legit and stopping any funny business with accounts. Without 'em, the services you're asking for just can't happen, and we only use these specific Cookies for that very purpose.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are like a little flag that tells us if you've already said 'yes' to us using Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These bad boys help us remember your choices when you're cruising on the Website – think remembering your login deets or what language you prefer. The whole point is to give you a super personalized experience, so you don't have to keep typing in your preferences every single time you visit.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what choices you have? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy. 🍪
