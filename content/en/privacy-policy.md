### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
Cookies and similar tracking technologies are our go-to for tracking activity on our Service and holding onto certain info. These tracking tools include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track data, plus help us tweak and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to say 'nope!' to all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't play along with Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've set your browser to reject Cookies, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out how popular a certain section is and making sure the system and server are running smoothly).

Cookies come in two flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your computer or mobile device even after you're offline. Session Cookies, however, disappear as soon as you close your web browser. See ya! 👋

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's why: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for getting you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its features. They help verify user identity and stop any dodgy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those specific services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about remembering if users have given the green light to using Cookies on the Website. It's how we know you're cool with it! 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or your language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep re-entering your preferences every single time you visit the Website. Pretty neat, huh?

For all the extra info on the Cookies we use and your choices about them, just head over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy. Easy peasy! 😉
