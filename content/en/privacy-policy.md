### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activities within our Service and store certain information. The tracking tech we're talking about includes things like Beacons, Tags, and Scripts, all working to gather and track info, and ultimately help us improve and analyze our Service. Here’s what we might use: 👇

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that gets placed on your device. You can totally tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But here's the catch: if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've already adjusted your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service will probably be using them. Just a heads-up! ⚠️
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service, and even our emails, might contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also called clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These clever little files let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They also help with other website stats, like tracking the popularity of a certain section and making sure our systems and servers are A-OK. Pretty neat, huh? 👀

Cookies can be either 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. The Persistent ones hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you're offline. Session Cookies, though? They get deleted the instant you close your web browser. Gone in a flash! ⚡

We actually use *both* Session and Persistent Cookies, and for some pretty specific reasons. Check it out: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered By**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely essential for delivering the services available through the Website and enabling you to use some of its awesome features. They're key to authenticating users and stopping any fraudulent use of user accounts. Honestly, without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't be possible, and we *only* use them to make sure you get those services. They're the foundation! 🏗️

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered By**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to remember if you've already accepted the use of cookies on the Website. Once you've clicked 'got it,' they keep that in mind! 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered By**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty cool because they let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website. Think remembering your login info or your language preference! The whole goal here is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you swing by. Talk about convenience! 😌

For even more juicy details about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding them, just pop over to our Cookies Policy or the dedicated Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. Knowledge is power! 💡
