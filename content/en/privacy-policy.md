### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're big fans of Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep an eye on activity on our Service and store certain bits of info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts – these are all about collecting and tracking info to help us fine-tune and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file dropped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to give all Cookies the boot or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But heads up, if you don't play ball with Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service might still use 'em.
-   **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for instance) count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out the popularity of a certain section and making sure the system and server are all hunky-dory).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for us to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They're also key in authenticating users and preventing any shady business with user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be delivered, and trust us, we only use 'em to bring you those services.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are here to figure out if users have given the thumbs-up to the use of Cookies on the Website.

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty neat because they let us remember choices you make when you're using the Website, like keeping your login deets or language preferences in mind. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you swing by the Website.

For more deets on the Cookies we use and your choices regarding 'em, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
