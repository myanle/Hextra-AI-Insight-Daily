### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep an eye on what's happening on our Service and store certain bits of info. These tracking technologies — like beacons, tags, and scripts — are all about collecting and tracking info, helping us level up and analyze our Service. The specific tech we use might include: 👇

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file that gets placed on your device. You can tell your browser to say "Nope!" to all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But just a fair warning, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service might just be using 'em!
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and even our emails might have tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other cool website stats (like checking out a section's popularity or making sure the system and servers are all good to go!).

Okay, so Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. The Persistent ones hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline, while Session Cookies totally disappear the second you close your web browser. Like magic! ✨

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's why: 👇

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're super helpful for authenticating users and keeping those user accounts safe from fraud. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply wouldn't work, and we only use these Cookies to make sure you get those services. No kidding!

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about checking if users have, you know, accepted the use of cookies on the Website. It's like a little digital handshake! 🤝

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty smart! They let us remember your choices when you're using the Website, like keeping your login deets or language preferences in mind. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you visit. Sweet! 😎

Want to know more about the Cookies we use and what choices you've got? Head over to our Cookies Policy or check out the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy. All the deets are there! 🤓
