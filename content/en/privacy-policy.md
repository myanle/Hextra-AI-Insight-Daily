### Tracking Tech & Cookies (How We Remember You)
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts. They help us collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file chilling on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They also help with other related website stats, like logging the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity. Sneaky, but useful!

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline. Session Cookies, on the other hand, vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below. Get ready for the breakdown:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for hooking you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help us authenticate users and stop any dodgy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to check if users have given the green light to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website. Sweet! 😉

For even more info on the Cookies we use and your choices regarding them, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. It's all there!
