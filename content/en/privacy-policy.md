### Tracking Tech & Cookies 🍪
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking tech we deploy includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to gather and track info, plus jazz up and analyze our Service. ✨ The tech we use could include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file that gets cozy on your device. You can tell your browser to kick all Cookies to the curb or to holler when a Cookie's coming. 🚫 But hey, if you don't play ball with Cookies, you might miss out on some bits of our Service. 😕 Our Service might just use Cookies unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give 'em the boot.
- **Web Beacons**: Some corners of our Service and our emails might pack tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for example, tally up users who've visited those pages or ripped open an email, and they're also for other website stats (like seeing how popular a section is or double-checking system and server health). 👀

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you log off, 🛌 while Session Cookies vanish the second you close your web browser. 💨

We're rocking both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies** ✅
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for dishing out the services available through the Website and letting you tap into some of its features. They're like bouncers, helping to verify user identities and prevent shady use of user accounts. 🚫🛡️ Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't happen, and we only use these Cookies to make those services a reality for you.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Notice Cookies** 👍
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have given a thumbs-up to Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies** ⚙️
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are memory keepers, letting us recall choices you make when you're using the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preference. 🧠 The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you hit the site.

Want more deets on the Cookies we use and your options? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. 🔗
