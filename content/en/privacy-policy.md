### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and other similar tracking tech to watch what's happening on our Service and save some bits of info. These tracking tools include beacons, tags, and scripts – they help us gather and track data to constantly improve and understand how you're using our Service. Here's what we might use:

-   **Cookies (or Browser Cookies)**: Think of a Cookie as a tiny file that lands on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, heads up: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Our Service usually uses Cookies unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give 'em the boot.
-   **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These nifty little guys let us, for example, count how many users have checked out those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other cool website stats, like tracking how popular a section is or making sure our systems and servers are all good.

Cookies can be either 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. Persistent ones hang out on your computer or phone even after you're offline, while Session Cookies totally disappear the second you close your browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's why:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Yours truly (Us)
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for getting you the services available on the Website and making sure you can use all its cool features. They're like bouncers, helping to verify users and keep out any fraudulent account activity. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't work, and we only use 'em to deliver those essential services to you.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies just remember if you've already given us the thumbs up for using cookies on the Website. 👍

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty clever! They help us remember the choices you make while using the Website – things like your login deets or your preferred language. The whole point is to give you a super personalized experience and save you the headache of re-typing your preferences every time you visit. Phew! 😮‍💨

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what your choices are? Just pop over to our Cookies Policy or hit up the Cookies section in this Privacy Policy.
