### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain information. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts. We deploy these to collect and track information, plus improve and analyze our Service. The technologies we use might include:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped on your Device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't take Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service will likely use them.
-   **Web Beacons**: Parts of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These handy files let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other related website stats, like checking out how popular a section is and verifying system and server integrity.

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. When you go offline, Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device, but Session Cookies get wiped the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following purposes:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for giving you services available through the Website and letting you use some of its features. They help us authenticate users and stop any dodgy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have given the green light to the use of Cookies on the Website.

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more info on the Cookies we use and your choices regarding Cookies, hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
