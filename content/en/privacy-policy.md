### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking technologies we're talking about include beacons, tags, and scripts. They help us collect and track info, and generally make our Service better and smarter. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file placed on your device. You can totally tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. However, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service might just keep using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They also get used for other website stats, like logging how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity.

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air as soon as you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and stop any shady, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to check if users have given their OK to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personal experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website. Sweet!

For more info on the Cookies we use, and your choices regarding cookies, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
