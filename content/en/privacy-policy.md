### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity within our Service and store certain information. This tracking tech – like beacons, tags, and scripts – helps us gather and monitor info, plus totally improve and analyze how our Service is doing. Here's a rundown of the tech we use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: These are just small files that get dropped onto your device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's trying to sneak in. But heads up: if you don't accept Cookies, you might find yourself locked out of some parts of our Service. So, unless you've specifically adjusted your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service will probably be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: You might spot these tiny electronic files (also called clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs) hanging out in some parts of our Service and our emails. They let the Company, for example, tally up users who've visited those pages or opened an email and they're also super useful for other website stats (like figuring out how popular a section is or checking if our systems and servers are all good).

Cookies? They can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline, but Session Cookies vanish into thin air the second you close your web browser.

We rock both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are totally essential for us to give you the services available via the Website and let you use some of its cool features. They're key for verifying user identities and stopping any sketchy, fraudulent use of accounts. Seriously, without these Cookies, the services you're looking for just can't be delivered, and we only use 'em to hook you up with those specific services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given the thumbs-up to using Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super handy because they let us remember your choices when you're using the Website – stuff like your login details or language preference. Their whole aim? To give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you swing by the site.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding them? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
