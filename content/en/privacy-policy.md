### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain information. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts to gather and track info, and to improve and analyze our Service. The technologies we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to let you know when one is being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've adjusted your browser setting to refuse Cookies, our Service will likely use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These allow the Company, for example, to count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like tracking how popular a section is and making sure the system and server are in tip-top shape).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes listed below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for providing you with services available through the Website and helping you use some of its features. They help verify user identity and stop fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about identifying if users have given the green light to use cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies help us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login details or language preferences. The goal here is to give you a more personalized experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every time you visit.

For more details about the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, feel free to check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
