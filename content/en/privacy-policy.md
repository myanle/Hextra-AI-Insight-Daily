### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep an eye on activity within our Service and stash away certain info. These tracking tools, like beacons, tags, and scripts, help us gather and track data, ultimately making our Service better and allowing us to analyze how it's being used. The tech we might employ includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: So, a Cookie is just a tiny file that lands on your Device. You've got the power to tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up whenever one's being sent. But just a fair warning, if you say "nope" to Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service will likely be using them. 🍪
- **Web Beacons**: You might spot these tiny electronic files, also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs, hanging out in certain parts of our Service and even our emails. Web beacons basically give us a little headcount – for instance, telling us how many users visited a page or opened an email. They're also handy for other website stats, like seeing how popular a section is or checking if our systems and servers are running smoothly.

Cookies actually come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies are the ones that stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've logged off. Session Cookies, on the other hand, vanish into thin air the moment you close your web browser. Poof!

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's why:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These cookies are super important for giving you the services available on our Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're like bouncers, helping to confirm who's who and prevent any funny business with user accounts. Without them, the services you're asking for just wouldn't work, so we only use these specific cookies to keep those services humming for you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These cookies basically remember if you've given us the thumbs-up to use cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These cookies are awesome because they help us remember your choices while you're cruising around the Website – like keeping your login deets or language preference saved. The whole point is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep punching in your preferences every single time you visit. Sweet!

Wanna know more about the cookies we use and what choices you have about them? Head over to our Cookies Policy or just check out the Cookies section right here in our Privacy Policy.
