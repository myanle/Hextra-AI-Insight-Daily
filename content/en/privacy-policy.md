### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and other savvy tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and to stash some info. These tracking tools include things like beacons, tags, and scripts—all designed to collect and track data, helping us fine-tune and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes: 📡

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that gets popped onto your Device. You can totally tell your browser to turn down all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But heads up: if you don't take Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've specifically tweaked your browser settings to reject them, our Service will likely be using Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Look, some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, say, count how many users visited a page or opened an email. They also help with other website stats, like checking how popular a section is or making sure our systems are all good. 💡

Cookies come in two main flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang out on your computer or mobile device even after you're offline. Session Cookies? Poof! They vanish the moment you close your web browser. 💨

We rock both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes, listed right below: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for us to actually deliver the services available on our Website and to let you use its cool features. They're like our bouncers, helping to verify users and keep fraudulent account use at bay. No these Cookies, no services for you! So, yeah, we only use them to make sure you get what you asked for. ✅

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are basically our little reminders, letting us know if you've already given your 'okay' to use cookies on the Website. So we don't bother you again! 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These smart Cookies help us remember your choices when you're cruising around our Website—stuff like your login info or what language you prefer. The whole point is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep punching in your preferences every single time you visit. Pretty neat, huh? ✨

For the full lowdown on the Cookies we use and what choices you've got regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in *this very* Privacy Policy. Go on, check it out! 👇
