### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity within our Service and store some info. These tracking tools – think beacons, tags, and scripts – help us collect and track data, ultimately making our Service better and giving us insights. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: This is a tiny file that gets popped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, heads up! If you don't play along with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service will likely use them.
- **Web Beacons**: You might spot these tiny electronic files (also called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs) in some parts of our Service and our emails. They let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other website stats (like checking how popular a section is or making sure our systems are humming along perfectly). 🕵️‍♀️

Cookies come in two flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. Persistent ones stick around on your computer or mobile device even after you go offline, but Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for getting you the services available on our Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're like bouncers, helping to verify users and keep out fraudulent account activity. No these Cookies, no service for you! So yeah, we only use them to make sure you get what you came for.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply remember if you've already given us the 'okay' to use cookies on the Website. One less thing for you to click! 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are awesome because they help us remember your choices when you're using the Website—things like your login deets or what language you prefer. The whole point is to give you a smoother, more personalized experience so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time. Convenience is key! 🔑

Wanna know more about the cookies we use and your options? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy. We've got you covered!
