### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies – think beacons, tags, and scripts – to keep an eye on activity within our Service and store some info. These help us collect and track data, then use it to improve and analyze what we do. The tech we use might include:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that lands on your Device. You can tell your browser to say 'no thanks' to all Cookies or to alert you when one's being sent. Heads up though, if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've specifically tweaked your browser settings to reject them, our Service might just use Cookies by default. 🍪
-   **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and even our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might know them as clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These clever little things help us, for example, count folks who've visited certain pages or opened an email. They're also super handy for other website stats, like seeing how popular a section is or checking if our systems are running smoothly.

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session." Persistent Cookies chill on your device even after you go offline, but Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your browser. Poof! 🌬️

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely essential to bring you the services available on our Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help confirm who you are and stop any shady, fraudulent use of user accounts. Honestly, without them, the services you're asking for just wouldn't work, so we only use them for that purpose.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies simply let us know if you've given the thumbs up to using cookies on our Website. 👍

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super handy because they help us remember your choices when you're using the Website – like your login info or language preference. Their whole point is to give you a more personalized experience, saving you the hassle of typing in your preferences every single time. Smart, right? 🧠

Want more details about the Cookies we use and your options? Just hop over to our Cookies Policy or check out the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy!
