### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep an eye on what's happening on Our Service and stash away some info. These tracking goodies include things like beacons, tags, and scripts. They help Us gather and track info, then polish and understand Our Service better. Here's a peek at what We use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie? It's just a tiny file plopped onto Your Device. You're in control! You can tell Your browser to kick all Cookies to the curb or to give you a heads-up when one's trying to get sent. But heads up: if You don't play nice with Cookies, some parts of Our Service might not work for You. Unless You've tweaked Your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, Our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: So, certain parts of Our Service and even Our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (sometimes known as clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These awesome little things let the Company, for example, tally up users who've visited certain pages or opened an email. They also help with other website stats, like seeing how popular a section is or double-checking the system's integrity. Pretty neat, huh?

Cookies themselves can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones chill out on Your computer or mobile device even after You go offline. Session Cookies? They vanish the second You close Your web browser. Poof! 💨

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the reasons we're about to lay out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: Alright, these Cookies are super crucial for giving You the services available on the Website and letting You use its cool features. They're like the bouncers that authenticate users and stop any shady, fraudulent account use. No these Cookies, no services you've asked for! We literally only use them to get You those services. 💪

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply check if users have given the thumbs-up to using cookies on the Website. 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are neat because they let us remember Your choices when You're using the Website – stuff like Your login deets or what language You prefer. The whole point? To give You a more personalized vibe and save You from typing in Your preferences every single time you swing by the Website. So convenient! ✨

Wanna know more about the cookies We use and Your choices? Just zip over to Our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy. 🤓
