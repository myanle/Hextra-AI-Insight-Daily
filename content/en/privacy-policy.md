### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activity on our Service and to store certain information. These tracking technologies include beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to collect and track info, and to improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies)**: A Cookie is a tiny file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to alert you when one is being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say no to Cookies, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These handy files let the Company, for example, count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use 'em to give you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Notice Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about checking if users have accepted the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you visit the Website.

For more deets on the Cookies we use and your choices about 'em, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
