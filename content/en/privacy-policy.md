### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash some info. This includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to gather and track data, helping us tweak and analyze our Service. Here's what we might use:

- 🍪 **Cookies (or Browser Cookies):** These are tiny files dropped onto your device. You can tell your browser to say 'nope' to all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's trying to send. But, if you don't accept them, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've set your browser to reject Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
- ✨ **Web Beacons:** Some parts of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (think clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other website stats (like checking how popular a section is or making sure our systems are humming along).

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session." Persistent Cookies hang around on your computer or mobile device even after you go offline, but Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

- 🍪 **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for making sure you can use the services on our Website and access all its cool features. They help verify who you are and stop any shady, fraudulent account use. Without 'em, the services you want just wouldn't work, and we only use these to deliver those services to you.

- 🍪 **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply tell us if you've already said "yes" to using cookies on the Website.

- 🍪 **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are designed to remember your choices as you use the Website – things like your login info or what language you prefer. Basically, they're here to give you a smoother, more personalized experience so you don't have to keep punching in your preferences every single time.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy.
