### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, which help us collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. Heads up though: if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who have bopped onto those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like tracking the popularity of a certain section and checking system and server integrity).

Cookies? They can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof!

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for hooking you up with the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and stop any dodgy, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you asked for just can't be delivered, and we only use them to give you those specific services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have given the thumbs-up to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more info on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
