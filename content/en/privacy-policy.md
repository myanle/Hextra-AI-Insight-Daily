### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep an eye on what's happening on our Service and stash away some info. We're talking beacons, tags, and scripts – all used to gather and track data, helping us fine-tune and understand our Service better. Here's a peek at what we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file chilling on your device. You can tell your browser to say 'nope!' to all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's coming. But hey, if you don't play along with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to reject them, our Service is probably using Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and even our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These are pretty neat because they let us, for instance, count who's visited certain pages or opened an email. They also help with other website stats, like checking how popular a section is or making sure our systems are running smoothly.

Cookies come in two flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session.' Persistent Cookies hang out on your computer or mobile device even after you log off, but Session Cookies? Poof! They disappear the second you close your browser. ✨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies** 🔑
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital to get you the services available on our Website and to let you use its cool features. They're like bouncers, helping us verify who you are and keeping those pesky fraudulent accounts away. Without 'em, we can't deliver the goodies you're asking for, so we only use these to make sure you get what you need.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies** ✅
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty straightforward: they just remember if you've already given us the nod to use Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies** 🛠️
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super helpful because they let us remember your choices when you're using the Website – stuff like your login info or what language you prefer. Their whole jam is to give you a smoother, more personalized experience so you don't have to keep punching in your preferences every single time!

Wanna know more about our Cookies and what choices you have? Go check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy!
