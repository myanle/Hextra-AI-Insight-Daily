### Peeking Behind the Curtains: Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and squirrel away some info. The tracking technologies we deploy include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies, if you wanna be fancy):** A Cookie is just a tiny file chillin' on your device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But here's the kicker: if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service might just be using them.
- **Web Beacons:** Some parts of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like noting the popularity of a certain section and making sure the system and server are running solid).

So, Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air the second you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We totally use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes laid out below:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type:** Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by:** Us
    -   **Purpose:** These Cookies are super crucial for hooking you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're like bouncers, helping to authenticate users and stop any dodgy, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to give you those specific services. Seriously, they're essential! 💪

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type:** Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by:** Us
    -   **Purpose:** These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given the nod to the use of Cookies on the Website.

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type:** Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by:** Us
    -   **Purpose:** These Cookies are pretty cool because they let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like remembering your login deets or your language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, saving you the hassle of having to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the Website. Sweet deal! 🤙

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what choices you've got regarding them? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. It's all there!
