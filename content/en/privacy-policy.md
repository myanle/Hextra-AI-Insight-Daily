### Our Tracking Tech & Cookies 🍪
We're big fans of Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away some info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, helping us level up and analyze our Service. Here’s a peek at the tech we might be using:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies:** What's a Cookie? It’s a tiny file that gets tucked onto your device. You can totally tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one’s coming your way. But be warned: if you don’t accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice with you. Unless you’ve tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service might just be using them. Just sayin'.

- **Web Beacons:** Some nooks and crannies of our Service, and even our emails, might contain tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These bad boys let us, for example, count users who've visited certain pages or opened an email. They also help with other cool website stats, like logging how popular a section is and double-checking the integrity of our systems and servers. Pretty neat, right?

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang around on your computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies:**
  - **Type:** Session Cookies
  - **Managed by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are super critical for getting you the services available through the Website and making sure you can use all its cool features. They're like our bouncers, helping to authenticate users and put a stop to any shady, fraudulent account usage. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't happen, and trust us, we only use them to provide those services to you. Can't live without 'em! 😉

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies:**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are pretty straightforward: they help us remember if you've already given us the thumbs up to use Cookies on the Website. Saves you from seeing that pesky pop-up every time! 👍

- **Functionality Cookies:**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are awesome because they let us remember your choices when you're using the Website. Think remembering your login deets or your preferred language. The whole point here is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep re-entering your preferences every single time you swing by. Super convenient! ✨

Want more deets on the Cookies we use and your options concerning them? Swing by our Cookies Policy or check out the Cookies section of this very Privacy Policy. We've got you covered!
