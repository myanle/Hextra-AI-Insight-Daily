### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activity within our Service and store specific bits of information. These technologies include beacons, tags, and scripts, which help us collect, track, and analyze data to improve our Service. Here’s a peek at what we use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies:** A Cookie is a tiny file placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to alert you when one is being sent. - **Web Beacons:** Some parts of our Service and emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These handy tools let the Company, for instance, count users who've visited certain pages or opened an email. They also help with other website stats, like tracking the popularity of a section or checking system and server integrity.

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" and "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline. Session Cookies, on the other hand, vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type:** Session Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are absolutely crucial for providing you with the services available through the Website and enabling you to use some of its features. They're key for authenticating users and preventing any sneaky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you’ve asked for just wouldn’t work – and we only use them to make those services happen!

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies simply help us know if you've accepted the use of cookies on our Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are pretty cool because they let us remember your choices when you use the Website. Think remembering your login details or your language preference! Their main goal is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you visit.

For more juicy details about the cookies we use and your options regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy.
