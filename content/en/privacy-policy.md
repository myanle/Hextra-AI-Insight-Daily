### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're using Cookies and other sneaky tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and to stash away some info. This tracking tech includes things like beacons, tags, and scripts – all designed to gather and track data, helping us polish and analyze our Service. Some of the tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: Think of a Cookie as a tiny file that gets plonked onto your device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's trying to come through. Heads up though, if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These nifty little guys let us, for instance, tally up users who've visited certain pages or opened an email. They're also super handy for other website stats, like seeing how popular a section is or double-checking our system and server health. 🔍

Cookies come in two main flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones stick around on your computer or phone even after you've gone offline. Session Cookies, on the other hand, vanish into thin air the second you close your browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's why:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Who runs 'em**: Us
  - **Why we use 'em**: These Cookies are absolutely non-negotiable for giving you the services available on our Website and letting you tap into certain features. They're like our bouncers, helping to verify user identities and kick out any shady, fraudulent account usage. Seriously, without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't happen, and we only use them to get those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Who runs 'em**: Us
  - **Why we use 'em**: These Cookies simply let us know if you've already given us the thumbs-up to use Cookies on the Website. 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Who runs 'em**: Us
  - **Why we use 'em**: These Cookies are the brainy ones! They help us remember your choices when you're cruising through the Website, like keeping your login deets or language preference handy. The whole point is to give you a smoother, more personalized experience, so you don't have to punch in your preferences every single time you visit.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what options you've got? Just zip over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy. 🍪
