### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪

We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity within our Service and store certain bits of information. The tracking tech we roll with includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, and naturally, to amp up and analyze our Service. The specific goodies we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file that gets dropped onto your device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't play along and accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain parts of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (you might also know them as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These files let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened emails, and they're also used for other neat website stats (like tracking how popular a section is and verifying system/server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies** ✨
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super critical for giving you access to the services available through the Website and letting you rock some of its features. They're a big help in verifying user identity and stopping any shady, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these essential Cookies, the services you're asking for just can't be delivered, and we only use them to make sure those services get to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Notification Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if you've already given the thumbs-up to using Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are neat because they let us remember your choices when you're using the Website. Think remembering your login deets or your language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, saving you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you hop on the Website.

For more juicy info on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
