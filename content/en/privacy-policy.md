### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our service and stash away certain info. These tracking tech goodies — including beacons, tags, and scripts — help us collect and track info, and generally make our service better and analyze its usage. Some of the tech we use might be:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on Your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when a Cookie is being sent. But, heads up, if you don't play nice with Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say "no way" to Cookies, our service might just use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Web Beacons are tiny electronic files (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) that some parts of our service and our emails might contain. These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity). Pretty neat, huh? ✨

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air as soon as you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for dishing out the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to give you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given the thumbs up to the use of cookies on the Website. 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're cruising around the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep re-entering your preferences every time you use the Website. Sweet! 🧠

For more details on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
