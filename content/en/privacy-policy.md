### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and other similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activity on our Service and store certain bits of information. These tracking tools, like beacons, tags, and scripts, help us collect and track data, ultimately improving and analyzing our Service. Here’s what we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when a Cookie is being sent. However, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to refuse Cookies, our Service might still use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other related website statistics (like seeing how popular a certain section is and checking system and server integrity).

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes listed below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for providing you with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its features. They help us authenticate users and stop fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies for that very reason.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies help us figure out if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login details or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personal experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more info about the cookies we use and your options regarding them, hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
