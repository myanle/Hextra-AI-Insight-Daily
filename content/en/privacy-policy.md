### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
Alright, let's talk tracking! 🕵️‍♀️ We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep an eye on activity on our Service and stash away some info. The tracking technologies we use include things like Beacons, Tags, and Scripts. These are all about gathering and tracking info, plus helping us level up and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: So, a Cookie is just a tiny file that gets placed on your device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, if you don't play nice with Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've told your browser 'no way' to Cookies, our Service might just use 'em. Just a fair warning! 🍪
- **Web Beacons**: These are tiny electronic files (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs) that might pop up in certain parts of our Service and our emails. What do they do? Well, they let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other cool website stats, like tracking how popular a section is or making sure our systems and servers are rock solid. Pretty smart stuff! 📊📧

Just so you know, Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. The "Persistent" ones hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while "Session" Cookies vanish into thin air as soon as you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few different reasons, like the ones we're about to spill below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: Okay, these Cookies are super crucial for us to even give you the services available via the Website and let you use some of its cool features. They're like the bouncers 🛂, helping to verify users and stop any shady, fraudulent use of accounts. Without 'em, the services you're asking for just wouldn't work, plain and simple. So yeah, we only use these to make sure you get what you came for!

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Persistent Cookies are pretty straightforward: they just help us remember if you've already given us the green light 👍 to use Cookies on the Website. Simple as that!

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about making your life easier! They let us remember your choices when you're cruising around the Website, like keeping your login deets saved or remembering your language preference. The whole point of these is to give you a super personalized experience, so you don't have to keep typing in your preferences every single time you visit. How convenient is that? ✨

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what options you've got? Head on over to our Cookies Policy or check out the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. It's all there! 📖
