### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash some info. These tracking gizmos include beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to grab and track info, and generally make our Service better and help us analyze it. Some of the tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: Okay, so a Cookie is just a tiny file chilling on your Device. You can tell your browser to kick all Cookies to the curb or to give you a heads-up when one's trying to send. But hey, if you're not cool with Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to straight-up deny Cookies, our Service might still be using 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Web Beacons, those tiny electronic files (also called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs), might pop up in certain parts of our Service or our emails. They let us, the Company, count folks who've checked out those pages or opened an email, and help with other website stats (like seeing what's popular or checking if our system is running smoothly).

Cookies come in two flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your computer or phone even when you're offline, but Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your browser.

We use both these types of Cookies for the reasons listed below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super key for giving you the services available on our Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help confirm who you are and stop sketchy activities on user accounts. No joke, without these, the services you're asking for can't happen, and we only use them to get those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies just check if you've given the thumbs-up to using cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like your login deets or what language you prefer. Basically, they're here to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of typing in your preferences every single time you visit.

Wanna know more about our Cookies and your options? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section right here in our Privacy Policy. 🍪
