### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and stash away some info. These tracking goodies include beacons, tags, and scripts – all used to collect, track info, and generally spiff up and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: Think of a Cookie as a tiny file that gets tucked onto your Device. You're the boss – you can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up whenever one's trying to send. But, heads up, if you don't accept Cookies, some cool parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've fiddled with your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and even our emails might feature these tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). They let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email, and they're super useful for other website stats (like checking out a section's popularity or making sure our systems and servers are all good).

Now, Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones hang around on your computer or mobile device even after you've logged off, but Session Cookies? They vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! ✨

We rock both Session and Persistent Cookies for the reasons listed below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies** 🍪
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for us to give you the services available via the Website and to let you use its cool features. They're like the bouncers, helping to verify user identities and prevent any fishy business with user accounts. Without 'em, the services you're asking for just can't happen, and we ONLY use these Cookies to make sure you get those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply let us know if you've given the thumbs-up to using cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These clever Cookies let us remember your choices when you're cruising around the Website – like keeping your login deets or language preference stored. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a super personalized experience, so you don't have to keep typing in your preferences every single time you visit. Sweet!

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what choices you have? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or peep the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
