### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We use Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and stash away some info. These tracking goodies include beacons, tags, and scripts – all used to collect and track data, plus help us tweak and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file chilling on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But here's the catch: if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some cool parts of our Service. So, unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say 'no' to Cookies, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They also help out with other website stats (like checking out how popular a certain section is and making sure systems and servers are all good to go).

So, Cookies can either be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies, alright? Persistent Cookies hang out on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline, while Session Cookies peace out the moment you close your web browser.

We totally use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's why:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help verify who you are and prevent any sneaky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't be possible, and we only use them to make sure you get those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to check if users have given the thumbs-up to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functional Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you hit up the Website.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what choices you have about them? Just pop over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. Easy peasy!
