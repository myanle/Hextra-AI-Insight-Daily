### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep an eye on what's happening on our Service and store some bits of info. 🍪 These tracking technologies include cool stuff like beacons, tags, and scripts. They help us gather and track info, making our Service better and giving us insights. The tech we use might include: 👇

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that lands on your Device. You totally have the power to tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's trying to get sent. Heads up though: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice with you. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to say 'no thanks' to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files, also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs. They're pretty cool because they let us, for example, count how many users visited a page or opened an email. They're also super handy for other website stats, like seeing how popular a certain section is or making sure our systems and servers are all good. ✅

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session." Persistent Cookies chill on your computer or phone even after you go offline, but Session Cookies? They vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's why: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies (super quick ones!)
  - **Administered by**: Yours truly (that's us!)
  - **Purpose**: These are absolutely crucial for getting our website services to you and letting you use all its cool features. They're like our bouncers, helping to verify users and keep out any dodgy activity on accounts. Seriously, without these, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies for that exact purpose. 🛡️

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies (they stick around!)
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These just let us know if you've given us the thumbs-up to use cookies on our Website. Once you say yes, it remembers! 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These are awesome because they help us remember your choices when you're using the Website—like keeping you logged in or remembering your language preference. The whole point is to give you a smoother, more personalized experience so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time. How convenient! ✨

Want to dive deeper into the Cookies we use and your options? Check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section right here in our Privacy Policy. It's all there! 📖
