### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and other tracking tech (think beacons, tags, and scripts) to keep an eye on what's happening on our Service and to save a bit of info. This helps us collect and track data, and ultimately, make our Service way better. The tech we use might include: 👇

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that lands on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to block all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's trying to sneak in. But here's the catch: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. So, unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them. Just a friendly FYI! 😉
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These little guys let us, for instance, count how many users have visited specific pages or opened an email. They also help with other website stats, like seeing how popular a certain section is or double-checking our system and server integrity. Pretty cool tech, right? ✨

So, Cookies can be either 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. The 'Persistent' ones stick around on your computer or mobile device even after you've logged off, while 'Session' Cookies vanish into thin air the second you close your browser. Poof! 💨

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us (that's us!)
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for us to even give you the services available on our Website and to let you use its cool features. They're like the bouncers that check your identity and stop any funny business with user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't work, and we *only* use them to give you those essential services. No funny business here! 👍

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are basically like our memory. They help us remember if you've already given us the thumbs-up to use cookies on the Website. No need to ask you twice! ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are awesome because they let us remember all the choices you make while using our Website, like your login deets or what language you prefer. The whole point is to give you a super personalized experience so you don't have to keep re-entering your preferences every single time you visit. How convenient is that? ✨

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding them? Pop over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy. All the deets are there! 📖
