### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep an eye on activity on our Service and stash away some info. We're talking beacons, tags, and scripts here! These help us gather and track data, plus improve and analyze what we're doing. Some of the tech we use might be:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies, as some call 'em)**: A Cookie is just a small file chilling on your device. You can tell your browser to totally reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But here's the catch: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. So, unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say "No thanks!" to Cookies, our Service will likely use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear 'em called clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These nifty little things let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other website stats, like checking out how popular a certain section is or making sure our systems and servers are all good.

So, Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones stick around on your computer or mobile device even after you've logged off, but Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 🌬️

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few reasons, which we'll spell out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: Okay, these Cookies are *super* important for making sure you get the services available on our Website and can use all its cool features. They're like bouncers, helping to confirm who you are and stop any shady business with user accounts. No these Cookies, no service for you! We only use them to get you what you asked for.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are just here to remember if you've already given a thumbs-up 👍 to using cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty cool because they help us remember your choices when you're cruising around the Website – like your login info or what language you prefer. The whole point is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep typing in your preferences every single time!

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy.
