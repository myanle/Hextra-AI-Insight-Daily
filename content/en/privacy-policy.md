### Tracking Tech & Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and hold onto certain info. These tracking tech tools include beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to collect and track info, plus boost and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies)**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped on your Device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service might just go ahead and use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome little files let the Company count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out the popularity of a certain section and making sure the system and server are all good to go).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies get wiped the moment you close your web browser.

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super essential for getting you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're all about authenticating users and keeping user accounts safe from fraudulent use. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't work, and we only use 'em to give you those specific services.

-   **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are here to figure out if users have given the green light to using Cookies on the Website.

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are what let us remember your choices when you're hanging out on the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, saving you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
