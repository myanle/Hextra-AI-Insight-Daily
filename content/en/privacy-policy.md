### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts. They help us collect and track info, then improve and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your Device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service will likely use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These handy little files let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and they help with other website statistics (like tracking the popularity of a certain section and making sure our systems and servers are all good).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help us verify users and prevent any dodgy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use them to get those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given us the green light to use cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies help us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login details or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep re-entering your preferences every single time you visit!

Want more deets about the cookies we use and your options concerning them? Swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
