### Our Go-To for Tracking: Technologies and Cookies
We're big fans of Cookies and other tracking tech to keep an eye on what's happening on our Service and to stash away some info. We're talking beacons, tags, and scripts – all used to gather and track data, helping us spruce up and analyze our Service. Here's what we might use: 🍪

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that lands on your device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, a little warning: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. So, unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and even our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These little guys let us do cool stuff, like counting how many users have checked out certain pages or opened an email. They're also super handy for other website stats, like seeing how popular a section is or just making sure our systems are running smoothly. ✨

Alright, so Cookies come in two main flavors: 'Persistent' and 'Session' Cookies. Persistent ones hang around on your computer or mobile device even after you've logged off. Session Cookies, on the other hand, vanish into thin air as soon as you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few specific reasons, which we'll lay out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for getting you the services available on the Website and letting you tap into some of its cool features. They're like the bouncers for user authentication, keeping fraudulent account use in check. Seriously, without these, the stuff you're asking for just wouldn't happen, and we only use 'em to deliver those services to you. No funny business! 🔒

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply let us know if you've given the green light to using cookies on the Website. Easy peasy! ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These bad boys let us remember all the awesome choices you make when you're using the Website – things like your login deets or your favorite language. The whole point is to give you a super personalized experience, so you don't have to keep typing in your preferences every single time you visit. Sweet, right? 😎

Wanna dive deeper into the Cookies we use and what your options are? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy. All the deets are there! 👇
