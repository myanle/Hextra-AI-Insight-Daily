### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪🔍
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our Service and store certain information. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, which help us collect and track information, and also improve and analyze our Service. The technologies we use might include:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to let you know when one is being sent. However, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service might just go ahead and use them.
-   **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys allow the Company, for instance, to count users who have visited those pages or opened an email. They also help with other website stats, like figuring out how popular a certain section is and making sure the system and server integrity is solid.

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish as soon as you close your web browser. Poof!

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely essential to provide you with services available through the Website and to enable you to use some of its features. They help verify user identity and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be delivered, and we use them *only* to provide you with those services.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to identify whether users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

-   **Functional Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies allow us to remember choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login details or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more information about the cookies we use and your choices regarding cookies, feel free to visit our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
