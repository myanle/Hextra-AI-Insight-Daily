### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain info. This tracking tech includes cool stuff like Beacons, Tags, and Scripts. They help us collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file that lands on your device. You can totally tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But here's the catch: if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service might just use 'em.

- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They also help with other website statistics, like checking out how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity. Sneaky, huh? 😉

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies get wiped out the moment you slam shut your web browser.

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following cool purposes:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Managed by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital to hook you up with services available through the Website and let you use some of its key features. They're super helpful for authenticating users and preventing any sneaky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you. So yeah, they're kind of a big deal! 😎

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Managed by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to spot whether users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website. So we know you're cool with it. 👍

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Managed by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are the ones that let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website. Think remembering your login deets or your language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you swing by the Website. Nifty, right? ✨

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what choices you have regarding them? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
