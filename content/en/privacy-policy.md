### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
So, we totally use Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and store a bit of info. These sneaky (but helpful!) tracking technologies include beacons, tags, and scripts. Their job? To collect and track info, helping us make our Service even better and understand how it's being used. The tech we deploy might include: 🍪🕵️‍♀️

- **Cookies (aka Browser Cookies)**: Think of a Cookie as a tiny file that gets plopped onto your Device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's trying to get sent. But just a fair warning, if you say 'no thanks!' to Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work perfectly. Unless your browser settings are set to ditch all Cookies, our Service will likely be using them. Just so you know! 😉
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and even our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email. They're also super handy for other website stats, like seeing how popular a section is or checking if our systems and servers are all good. It's all about making things run smoothly! ✨

Cookies come in two main flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your computer or phone even when you're offline. Session Cookies, on the other hand, vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are like the VIP pass for getting the services you need from our Website and using its cool features. They're super important for checking who's who and stopping any sneaky, fraudulent account usage. Without these bad boys, the services you're asking for just wouldn't happen, and trust us, we only use them to get those services to YOU! ✅

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are simply for remembering if you've given us the 'okay' to use Cookies on the Website. Once you've said yes, they remember it so you don't get asked a million times! 😉

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are awesome because they help us remember your choices when you're rocking the Website—like keeping your login info handy or remembering your favorite language. The whole point? To give you a super personalized experience so you don't have to type in your preferences every single time. Convenience for the win! 🏆

Wanna know even *more* about the Cookies we use and your options with them? Swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy. We've got all the deets! 📖
