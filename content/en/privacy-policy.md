### Tracing Tech & Cookies: What's the Deal?
We're all about using Cookies and similar tracing tech to keep an eye on activity within our Service and store some handy info. These tracing technologies? They include cool stuff like Beacons, Tags, and Scripts, all designed to gather and track info, helping us level up and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: Think of a Cookie as a tiny file that gets plonked right onto your Device. You can totally tell your browser to give all Cookies the boot or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But here's the catch: if you don't play nice with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not be available to you. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to reject 'em, our Service might just be using Cookies. Just sayin'! 🤷‍♀️
- **Web Beacons**: Some bits of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These bad boys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited certain pages or opened an email. They're also super useful for other cool website stats, like tracking how popular a section is or double-checking the integrity of our systems and servers. Sneaky, but helpful! 😉

Cookies can come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've logged off, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We're using both Session and Persistent Cookies for some specific reasons, check it out:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are totally crucial for making the Service available to you via the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're key to verifying users and putting a stop to any sneaky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't happen, and we only use 'em to bring those services to you. Non-negotiable! 🛡️

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are here to figure out if you've already given a nod to using Cookies on the Website. One less thing for you to worry about! ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super handy because they let us remember your choices when you're using the Website—think remembering your login deets or your language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, saving you from having to re-enter your preferences every single time you swing by the Website. Smart, right? 🧠

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options concerning them? Swing by our Cookies Policy or hit up the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. All the deets are there! 📖
