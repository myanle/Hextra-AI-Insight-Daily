### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain information. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, which help us collect and track information, as well as improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to alert you when a Cookie is being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to refuse Cookies, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome files let the Company, for example, count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out a section's popularity and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely essential to give you the services available through the Website and enable you to rock some of its features. They help authenticate users and prevent sketchy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to check if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more deets about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding Cookies, hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
