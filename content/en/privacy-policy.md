### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're big fans of Cookies and similar tracking technologies. We use them to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. These tech tools, like beacons, tags, and scripts, help us gather and track data, ultimately making our Service better and letting us analyze its usage. Here's a peek at what we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: Think of a tiny file that lands on your Device. You're totally in control – you can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's trying to send. Just a heads-up, though: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. But hey, unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say 'no thanks' to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and even our emails might feature tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited certain pages or opened an email. They're also used for other cool website stats, like tracking the popularity of a section or ensuring system and server integrity.

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for dishing out the services available via the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're super helpful for authenticating users and keeping those pesky fraudulent account uses at bay. Seriously, without these Cookies, the services you've asked for just wouldn't happen, and we only deploy them to make sure you get those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about remembering if you've given us the thumbs-up to use Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are like your personal assistant, enabling us to remember your choices as you navigate the Website – think remembering your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more tailor-made experience, saving you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you visit the Website.

Want to know more about the Cookies we use and what choices you've got? Head over to our Cookies Policy or just check out the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
