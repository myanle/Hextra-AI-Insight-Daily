### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. These tracking technologies include cool stuff like beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, plus seriously boost and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your Device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But here's the catch: if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service might just use 'em anyway.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might feature tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome files let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other juicy website stats, like tracking the popularity of a certain section and making sure our systems and servers are all good to go. ✅

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang out on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few key reasons, like:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're ace at authenticating users and stopping any sketchy, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just can't happen, and we only use them to bring those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to check if users have given their 'ok' to the use of cookies on the Website. Simple as that! ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are our secret sauce for remembering choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you visit the Website. Sweet, right? 😉

For more juicy details about the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or hit up the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. You'll find it all there! 👇
