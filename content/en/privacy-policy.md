### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We utilize Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain bits of information. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, and generally improve and analyze our Service. The tech we deploy might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one is being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to enjoy some parts of our Service. Unless you've adjusted your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service might use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome little files allow the Company, for instance, to count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website statistics (like checking out how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity). ✨

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for giving you the services available through the Website and enabling you to use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those specific services to you. 🙏

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to check if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website. So, basically, they remember if you're cool with cookies. 😉

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of having to re-enter your preferences every single time you visit the Website. ✨

For more info about the Cookies we use and your options concerning them, feel free to check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
