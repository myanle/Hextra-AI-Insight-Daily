### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're using Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and to stash some info. These tracking goodies include things like beacons, tags, and scripts – all used to collect info, track stuff, and seriously improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that gets placed on your device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But heads up, if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and even our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). They're pretty cool because they let us, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other website stats, like checking how popular a section is or making sure our systems and servers are all in tip-top shape.

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones stick around on your computer or mobile device even after you go offline. Session Cookies? Poof! They're gone as soon as you close your web browser.

We rock both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Who runs 'em**: Us, of course!
  - **Why we use 'em**: These Cookies are super important for getting you the services available via our Website and letting you use some of its awesome features. They're key to verifying users and shutting down any dodgy use of user accounts. No these Cookies, no services for you! So yeah, we only use 'em to make sure you get what you asked for.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Who runs 'em**: Still us!
  - **Why we use 'em**: These little guys simply help us remember if you've already given us the nod to use Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Who runs 'em**: You guessed it, us!
  - **Why we use 'em**: These Cookies are neat because they help us remember the choices you make while using the Website – like keeping you logged in or recalling your language preference. The whole point is to give you a smoother, more personalized experience so you don't have to keep punching in your preferences every single time.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what choices you've got? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy.
