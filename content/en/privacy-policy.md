### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking tech we deploy includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, plus to supercharge and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file plonked on your device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't play nice with Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject 'em, our Service might just use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain bits of our Service and our emails might pack tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These snazzy files let the Company (for instance) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like tracking how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline, while Session Cookies get the boot the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and stop any dodgy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies figure out if users have given the thumbs-up to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For the full scoop on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
