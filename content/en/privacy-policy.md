### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
Okay, let's talk *tracking tech*! We use Cookies and similar goodies to watch what's happening on our Service and save some data. We're talking beacons, tags, and scripts – all tools to gather info, track stuff, and ultimately make our Service better. Here's a peek at what we use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: These are tiny files that land on your device. You're in control, though! You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's coming. Just remember, if you opt out, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've specifically told your browser "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service will likely use them.
- **Web Beacons**: You might find these tiny electronic files (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) lurking in some parts of our Service or in our emails. They let us do things like count how many folks have visited a page or opened an email. Basically, they help us with website stats, like seeing how popular a section is or double-checking our systems.

FYI: *Cookies* come in two flavors – "Persistent" and "Session." Persistent ones hang out on your device even after you're offline, while Session Cookies pack up and leave as soon as you close your browser. Poof! 💨

Why do *we use both Session and Persistent Cookies*? We've got our reasons, spelled out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: Okay, first up: *Necessary / Essential Cookies*. These are Session Cookies, and yep, we manage them. Their whole point? They're totally vital for getting you the services you need on our Website and letting you use all its cool features. They're like the bouncers, helping to verify who you are and stop any funny business with accounts. Seriously, without 'em, the services you want just wouldn't work, and we *only* use these to make sure you get 'em.

- **Cookies Policy / Notification Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: Next, *Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies*. These are Persistent Cookies, and again, we're the managers. Their gig? They simply remember if you've already given a thumbs-up to using cookies on our Website. Easy peasy! 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: Last but not least, *Functionality Cookies*. These are also Persistent Cookies, managed by us. Their goal? To remember all the smart choices you make while using our Website – things like your login deets or language preference. They're all about giving you a smoother, more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep typing in the same stuff over and over. Smart, right? 🧠

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what your options are? Just hop over to our Cookies Policy or check out the Cookies section within this Privacy Policy. All the deets are there! 🍪
