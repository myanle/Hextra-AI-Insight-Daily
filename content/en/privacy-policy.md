### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
Alright, so we're using Cookies and other sneaky tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and tuck away some info. We're talking beacons, tags, and scripts here – all to gather and track data, helping us make our Service better and analyze its usage. These technologies could include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: First up: Cookies or Browser Cookies. They're just tiny files chilling on your Device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's coming. But beware, if you snub Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've already told your browser to say 'no thanks' to Cookies, our Service will probably use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Then there are Web Beacons. Think of these as tiny digital snoops – little electronic files (also called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs) hidden in parts of our Service and emails. They let us do things like count how many folks visited certain pages or opened an email and help with other website stats, like seeing what's popular or checking if our systems are running smoothly.

Cookies come in two main flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile even after you're offline. Session Cookies, though? Poof! They're gone the moment you close your web browser.

We're using both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are essential to provide You with services available through the Website and to enable You to use some of its features. They help to authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services that You have asked for cannot be provided, and We only use these Cookies to provide You with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies identify if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functional Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies allow us to remember choices You make when You use the Website, such as remembering your login details or language preference. The purpose of these Cookies is to provide You with a more personal experience and to avoid You having to re-enter your preferences every time You use the Website.

Wanna know more about our Cookies and what your options are? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in this Privacy Policy. 🍪
