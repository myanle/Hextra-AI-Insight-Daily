### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and other similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and to store some info. These tracking tools include things like beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to gather and track data, helping us to improve and understand how you use our Service. Here’s a peek at what we might use:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your device. You're free to tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to alert you when one's trying to get sent. But heads up: if you don't play along with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Our Service will likely use Cookies unless you've specifically told your browser to refuse them.
-   **Web Beacons**: Think of Web Beacons as tiny electronic files (sometimes called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs) tucked into parts of our Service and emails. These little guys help us do things like count how many folks have checked out those pages or opened an email, and they help with other website stats too, like figuring out how popular a section is or just making sure our systems and servers are running smoothly.

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" and "Session." Persistent Cookies stick around on your computer or mobile device even after you've logged off, but Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few reasons, which we'll spell out below:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These are crucial for getting you the services available through our Website and letting you use all its cool features. They help us confirm who you are and stop any funny business with user accounts. Seriously, without these, we can't give you the services you're asking for, and that's the *only* reason we use them.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: Their simple task is to remember if you've already given us the thumbs-up to use cookies on the Website.

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These smart cookies help us recall your choices when you're using the Website, like remembering your login info or what language you prefer. Basically, they're here to give you a smoother, more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep punching in your preferences every single time.

Want more details on the Cookies we use and your options? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in this Privacy Policy.
