### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away some info. The tracking technologies we roll with include beacons, tags, and scripts. These are all about collecting and tracking info, and then using it to improve and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to let you know when a Cookie is being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've adjusted your browser setting to refuse Cookies, our Service might just use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome files let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email and for other related website stats (like recording the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. When you're offline, Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device. On the flip side, Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following awesome purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial to give you services available through the Website and to enable you to use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about identifying if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functional Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more deets on the Cookies we use and your choices regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or hit up the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. Easy peasy! 😉
