### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and to store certain information. These tracking tech tools include beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to collect and track info, helping us improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include: 🛠️

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file that gets cozy on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, if you don't play nice with Cookies, you might find some parts of our Service aren't quite accessible. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service might just use 'em. 🍪
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for instance, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other cool website stats, like noting the popularity of a certain section and double-checking system and server integrity. Sneaky smart! 🧐

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill out on your personal computer or mobile device even after you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 👻

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following purposes: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for providing you with available services through the Website and letting you use some of its features. They're super helpful in authenticating users and slamming the door on fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be delivered, and we only use them to get those services to you. So yeah, they're kind of a big deal! 💪

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies help us figure out if users have given the thumbs-up to using cookies on the Website. 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are awesome because they let us remember choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or your language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you visit. Convenient, right? ✨

For more deets about the cookies we use and your options regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. You got this! 🤓
