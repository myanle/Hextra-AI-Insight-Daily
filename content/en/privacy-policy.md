### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. These tracking goodies include beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to collect, track, and generally beef up and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one’s being sent. But hey, if you don't play along with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've tweaked your browser to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These bad boys let the Company (for example) count users who have hit up those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like noting how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies get wiped the moment you close your web browser. 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and stop any sneaky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to bring you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are for figuring out if users have given the thumbs-up to the use of cookies on the Website. 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more customized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you visit the Website. Pretty neat, huh?

For more deets on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, feel free to swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
