### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about using **Cookies and similar tracking technologies** to keep tabs on what's happening on our service and to stash away certain info. These tracking technologies – think beacons, tags, and scripts – are awesome for gathering and tracking data, all to help us beef up and analyze our service. Here's a peek at what we might use: 👀

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: Alright, so a **Cookie** is just a tiny file that gets popped onto your device. You're the boss here; you can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a nudge when one's trying to send. But just a friendly warning: if you don't play nice with Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our service. Unless you've gone into your browser settings and specifically said "no way" to Cookies, chances are our service will be using 'em. 💻
- **Web Beacons**: Ever heard of **Web Beacons**? These are tiny electronic files (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs) that might be hiding in some parts of our service or even in our emails. They're pretty neat because they let us, for instance, count how many users have visited those pages or opened an email. Plus, they help us with other cool website stats, like tracking a section's popularity and making sure our system and server integrity are solid. 💪

Just a heads-up: Cookies come in two main flavors – "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. The **Persistent Cookies** stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline. On the flip side, **Session Cookies**? They get wiped out the second you close your web browser. Poof! 🌬️

We actually use both **Session and Persistent Cookies** for a few good reasons, which we've laid out right below: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These **Cookies** are absolutely crucial for getting you the services available via our Website and letting you use some of its neat features. They're like our security guards, helping us authenticate users and preventing any sneaky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Seriously, without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't be possible, and we only use them to make sure you get those essential services. They're non-negotiable! ✅

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These **Cookies** are pretty straightforward – they just help us remember if you've already given your "okay" to our use of Cookies on the Website. One less pop-up for you! 😉

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These **Cookies** are pretty smart! They help us remember the choices you make while using the Website – things like your login deets or your preferred language. The main goal here is to give you a super personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you pop onto the site. Talk about convenience! 🥳

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what choices you've got regarding them? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or hit up the Cookies section of *this* Privacy Policy. We've got all the deets there! 🤓
