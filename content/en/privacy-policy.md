### Tracking Tech & Cookies
Cookies and similar tracking technologies are what we use to keep tabs on activity on our service and stash away certain info. These tracking tools include beacons, tags, and scripts – all designed to collect and track data, helping us polish and analyze our service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your device. You can tell your browser to give all Cookies the cold shoulder or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But here's the catch: if you don't play nice with Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our service. Unless you've fiddled with your browser settings to reject 'em, our service will likely use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Some corners of our service and our emails might contain these tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These bad boys let the Company (for instance) count users who've visited certain pages or opened an email. They also help with other website stats, like noting how popular a section is and making sure our systems and servers are all good.

Cookies come in two flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both session and persistent Cookies for these cool reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for hooking you up with the services available through the Website and letting you use its awesome features. They help us confirm who you are and put a stop to any shady use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't happen, and we only use them to give you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Notice Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are for figuring out if users have given us the nod to use Cookies on the Website. Like, "Yep, I'm cool with that!" 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super helpful because they let us remember your choices when you're using the Website – like keeping your login info or language preferences locked in. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your faves every single time you visit.

For more deets on the Cookies we use and your options concerning them, just hop over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
