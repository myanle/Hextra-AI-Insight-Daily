### Tracking Technologies and Cookies

We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, which help us collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to give all Cookies the cold shoulder or to ping you when one's being sent. - **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and they're used for other related website stats (like noting a section's popularity and checking the system and server's integrity).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies get wiped the moment you shut down your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent sketchy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to spot if users have given the thumbs-up to Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more info about the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
