### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
**Cookies and similar tracking technologies** are what we use to track activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all to collect and track info, and to improve and analyze our Service. The technologies we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: **A Cookie** is a tiny file dropped on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to flag when a Cookie is being sent. But, if you don't take Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: **Certain sections of our Service and our emails** may contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also called clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These files let the Company, for example, count users who have hit those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out a section's popularity and verifying system and server integrity).

**Cookies** can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. **Persistent Cookies** stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while **Session Cookies** vanish as soon as you close your web browser.

**Both Session and Persistent Cookies** are used by us for the purposes outlined below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: **These Cookies** are crucial for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: **These Cookies** figure out if users have given the green light to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: **These Cookies** let us remember choices you make when you use the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preference. The goal of these Cookies is to give you a more personal experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every time you use the Website.

**For more info about the Cookies we use** and your choices regarding them, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
