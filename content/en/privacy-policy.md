### Tracking Technologies & Cookies 🍪
**We** use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep an eye on activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file chillin' on your Device. You can tell your browser to give all Cookies the cold shoulder or to ping you when a Cookie's about to be sent. But hey, if you don't play nice with Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These bad boys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out how popular a certain section is and making sure the system and server are running solid).

**Cookies** can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. When you're offline, Persistent Cookies stay put on your personal computer or mobile device. Session Cookies, on the other hand, vanish quicker than a free pizza slice once you close your web browser.

**We** use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for hookin' you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help verify users and prevent sketchy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to serve 'em up to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given the green light to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're cruising around the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every time you use the Website.

For more info about the Cookies we use and your options regarding Cookies, zip over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
