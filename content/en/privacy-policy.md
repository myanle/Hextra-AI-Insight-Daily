### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're big fans of Cookies and other tracking tech. 🍪 Why? To keep an eye on activity on our Service and stash away some info. We're talking about beacons, tags, and scripts – all used to collect and track data, helping us improve and understand our Service better. Here's what we might use:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies:** These are tiny files dropped onto your device. You're in control! Tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But just a warning: if you don't play nice with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've specifically told your browser "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
-   **Web Beacons:** Think of these as tiny invisible electronic files hiding in some parts of our Service and our emails. (They go by fancy names like clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs too!) What do they do? They help us, for instance, count how many users popped into those pages or opened an email. Plus, they contribute to other cool website stats, like tracking a section's popularity or double-checking system and server health.

Cookies come in two main flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. Ever wonder what happens when you go offline? Persistent Cookies stick around on your computer or mobile device. But Session Cookies? Poof! They vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We're using both Session and Persistent Cookies for some specific reasons, which we'll spill below:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type:** Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by:** Us
    -   **Purpose:** These bad boys are absolutely crucial for giving you all the services on our Website and letting you use its cool features. They're like our bouncers, helping to verify user identities and kick out any fraudulent account use. Without 'em, the services you're asking for just wouldn't happen, so we only use these to make sure you get what you came for!

-   **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type:** Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by:** Us
    -   **Purpose:** These Cookies simply remember if you've already given us the thumbs-up to use cookies on the Website. One less pop-up for you! 😉

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type:** Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by:** Us
    -   **Purpose:** These clever Cookies help us recall your choices as you navigate the Website – things like your login deets or what language you prefer. The goal? To give you a super personalized experience, so you don't have to keep punching in your preferences every single time. How handy is that? ✨

Want more info on the Cookies we use and your options? 🍪 Just hop over to our Cookies Policy or check out the Cookies section of this very Privacy Policy!
