### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activity on our Service and stash away some info. These tracking tech tools include beacons, tags, and scripts – they're all about gathering and tracking info, plus helping us spruce up and analyze our Service. The specific tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file dropped onto your device. You're in control! You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's trying to send. But here's the catch: if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say 'no thanks' to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.

- **Web Beacons**: So, some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These little guys let the Company, say, count how many folks visited certain pages or opened an email. They're also super handy for other website stats, like checking how popular a section is or making sure our systems and servers are running smoothly.

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your computer or mobile device even after you go offline, but Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the reasons listed below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital for us to give you the services available on the Website and let you use some of its cool features. They're super helpful for verifying who's who and stopping any funky business with user accounts. Honestly, without these Cookies, the services you're after just wouldn't be possible, and we only use them to make those services happen for you. ✨

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about remembering if you've given us the thumbs-up to use Cookies on the Website. 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty neat because they let us remember your choices when you're using the Website – stuff like your login details or what language you prefer. The whole point of these is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep typing in your preferences every single time you swing by the Website. It's all about making things smoother! 😎

Wanna dive deeper into the Cookies we use and your options? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in this Privacy Policy. You got this! 👇
