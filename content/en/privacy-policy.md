### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're talking Cookies and similar Tracking Technologies here! We use them to keep tabs on what's happening on Our Service and to stash away certain info. The Tracking Technologies We employ include beacons, tags, and scripts – these are all about gathering and tracking data to make Our Service better and to analyze its usage. What kind of tech are We talking about? It might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file placed on Your Device. You can tell Your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give You a heads-up when one is being sent. But, if You don't accept Cookies, You might not be able to use certain parts of Our Service. Unless You've tweaked Your browser setting to refuse Cookies, Our Service might just go ahead and use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of Our Service and Our emails might feature small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These cool little files let the Company (for example) count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like noting the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

Here's the deal with Cookies: they can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies, as the name suggests, stick around on Your personal computer or mobile device even after You've gone offline. Session Cookies, on the other hand, just poof! — they're deleted the instant You close Your web browser.

So, why do We use both Session and Persistent Cookies? Here's the rundown:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are an absolute must to get You the services available through the Website and to let You use some of its features. They help verify user identity and stop any dodgy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services You've asked for can't be provided, and We only use these Cookies to bring You those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are here to figure out if users have given the thumbs-up to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let Us remember the choices You make when You use the Website, like remembering Your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give You a more personalized experience and save You the hassle of re-entering Your preferences every single time You use the Website.

Wanna know more about the Cookies We use and Your options concerning them? Swing by Our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of Our Privacy Policy!
