### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪
We use Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash some info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. Here's a peek at the tech we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies:** These are just small files placed on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't play nice with Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service might just use them anyway.
- **Web Beacons:** Some parts of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These nifty little things let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and help out with other website stats (like checking a section's popularity and verifying system and server integrity). So, yeah, they're pretty useful!

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type:** Session Cookies
  - **Managed by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are super important for dishing out the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its features. They help verify user identities and put a stop to any dodgy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't happen, and we only use these Cookies to make sure you get those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are used to check if users have given the thumbs up to Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website. Sweet! 👍

For more info about the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
