### Tracking Technologies and Cookies

Alright, so we're using Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and squirrel away some info. These tracking technologies — think beacons, tags, and scripts — help us gather and track info, and generally make our Service better and analyze its usage. Some of the tech we use might include:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a small file chilling on your device. You can tell your browser to give all Cookies the boot or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, heads-up: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to reject 'em, our Service might just be using Cookies.
-   **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These little guys let us, for example, count users who've visited certain pages or opened an email. They're also handy for other website stats, like seeing how popular a section is or making sure our systems and servers are all good to go. 🕵️‍♀️

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you're offline. Session Cookies? Poof! They vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital to give you the services available through the Website and let you use some of its cool features. They help verify users and put a stop to any funky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't happen, and we only use them to provide those services to you. Pretty important, right?

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to check if users have given a thumbs-up to the use of Cookies on the Website.

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember your choices when you're using the Website, like keeping your login deets or language preferences in mind. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, saving you from having to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the Website. Sweet!

Want more deets on the Cookies we use and your options concerning them? Swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy. 🍪
