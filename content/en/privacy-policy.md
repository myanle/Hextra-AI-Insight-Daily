### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and squirrel away certain information. The tracking technologies we're talking about include beacons, tags, and scripts. We deploy these to collect and track information, helping us spruce up and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

-   **Cookies, or Browser Cookies**: These are tiny files tucked onto your Device. You're in control, and can tell your browser to just say no to all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But here's the catch: if you don't play ball with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to reject them, our Service is likely using Cookies.
-   **Web Beacons**: These are little electronic files you might find lurking in certain parts of our Service and our emails. They're also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs. These handy files let the Company, for instance, count who's visited specific pages or opened an email. Plus, they're super useful for other website stats, like seeing how popular a section is or checking that our system and servers are running smoothly.

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. The Persistent ones stick around on your computer or mobile device even after you're offline. Session Cookies, on the other hand, vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for specific reasons, outlined right here:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are the real MVPs here. They're absolutely vital for us to deliver the services available on the Website and let you use its cool features. They help us verify who you are and put a stop to any fishy business with user accounts. Seriously, without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't happen. We only use them to get you those services.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: Their whole deal is to remember if you've already given us the thumbs-up to use Cookies on the Website.

-   **Functional Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These are pretty neat. They let us remember your choices when you're using the Website—things like your login deets or your preferred language. The big idea here is to give you a super personalized experience, so you don't have to punch in your preferences every single time you swing by the Website.

For more details on the Cookies we use and your choices regarding them, just pop over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
