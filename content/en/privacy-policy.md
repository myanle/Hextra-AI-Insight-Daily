### Cookies and Other Cool Trackers
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep an eye on activity on our Service and store some bits of info. The tracking tech We use includes things like beacons, tags, and scripts. They help Us collect and track info, and generally make our Service better and more insightful. The technologies We use might include:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies** 🍪: A Cookie is a tiny file that gets placed on Your Device. You can tell Your browser to give all Cookies the boot or to alert You when one's being sent. But heads up: if You don't take Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for You. Unless You've tweaked Your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service will likely use them.
-   **Web Beacons** ✨: These are tiny electronic files (also called clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs) that might pop up in certain sections of our Service and our emails. They let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other cool stuff like website statistics (e.g., checking out popular sections and making sure our systems are solid).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on Your personal computer or mobile Device even when You're offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment You close Your web browser.

Why do We use both Session and Persistent Cookies? Glad You asked! Here's the lowdown:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies** 🔑
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for Us to deliver the services available through the Website and to let You use its awesome features. They help verify user identity and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these, the services You're asking for just wouldn't happen, and We only use these Cookies to get those services to You.
-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies** ✅
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies simply confirm if You've accepted our use of Cookies on the Website.
-   **Functionality Cookies** 🛠️
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies let Us remember the choices You make when You're using the Website, like keeping Your login deets or language preference in mind. The goal is to give You a more personalized experience, so You don't have to re-enter Your preferences every single time You visit.

For even more juicy details about the Cookies We use and Your choices regarding them, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
