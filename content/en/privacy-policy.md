### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and to save a few bits of info. These tracking goodies include things like Beacons, Tags, and Scripts, all helping us gather data, keep track, and ultimately make our Service even better. Here's a peek at what we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that lands on your device. You can totally tell your browser to say "no thanks" to all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's trying to get sent. But heads up, if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've fiddled with your browser settings to block 'em, our Service might still use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear 'em called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These let the Company, for instance, tally up users who've checked out those pages or opened an email, and help with other website stats (like tracking how popular a section is or double-checking system and server health).

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones stick around on your computer or phone even after you're offline, but Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof!

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies** <br> - **Type**: Session Cookies <br> - **Administered by**: Us <br> - **Purpose**: These Cookies are a must-have for giving you the services available on the Website and letting you use all its cool features. They're super helpful for verifying who you are and keeping shady folks from messing with user accounts. No jokes, without these Cookies, the stuff you're asking for can't happen, and we only use 'em to bring you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies** <br> - **Type**: Persistent Cookies <br> - **Administered by**: Us <br> - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply let us know if you've given the green light to use cookies on our Website. Easy peasy.

- **Functionality Cookies** <br> - **Type**: Persistent Cookies <br> - **Administered by**: Us <br> - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super handy because they help us remember your choices when you're using the Website—things like your login deets or your preferred language. The whole point is to give you a smoother, more personalized experience so you don't have to keep typing in your preferences every single time you visit.

Wanna know more about the cookies we use and your options? Just hop over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy!
