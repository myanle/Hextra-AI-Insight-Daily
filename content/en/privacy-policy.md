### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking technologies we deploy include beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to collect and track information, and to boost and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file dropped onto your device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to alert you when one is being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to decline Cookies, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome files let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like noting the popularity of a certain section and double-checking system and server integrity).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies get nuked the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital to get you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website. 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personal experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you hit up the Website. 😎

For more info about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding cookies, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
