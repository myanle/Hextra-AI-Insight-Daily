### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and other similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activity within our Service and store some information. These tracking tools include beacons, tags, and scripts, all aimed at collecting and monitoring info, plus helping us improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your device. You can set your browser to reject all Cookies or to alert you when one is being sent. But, heads up! If you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say 'no' to Cookies, chances are our Service will be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service, and even our emails, might contain tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let us, for example, count users who've visited certain pages or opened an email, and help with other website stats like checking a section's popularity or ensuring system and server integrity.

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies remain on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, while Session Cookies are deleted as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes set out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely essential for providing you with the services available through our Website and enabling you to use some of its features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent activity on user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've requested simply can't be delivered, and we use them solely for that purpose.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply remember if you've given us the green light to use cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember your choices when you're using the Website – things like your login details or language preference. Basically, they're here to give you a more personal experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you visit!

Want to know more about the Cookies we use and your options? Just hop over to our Cookies Policy or check out the Cookies section in this Privacy Policy.
