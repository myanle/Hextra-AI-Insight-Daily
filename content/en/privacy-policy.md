### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
🍪 We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. These tracking goodies include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, plus make our Service even better and help us analyze how it's being used. Here's a peek at the tech we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is essentially a tiny file chillin' on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. Just a friendly warning, though: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. But hey, unless you've set your browser to refuse 'em, our Service will likely be using Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These handy files let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email, and they're super useful for other website stats too (like checking out a section's popularity or making sure our system and server are running smoothly).

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few key reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital for us to deliver the services available through the Website and help you rock its features. They're all about authenticating users and stopping any shady, fraudulent use of accounts. Seriously, without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't happen, and We only use 'em to give you those services. ✅

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are the little identifiers that tell us if you've given a thumbs-up to using cookies on the Website.

- **Functional Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make while browsing the Website – stuff like your login deets or language preference. Their whole point is to give you a more personal experience, so you don't have to keep re-entering your preferences every single time you visit.

Want more info on the Cookies we use and what choices you have about 'em? Just head over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
