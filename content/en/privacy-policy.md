### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash certain information. The tracking technologies we deploy include beacons, tags, and scripts, all aimed at collecting and tracking info, plus improving and analyzing our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one is being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service might just go ahead and use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also called clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These nifty files allow the Company, for example, to count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website statistics (like noting the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital to hook you up with services available through the Website and enable you to use some of its neat features. They help authenticate users and prevent dodgy, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notification Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have given the green light to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, such as recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to serve you a more personal experience, saving you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more info on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
