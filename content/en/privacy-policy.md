Usage Data might include stuff like your device's Internet Protocol address (your IP, for short), what browser you're using (and its version), which pages you hit up on our Service, when you visited and at what time, how long you lingered on those pages, unique identifiers for your device, and other techy diagnostic bits.

We might also collect info your browser sends our way when you visit our Service, whether you're on a computer or cruising on your mobile.

### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We totally use Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away some info. These tracking goodies include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track data, helping us spruce up and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file plonked on your device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But heads up, if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject 'em, our Service might just use Cookies.

- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These little guys let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email, and are used for other website stats (like checking a section's popularity or making sure our systems and servers are all good).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones stick around on your computer or mobile even after you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof!

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the reasons we're about to lay out for ya: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for us to even give you the services available via the Website and let you use some of its cool features. They help verify who's who and stop any shady, fraudulent use of accounts. Seriously, without these, the services you're asking for just wouldn't happen, and we only use 'em to deliver those very services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if you've given the green light to use cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember your choices when you're using the Website, like your login deets or what language you prefer. The whole point is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to punch in your preferences every single time you visit. So convenient!

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what choices you've got? Just pop over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this very Privacy Policy.
