### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away some info. The tracking technologies we deploy include beacons, tags, and scripts. These are all about gathering and tracking info, plus improving and analyzing our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to say 'nope' to all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't play along with Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service might just use them anyway.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain these tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These bad boys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other cool website stats (like checking a section's popularity and verifying system and server integrity). ✨

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 👻

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for hooking you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its snazzy features. They help verify user identities and prevent any fishy, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to make sure you get those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about checking if users have given the green light to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty cool because they let us remember choices you make while using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you visit the Website. 🙌

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options regarding them? Swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
