### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We leverage **Cookies and similar tracking technologies** to keep an eye on activity within our Service and store certain bits of information. These tracking tech tools — things like beacons, tags, and scripts — help us gather and track info, and ultimately, make our Service better and analyze how it's being used. Here are some of the technologies we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to alert you when one's being sent. Heads up, though: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service will likely use them.

- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and even our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These handy files let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other website stats (like checking how popular a section is or verifying system and server integrity). Pretty neat, huh? ✨

**Cookies** can be either 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof!

We use both **Session and Persistent Cookies** for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and stop any dodgy, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use 'em to give you those services. No kidding! 🛠️

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about checking if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website. Simple as that! ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super helpful because they let us remember choices you make when you're using the Website, like keeping your login deets or language preferences in mind. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep re-entering your preferences every single time you visit. Sweet! ✨

For more deets about the **Cookies we use and your options** concerning them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. We've got you covered!
