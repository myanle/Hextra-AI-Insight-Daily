### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We roll with Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activity on our Service and store certain info. These tracking tech tools — think beacons, tags, and scripts — help us gather and track info, plus boost and analyze our Service. The tech we use could include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file chilling on your Device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But heads up: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Our Service might just use Cookies unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain parts of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (aka clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These guys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and they're also used for other related website stats (like checking how popular a section is and making sure the system and servers are working right).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are crucial for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies remember if users have given the thumbs-up to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us recall the choices you make when you use the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the Website.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options concerning them? Swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
