### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and other similar tracking tech (think beacons, tags, and scripts) to keep tabs on activity within our Service and store certain info. This helps us gather and track data, ultimately making our Service better and giving us cool insights. 😎 Here's a peek at the tech we might be using:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies)**: These are those tiny files that land on your device. You've got the power to tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. 🛑 Just a heads-up though: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say 'no thanks' to Cookies, our Service will likely use them.
- **Web Beacons**: These are like tiny digital spies! 🕵️‍♀️ You might find these small electronic files (sometimes called clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs) tucked into parts of our Service or our emails. They help us, for instance, count how many users have visited certain pages or opened an email, and gather other cool website stats (like seeing how popular a section is or making sure our systems are running smoothly).

So, Cookies come in two flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. Think of Persistent Cookies as the ones that stick around on your computer or phone even after you log off. 🛋️ Session Cookies? They're more fleeting—gone in a flash once you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few key reasons, which we've laid out below: 👇

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are non-negotiable! 🔑 They're absolutely essential for delivering the services you find on our Website and letting you tap into certain features. They're also super helpful for verifying who's who and stopping any funky business with user accounts. Seriously, without these, the services you're asking for just wouldn't work, and we *only* use them to give you those vital services.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are our little memory keepers for whether you've given us the thumbs-up on using cookies on the Website. 👍

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about making your life easier! 🧠 They let us remember your choices when you're cruising the Website, like your login info or what language you prefer. The whole point is to give you a smoother, more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep typing in your preferences every single time. Awesome, right? ✨

Wanna know more about the cookies we use and your options? 🍪 Swing by our Cookies Policy or just check out the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy!
