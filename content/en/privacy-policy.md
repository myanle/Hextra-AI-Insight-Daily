### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪

**We use Cookies and similar tracking tech** to keep tabs on activity within our Service and store certain info. This tracking tech includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all working to gather and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file popped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't take Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service might just use 'em.
-   **Web Beacons**: Some bits of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These handy files let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other related website stats (like checking out how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity).

**Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies.** While you're offline, Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device. Session Cookies, on the other hand, vanish the moment you close your web browser.

**We use both Session and Persistent Cookies** for the following cool reasons:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Managed by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital to hook you up with services available through the Website and let you use some of its neat features. They help us confirm user identity and keep fraudulent user account use at bay. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't happen, and we only use 'em to deliver those services to you.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Managed by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are here to figure out if users have given the green light to using Cookies on the Website.

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Managed by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super helpful because they let us remember choices you make while using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. Their whole point is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you visit.

**For more deets** on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, feel free to swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
