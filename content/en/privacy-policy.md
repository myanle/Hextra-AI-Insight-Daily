### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're using Cookies and other cool tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and store some info. 🍪 These trackers, like beacons, tags, and scripts, help us gather and follow data, making our Service better and giving us insights. Here's a peek at what we're using:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: Think of a Cookie as a tiny file chilling on Your Device. You can totally tell Your browser to say 'no thanks!' to all Cookies or to give You a heads-up when one's being sent. But, heads up, if You don't accept Cookies, some bits of our Service might not work for You. Unless Your browser settings are set to reject Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
-   **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (sometimes called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These clever little things help the Company count users who've popped onto those pages or opened an email, along with other cool website stats (like how popular a section is or checking if our systems are all good). ✨

Cookies come in two flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. Persistent ones hang around on Your personal computer or mobile device even after You've logged off, but Session Cookies vanish into thin air as soon as You close Your web browser. Poof!

We rock both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    *   **Type**: Session Cookies
    *   **Administered by**: Us (that's us!)
    *   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super essential! 🌟 They help us deliver the services You find on our Website and let You tap into all those cool features. They're key for authenticating users and keeping those pesky fraudulent account uses at bay. Without 'em, the services You want just wouldn't happen, and we only use these Cookies to make sure You get those services.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    *   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    *   **Administered by**: Us
    *   **Purpose**: These Cookies are like a little memory trick! They help us remember if You've given the green light to use cookies on our Website. ✅

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    *   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    *   **Administered by**: Us
    *   **Purpose**: These smart Cookies help us remember Your choices when You're using the Website – stuff like Your login details or what language You prefer. 🧠 The big idea? To give You a super personalized experience and save You from typing in Your preferences over and over again. No thanks to that! 🙅‍♀️

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what Your choices are? 🧐 Just pop over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy for all the juicy details!
