### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We totally use Cookies and other similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain info. These technologies include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to snag and track info, and generally pump up our Service's performance and analysis. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file dropped onto your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't play along with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say no to Cookies, our Service might just use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some bits of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (aka clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other cool website stats (like checking out a section's popularity or verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help verify users' identities and stop any sneaky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you ask for simply can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to make sure you get those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Notice Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to check if users have given their OK to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are awesome because they let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more details on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
