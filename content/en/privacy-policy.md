### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our Service and store certain information. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, which help us collect and track information, as well as improve and analyze our Service. The technologies we use might include:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to alert you when a Cookie is being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Our Service might use Cookies unless you've adjusted your browser setting to refuse them.
-   **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These files allow the Company, for example, to count users who have visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other related website statistics (like recording the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies get deleted the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following purposes:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super essential to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to bring you those services.

-   **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Notice Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies figure out if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like remembering your login details or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every time you use the Website.

For more info about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding them, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
