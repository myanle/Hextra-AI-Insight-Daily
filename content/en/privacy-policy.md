### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We totally use Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep an eye on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. These tracking technologies — like beacons, tags, and scripts — are all about collecting and tracking info, plus improving and analyzing our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a small file tucked away on your Device. You can tell your browser to totally reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. Heads up though, if you don't take Cookies, you might not be able to use certain cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies? They can either be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you slam shut your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help verify users and put a stop to any dodgy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't happen, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have given the green light to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of having to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the Website.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options concerning them? Swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
