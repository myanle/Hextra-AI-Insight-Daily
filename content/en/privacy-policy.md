### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. These tracking technologies include things like beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to gather and track info, plus polish and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your device. You can tell your browser to straight-up refuse all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. However, if you don't play along with Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject 'em, our Service might just be using Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Some slices of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also know them as clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These bad boys let the Company, for example, tally up users who've visited those pages or opened emails, and they're also used for other juicy website stats (like tracking how popular a certain section is and making sure our systems and servers are all good).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air the moment you close your web browser. ✨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for us to deliver the services available through the Website and let you use its awesome features. They're key to authenticating users and stopping any shady, fraudulent use of user accounts. No joke, without these Cookies, the services you ask for simply can't happen, and we only use 'em to give you what you need.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are basically our little helpers to remember if you've already said "yes!" to using Cookies on the Website. 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are awesome because they let us remember your choices when you're rocking the Website, like keeping your login info or language preference stored. The whole point is to give you a super personalized experience and save you the hassle of typing in your preferences every single time you visit. 🙏

For even more deets on the Cookies we use and what choices you have about 'em, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
