### Tracking Tech and Cookies
Cookies and similar tracking tech are our go-to for keeping tabs on activity within our Service and storing some deets. 🕵️‍♀️ The tracking tech we roll with includes beacons, tags, and scripts – all used to collect and track info, and of course, to jazz up and analyze our Service. Here's a peek at what we use:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies):** What's a Cookie? It's a tiny file chilling on your device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But a fair warning: if you don't play nice with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not be accessible to you. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to say 'no thanks' to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons:** Some corners of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've hit up specific pages or opened emails. They're also used for other cool website stats, like tracking how popular a section is or double-checking system and server integrity.

So, Cookies can be either 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. The Persistent ones stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline. Session Cookies, on the other hand, peace out the moment you close your web browser.

We actually rock both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few different reasons, like these:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type:** Session Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Essential Cookies are super critical for us to deliver the services available via the Website and to let you use all its cool features. They're all about verifying users and stopping any sketchy, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you ask for just wouldn't be possible, and frankly, we only use 'em to bring those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are specifically for checking if you've given the green light to use Cookies on the Website. So, yeah, they remember your choice! 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** Functionality Cookies are pretty neat because they let us remember your choices when you're cruising around the Website. This could be remembering your login deets or your language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you from having to type in your preferences every single time you visit.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options? Head over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy for the full scoop! 🍪
