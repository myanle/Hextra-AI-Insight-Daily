### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar Tracking Technologies to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and to stash away certain info. The Tracking Technologies we deploy include cool stuff like beacons, tags, and scripts. They're all about collecting and tracking info, helping us level up and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include: 👇

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie? It's just a tiny file chillin' on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one is about to drop. But, fair warning: if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've specifically adjusted your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service will probably be using them. Just sayin'!

- **Web Beacons**: Heads up! Some parts of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (you might also hear them called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also super handy for other website stats, like tracking the popularity of a certain section or making sure our systems and servers are all good. Pretty neat, huh? ✨

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Think of it this way: Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline. Session Cookies? They get wiped out the second you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We rock both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely essential for us to deliver the services you find on the Website and to let you use some of its coolest features. They're like our security guards, helping verify user identities and stopping any dodgy use of user accounts. Honestly, without these Cookies, the services you're after just wouldn't work, and we only use 'em to give you those services. No kidding! 🔒

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply remember if you've given us the thumbs-up to use Cookies on the Website. One click, done! 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty awesome because they let us remember your choices when you're cruising around the Website – stuff like your login info or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a super personalized experience and save you the headache of re-typing your preferences every single time you visit. Sweet! 🧠
