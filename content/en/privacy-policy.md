### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activity on our Service and store certain information. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, and to improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include: 👇

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file plopped onto your device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to let you know when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've adjusted your browser settings to say 'no thanks' to Cookies, our Service might just use 'em anyway. 🤷‍♀️
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys allow the Company to, for example, count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website statistics (like recording the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity). Sneaky, but useful! 😉

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! Gone. 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services. No kidding! 🔒

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies figure out if users have given the thumbs-up to the use of cookies on the Website. 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login details or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of having to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the Website. How convenient! ✨

For more info about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding cookies, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. Get informed! 📚
