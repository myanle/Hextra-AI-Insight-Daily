### Tracking Tech & Cookies 🍪
**Tracking Technologies and Cookies:** We totally use Cookies and other similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts. They help us collect and track info, plus make our Service even better and analyze how it's used. The tech we might employ includes:

- **Cookies (aka Browser Cookies):** A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service might just be using them. Just sayin'! 😉
- **Web Beacons:** Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other related website stats, like seeing how popular a certain section is or making sure the system and servers are all good to go.

**Persistent vs. Session Cookies:** Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline, while Session Cookies get the boot the second you close your web browser. 👋

**Our Cookie Usage:** We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following super important purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type:** Session Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are absolutely crucial for hookin' you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its features. They help verify users and prevent any funky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to give you those services. No joke! 💯

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies figure out if users have given us the green light to use Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website. Sweet! 🧠

**More Cookie Info:** For more deets on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, feel free to swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
