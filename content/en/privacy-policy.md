### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and to store certain info. This tracking tech includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to collect and track information, plus to improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file popped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to say "nope" to all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But heads up: if you don't take Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service might just use them anyway.
-   **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These bad boys let the Company, for instance, count users who've visited certain pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for hooking you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and give fraudulent user accounts the boot. Without these Cookies, the services you asked for just can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to give you those specific services.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to check if users have given a thumbs-up to the use of cookies on the Website.

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website. Sweet, right? 😎

For more deets on the Cookies we use and your options regarding Cookies, hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
