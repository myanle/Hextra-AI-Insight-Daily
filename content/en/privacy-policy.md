### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
Alright, let's talk tracking! 🍪 We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our service and stash away some info. The tracking tech we're using includes beacons, tags, and scripts – these bad boys help us collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our service. The tech we might use includes: 👇

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file chilling on your device. You can totally tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one is being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our service might be off-limits for ya. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our service might just use 'em anyway. Fair warning! 🤷‍♀️
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who have hit up those pages or opened an email, and they're also used for other related website stats (like tracking how popular a certain section is and checking the integrity of the system and server). Sneaky but useful! 😉

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. When you go offline, Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device. Session Cookies, however, vanish into thin air the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following awesome reasons: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for hooking you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're a big help in authenticating users and keeping user accounts safe from sketchy, fraudulent use. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't work, and we only use 'em to deliver those services right to ya. Straight up! 🔒

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given the green light 🚦 to using Cookies on the Website. Simple as that!

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies totally let us remember choices you've made while using the Website, like keeping track of your login info or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep re-entering your preferences every single time you hit up the Website. Super convenient! ✨

Wanna know even more about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding 'em? No sweat! Head over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy for all the deets. 📖
