### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
Cookies and similar tracking tech? We totally use 'em to keep tabs on what you're doing on Our Service and stash away certain info. We're talking about beacons, tags, and scripts – these guys help us gather and track info to constantly improve and analyze Our Service. The specific tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A **Cookie** is just a tiny file that lands on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to kick all Cookies to the curb or to give you a heads-up when one's trying to get sent. But heads up: if you don't play nice with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: **Web Beacons**? You might find these little electronic files (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs) hanging out in certain parts of our Service and even in our emails. They let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email. They're also super handy for other website stats, like seeing how popular a section is or just making sure our system and servers are all good to go.

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've logged off, while Session Cookies totally vanish the second you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

Session and Persistent Cookies? We use both, and here's why:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - These **Necessary / Essential Cookies** are absolutely crucial! They're like the backbone for getting you the services available through the Website and making sure you can actually use its cool features. They also help us verify who you are and stop any shady, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without 'em, the services you're asking for just can't happen, and honestly, we only use them to get those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - These **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies** are here to remember if you've already given your "okay" to use cookies on the Website. One less pop-up for you! 😉

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - These **Functionality Cookies** are pretty neat! They help us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website – like your login deets or what language you prefer. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep re-entering your preferences every single time you swing by the Website.

Want more deets about the cookies we use and what choices you have? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or peep the Cookies section in our main Privacy Policy. It's all there!
