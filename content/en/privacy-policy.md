### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🕵️‍♀️🍪

We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. This includes cool stuff like beacons, tags, and scripts – all used to collect, track, and ultimately make our Service even better and more insightful for you. Here's a peek at what we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: Cookies are just tiny files placed on your device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Web Beacons? These are tiny electronic files (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs) that might be chillin' in some parts of our Service and our emails. They let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. Plus, they help us with other sweet website stats, like tracking a section's popularity and making sure our systems and servers are all good.

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air as soon as you close your web browser.

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few specific reasons:

- **Necessary/Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These bad boys are crucial for getting you the services available through the Website and making sure you can use all its cool features. They help verify user identities and put the brakes on any dodgy user account activity. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't work, and we *only* use them to provide those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notification Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are our way of knowing if you've already given the green light to using cookies on the Website. So, we don't keep bugging you!

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are like your personal assistant, letting us remember choices you make when using the Website, such as your login deets or language prefs. The whole point? To give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you visit the Website. Smart, right? 🧠

Want the full lowdown on the Cookies we use and your options? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. We've got all the deets there!
