### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
**We** use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to monitor activity on our Service and store certain information. Tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts. We use them to collect and track information, and to improve and analyze our Service. The technologies We use may include:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies)**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to alert you when a Cookie is being sent. However, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Our Service might use Cookies unless you've adjusted your browser settings to refuse them.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These allow the Company, for example, to count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website statistics (e.g., checking how popular a section is and verifying system and server integrity).

**Cookies** can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stay on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, while Session Cookies are deleted as soon as you close your web browser.

**We** use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are crucial for providing you with services available through the Website and enabling you to use some of its features. They help verify user identity and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to give you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies help us recognize if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember choices you make when using the Website, such as your login details or language preference. Their goal is to give you a more personalized experience and save you from re-entering your preferences every time you use the Website.

**Want more info** about the cookies we use and your choices regarding cookies? Check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy.
