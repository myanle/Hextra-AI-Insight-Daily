### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking technologies we deploy include beacons, tags, and scripts, all aimed at collecting and tracking info to improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to alert you when one is being sent. However, if you don't accept Cookies, you might find some parts of our Service unavailable. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service might just use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These nifty files let the Company, for example, count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website statistics (e.g., tallying the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies get wiped clean the moment you close your web browser.

We rock both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely key to giving you the services available through the Website and enabling you to use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and stop any dodgy fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to check if users have given the green light to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies help us remember choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login details or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personal experience, saving you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you hit up the Website.

For more details about the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
