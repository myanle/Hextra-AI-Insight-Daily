## Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

### Types of Data Collected

- Usage Data

#### Usage Data
Usage Data? That's collected automatically, just by You using Our Service.

So, what's in this Usage Data? It can be stuff like Your Device's Internet Protocol address (yep, that's Your IP address), browser type and version, which of Our pages You check out, when You visited and for how long, unique device IDs, and other techy diagnostic bits.

Hitting up Our Service on Your mobile device? We might automatically snag some info like Your device type, its unique ID, IP address, operating system, the mobile browser You're using, and other diagnostic tidbits.

Also, Your browser sends Us info, and We might collect that too, whether You're on a computer or mobile.

### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and other savvy tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on Our Service and to stash away some info. We're talking beacons, tags, and scripts – all to gather data, track stuff, and seriously level up Our Service. Here's what We might use:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies, if You're fancy):** These are tiny files dropped onto Your Device. You've got the power to tell Your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give You a heads-up when one's being sent. Fair warning though, if You don't play nice with Cookies, some parts of Our Service might not work for You. So, unless You've specifically told Your browser "no" to Cookies, Our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons:** You might spot these tiny electronic files (also called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs) in some parts of Our Service or emails. They let Us, for instance, tally up users who've checked out a page or opened an email and help with other website stats like seeing what's popular or making sure Our systems are running smoothly.

Cookies are either "Persistent" or "Session" types. Think of Persistent Cookies as the ones that hang around on Your device even after You've logged off, while Session Cookies are like ghosts – gone the second You close Your browser.

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's why:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type:** Session Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** Okay, these Cookies are non-negotiable! They're absolutely essential for delivering the services You see on Our Website and letting You tap into all its cool features. They're like Our bouncers, helping to verify users and shut down any funky business with accounts. Seriously, without these, the stuff You want won't work, and We only use them to provide those core services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These little guys just remember if You've said "yes" to cookies on the Website. Simple as that!

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These are the "smart" Cookies! They help Us remember Your choices when You're using the Website – think login info or Your preferred language. Their main goal? To give You a smoother, more personal experience so You don't have to keep telling Us Your preferences every single time.

Want the full lowdown on Our Cookies and Your options? Just hop over to Our Cookies Policy or check out the Cookies section right here in Our Privacy Policy.
