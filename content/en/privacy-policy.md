### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We're using Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. These tracking goodies, like beacons, tags, and scripts, help us gather and track data, all so we can supercharge and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies, if you're fancy)**: Picture a Cookie as a tiny file tucked away on your Device. You're totally in control – you can tell your browser to give all Cookies the boot or to give you a heads-up when one's coming your way. Just a friendly warning: if you don't play nice with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work as smoothly for you. Unless you've told your browser otherwise, our Service might just use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Look out for tiny electronic files, sometimes called web beacons (or clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs), chilling in certain parts of our Service and our emails. These little guys let us, for example, count how many users have visited specific pages or opened an email. They're also super handy for other website stats, like tracking the popularity of a section or making sure our systems and servers are running smoothly.

So, Cookies come in two flavors: 'Persistent' and 'Session.' Persistent Cookies stick around on your computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few different reasons, as detailed below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are the absolute must-haves for making our Website's services available to you and letting you use its coolest features. They're like our bouncers, helping to verify user identity and stop any sneaky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Honestly, without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't happen – we only use them to get those services to you!

- **Cookies Policy / Notification Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are like a little memory trick, helping us remember if you've already given us the thumbs-up to use Cookies on the Website. 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These clever Cookies let us remember your choices when you're cruising around the Website, like keeping your login deets or language preference stored. The whole point? To give you a super personalized experience so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you visit. Sweet! ✨

Want to dig deeper into the Cookies we use and what choices you've got? Head over to our dedicated Cookies Policy or check out the Cookies section right here in our Privacy Policy.
