### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We totally use Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep an eye on activity within our Service and store some info. These tracking goodies include stuff like Beacons, Tags, and Scripts, all designed to grab and track data, helping us fine-tune and analyze our Service. The tech we use might look something like this: 👇

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that gets placed on your Device. You're the boss here: you can tell your browser to totally reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But a fair warning: if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say 'no way' to Cookies, our Service might just be using them. Just sayin'! 😉
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and even our emails might have these tiny electronic files, often called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (us!) do things like count how many users checked out those pages or opened an email. They're also super handy for other website stats, like tracking how popular a certain section is or making sure our systems and servers are rock solid. Pretty neat, huh? 💡

Cookies come in two flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your computer or mobile device even after you go offline. Session Cookies? Poof! ✨ They vanish as soon as you close your web browser. Out of sight, out of mind!

We totally use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's the lowdown on why: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are an absolute must-have to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use its cool features. They help us authenticate users and stop any dodgy, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to make sure you get those services. No kidding! 🔒

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about checking if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website. It's like a little digital handshake! 👋

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies help us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or your preferred language. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website. Smart, right? 🧠

Want more deets on the Cookies we use and what choices you've got? Feel free to check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section right here in our Privacy Policy. Knowledge is power! 🚀
