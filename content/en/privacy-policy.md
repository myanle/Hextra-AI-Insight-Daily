### Tracking Tech & Cookies 🍪

**We totally use Cookies and similar tracking technologies** to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store some info. These tracking technologies also include things like beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: **A cookie** is just a tiny file that lands on your Device. You can tell your browser to kick all Cookies to the curb or to give you a heads-up when one's trying to sneak in. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to refuse 'em, our Service might just use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: **Certain parts of our Service and our emails** might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These handy little things let the Company, for example, count users who've checked out those pages or opened an email, and they're used for other cool website stats (like seeing which section is popular or making sure our systems and servers are all good).

**Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies.** Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish as soon as you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

**We use both Session and Persistent Cookies** for the reasons listed below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its features. They're super helpful for authenticating users and stopping any fishy business with user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies for that very purpose.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given the green light to use cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like keeping your login deets or language preference in mind. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience so you don't have to type in your preferences every single time you visit. Phew!

**Want more deets** on the cookies we use and your options? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
