### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're big fans of Cookies and other sneaky tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and store some info. These tracking tools include things like beacons, tags, and scripts. They help us gather and follow data, ultimately making our Service better and letting us analyze its usage. The specific tech we might use includes:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that lands on your device. You can totally tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or to ask you before one gets sent. But here's the catch: if you don't accept 'em, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Heads up, unless you've tweaked your browser to reject Cookies, our Service will likely be using them. Just sayin'! 😉
-   **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These little guys let us, for instance, count how many folks have visited certain pages or opened an email. They're also used for other cool website stats, like checking how popular a section is or making sure our systems and servers are running smoothly.

So, Cookies come in two flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session.' Persistent Cookies stick around on your computer or mobile device even after you've logged off, like a digital echo. Session Cookies, though? Poof! They vanish the moment you close your browser. They're more of a 'here today, gone today' kind of deal. 👋

We totally use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's why: (We've got reasons, alright! 😉)

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**<br>
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies<br>
    -   **Managed by**: Us (that's the Company!)<br>
    -   **Why we use 'em**: These are the absolute MVPs! 🌟 They're crucial for giving you the services available on our Website and letting you use all its cool features. They're also super important for verifying who you are and keeping those pesky fraudsters away from your account. No joke, without these, the stuff you're asking for just wouldn't work. We only use them to make sure you get the Service you came for.

-   **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Notice Cookies**<br>
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies<br>
    -   **Managed by**: Us<br>
    -   **Why we use 'em**: These little guys simply remember if you've already given us the thumbs-up to use Cookies on our Website. Once you say yes, they help us remember that choice. 👍

-   **Functionality Cookies**<br>
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies<br>
    -   **Managed by**: Us<br>
    -   **Why we use 'em**: These smart Cookies help us recall your choices when you're cruising through our Website. Think remembering your login info or your fave language. Their whole gig is to give you a smoother, more personalized experience so you don't have to punch in your preferences every single time. Convenience, right? ✨

Wanna dive deeper into the world of Cookies we use and what choices you've got? Head over to our Cookies Policy or just check out the Cookies section right here in our Privacy Policy. It's all there!
