### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain information. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all working to collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to let you know when one is being sent. However, if you don't play along with Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've adjusted your browser setting to refuse Cookies, our Service might just go ahead and use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These handy files let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like noting the popularity of a certain section and double-checking system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help verify user identities and prevent any fishy business with user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to make sure you get those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are here to figure out if users have given their OK to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, such as your login details or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more information about the Cookies we use and your options concerning Cookies, feel free to swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
