### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about **Cookies and similar tracking technologies** to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain info. These tech tools, like beacons, tags, and scripts, help us collect and track information, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies)**: A Cookie is a small file dropped onto your device. You can totally tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, heads up! If you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject 'em, our Service might just use Cookies. 🍪
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and emails might have these tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These bad boys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and help out with other website stats (like checking out a section's popularity or making sure systems and servers are all good).

**Cookies** can come in two flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We totally use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital to hook you up with services available through the Website and let you use its cool features. They're key to authenticating users and stopping fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be delivered, and we only use 'em to give you those services. No kidding! 👍

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are here to figure out if users have given the green light to the use of cookies on the Website. It's all about your consent! ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are awesome because they let us remember your choices when you're using the Website, like your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the Website. Talk about convenience! 😎

Wanna know more about the **Cookies we use** and your choices about 'em? Just cruise over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. All the info's there! 👇
