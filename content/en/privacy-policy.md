### Tracking Tech & Cookies: The Lowdown
We use Cookies and other similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking tech we roll with includes beacons, tags, and scripts. These are all about collecting and tracking info, helping us level up 🚀 our Service and analyze how you're using it. Here’s what we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your Device. You can totally tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. So, unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject 'em, our Service might just use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain these little electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome files let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other related website stats (like checking out a section's popularity and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's why:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital to hook you up with services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and keep fraudulent use of user accounts at bay. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to make sure you get those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given a nod to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty neat! They let us remember choices you make while using the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website. Talk about convenience! ✨

For even more deets on the Cookies we use and your options concerning 'em, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
