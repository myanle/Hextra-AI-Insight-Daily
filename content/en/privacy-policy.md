### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our service and stash away certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, helping us to improve and analyze our service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file chillin' on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our service might just go ahead and use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and they're used for other related website stats (like checking out a section's popularity and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either “Persistent” or “Session” Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies get wiped out the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes listed below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're super helpful for authenticating users and keeping user accounts safe from fraudulent use. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't work, and we only use 'em to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're cruising around the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more information on the Cookies we use and your choices regarding Cookies, feel free to swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
