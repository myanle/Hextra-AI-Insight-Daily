### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. Think of the tracking tech we use — like beacons, tags, and scripts — as tools to collect, track, and then totally fine-tune and analyze our Service. The tech we use could include: 🍪

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie? It's just a tiny file plopped onto your device. You can tell your browser to totally reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But heads up: if you don't take the Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say 'no thanks' to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them. Just sayin'! 🤷‍♀️
- **Web Beacons**: Some juicy parts of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files, known as web beacons (or clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for example, count users who've popped onto those pages or opened an email, and they're also super handy for other website stats (like checking out a section's popularity and keeping our system and servers shipshape). Pretty neat, huh? ✨

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies? They stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline. Session Cookies, however, peace out the moment you slam shut your web browser. Poof! 💨

We bust out both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital for hookin' you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're like bouncers, helping to authenticate users and put a stop to any dodgy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't fly, and we only use them to bring you those services. No kidding! 🙏

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to check if users have given the green light to the use of Cookies on the Website. Simple as that! ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super helpful because they let us remember your choices when you're rocking the Website – things like recalling your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized vibe and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you visit the Website. Pretty sweet, right? 🤩

For more deets on the Cookies we use and your options when it comes to Cookies, swing by our [Cookies Policy](我们的 Cookies 政策的 Cookies 部分) or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. Get informed! 🤓
