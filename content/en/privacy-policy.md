### Tracking Tech & Cookies: What's the Deal? 🍪
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain bits of info. The tracking tech we roll with includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to gather and track info, and generally make our Service better and analyze how it's used. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies):** A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But heads up, if you don't play along with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service will likely use 'em.
- **Web Beacons:** Some sections of our Service and our emails might contain these little electronic files (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These bad boys let the Company (for example) count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like seeing how popular a certain section is and checking the integrity of the system and server).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital to get you the services available through the Website and to enable you to use some of its features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to bring you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies figure out if users have given their thumbs-up to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies help us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more deets about the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, hop over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
