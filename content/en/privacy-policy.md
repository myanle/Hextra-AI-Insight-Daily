### Tracking Technologies and Cookies

We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts. They help us gather and track info, then level up and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

*   **Cookies or Browser Cookies:** A Cookie is a small file dropped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to kick all Cookies to the curb or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service might just use them.

*   **Web Beacons:** Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These files let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other related website stats, like checking out a section's popularity and verifying system and server integrity.

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the second you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

*   **Essential / Necessary Cookies**
    *   **Type:** Session Cookies
    *   **Administered by:** Us
    *   **Purpose:** These Cookies are super important for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its awesome features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

*   **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
    *   **Type:** Persistent Cookies
    *   **Administered by:** Us
    *   **Purpose:** These Cookies figure out if users have said "yes" to Cookies on the Website.

*   **Functionality Cookies**
    *   **Type:** Persistent Cookies
    *   **Administered by:** Us
    *   **Purpose:** These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preference. The goal of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every time you hit up the Website.

For more juicy details about the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
