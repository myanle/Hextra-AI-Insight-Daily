We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to monitor activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts, which are used to collect and track info, and to improve and analyze our Service. The technologies we might use include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies** `[Cookies 或浏览器 Cookies]`: A Cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to indicate when a Cookie is being sent. - **Web Beacons** `[Web Beacons]`: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These files allow the Company, for example, to count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website statistics (e.g., recording the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. When you go offline, Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device, while Session Cookies get deleted the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies** `[必要/基本 Cookies]`
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are essential for enabling you to use some features, and for providing you with services available through the Website. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notification Acceptance Cookies** `[Cookies 政策 / 通知接受 Cookies]`
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies identify if users have accepted the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies** `[功能性 Cookies]`
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies enable us to remember choices you make when you use the Website, like remembering your login details or language preference. The goal of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every time you use the Website.
