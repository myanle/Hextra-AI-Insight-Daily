### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
Cookies and similar tracking technologies are what we use to track activity on our Service and hold onto certain info. These tracking technologies include beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to collect and track information, plus to improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to let you know when one is being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've adjusted your browser setting to refuse Cookies, our Service might use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These files let the Company, for example, count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website statistics (like checking the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely essential to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its features. They help authenticate users and stop fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to give you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies figure out if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember choices you make when you use the Website, like remembering your login details or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personal experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every time you use the Website.

For more info about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding them, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
