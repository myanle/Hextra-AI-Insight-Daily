### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away some info. This tracking tech includes things like beacons, tags, and scripts. Their job? To collect and track info, and help us improve and analyze our Service. Here's a peek at the tech we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that lands on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But here's the catch: if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to outright refuse 'em, our Service will likely be using Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: You might spot these tiny electronic files, also called web beacons (or clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs!), in certain parts of our Service and our emails. They let us do cool things like count how many users visited a page or opened an email, and give us other website stats (like checking how popular a section is or making sure our systems are all good).

So, Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones stick around on your computer or phone even when you're offline. Session Cookies? They vanish as soon as you close your browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few reasons, which we've laid out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for giving you the services available on our Website and letting you use its features. They're super helpful for authenticating users and stopping any sneaky, fraudulent use of accounts. Seriously, without these, the services you're asking for just can't happen, and we only use them to get those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply tell us if you've given the thumbs-up to using cookies on our Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty neat because they let us remember your choices when you're using the Website – like your login info or what language you prefer. The whole point? To give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep re-entering your preferences every single time you visit. Sweet!

Want more deets on the Cookies we use and what your options are? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy.
