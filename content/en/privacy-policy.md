### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking tech we roll with includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The technologies we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file planted on your Device. You can tell your browser to totally refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've adjusted your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service might just go ahead and use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for instance, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies totally vanish the second you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes, FYI:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are a must-have for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Notice Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given a nod to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're rocking the Website, like keeping your login deets or language preferences in mind. The whole point of these Cookies is to hook you up with a more personalized experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more info on the Cookies we use and the choices you have about Cookies, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
