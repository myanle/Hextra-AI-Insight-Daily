### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
**Heads up! We use Cookies and other similar tracking tech** to keep an eye on what's happening on our Service and to save some info. We're talking beacons, tags, and scripts – all to gather and track data, so we can continuously improve and understand how you're using our Service. Some of the tech we use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: These are just tiny files chillin' on your device. You can totally tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's coming. But here's the catch: if you don't accept 'em, some parts of our Service might not play nice. So, unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say 'no' to Cookies, chances are our Service will be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: You might spot these tiny electronic files in certain parts of our Service or in our emails (they're also called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). They help us, for example, count how many folks have visited certain pages or opened an email. Plus, they're useful for other cool website stats, like tracking a section's popularity and making sure our systems and servers are all good.

**So, Cookies** come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones stick around on your computer or phone even after you're offline. Session Cookies, though? Poof! They're gone the moment you close your web browser.

**We** use both types of Cookies, Session and Persistent, for the reasons listed below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for giving you the services available on our Website and letting you use all its cool features. They're like the bouncers, helping to verify users and stop any shady, fraudulent account use. Honestly, without these, the services you're asking for just wouldn't be possible, and we only use them to get those services to you. Simple as that!

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply remember if you've already given us the thumbs-up to use cookies on our Website. Easy!

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty handy because they let us remember your choices when you're cruising around the Website – like keeping your login deets or language preference stored. Basically, they're here to give you a more personal experience, so you don't have to re-type your preferences every single time. Score! 🤩

**Wanna know more about the Cookies** we use and your options? Just hop over to our Cookies Policy or check out the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy!
