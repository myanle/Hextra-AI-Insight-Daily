### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away some info. The tracking tech we roll with includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track data, and to beef up and analyze our Service. The specific tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file plopped onto your Device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, heads up: if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use certain awesome parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service might still use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain these tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for instance, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and they're also super handy for other website stats (like tracking a section's popularity and checking system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you're offline, but Session Cookies get zapped as soon as you close your web browser.

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the reasons we're about to lay out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are totally essential for delivering the services available through the Website and letting you tap into some of its cool features. They're super helpful for authenticating users and putting a stop to any shady use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply wouldn't be possible, and we only use 'em to make sure you get those services. 👍

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are basically how we know if you've given the green light to use Cookies on the Website. ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are the brainy ones that let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like keeping tabs on your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a smoother, more personalized experience and save you the hassle of typing in your preferences every single time you visit. ✨

For more juicy details about the Cookies we use and what choices you have concerning them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section right here in our Privacy Policy.
