### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪🕵️‍♀️
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, helping us improve and analyze our Service. The tech we deploy might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file snuggled onto your Device. You're in control! You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, if you don't play ball with Cookies, you might find some parts of our Service a bit tricky to use. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service might just use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also called clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These nifty files let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other related website stats (like checking out how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. When you go offline, Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you slam shut your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to make sure you get those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given the green light to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super handy because they let us remember choices you make when you use the Website – like keeping your login deets or language preferences in mind. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, saving you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you visit the Website.

Want more info on the Cookies we use and your options concerning them? Swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy!
