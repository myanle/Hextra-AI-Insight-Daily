### Tracking Tech & Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking tech we roll with includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all for grabbing and tracking info, plus improving and analyzing our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies** 👀: A Cookie is a tiny file plonked on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, if you don't say yes to Cookies, you might not be able to use certain bits of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons** (aka transparent GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs): Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called Web Beacons. They let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and they're also used for other related website stats (like logging a section's popularity and checking system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. When you're offline, Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air as soon as you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies** 🔑
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are the real MVPs for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent sketchy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies** ✅
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These bad boys are used to figure out if users have given the thumbs-up to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies** 🛠️
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super handy because they let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For the full lowdown on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. 🍪
