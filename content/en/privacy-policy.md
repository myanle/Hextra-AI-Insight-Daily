### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
**We** use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep an eye on activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts to collect and track information and to improve and analyze our Service. **These technologies** might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file popped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to let you know when one is being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser setting to refuse Cookies, our Service might just go ahead and use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). **These beacons** permit the Company, for example, to count users who have visited those pages or opened an email and for other related website statistics (e.g., recording the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

**Cookies** can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stay on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, while Session Cookies get nuked as soon as you close your web browser.
**We** use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered By**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are crucial for giving you services available through the Website and enabling you to use some of its cool features. **They** help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered By**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies figure out if users have given the thumbs-up to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered By**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preferences. **The whole point of these Cookies** is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

**For more info** about the cookies we use and your choices regarding them, hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
