### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on Our Service and store certain information. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track information, as well as to improve and analyze Our Service. The technologies we deploy might be: 🧐

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on Your Device. You can tell Your browser to refuse all Cookies or to alert You when one is being sent. But hey, if You don't accept Cookies, You might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless You've tweaked Your browser settings to reject Cookies, Our Service is likely using them. 🍪
- **Web Beacons**: Look out for tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) in some parts of our Service and emails. These allow the Company, for example, to count users who've visited specific pages or opened an email. They're also used for other cool website statistics, like checking out a section's popularity and verifying system and server integrity.

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" and "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on Your personal computer or mobile device even after You go offline, but Session Cookies vanish into thin air as soon as You close Your web browser. ✨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following awesome purposes: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies** 🤩
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely essential to deliver the services available through the Website and let You use some of its cool features. They're key to authenticating users and preventing any sneaky fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these bad boys, the services You've asked for simply can't be provided, and We use them *only* for that reason.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are like a little flag that tells us if You've accepted the use of Cookies on the Website. Simple, right? 😉

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super handy because they let Us remember the choices You make when using the Website, like Your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these is to give You a more personalized experience, so You don't have to keep re-entering Your preferences every single time You visit. ✨

For more info about the Cookies we use and Your choices regarding them, head over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. Got questions? We've got answers! 👇
