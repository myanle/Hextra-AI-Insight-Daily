### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away some info. The tracking tech we roll with includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track information, plus juice up and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes: 💻

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file plunked down on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't play ball with Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service might just use them. 🍪
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for instance, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out the popularity of a certain section and making sure the system and server are running smooth). ✨

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. When you go offline, Persistent Cookies hang out on your personal computer or mobile device. Session Cookies, though, vanish the moment you slam shut your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for hooking you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help verify users' identities and stop shady, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these bad boys, the services you're asking for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to give you those services. No joke! 🔒

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Notice Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to check if users have given the green light to the use of cookies on the Website. So, did you? 🤔

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website. Sweet, right? 🤩

For even more juicy info about the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. Happy reading! 📖
