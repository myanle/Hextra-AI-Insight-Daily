### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
Alright, let's talk tracking! 🧐 We use Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity within our Service and store a few bits of info. These cool technologies include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to gather and track data, helping us supercharge our Service and figure out how folks are using it. Here’s a peek at what we might use:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies, if you want to get technical!)** 🍪. Think of a Cookie as a tiny file nestled onto your device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up whenever one's being sent. But here's the catch: if you don't accept Cookies, some cool parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, chances are our Service will be using them.
- **Web Beacons.** Some corners of our Service and even our emails might feature tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These nifty little guys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited certain pages or opened an email and help with other website stats (like checking out a section's popularity or making sure our systems and servers are all good).

Okay, so Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" and "Session" Cookies. Think of Persistent Cookies as the ones that stick around on your computer or mobile device even after you've logged off. Session Cookies, on the other hand, are like fleeting memories—they vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's why:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital! They're what allow us to provide you with the services available through the Website and enable you to use some of its super cool features. They help verify user identities and prevent any sneaky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply wouldn't be possible, and we only use them to deliver those specific services to you. Think of them as the backbone of our service! 💪

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty straightforward: they're used to figure out if users have given us the thumbs-up to use Cookies on the Website. It's all about making sure you're in the loop! 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These smart Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website – like keeping your login deets or language preferences in mind. The whole point? To give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you visit. Super convenient! ✨

Want more deets on the Cookies we use and what choices you have? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section right here in our Privacy Policy!
