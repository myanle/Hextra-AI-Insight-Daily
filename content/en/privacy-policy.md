### Tracking Tech and Cookies 🍪
**We're big on using Cookies and similar tracking tech** to keep tabs on activity on our Service and squirrel away certain info. The tracking tech we roll with includes beacons, tags, and scripts. They're all about collecting and tracking info, plus helping us spruce up and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies):** A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your Device. You're the boss here: you can tell your browser to straight-up refuse all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. Heads up though, if you don't accept Cookies, some bits of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service will likely use 'em.
- **Web Beacons:** Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also called clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other related website stats, like logging a section's popularity and double-checking system and server integrity.

**Cookies** can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you're offline. Session Cookies, on the other hand, vanish into thin air as soon as you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

**We use both Session and Persistent Cookies** for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type:** Session Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are super important for getting you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and stop any shady use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are all about checking if users have given the thumbs-up to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personal experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you hit up the Website.

**More info about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding Cookies?** You can find that in our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
