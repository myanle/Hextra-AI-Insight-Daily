### Tracking Technologies & Cookies 🍪
We're using Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store some info. The tracking technologies we deploy include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track information, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But here's the kicker: if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service will likely use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Web Beacons are those tiny electronic files (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) that some parts of our Service and our emails might contain. These little guys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and are used for other related website stats (like tracking how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We totally use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super critical for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're all about authenticating users and putting a stop to fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have given a thumbs-up to the use of cookies on the Website. 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you hit up the Website.

For more juicy details about the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
