### Tracking Technologies and Cookies

We totally use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track information, and generally make our Service better and help us analyze how you use it. The tech we deploy might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file dropped on your Device. You can tell your browser to give all Cookies the boot or to alert you when one's being sent. But heads up, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain these tiny electronic files, called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out how popular a certain section is and making sure the system and server are running smoothly).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and stop any dodgy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies figure out if users have given the green light to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For the full scoop on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
