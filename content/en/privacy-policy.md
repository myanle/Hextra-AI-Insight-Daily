### Tracking Technologies & Cookies 🍪
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track information, plus improve and analyze our Service. Here's a peek at what we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies** are tiny files dropped onto your device. You can tell your browser to kick out all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But heads up: if you don't play ball with Cookies, you might not be able to use some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons:** Some sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out the popularity of a certain section and making sure the system and server are running smoothly).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type:** Session Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are super crucial for hooking you up with services available through the Website and letting you use its awesome features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to get those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given the thumbs-up to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every single time you hit up the Website. So convenient! ✨

Want more deets on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
