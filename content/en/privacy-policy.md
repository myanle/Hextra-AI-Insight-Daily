### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activity within our Service and store certain info. These tech tools include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file tucked away on your Device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service will likely use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also called clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out a section's popularity and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. When you're offline, Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device. Session Cookies, on the other hand, vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof!

We roll with both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for hooking you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're super important for authenticating users and stopping any shady, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to give you those services. No kidding!

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about checking if users have given their OK to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember your choices when you're cruising around the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every single time you visit the Website. Sweet, right? 😎

For more deets on the Cookies we use and your choices, just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
