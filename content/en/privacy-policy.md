### Tracking Tech & Cookies 🍪
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep an eye on activity on our Service and to stash some info. These tracking goodies include things like beacons, tags, and scripts. They help us collect and track info, and generally make our Service better and more insightful. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies, as some folks call 'em)**: So, a Cookie is just a tiny file that gets popped onto your Device. You totally have the power to tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, if you don't play nice with Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Just a heads-up: unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say 'no thanks' to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them. 🍪
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons. (You might also hear them called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs.) These little guys let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other cool website stats, like checking how popular a section is or making sure our systems and servers are all good. ✨

Cookies come in two main flavors: 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. Persistent ones hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline. Session Cookies, on the other hand, vanish into thin air as soon as you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

So, why do we use both Session and Persistent Cookies? Glad you asked! Here's why: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use them to get those services to you. Think of them as the unsung heroes! 🦸

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have given the green light to the use of Cookies on the Website. It's how we know you're cool with it! ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super handy because they let us remember choices you make when you're using the Website. Think remembering your login deets or your language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you visit. Smart, right? 🧠

Wanna dive deeper into the Cookies we use and what choices you've got? Head on over to our Cookies Policy or just check out the Cookies section of *this very* Privacy Policy! 📖
