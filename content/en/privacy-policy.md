### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts – all for collecting and tracking info, plus improving and analyzing our Service. The tech we might use includes: 👇

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a small file dropped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to give all Cookies the boot or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But heads up, if you don't play nice with Cookies, you might not be able to use some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to deny Cookies, our Service might just use 'em. 🚫🍪
- **Web Beacons**: Certain bits of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for example, count users who've popped onto those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity). They're pretty neat! ✨

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 👻

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital for hooking you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and stop any dodgy, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for just can't be delivered, and we only use 'em to give you those services. Can't live without 'em! 🙌

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to see if users have given the green light to the use of Cookies on the Website. Simple as that! ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are what let us remember the choices you make when you're cruising the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you from having to type in your preferences every single time you use the Website. Super handy! ✨

For more juicy details about the Cookies we use and your options regarding Cookies, head over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. Get informed! 📚
