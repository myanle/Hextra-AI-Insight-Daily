### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to monitor activity on our Service and store certain information. The tracking technologies we employ include beacons, tags, and scripts, which help us collect and track information, as well as improve and analyze our Service. Our technologies might include:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to alert you when one is being sent. -   **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These files enable the Company, for example, to count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website statistics (e.g., recording the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies get wiped immediately once you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Managed by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for providing you with services available through the Website and enabling you to use some of its features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use them to get you those services.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Managed by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies tell us if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Managed by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like remembering your login details or language preferences. The idea behind these Cookies is to give you a more personal experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every time you use the Website.

For more info on the Cookies we use and your choices regarding cookies, feel free to check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
