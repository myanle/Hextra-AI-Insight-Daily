### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity within Our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking technologies we deploy include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, and generally give Our Service a glow-up through improvement and analysis. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file that lands on your Device. You can tell your browser to totally reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. Heads up, though: if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of Our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, Our Service might just go ahead and use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of Our Service and Our emails might feature tiny electronic files, known as web beacons (also called clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other juicy website stats (like noting the popularity of a certain section and making sure systems and servers are running smoothly).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies get wiped clean the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for hooking you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and slam the door on fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to make those services happen for you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given their thumbs-up to the use of Cookies on the Website. 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let Us remember the choices you make when you're cruising the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website. Smart, right? 🧠

For even more info on the Cookies We use and your options regarding them, just swing by Our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of Our Privacy Policy.
