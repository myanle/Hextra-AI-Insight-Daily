### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activity on our Service and to stash away certain info. These tracking technologies include things like beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to collect and track info, plus to boost and analyze our Service. Here's a peek at what we use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file that gets cozy on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But here's the kicker: if you don’t accept Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've fiddled with your browser settings to decline Cookies, our Service will likely use 'em. Just sayin'! 🍪
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These nifty little files let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened emails, and they're also used for other related website stats (like tracking how popular a certain section is and making sure the system and server are running shipshape). ✨

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies get deleted the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**<br>- **Type**: Session Cookies<br>- **Managed by**: Us<br>- **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for hooking you up with the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help verify who you are and stop any funny business with user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you ask for just wouldn't fly, and we only use 'em to bring those services to you. Period. 🛡️

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**<br>- **Type**: Persistent Cookies<br>- **Managed by**: Us<br>- **Purpose**: These Cookies are here to check if users have given the green light to the use of Cookies on the Website. Simple as that! ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**<br>- **Type**: Persistent Cookies<br>- **Managed by**: Us<br>- **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty neat because they let us remember your choices when you're using the Website – like keeping your login info or language preferences tucked away. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you drop by the Website. How convenient is that? ✨

For more deets on the Cookies we use and your options regarding 'em, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. You got this! 👇
