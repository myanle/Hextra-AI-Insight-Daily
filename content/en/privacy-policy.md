### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to monitor activity on our Service and store certain information. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, which help us collect and track information, and also improve and analyze our Service. Our technologies might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies:** A Cookie is a small file placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to indicate when a Cookie is being sent. However, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've adjusted your browser setting to refuse Cookies, our Service might use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons:** Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These files permit the Company, for example, to count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website statistics (e.g., recording a section's popularity and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stay on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, while Session Cookies are deleted as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type:** Session Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are essential to provide you with services available through the Website and to let you use some of its features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are used to determine if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies let us remember choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login details or language preferences. The goal of these Cookies is to give you a more personal experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every time you use the Website.

For more information about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding Cookies, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
