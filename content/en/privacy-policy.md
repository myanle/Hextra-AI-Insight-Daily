### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪
We totally use Cookies and other similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to gather and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. *Here's a peek at what we might use:*

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file planted on your device. You can tell your browser to totally reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when a Cookie is being sent. But, if you don't play nice with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not be available to you. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These cool files allow the Company (for example) to count users who've swung by those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like, checkin' out the popularity of a certain section and making sure the system and server are running smoothly).

**Cookies** can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline, while Session Cookies get the boot the second you close your web browser.

*We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:*

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help us double-check user identities and prevent any sneaky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for totally can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have given the green light to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of typing in your preferences every single time you use the Website.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options regarding them? Swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
