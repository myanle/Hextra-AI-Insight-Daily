### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
**We** use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts. These are all about collecting and tracking info, plus improving and analyzing our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to let you know when one is being sent. But, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser setting to refuse Cookies, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like, say, recording the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

**Cookies** can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. When you're offline, Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device, while Session Cookies get deleted the instant you close your web browser.

**We** use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: **These Cookies** are super important for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use 'em to give you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance of Cookies Notice**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: **These Cookies** are all about checking if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: **These Cookies** let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every single time you hit up the Website.

**For more info** about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding them, go ahead and visit our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
