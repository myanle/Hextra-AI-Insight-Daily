### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and other similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our service and to stash certain info. The tracking tech we roll with includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to gather and track info, plus boost and analyze our service. The tech we use could include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file plonked onto your device. You can tell your browser to straight-up refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to wave goodbye to Cookies, our service might just use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our service and our emails might contain these little electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These bad boys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other juicy website stats (like tracking a section's popularity and making sure systems and servers are all good).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital to hook you up with services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help verify users and put a stop to any shady use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for can't be delivered, and we only use 'em to give you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given a thumbs-up to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of typing in your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more info on the Cookies we use and what choices you've got regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
