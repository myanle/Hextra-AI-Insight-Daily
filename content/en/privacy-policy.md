### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity within our Service and store some info. The tracking tech we roll with includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track data, helping us fine-tune and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file dropped onto your device. You can tell your browser to give all Cookies the cold shoulder or to alert you when one's being sent. But, heads up, if you don't play along with Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject 'em, our Service might just use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These nifty little files let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like tracking the popularity of a certain section and making sure the system and server are all good).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for dishing out services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help verify user identity and put a stop to any fishy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use 'em to bring those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to spot whether users have given the thumbs-up to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're rocking the Website, like keeping your login deets or language preference in mind. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more custom experience and save you from having to punch in your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more deets on the Cookies we use and your options concerning 'em, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
