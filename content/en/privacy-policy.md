### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking technologies we deploy include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, and to improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file plonked onto your device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. However, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser setting to refuse Cookies, our Service might just use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also dubbed clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These snazzy files let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like noting the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes laid out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are crucial for giving you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to check if users have given the green light to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every time you use the Website.

For more info about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding cookies, feel free to swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
