### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to monitor activity on our Service and store certain information. Our tracking technologies include beacons, tags, and scripts, which help us collect and track information, as well as improve and analyze our Service. The technologies we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on Your Device. You can tell Your browser to refuse all Cookies or to alert You when a Cookie is being sent. However, if You don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless You've adjusted your browser settings to decline Cookies, our Service may use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails may contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These allow the Company to, for example, count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website statistics (like tracking a section's popularity and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stay on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies are erased as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital for providing you with services available through the Website and enabling you to use some of its features. They help verify user identities and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've requested cannot be provided, and we use them solely for that purpose.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies identify whether users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies enable us to remember choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login details or language preferences. Their goal is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every time you visit.

For more details about the cookies we use and your options regarding them, please check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
